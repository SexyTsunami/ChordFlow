Yum Yum Breakfast Burrito
Artist: Parry Gripp

Key: F major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar commonly uses D shapes, capo 3 (`D – A – Em – G`, concert `F – C – Gm – Bb`). Chordify’s original-pitch chart is F; Ultimate Guitar’s “Key: C” label on the capo-3 D-shape chart is a mislabel.

**Intro**

`F – C – Gm – Bb`

**Chorus**

`F – C – Gm – Bb`

**Verse**

`F – C – Gm – Bb`

Each line of the verse is this four-bar loop.

**Bridge**

`C – Bb – F`
`C – Bb – C`

**Break**

`F – C – Gm – Bb`

Same vamp as the intro, twice.

**Outro**

`F – C – Gm – Bb – F`

**Form**

Intro → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Bridge → Break → Verse → Chorus → Outro
