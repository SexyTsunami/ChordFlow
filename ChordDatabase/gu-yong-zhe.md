孤勇者 (Gu Yong Zhe / Lonely Warrior)
Artist: Eason Chan (陳奕迅 / 陈奕迅)

Mandarin theme for Arcane (League of Legends: 雙城之戰) season 1. Key: G# minor (relative B major), 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar commonly uses Em shapes, capo 4. Chordify and kanpu agree on G# minor; some guitar sites label B major from the relative-major charts (G shapes, capo 4). Chord4’s capo-2 Em chart sits a whole step below concert (F# minor). Verse voicings vary: denser charts use G#m–G#m/F#–Emaj7; many busking charts use G#m–Bmaj7–E instead.
