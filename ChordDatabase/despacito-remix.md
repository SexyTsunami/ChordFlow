Despacito (Remix)
Artist: Luis Fonsi, Daddy Yankee, Justin Bieber

Key: B minor, 4/4. Wikipedia lists the original in B minor at about 89 BPM with `Bm – G – D – A`; the Bieber remix keeps that loop and adds an English verse. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar often uses capo 2 and plays `Am – F – C – G`.

**Intro**

`Bm – G – D – A`

**Verse**

`Bm – G – D – A`

**Pre-Chorus**

`Bm – G – D – A`

**Chorus**

`Bm – G – D – A`

**Bridge**

`Bm – G – D – A`

Bieber’s English verse uses the same four chords as Fonsi and Daddy Yankee.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Verse (Bieber) → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus
