New Rules
Artist: Dua Lipa

Key: A minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original tropical-house arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Wikipedia, Chordify, E-Chords, GuitarTuna, and Hooktheory agree on A minor and `Am – G – F – G`. Guitar stays in A minor with no capo. Easy charts that print only `Am – F – G` drop the `G` after `Am`. Auto-detectors sometimes add `Em`, `Dm7`, or `Fmaj7` as passing color; the four-chord loop is the reduction.

**Intro**

`Am – G – F – G`

Counted “one” hits over the verse loop.

**Verse**

`Am – G – F – G`

**Pre-Chorus**

`Am – G – F – G`

The numbered rules sit on the same loop. Some guitar charts hang on `Am` for a full line, then `F – G` on the answer.

**Chorus**

`Am – G – F – G`

**Bridge**

`Am – G – F – G`

Practice-makes-perfect middle eight; still A minor, no new harmony.

**Outro**

`Am – G – F – G`

Last chorus repeats the loop to the end.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Bridge → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Outro
