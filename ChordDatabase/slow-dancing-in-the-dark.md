SLOW DANCING IN THE DARK
Artist: Joji

Key: Eb major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction of the BALLADS 1 studio cut; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Musicnotes and the more detailed guitar charts agree on Eb major; some guitar sites spell the same concert pitch as D#. Guitar usually uses capo 3 and plays in C (`Cmaj7 – Am7 – Dm7 – Em7 – E7`). The record’s harp loop is closer to `Ebmaj7 – Cm9 – Fm9 – Gm7 – G7`; beginner charts drop the sevenths to `Eb – Cm – Fm – Gm – G`.

**Intro**

`Ebmaj7 – Cm7 – Fm7 – Gm7 – G7`

**Verse**

`Ebmaj7 – Cm7 – Fm7 – Gm7 – G7`

**Pre-Chorus**

`Ebmaj7 – Cm7 – Fm7 – Gm7 – G7`

Same loop as the verse for the first pre-chorus. The second pre-chorus (“used to be the one”) often starts on `C – C7` before returning to `Fm7 – Gm7 – G7`.

**Chorus**

`Ebmaj7 – Abmaj7`

The title tag stays on this `Ebmaj7 – Abmaj7` vamp. Beginner charts print `Eb – Ab`.

**Outro**

`Ebmaj7 – Abmaj7`

Later hits sometimes color the last pass with `Fm` before the final `Ab`.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Outro
