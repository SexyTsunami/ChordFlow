In Love With You
Artist: Jacky Cheung (Jackie Cheung / 張學友 / 张学友) and Regine Velasquez (黎晶)

English-title duet from 真愛 新曲 + 真正精選 / Listen Without Prejudice. Key: Eb major, 4/4, with the chorus modulating through F# and E and a late lift into C. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar commonly uses C shapes, capo 3.

**Intro**

`Eb – Bb/D – G7/B – Cm – Eb/Bb – Ab – Eb/G – Fm7`

**Verse**

`Eb – Eb/G – Ab – Cm7`
`Ab – Eb/G – Fm7 – Bb`
`Eb – Eb/G – Ab – Cm7`
`Fm7 – Bb – Eb`

**Pre-chorus**

`B – F#/A# – G#m – C# – F#`
`B – F#/A# – G#m`
`B – D#m – C#`

**Chorus**

`F# – C# – A#7 – D#m – F# – B`
`E – A – E – C# – F#m – A – B`
`D – E`

**Late chorus lift**

`C – G – E7 – Am – C – D – F – G – A`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Interlude (pre-chorus loop) → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Late chorus lift → Outro
