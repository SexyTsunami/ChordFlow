Thinking out Loud
Artist: Ed Sheeran

Key: D major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Musicnotes’ published key is D major (shuffle feel). Guitar often uses capo 2 and plays in C (`C – C/E – F – G`, concert `D – D/F# – G – A`). No-capo charts stay on the D shapes.

**Intro**

`D – D/F# – G – A`

**Verse**

`D – D/F# – G – A`

**Pre-Chorus**

`Em – A – D`
`Em – A`
`Em – A – Bm`
`Em – A`

The walk back into the chorus is `D – D/F#`.

**Chorus**

`G – A – D – D/F#`
`G – A – D – D/F#`
`G – A – D – D/F#`
`G – A`
`Bm – A – G – D – Em – A – D`

Some beginner pages print the chorus as the verse loop `D – D/F# – G – A`; the record’s title lines start on `G`.

**Outro**

`Bm – A – G – D – Em – A – D`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Chorus → Outro
