Scared to Be Lonely
Artist: Martin Garrix, Dua Lipa

Key: C-sharp minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original future-bass arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices (Wikipedia lists `Aadd9 – C#m7/E – Badd4 – G#m(b6)`). Hooktheory and production notes agree on C-sharp minor; Chordify labels the relative E major because the chorus cadences on `E`. Guitar often uses capo 2: verses `G – Bm – A – F#m`, chorus `G – D – A – Bm`. Ultimate Guitar’s popular capo-2 chart uses the chorus shapes for the whole song and skips the verse `G#m`.

**Intro**

`A – C#m`

Piano and strings enter on the verse colors before the full loop.

**Verse**

`A – C#m – B – G#m`

**Pre-Chorus**

`A – C#m – B – G#m`

Same four-bar loop as the verse; the vocal sits higher into the chorus.

**Chorus**

`A – E – B – C#m`

This is the only new harmony: `E` replaces `G#m`. The drop and post-chorus keep this loop.

**Bridge**

`A – E – B – C#m`

Middle eight over the chorus changes, then the drop returns.

**Outro**

`A – E – B – C#m`

The chorus/drop loop holds to the fade.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus → Outro
