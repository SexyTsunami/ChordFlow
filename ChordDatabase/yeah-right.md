YEAH RIGHT
Artist: Joji

Key: Ab major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction of the BALLADS 1 studio cut; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Chordify, ChordZone, GuitarTuna, and Musicnotes piano editions agree on Ab major; Tunebat sometimes tags C# / Db because the loop sits on `Db` as often as `Ab`. Guitar can stay in Ab with no capo, or use capo 1 and play `G – Em – Bm – C`. Chordify colors the last chord `Dbmaj7`; keep `Db` for the common triad reduction. Auto-chord pages that print `F7` / `C7` / `Ab7` are hearing dominant voicings on the same roots—ignore those as the main chart.
