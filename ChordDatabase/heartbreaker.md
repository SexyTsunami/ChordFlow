Heartbreaker
Artist: Mariah Carey, JAY-Z (Shawn Carter / Jay-Z / JAŸ-Z)

Key: Db major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction of the Rainbow single with the official JAŸ-Z feature; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Musicnotes PVG publishes Db major (about 92–100 BPM). Wikipedia cites the published loop `Db – Bbm – Db`. Ultimate Guitar, E-Chords, and Chordify commonly print a guitar-friendly C-shape vamp `C – D – Bm – C` a half-step under concert — capo 1 and those shapes sound `Db – Eb – Cm – Db`. Keep Db as the tonic. The track samples Stacy Lattisaw’s “Attack of the Name Game.”

**Intro**

`Db – Bbm – Db`

Guitar charts that start `C – D – Bm – C` should be capo 1 for concert. The opening hook and JAY-Z’s rap sit on this vamp.

**Verse**

`Db – Eb – Cm – Db`

Musicnotes/Wikipedia reductions also hear `Db – Bbm` as the harmonic bed under the Name Game guitar lick. Easy sheets stay on two chords; keep the passing `Eb`.

**Pre-chorus**

`Db – Eb – Cm – Db`

**Chorus**

`Db – Eb – Cm – Db`

**Bridge** (rap)

`Db – Eb – Cm – Db`

JAY-Z’s verse stays on the loop.

**Outro**

`Db – Bbm – Db`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus → Outro
