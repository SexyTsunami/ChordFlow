Beanie
Artist: Penguin Piano

Key: F major, 6/8. This is the common guitar/piano reduction of Penguin Piano’s instrumental cover of Chezile’s Beanie; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Musicnotes’ Chezile piano/vocal edition is F major in 6/8 (some waltz charts mark 3/4 at the same feel). Ultimate Guitar and piano lead sheets agree on the loop `Gm7 – C7 – Fmaj7 – Dm7`. Easy G-major tutorials are transpositions. No capo.

**Intro**

`Gm7 – C7 – Fmaj7 – Dm7`

**Verse**

`Gm7 – C7 – Fmaj7 – Dm7`

**Chorus**

`Dm7 – Gm7 – C7 – Fmaj7`

The title tag often starts on `Dm7` (“so cold”) before returning to the verse loop. Beginner charts drop the sevenths to `Gm – C – F – Dm`.

**Outro**

`Gm7 – C7 – Fmaj7 – Dm7`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Outro
