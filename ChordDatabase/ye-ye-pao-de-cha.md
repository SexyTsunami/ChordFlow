爺爺泡的茶 (Ye Ye Pao De Cha / Grandpa's Tea)
Artist: Jay Chou (周杰倫 / 周杰伦)

Chinese-style track from 八度空間. Key: D major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar commonly uses C shapes, capo 2, or plays in D with no capo. Chord4, 易譜庫, and 吉他世界 agree on concert D. The last chorus lifts a half step to Eb.
