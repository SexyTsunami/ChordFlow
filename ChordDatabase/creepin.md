Creepin'
Artist: Metro Boomin, The Weeknd, 21 Savage

Key: C-sharp minor, 4/4. Musicnotes and Hooktheory publish C-sharp minor; Ultimate Guitar’s common chart is capo 4 in A minor (`Am – Em – F – C – E`), which is the same concert pitch. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. The Mario Winans sample sits a whole step lower (`Bm – F#m – G – D`); keep the Metro cut in C-sharp minor. Easy no-capo A-minor charts sit two whole steps below the record. Chorus charts disagree on `G#` vs `G#7` for the “can’t take it” hit; use the major/dominant `G#7`.

**Intro**

`C#m – G#m – A – E – G#7`

Synth-and-bass interpolation of the Winans loop, with Metro’s tag over the first cycle.

**Verse**

`C#m – G#m – A – E – G#7`

Same loop under The Weeknd and 21 Savage. Some guitar charts drop `G#7` and sit on `E` for the last beat.

**Chorus**

`C#m – G#m – A – E – G#7`
`C#m – G#m – A – E`

The `G#7` is the lift into “anymore.” The last phrase often lands back on `C#m` without restating the dominant.

**Bridge**

`C#m – G#m – A – E – G#7`

Same harmony as the verse; the questions sit on the loop instead of a new progression.

**Outro**

`C#m – G#m – A – E – G#7`

The C-sharp minor vamp holds through the last “if you’re creepin’” tag.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Bridge → Verse → Chorus → Outro
