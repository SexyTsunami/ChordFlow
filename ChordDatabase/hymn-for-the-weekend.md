Hymn for the Weekend
Artist: Coldplay

Key: C minor, 4/4. Wikipedia lists C minor at about 90 BPM. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original production adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar often uses capo 3 and plays Dm – G – Am for the verse. Some ukulele charts keep the chorus on Fm – Bb – Cm; guitar/piano sources put the hook on Ab – Bb – Cm.

**Intro**

`Fm – Bb – Cm`

**Verse**

`Fm – Bb – Cm`

**Pre-chorus**

`Cm – Ab – Fm – Bb`
`Cm – Ab – Fm – Bb`

**Chorus**

`Ab – Bb – Cm`

**Outro**

`Ab – Bb – Cm`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Chorus → Outro
