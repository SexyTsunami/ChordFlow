Uptown Funk
Artist: Mark Ronson, Bruno Mars

Key: D minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. The groove is a repeating `Dm7 – G7` (G9 on some hits). A few chorus charts insert F before G; most of the hook is drums/N.C. then back to the two-chord vamp.

**Intro**

`Dm7 – G7`

**Verse**

`Dm7 – G7`

**Pre-Chorus**

`Dm7 – G7`

**Chorus**

`N.C.`
`Dm7 – G7`

The chanted hook is often unpitched (N.C.); the answer phrase returns to `Dm7 – G7`.

**Bridge**

`Dm7 – G7`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus → Outro
