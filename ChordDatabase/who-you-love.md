Who You Love
Artist: John Mayer, Katy Perry

Key: A major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. No capo. The guitar vamp starts on E, so many chord sites label the song E major; Musicnotes piano/vocal/guitar (original published key) and Chordify list A major. Concert harmony is `E – A – Dmaj7` (not a capo-transposed E-shape chart).

**Intro**

`E – A – Dmaj7`

Twice. Same three-chord guitar figure as the rest of the song.

**Verse**

`E – A – Dmaj7`

Mayer’s verse and Katy Perry’s verse both sit on this loop.

**Chorus**

`E – A – Dmaj7`

The title hook uses the same three chords as the verse.

**Pre-Chorus**

`C#m – F#m – Bm`
`E – A – Dmaj7`

The minor walk, then the hook cadence returns.

**Bridge**

`C#m – F#m – Bm`

Held for several cycles (Katy’s mid-song break), then the `E – A – Dmaj7` hook comes back.

**Outro**

`E – A – Dmaj7`

Final title repeats over the intro vamp.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Pre-Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Pre-Chorus → Bridge → Chorus → Outro
