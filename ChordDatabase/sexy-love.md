Sexy Love
Artist: Ne-Yo

Key: E-flat major, 4/4. Musicnotes’ piano/vocal/guitar edition is published in E-flat major. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar charts (Ultimate Guitar, E-Chords, ChordLines) usually call the song A-flat major because the verse starts on `Ab`. Same sounding chords either way: `Ab – Bb – Gm – Ab` with `Eb – Bb` turnarounds. Guitar often uses capo 3 and plays `F – G – Em – F`. Chordify’s E-major listing is a cover, not the studio cut. Musicnotes counts the ballad in half-time (~74); most play-along charts sit near 90–96 BPM.

**Intro**

`Ab – Bb – Gm – Ab`
`Eb – Bb`

**Verse**

`Ab – Bb – Gm – Ab`

The turnaround is `Eb – Bb`. Some guitar tutorials color `Gm` as `Gm7` and the last hit as `Cm7`.

**Pre-Chorus**

`Ab – Bb – Gm – Ab`
`Eb – Bb`

**Chorus**

`Ab – Bb – Gm – Ab`
`Eb – Bb`

**Bridge**

`Eb – Bb – Db – Ab`

Ultimate Guitar uses this lift. E-Chords simplifies the bridge to `Bb – Gm – Ab`.

**Outro**

`Ab – Bb – Gm – Ab`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus → Outro
