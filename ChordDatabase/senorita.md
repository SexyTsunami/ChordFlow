Señorita
Artist: Shawn Mendes, Camila Cabello

Key: A minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction of the 2019 studio cut; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Musicnotes, Guitar Down Under, and ChordLines agree on Am with no capo. The loop is `Am – Cmaj7 – Fmaj7 – Em7 – G`; beginner charts drop the sevenths to `Am – C – F – Em – G`. Capo 5 with E-minor shapes is a common guitar shortcut to the same concert pitch.

**Intro**

`Am – Cmaj7 – Fmaj7 – Em7 – G`

**Chorus**

`Am – Cmaj7 – Fmaj7 – Em7 – G`

The title hook is the intro loop; the last `G` is a pickup back to `Am`.

**Verse**

`Am – Cmaj7 – Fmaj7 – Em7 – G`

Same vamp. The “ooh la la la” turn sits on `Fmaj7 – Em7 – G`.

**Bridge**

`Am – Cmaj7 – Fmaj7 – Em7 – G`

Hotel-room verse colors; still the same five chords. Later “call me señorita” reprises stack two chorus cycles.

**Outro**

`Am – Cmaj7 – Fmaj7 – Em7 – G`

“Coming for you” tags the loop and ends on `Am`.

**Form**

Intro → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Bridge → Chorus → Outro
