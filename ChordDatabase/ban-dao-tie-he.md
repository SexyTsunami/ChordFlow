半島鐵盒 (Ban Dao Tie He / Peninsula Ironbox)
Artist: Jay Chou (周杰倫 / 周杰伦)

Key: B major verse, Ab major chorus; the last chorus lifts a half step to A. 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Some high-reduction guitar charts place the sung verse in Eb instead of B.

**Intro** (spoken bookstore lines; some charts omit this)

`Eb – Bb – Cm – Gm – Ab – Gm`

**Verse**

`B – F# – G#m – D#m`
`E – Em – F#`
`B – F# – G#m – D#m`
`E – Em – F#`

**Pre-chorus**

`Fm – Cm – Db – Eb`
`Fm – Cm – Db – Eb`

**Chorus** (two matching halves)

First half:
`Ab – Eb – Fm – Cm – Db – Ab – Bbm – Eb`
`Ab – C – Fm – Bb – Db – Ab – Bbm – Eb`

Second half:
`Ab – Eb – Fm – Cm – Db – Ab – Bbm – Eb`
`Ab – C – Fm – Bb – Db – Ab – Bbm – Eb – Ab`

**Interlude**

`Db – Ab – Db`

**Last chorus**

Same pattern as the chorus, up a half step:

`A – E – F#m – C#m – D – A – Bm – E`
`A – C# – F#m – B – D – A – Bm – E`
then the matching second half ending on **A**

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Interlude → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Last chorus in A
