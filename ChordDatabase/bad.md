Bad
Artist: Michael Jackson

Key: A minor (A Dorian on the groove), 4/4. Wikipedia, Musicnotes, and Hooktheory agree on A minor; the album mix is pitched sharp of A440 (around A454), so some guitar charts write the same changes a half step up in B-flat minor. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original synth-bass and brass arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar usually plays Am shapes with no capo.

**Intro** (bass riff)

Walking `Am` blues-scale line: `A – C – D – D# – E`, over

`Am7 – D7`

**Verse**

`Am7 – D7`

**Chorus**

`Am7 – D9`

(Some reductions keep `D7` for the whole song.)

**Bridge**

`Bm7 – C#m7`
`Bm7 – C#m7`
`Bm7 – C#m7 – E7#9`

Some charts swap the last chord for `G`.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Bridge → Chorus → Verse → Bridge → Chorus → Solo (bridge changes) → Chorus (repeat / fade)
