以父之名 (Yi Fu Zhi Ming / In the Name of the Father)
Artist: Jay Chou (周杰倫 / 周杰伦)

Key: D minor, 4/4 (some guitar charts mark 2/4; relative F major). From *葉惠美*. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar often uses capo 5 and plays in A minor / C.

**Intro**

`Dm – Dm – Dm – A`
`Gm – A – Bb – A`

**Verse**

`Dm – Dm – Dm – A`
`Gm – A – Bb – A – Dm`

**Rap**

Same loop as the verse:

`Dm – Dm – Dm – A`
`Gm – A – Bb – A`

**Pre-chorus**

`Dm – Dm – Dm – A`
`Gm – A – Bb – A – Dm`

**Chorus** (two matching halves)

First half:
`Dm – Dm – Dm – A – Gm – A – Bb – A – Dm`

Second half:
`Dm – Dm – Dm – A – Gm – A – Bb – A – Dm`

**Bridge**

`Dm – Gm – Dm – A`
`Gm – Dm – Bb – Gm – Bb – A`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Rap → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Bridge → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Chorus
