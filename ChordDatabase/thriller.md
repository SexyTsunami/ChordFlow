Thriller
Artist: Michael Jackson

Key: C-sharp minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original synth-horn arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar often uses capo 4 and plays in A minor (`Am7 – D7`, chorus `Am – C – D – Am`). Easy A-minor charts with no capo sit a major third below the record.

**Intro**

`C#m7 – F#7 – G#aug`
`C#m – E – F# – C#m`
`C#m7 – F#7`

**Verse**

`C#m7 – F#7`
`C#m7 – F#7`
`C#m7 – G#m`

Most of the verse is the `C#m7 – F#7` vamp. The last line lands `G#m`.

**Chorus**

`C#m – E – F# – C#m`
`F#7 – F#m7`
`C#m – E – F# – C#m`
`F#7 – A7 – F#7`

**Bridge**

**Outro**

`C#m – E – F# – C#m`
`F#7 – A7 – F#7`

Chorus vamp into the laugh/howl ending.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus → Rap → Chorus / Outro
