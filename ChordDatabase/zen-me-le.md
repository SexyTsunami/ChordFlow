怎麼了 (Zen Me Le / What's Wrong)
Artist: Eric Chou (周興哲 / 周兴哲)

Also written 怎么了. From 終於了解自由. Key: A major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar commonly uses G shapes, capo 2. Kanpu and 吉他世界 agree on concert A.

**Intro** (twice)

`A – F#m7 – D – E`

Some charts color `D` as `Dadd9` and split the last bar `Bm7 – E`.

**Verse**

`A – F#m7 – D – E`

**Pre-Chorus**

`F#m7 – C#m7 – D – E`

**Chorus**

`A – E/G# – F#m7 – C#m/E`
`D – A/C# – F#m7 – Bm7 – E`
`A – E/G# – F#m7`
`D – A/C# – F#m7 – Bm7 – E`

Second half often lifts the `E/G#` into `C#7/F`. Turnaround back to the verse vamp is `A – F#m – D – E`.

**Interlude**

`A – F#m7 – D – E`

Same four-bar loop as the intro.

**Bridge**

`A – F#m – D – E`

Same four-bar loop as the intro, sung once, then back into the chorus.

**Outro**

`A – F#m7 – D – Bm7 – E – A`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Interlude → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus → Outro
