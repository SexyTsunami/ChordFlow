軌跡 (Gui Ji / Trajectory)
Artist: Jay Chou (周杰倫 / 周杰伦)

Key: A major, 4/4. The last chorus lifts a major third to Db. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar often uses capo 2 and plays in G.

**Intro**

`A – Asus – A – F – E`

**Verse**

`A – A/G# – F#m – A/E`
`D – C#m – F#m – B7 – E`

**Pre-chorus**

`C#7 – F#m – Bm – E – A`
`C#7 – F#m – Bm – E`

**Chorus** (two matching halves)

First half:
`A – A/G# – C#7 – F#m – C#m`
`D – C#7 – F#m – F – E`

Second half:
`A – E/G# – C#7 – F#m – C#m`
`D – C#7 – F#m – Bm – E`

**Interlude**

`D – E – A – F#m – Dm – E`

**Last chorus** (in Db)

Same pattern, up a major third:

`Db – Ab/C – F7 – Bbm – Fm`
`Gb – F7 – Bbm – A – Ab`

then the matching second half ending toward **Db**

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Interlude → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → last chorus in Db
