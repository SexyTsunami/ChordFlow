The Cat Song
Artist: Bryant Oden

Songdrops kids cut from The Songdrops Collection, Vol. 4. Key: C major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. songdata.io and Tunebat-style listings agree on C major (Camelot 8B); detectors also print ~187 BPM, which is double-time for a ~94 feel. Printed guitar tabs for this title are scarce (Songdrops is still posting chords song by song). Bryant’s other Songdrops sheets stay on three-chord major loops: Duck Song is `F – C – G`, Tomato Song is `C – F – G`. Keep this reduction on concert `C – F – G` with no capo. Auto-charts that add `Am` are filling in passing bass, not a new loop.

**Verse**

`C – F – G – C`

The couplet verses and the “meow” tag both sit on this `C – F – G` loop.

**Bridge**

`F – G – C`
`F – G – C`

The “in and out” and “dogs / cats” middle eight stay on `F – G – C`; some ear charts insert `Am` on the first bar.

**Outro**

`C – F – G – C`

**Form**

Verse → Verse → Bridge → Verse → Bridge → Verse → Outro
