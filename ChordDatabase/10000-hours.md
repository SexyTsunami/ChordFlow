10,000 Hours
Artist: Dan + Shay, Justin Bieber

Key: Bb major, 4/4. Wikipedia lists Bb major at about 90 BPM. Easy-piano Musicnotes is transposed to G and is not the concert key. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar often uses capo 3 and plays `G – Em – C – G`.

**Intro**

`Bb – Gm – Eb – Bb`

**Verse**

`Bb – Gm – Eb – Bb`

**Pre-Chorus**

`Bb – Gm – Eb – Bb`

**Chorus**

`Bb – Gm – Eb – Bb`

**Bridge**

`Dm – Eb`

Then back to the chorus loop.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus
