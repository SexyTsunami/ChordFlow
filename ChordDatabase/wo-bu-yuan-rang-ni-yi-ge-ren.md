我不願讓你一個人 (Wo Bu Yuan Rang Ni Yi Ge Ren / I Don't Want to Leave You Alone)
Artist: Mayday (五月天)

Also written 我不愿意让你一个人. Key: G major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. No capo. Chordify and Kanpu agree on concert G.

**Intro**

`G – Am7 – D/F# – G`
`C – G/B – Am7 – G`

Denser charts continue `C – D – Bm7 – Em – C – A7 – D` before the vocal.

**Verse**

`G – Am7 – D/F# – G`
`C – G/B – Am7 – G`
`Bm7 – E7 – Am7 – Bm7`
`Am7 – D – G – C – D`

**Chorus**

`C – D – Bm7 – Em`
`Am7 – B7 – Em – Em/D# – Em/D`
`C – D – Bm7 – Em`
`Am7 – D – C – G – Am7 – D`

**Bridge**

`C – D – Bm7 – Em`
`Am7 – Bm7 – Eb – D`

**Verse 3**

`G – Am7 – D/F# – G`
`C – G/B – Am7 – G`
`Bm7 – E7 – Am7 – Bm7`
`Am7 – D – G`
`Em – Em/D# – Em/D – Em/C# – Am7 – D`

Last line often walks `Eb – D` before the final `Am7 – D – G`.

**Outro**

`G – Am7 – D/F# – G – Eb – D7 – G`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Bridge → Chorus → Verse 3 → Outro
