COME BACK HOME
Artist: 2NE1

Key: C minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction of the Crush studio cut; the original electro-reggae arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Wikipedia and AcousticBrainz agree on C minor around 150 BPM. Guitar usually uses capo 3 and plays in A minor (`Am – G – C – G – F`, sounding `Cm – Bb – Eb – Bb – Ab`). The unplugged video uses those same shapes. Capo-1 B-minor tabs (`Bm7 – A – Dsus2`) are the same concert pitch. Easy open-Am charts sit a minor third below the record.

**Verse**

`Cm – Bb – Eb – Bb – Ab – Gm`
`Cm – Bb – Eb – Bb – Ab`

**Pre-Chorus**

`Fm – Bb – Eb – Ab`
`Fm – Bb – Cm`

Last turn is often `Bbsus4 – Bb` (capo 3 `Asus4 – A`) into the chorus.

**Chorus**

`Ab – Eb – G – Cm – Bb`
`Ab – Eb – G – Ab`
