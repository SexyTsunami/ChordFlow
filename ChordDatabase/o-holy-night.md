O Holy Night
Artist: Mariah Carey

Key: E major, 6/8. This is the common guitar/piano reduction of Mariah’s Merry Christmas recording; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Musicnotes PVG from the Merry Christmas book publishes E major (moderately slow, about 60 dotted-quarter). Ultimate Guitar, E-Chords, and Chordify agree on E. Guitar stays in E with no capo, or capo 2 and plays D-shapes. This is Mariah’s 1994 studio cut, not a generic hymn leadsheet — keep her `C#m` chorus start and the `Bm7` outro vamp.
