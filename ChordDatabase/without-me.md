Without Me
Artist: Halsey

Key: B minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction of the Manic single; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Yalle Media and the widely circulated guitar riff agree on `Bm – F#m – A – Em`. Musicnotes PVG editions publish E minor (same loop a fifth down as `Em – Bm – D – Am`); Hooktheory tags D# minor. Keep concert B minor for the record’s guitar figures. No capo, or capo 7 with `Em – Bm – D – Am` shapes.

`Bm – F#m – A – Em`

**Bridge**

`Bm – F#m – A – Em`

The Cry Me a River interpolation sits on this vamp. Some piano reductions color the last `Em` as `Em7`.
