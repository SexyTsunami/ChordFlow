Key: C major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction of the PINK PLANET studio cut; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Cifra Club, GuitarTuna’s original-key chart, and GuitarTabsExplorer agree on C with no capo. Tunebat sometimes labels F major from the chorus `F`; keep C as the concert key. GuitarTuna’s denser voicings are `C – Am7 – Fadd9 – Gsus4`; beginner charts use plain `C – Am – F – G`.
