Heartless
Artist: Kanye West

808s & Heartbreak studio cut, not The Weeknd. Key: Bb minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original 808-and-auto-tune arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. GuitarTuna, Hooktheory, and Chordify agree on Bb minor (`Bbm – Ab – Gb – Fm`). Guitar often uses capo 1. Easy capo-1 pages that loop `Am – Dm – F – G` are a different (less accurate) four-chord reduction; keep the chorus as `Bbm – Ab – Gbmaj7 – Fm7`.

**Intro**

`Bbm – Ab – Gbmaj7 – Fm7`

**Chorus**

`Bbm – Ab – Gbmaj7 – Fm7`

Beginner charts flatten `Gbmaj7` / `Fm7` to `Gb` / `Fm`, and `Ab6` to `Ab`.

**Verse**

`Bbm`
`Bbm/Ab – Bbm`

The verse sits on a `Bbm` pedal; capo-1 easy charts reduce the line-end walk to `Bbm – Ab`.

**Bridge**

`Bbm – Ab – Gbmaj7 – Fm7`

Same loop as the chorus; the vocal sits on the “talk and talk” chant.

**Outro**

`Bbm – Ab – Gbmaj7 – Fm7`

**Form**

Intro → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus → Outro
