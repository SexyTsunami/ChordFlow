NIGHTS LIKE THESE
Artist: Benson Boone

From Walk Me Home... (2022), not Pulse (2023). Key: G minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Chordify and Tunebat agree on G minor. The loop starts on `Eb`, so ChordsEasy and some ukulele sheets tag Eb — keep G minor as the tonic. Guitar usually uses capo 3 and plays C-shapes (`C – Em – D – Am`, sounding `Eb – Gm – F – Cm`). UkuTabs, GuitarTwitt, and ChordsEasy agree on that four-chord loop for intro, verse, and chorus.

**Intro**

`Eb – Gm – F – Cm`

**Verse**

`Eb – Gm – F – Cm`
`Eb – Gm – F – Cm`

**Pre-chorus**

`Gm – Eb`
`Gm – F – Eb`

**Chorus**

`Eb – Gm – F – Cm`
`Eb – Gm – F – Cm`

Title line often hits N.C. before landing back on `Eb`.

**Bridge**

`Eb – Gm – F`
`Eb – Gm – F`
`Eb – Gm`

**Outro**

`Eb – Gm – F – Gm – Eb`

Last chorus folds into this tag.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus → Outro
