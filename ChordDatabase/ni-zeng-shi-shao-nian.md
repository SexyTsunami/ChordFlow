你曾是少年 (Ni Zeng Shi Shao Nian / You Were Once Young)
Artist: S.H.E (Selina 任家萱 / Hebe 田馥甄 / Ella 陳嘉樺 / 陈嘉桦)

Theme for the film 少年班. Key: G major, chorus in E major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. No capo. Chord4, yipuku, ccguitar, and pop-guitar blogs agree the S.H.E studio cut is G, with the chorus lifting to E. Ultimate Guitar writes the same G/E shapes but puts capo 1 (concert Ab/F); treat that as a vocal transposition. Easy G-only sheets skip the chorus modulation. Male covers often sit in C with the chorus in A.

**Intro**

`G – D – Em – C`

**Verse**

`G – D – Em – Bm`
`C – D – G – D/F# – Em`
`A – C – D`

Second verse cadence:
`G – D – Em – Bm`
`C – D – G – Em`
`F – D – G`

**Pre-chorus**

`C – B – Em – A`
`C – D – G`
`C – B – Em – A`
`C – B`

**Chorus** (two matching halves; now in E)

First half:
`E – B – C#m – G#m`
`A – B – G#m – C#m`
`F#m – B`

Second half:
`E – B – C#m – G#m`
`A – B – G#m – C#m`
`F#m – B – E`

**Interlude** (back toward G)

`A – D`

**Outro**

`E – B – C#m – A – E`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Interlude → Verse 2 → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Outro
