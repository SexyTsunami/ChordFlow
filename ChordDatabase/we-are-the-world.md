We Are The World
Artist: U.S.A. For Africa

Key: E major (final choruses in F major), 4/4. This is the common piano/guitar reduction of the 1985 studio cut; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. AmChords, Cifra Club, and ChordU agree on E, then a last-chorus lift to F. Guitar can stay in E with no capo. Easy three-chord `E – A – B` sheets drop `C#m` and `G#m`.

**Intro**

`E – A/E – B/E`

Piano figure over a tonic pedal; some reductions sit on `E` alone.

`A – B`

**Bridge**

`C – D – E`
`C – D – E`
`C#m – G#m`

The `C – D – E` bars are the borrowed lift (“when you’re down and out”). Then the verse-family `C#m – G#m – A – B` returns.

**Final Chorus** (up a half step)

`Bb – C – F`
`Bb – C – F`
`Dm – Am`
`Gm – C – F`

Same chorus colors in F. Repeats to the fade; some charts write `C11` on the last `C`.

**Form**
