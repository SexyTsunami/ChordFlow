算什麼男人 (Suan Shen Me Nan Ren / What Kind of Man)
Artist: Jay Chou (周杰倫 / 周杰伦)

Key: C# major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar often uses capo 1 and plays in C.

**Intro** (twice)

`C# – D#/G – F#m – C#`

**Verse**

`C# – D#/G – F#maj7 – F#m`
`Fm – Bbm – D#m – G#`
`C# – D#/G – F#maj7 – F#m`
`Fm – Bbm – D#m – G#`

**Pre-chorus**

`Bbm – C#/G# – F# – C#/F – D#m – G#`

**Chorus** (two matching halves)

First half:
`F# – G# – Fm – Bbm – D#m – G# – C# – C#7`

Second half:
`F# – F#dim – Fm – Bbm – D#m – G# – C#`

**Interlude**

`C# – D#/G – F#m – C#`

**Outro**

`C# – D#/G – F#m – C#`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Interlude → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Chorus → Outro
