Viva La Vida
Artist: Coldplay

Key: Ab major, 4/4. Wikipedia’s voicing is Db5 – Absus4/Eb – Ab – Fm; most reductions write Db – Eb – Ab – Fm. Guitar commonly uses capo 1 and plays C – D – G – Em.

**Intro**

`Db – Eb – Ab – Fm`

**Verse**

`Db – Eb – Ab – Fm`

**Chorus**

`Db – Eb – Ab – Fm`

Last phrase of the chorus inserts Cm:

`Db – Eb – Cm – Fm`
`Db – Eb – Ab – Fm`

**Breakdown**

`Db – Fm`

Then a held Eb before the last chorus.

**Outro**

`Db – Eb – Cm – Fm`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Intro → Verse → Chorus → Intro → Verse → Chorus → Breakdown → Chorus → Outro
