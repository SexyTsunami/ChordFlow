愛情轉移 (Ai Qing Zhuan Yi / Love Transfer)
Artist: Eason Chan (陳奕迅 / 陈奕迅)

Also written 爱情转移. Mandarin hit from 認了吧; the Cantonese counterpart is 富士山下. Key: F major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar commonly uses C shapes, capo 5. Kanpu, yinban, and 7T guitar charts agree on F. Verse is a canon walk; denser charts use Am7/G (concert Dm7/C) in place of Am, and D7/F# or F#m7b5 (concert G7/B or Bm7b5) in the chorus. Simplified C-shape charts with capo 1 sit a tritone away and are for vocal range, not concert pitch.

**Intro** (twice)

`Dm – Am – Bb – C – F`

**Verse**

`F – C/E – Dm – Am`
`Bb – F – Gm7 – C`
`F – C/E – Dm – Am`
`Bb – F – Gm7 – C – F`

**Chorus**

`Dm – Am – Bb – C – F`
`Dm – G7 – Dm/B – C`
`Dm – Am – Bb – C – F`
`Dm – G7 – Dm/B – Bb – C`

First-chorus ending often hits F then walks back `F – C` into verse 2.

**Outro**

`F – Bb – C – F`

Last chorus tag stays on `Bb – C – F`.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Chorus → Outro
