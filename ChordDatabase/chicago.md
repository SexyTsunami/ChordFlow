Chicago
Artist: Michael Jackson

Key: D minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original Xscape arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar is usually played with no capo in D minor. Capo 5 in A minor (`Am – Dm – E7`) is a common easy-shape option. Beatport and chord charts agree on D minor for the 2014 album cut (Timbaland version and the original mix both sit here).

**Intro**

`Dm`

Groove on D minor into the first verse.

**Verse**

`Dm – Gm`

`Dm` holds the story lines; `Gm` answers at the end of each pair.

**Chorus**

`Dm`
`Gm`
`A7`

The first half of the hook stays on `Dm` / `Gm`. The cadence is `A7` back to `Dm`.

**Chorus 2**

`Gm – Dm`
`Gm – A7`

This chorus starts on `Gm` instead of `Dm`, then the same `A7` turnaround.

**Interlude**

`Dm`

**Outro**

`Dm – Gm – A7`

Chorus loops to the end.

**Form**

Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Chorus 2 → Interlude → Verse → Chorus → Chorus 2 → Chorus → Outro
