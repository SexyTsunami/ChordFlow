Gaslight
Artist: Derik Fein

Key: C minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Ultimate Guitar’s chart is C minor with capo 3 (`Am – Dm – G – E7`, concert `Cm – Fm – Bb – G7`). 12NOTEZ also lists C minor at about 142 BPM. Beginner charts drop `G7` to `G`.

**Intro**

`Cm`

Held vamp before the first verse.

**Verse**

`Cm – Fm – Bb – G7`

**Pre-Chorus**

`Fm – Bb – G7 – Cm – Bb`

The second half often walks `Fm – Bb – G – G7` into the chorus.

**Chorus**

`Cm – Fm – Bb – G7`

**Outro**

`Cm – Fm – Bb – G7`

Last pass can sit on `G` before the final `Cm`.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Outro
