因為愛情 (Yin Wei Ai Qing / Because of Love)
Artist: Eason Chan (陳奕迅 / 陈奕迅), Faye Wong (王菲)

Also written 因为爱情. Duet from the film 將愛情進行到底. Key: F major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar commonly uses C shapes, capo 5. Kanpu, Chord4, and yipuku agree on F. Easy C-shape charts with no capo sit a fourth below concert. Verse often uses Bbadd9 and Fmaj7 in place of plain Bb and F; chorus second bar is Am or Am7.

**Intro**

`F – Gm7 – Bbadd9 – C`

**Verse**

`F – Gm7 – Bb – Dm`
`Gm7 – C – F`

Second verse ends `Gm7 – Bb – C` instead of the F cadence.

**Chorus** (two matching halves)

First half:
`F – Am – Dm7 – Am`
`Bb – Dm – Gm7 – C`

Second half:
`F – Am – Dm7 – Am`
`Bb – Dm – Gm7 – C – F`

**Interlude**

`Dm7 – C – Bb`
`Fmaj7 – Bb`

**Outro** (short verse)

`F – Gm7 – Bb – Dm`
`Gm7 – C – F`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Verse → Chorus → Interlude → Verse → Chorus → Outro
