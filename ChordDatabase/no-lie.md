No Lie
Artist: Sean Paul, Dua Lipa

Key: D minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original dancehall-pop arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. GuitarTuna’s original-key chart and most guitar/ukulele sheets sit in D minor on `Dm – F – Am – G`. Chordify and Klangio label A minor because `Am` is in the loop; DJ Camelot keys sometimes say G major. Keep D minor: the riff starts on `Dm`. Guitar usually stays in D minor with no capo; capo 5 (`Am – C – Em – D`) matches concert pitch. Seventh charts color `Dm` as `Dm9` and `F` as `Fmaj7`. Ultimate Guitar’s no-capo sheet sometimes writes a stray `D` major where the loop is `Dm`.

**Intro**

`Dm – F – Am – G`

**Verse**

`Dm – F – Am – G`

Sean Paul’s verses and Dua’s later verse both stay on the four-bar loop.

**Chorus**

`Dm – F – Am – G`

Title tag and the “lit tonight” hook are the same vamp.

**Bridge**

`Dm – F – Am – G`

**Outro**

`Dm – F – Am – G`

Final chorus repeats to the end.

**Form**
