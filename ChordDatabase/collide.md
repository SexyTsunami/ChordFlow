Collide
Artist: Howie Day

Key: B major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction of the Stop All the World Now studio cut; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Musicnotes’ original published key is B major at about 96 BPM; Wikipedia cites that sheet. Guitar usually uses capo 4 and plays `G – G/F# – Em – C` (concert `B – F#/A# – G#m – E`). Some capo-4 charts write `G/B` in place of `Em`. Easy G-major charts with no capo sit a major third below the record.

**Intro**

`B – F#/A# – G#m – E`

**Verse**

`B – F#/A# – G#m – E`

**Chorus**

`B – F#/A# – G#m – E`

Same four-bar loop as the verse. `F#/A#` is the bass walk; beginner charts print plain `F#`.

**Bridge**

`G#m – E – F#`

**Outro**

`B – F#/A# – G#m – E – B`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus → Outro
