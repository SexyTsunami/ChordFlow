To Love Someone
Artist: Benson Boone

2023 single; later on Fireworks & Rollerblades (2024). Key: F major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Musicnotes PVG (MN0304474) and Faber publish F. Chordify’s chord set is `F – Dm7 – Am – C` (it mis-tags the key as C because C is the dominant). E-Chords’ C-shape chart is a beginner transpose down a fourth, not concert. Guitar stays in F with no capo, or uses C shapes, capo 5 (`C – Am – Em – G`, sounding `F – Dm – Am – C`). Denser tabs add `C/B`, `Bb/A`, and `F/E` as bass walks.
