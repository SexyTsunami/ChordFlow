Beauty And A Beat
Artist: Justin Bieber, Nicki Minaj

Key: A minor, 4/4. Wikipedia lists A minor at 128 BPM with `F – Dm7 – Am – Gsus`. Easy-piano sheets are in relative C major. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar often uses `F – Dm – Am – G`.

**Intro**

`F – Dm7 – Am – Gsus`

**Verse**

`F – Dm7 – Am – Gsus`

**Pre-Chorus**

`F – Dm7 – Am – Gsus`

**Chorus**

`F – Dm7 – Am – Gsus`

Same loop under the hook. Some pop charts rewrite the chorus as `C – F – Am – G`.

**Bridge**

`F – Dm7 – Am – Gsus`

Nicki Minaj’s verse stays on the same loop.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus
