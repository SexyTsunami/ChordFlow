Colors of the Wind
Artist: Judy Kuhn, Disney

1995 Pocahontas film soundtrack. Key: D-flat major, 4/4. This is the common piano/guitar reduction of the Kuhn studio cut; the original orchestra adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Chordify and matching original-key charts agree on Db; Hooktheory labels C# minor / Db major. Musicnotes’ beginner PVG is in C and is not the concert key. Guitar usually uses C shapes, capo 1 (`C – Am – C – Em`, concert `Db – Bbm – Db – Fm`). The Vanessa Williams pop single is a different cut.
