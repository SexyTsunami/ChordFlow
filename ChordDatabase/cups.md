Cups (When I'm Gone)
Artist: Anna Kendrick

Pitch Perfect version of the Carter Family / “When I’m Gone” cup-game arrangement. Key: C major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original vocal-and-cups arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Super Simple Piano and KhmerChords agree on C. Guitar often uses G shapes, capo 5 (`G – C – Em – D`, sounding `C – F – Am – G`). Ultimate Guitar’s popular capo-5 chart is that G-shape edition. Easy no-capo G charts sit a fourth below the single.

**Intro**

Cups only; guitar often tacet, or ghosts `C`.

**Verse**

`C – F – C`
`Am – G – F`
`Dm7 – G – C`

Some sheets keep plain `Dm` and write the last turn `G7 – C`.

**Chorus**

`Am – F`
`Am – G`
`Am – G – F`
`Dm7 – G – C`

Last chorus sometimes colors the “everywhere / talk” bar `F – Fm` before `C`.

`Am – G – F`
`Dm7 – G – C`

**Chorus**

**Form**
