Amani
Artist: Beyond (黃家駒 / 黄家驹 / Wong Ka Kui)

English/Swahili title (amani = peace); Cantonese verses, Swahili hook. Key: C major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. No capo. Chordify, Guitarians, Ultimate Guitar, and Chinese C-shape charts agree on C. Later choruses add an extra `F – G` tag before repeating the hook; Guitarians omits that bar.

**Intro**

`C – G – C – G`

**Chorus**

`C – G – F – C`
`F – G – C`

Later choruses insert `F – G` after the second line before repeating the hook. The studio cut has guitar fills over the hook but no separate solo section.

**Verse**

`C – G – Am – G`
`C – G – Am – G`

**Pre-Chorus**

`C – G – Am – G`
`F – G`

**Bridge**

`C – G – Am – G`
`F – G`
`F – G`

The last `F – G` is the extra lift into the final choruses.

**Outro**

`C – G – F – C`
`F – G – C`

Repeats the Swahili hook to a close on C.

**Form**

Intro → Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus → Outro
