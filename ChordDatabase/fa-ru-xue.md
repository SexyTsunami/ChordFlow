髮如雪 (Fa Ru Xue / Hair Like Snow)
Artist: Jay Chou (周杰倫 / 周杰伦)

Key: B major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar often uses capo 2 and plays in A.

**Intro**

`B – F# – B – F#`

**Verse**

`B – F#`
`G#m – F# – E`
`B – E – F#`
`B – F#`
`G#m – F# – E`
`B – C#m – F#`

**Pre-chorus**

`G#m – B – E – F#`

**Chorus** (two matching halves)

First half:
`B – F# – G#m – F# – E – B – E – F#`

Second half:
`B – F# – G#m – F# – E – B – C#m – F# – B`

**Rap chorus**

Same harmonic loop, reduced:

`B – F# – G#m – F# – E – B – E – F#`

**Coda**

`B – F# – G#m – F#`
`E – B – E – F#`

**Outro**

`B – Em – B – Em`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Rap chorus → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Rap chorus → Chorus → Coda → Coda → Outro
