All of Me
Artist: John Legend

Key: A-flat major, 2/2. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Musicnotes’ published key is Ab major in cut time (guitar charts usually mark 4/4). Guitar usually uses capo 1 and plays in G (`Em – C – G – D`, concert `Fm – Db – Ab – Eb`).

**Intro**

`Fm – Db – Ab – Eb`

**Verse**

`Fm – Db – Ab – Eb`

The verse cadence often lands `Bbm` going into the pre-chorus.

**Pre-Chorus**

`Bbm – Ab – Eb`

Capo-1 charts write `Am – G – D`. The “cards on the table” later hit repeats this lift.

**Chorus**

`Ab – Fm – Bbm – Db – Eb`
`Fm – Db – Ab – Eb`

The title tag returns to the intro loop. Beginner charts drop `Bbm` and stay on `Ab – Fm – Db – Eb`.

**Outro**

`Fm – Db – Ab – Eb`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Outro
