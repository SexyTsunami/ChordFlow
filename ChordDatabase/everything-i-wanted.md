everything i wanted
Artist: Billie Eilish

Key: A major (relative F# minor), 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original piano part adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar often uses capo 2 and plays in G (`Cmaj7 – D – Bm`). Some analyses call the key F# minor because of the mood; the loop is diatonic to A.

**Intro**

`Dmaj7 – E – C#m`

**Verse**

`Dmaj7 – E – C#m`

**Interlude**

`Dmaj7 – E – C#m`

**Chorus**

`Dmaj7 – E – C#m`
`Dmaj7 – E – C#7`

The first two chorus cycles keep C#m; later lines swap C#m for C#7. Simplified charts use `D – E – C#m` (triads).

**Outro**

`Dmaj7 – E – C#m`
`Dmaj7 – E – C#7`

Same cycle as the chorus.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Interlude → Verse → Chorus → Interlude → Chorus → Outro
