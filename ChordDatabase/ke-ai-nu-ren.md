可愛女人 (Ke Ai Nu Ren / Adorable Woman)
Artist: Jay Chou (周杰倫 / 周杰伦)

Key: F major, 4/4. The last chorus lifts a whole step to G. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices.

**Intro** (twice)

`F – Dm – Bb – C`

**Verse**

`F – A – Bb – A`

**Pre-chorus**

`Bb – Dm`
`Bb – C`

**Chorus** (two matching halves)

`F – Dm – Bb – C`

**Interlude**

`F – Dm – Bb – C`

**Bridge**

`Bb – Am – G – C`

**Last chorus**

Same pattern, up a whole step:

`G – Em – C – D`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Chorus → Interlude → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Chorus → Bridge → last chorus in G
