透明 (Tou Ming / Transparent)
Artist: G.E.M. (鄧紫棋 / 邓紫棋)

Also called Selfless. Key: Ab major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original piano part loops the intro figure with extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar commonly uses G shapes, capo 1 (Em – C – G – D). Some guitar tabs label the song F minor because the loop starts on Fm.
