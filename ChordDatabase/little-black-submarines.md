Little Black Submarines
Artist: The Black Keys

Key: A minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar stays in A minor with no capo (acoustic fingerpicking, then the same shapes strummed when the distorted guitar enters). E-Chords, Chordify, and songparts agree on A minor. Charts agree on `Am – G – D – A`; they differ on whether the tag walk is `A – B – C – D – A` or just `C – D – A`.
