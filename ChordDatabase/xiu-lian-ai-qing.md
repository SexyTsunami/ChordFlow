修煉愛情 (Xiu Lian Ai Qing / Practice Love)
Artist: JJ Lin (林俊傑 / 林俊杰)

Also written 修炼爱情. Key: Eb major, last chorus in E, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar commonly uses C shapes, capo 3. Simplified charts stay in Eb for the last chorus; intro voicings vary between Ebadd9–Abadd9 and Ebadd9–Ebsus4.
