Low Life
Artist: Future, The Weeknd

Key: C minor, 4/4. Hooktheory and Chordify agree on C minor. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar usually uses capo 3 and plays `Am – F – C – Em` (sounding `Cm – Ab – Eb – Gm`). Easy no-capo A-minor charts sit a minor third below the record. Chordify collapses the song to `Cm – Fm – Eb – Ab`; Ultimate Guitar’s capo-3 chart is more specific: Future’s hook is `Cm – Ab – Eb`, and The Weeknd’s chorus leans `Fm – Gm – Cm`.

**Intro**

`Cm – Ab – Eb`

High “getting high” ad-libs over the Future loop before the first verse.

**Verse**

`Cm – Ab – Eb`

Future’s “what else” verses sit here. The Weeknd’s verse walks `Cm – Ab – Gm` and uses `Fm – Eb` on the later lines.

**Chorus**

`Fm – Gm – Cm`
`Ab – Gm – Cm`

The hook is this three-chord cell. `Ab – Gm` is the turnaround some auto-charts fold into a generic `Cm – Fm – Eb – Ab` loop.

**Bridge**

`Cm – Ab – Gm`
`Fm – Eb`

Future’s “stereotyping” break stays in C minor with the same palette as The Weeknd’s verse.

**Outro**

`Cm – Ab – Eb`

Returns to the opening “what else” vamp.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Bridge → Outro
