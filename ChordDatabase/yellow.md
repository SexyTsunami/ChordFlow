Yellow
Artist: Coldplay

Key: B major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original acoustic uses an unusual tuning (E–A–B–G–B–D#) and extra color (Badd11, F#6, Emaj7). Guitar often uses capo 2 and plays in A, or capo 4 and plays in G. Some easy charts loop `B – F# – E – G#m` through every section; verse/chorus actually split as below.

**Intro**

`B – F# – E`

**Verse**

`B – F# – E`

**Chorus**

`E – G#m – F# – B`

**Bridge**

`B – F# – E`

**Outro**

`B – F# – E`

Same loop as the verse; later passes add G#m, and some charts insert an F#m color chord near the end.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus → Outro
