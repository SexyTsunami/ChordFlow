可惜沒如果 (Ke Xi Mei Ru Guo / If Only)
Artist: JJ Lin (林俊傑 / 林俊杰)

Also written 可惜没如果. Key: C major (verse in A minor), 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. No capo. Some charts label the song A minor because the intro and verse sit there; chorus is C. Denser charts use Am7/G and Dm7–C/E in place of Em/G and Dm–Em.

**Intro** (four times)

`Am – G`

`Am – G – C – G/B`
`Am – G – F – G`

First half:

`F – G – Em – Am`
`Dm – Em – Fm – G`

Second half:
`C – G/B – Am – Em/G`
`F – G – Em – Am`
`Dm – Em – Fm – G – C`

**Interlude**

`Am – G – Am – G`

**Verse 2** (short)

**Bridge**

`C – B – F – C`

**Outro**

`Am – G – Am – G`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Interlude → Verse 2 → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus → Outro
