Hate That I Love You
Artist: Rihanna, Ne-Yo

Key: A-flat major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Studio piano editions and GuitarTuna place the record in A-flat major. Guitar usually uses capo 1 and plays `Cadd9 – D – G/B – Am7`, sounding `Db – Eb – Ab/C – Bbm7`. Ultimate Guitar and E-Chords use the same capo-1 shapes, sometimes simplifying `G/B` to `G` and `Am7` to `Cadd9`. Chordify’s Radio 1 Live Lounge pass is in D-flat (a different performance). Some charts call the original key D-flat because the loop starts on `Db`; that chord is the first color, not the tonic.

**Intro**

`Db – Eb – Ab/C – Bbm7`

The opening “that’s how much I love you” hook is this loop. Guitar often colors `Eb` as `Eb – Ebsus4`.

**Verse**

`Db – Eb – Ab/C – Bbm7`

**Pre-Chorus**

`Db – Eb – Ab/C – Bbm7`

Same cycle; the last line can sit on `Db – Eb` before the downbeat of the chorus.

**Chorus**

`Db – Eb – Ab/C – Bbm7`
`Db – Eb – Bbm7`

The “can’t let you go” turn lands on `Bbm7` instead of walking back through `Ab/C`.

**Bridge**

`Gbmaj7 – Fm7 – Dbmaj7 – Cm7`
`Gbmaj7 – Fm7 – Dbmaj7 – Cm7 – Bbm7`

Capo 1 is `Fmaj7 – Em7 – Cmaj7 – Bm7`. Simplified guitar charts stay in the verse shapes through this section.

**Outro**

`Db – Eb – Ab/C – Bbm7`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus → Outro
