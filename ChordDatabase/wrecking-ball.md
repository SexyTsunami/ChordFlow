Wrecking Ball
Artist: Miley Cyrus

Key: D minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Musicnotes’ published key is D minor; Tunebat’s F major is the relative major (the chorus starts on `F`). Guitar charts often transpose to Bm; to stay at concert pitch, use capo 5 and play in A minor (`Am – C – G – Dm`). Capo 3 with Am shapes is a whole-step low.

**Intro**

`Dm – F – C – Gm`

**Verse**

`Dm – F – C – Gm`

**Pre-Chorus**

`Bb – Dm – F – Bb`

**Chorus**

`F – C – Dm – Bb`

The “yeah, you wreck me” tag is `Dm – Bb`.

**Bridge**

`Bb – Dm – F – Am`
`Bb – Dm – F – Am`

Then the pre-chorus returns (`Bb – Dm – F – Bb`) into the last chorus.

**Outro**

`F – C – Dm – Bb`
`Dm – Bb`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Bridge → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Outro
