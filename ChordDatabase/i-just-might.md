I Just Might
Artist: Bruno Mars

Key: F major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. No capo is common. Verse is a two-chord vamp; the chorus adds a chromatic walk into `Am7`.

**Intro**

`F – Gm7`

**Verse**

`F – Gm7`

**Pre-Chorus**

`F – Gm7`

**Chorus**

`Gm7 – Abm7 – Am7 – Gm7`
`Am7 – Gm7`
`Am7 – Bbmaj7 – Gm7/C`

The last bar is often simplified to `Am7 – Bb – C`. Then the `F – Gm7` vamp returns as a post-chorus tag.

**Bridge**

`Am7 – Gm7`

Repeat. The last pass walks `Am7 – Bbmaj7` into the chorus.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus
