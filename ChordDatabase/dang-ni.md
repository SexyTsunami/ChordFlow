當你 (Dang Ni / When You)
Artist: JJ Lin (林俊傑 / 林俊杰)

Also written 当你. JJ Lin’s recording; Cyndi Wang’s (王心凌) original hit is in E (C shapes, capo 4). Key: C major, last choruses in D, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. No capo on the JJ version. Chorus second line often uses C/G in place of Am/G; the last choruses lift a whole step after the bridge.

**Intro**

`C – Dm7 – C/E – F`
`C – Dm7 – Fmaj7 – F`

**Verse**

`C – G/B – Am – Am/G – F – C/E – Dm7 – G`
`C – G/B – Am – Am/G – F – C/E – Dm7 – Fm – C`

**Chorus** (two matching halves)

First half:
`C – G – Am – C/G`
`F – C/E – Dm7 – G`

Second half:
`C – G – Am – Gm7 – C7`
`F – C/E – Dm7 – G – C`

After chorus 1:

`C – Fm – F`

Chorus 2 ends on Am, then:

`Am – G/B – G – C – Am – G/B – F – C/E – Dm – C`

**Bridge**

`Bb – F/A – Am – Dm`
`Bb – Gm – Asus4 – A`

**Final chorus** (in D)

`D – A/C# – Bm – D/A`
`G – D/F# – Em7 – A`
`D – A – Bm – Am7 – D7`
`G – D/F# – Em7 – A – D`

Repeat, then:

`D – Em7 – D/F# – G`
`D – Em7 – Gmaj7 – G`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Bridge → Final chorus → Final chorus
