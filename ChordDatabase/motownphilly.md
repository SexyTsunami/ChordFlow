Motownphilly
Artist: Boyz II Men

From Cooleyhighharmony (1991). Official title is Motownphilly (one word). Key: D minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Chordify, TempoPeek/AcousticBrainz, and Isolated Tracks agree on concert D minor around 113–115 BPM. The studio loop is `Dm – Gm – A7` with `Bb` color (D harmonic minor on the `A7`); acoustic Ultimate Guitar / AmChords / KhmerChords sheets add `F`, `C`, and `Am` as guitar fillers around that same tonic. No capo in D minor. Guitar can capo 5 and play Am-shapes (`Am – F – Dm – E7`, sounding `Dm – Bb – Gm – A7`). Keep `A7` rather than plain `A` on the turnaround.
