Cold Heart
Artist: Elton John, Dua Lipa

Key: B-flat minor, 4/4. PNAU remix of Elton John interpolating Rocket Man, Sacrifice, Kiss the Bride, and Where's the Shoorah?; this is that hit mix, not the 1995 album cut Cold. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original synth-pop arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Wikipedia, Hooktheory, and GuitarTuna’s original-key chart agree on B-flat minor and the loop `Bbm7 – Ebm7 – Gb – Fm7`. Ultimate Guitar’s popular chart uses capo 1 (`Am7 – Dm7 – F – Em`); capo 6 (`Em7 – Am7 – C – Bm7`) matches the same concert pitch. Wikipedia prints `Gb` as a triad; seventh-heavy charts spell `Gbmaj7`.

**Intro**

`Bbm7 – Ebm7 – Gbmaj7 – Fm7`

Four cycles of the remix vamp before the Sacrifice verse.

**Verse**

`Bbm7 – Ebm7 – Gbmaj7 – Fm7`

**Pre-Chorus**

`Bbm7 – Ebm7 – Gbmaj7 – Fm7`

Same four chords; the vocal sits on the Sacrifice hook.

**Chorus**

`Bbm7 – Ebm7 – Gbmaj7 – Fm7`

Rocket Man chorus over the same loop. No new harmony.

**Bridge**

`Bbm7 – Ebm7 – Gbmaj7 – Fm7`

Half-time feel, still the vamp; the Where's the Shoorah? tag is the same chords at the end.

**Outro**

`Bbm7 – Ebm7 – Gbmaj7 – Fm7`

Shoorah repeats to the fade.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus → Outro
