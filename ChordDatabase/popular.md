Popular
Artist: The Weeknd, Playboi Carti, Madonna

Key: B-flat minor, 4/4. Chordify, Klangio, and GuitarTuna’s original-key chart agree on B-flat minor; some auto-keys call the relative D-flat major. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar usually uses capo 1 and plays `F – G – Am` (sounding `Gb – Ab – Bbm`). Easy no-capo A-minor charts sit a half step below the record. Official-style reductions keep the synth as `Gbmaj7 – Fm7`; Ultimate Guitar’s capo-1 triads (`F – G – Am`) simplify that vamp.

**Intro**

`Gb – Ab – Bbm`
`Gbmaj7 – Fm7`

“I’ve seen the devil down Sunset” is the triad walk, then the song locks to the major-seventh / minor-seventh groove.

**Verse**

`Gbmaj7 – Fm7`

Madonna’s character sketch and The Weeknd’s second verse sit on this two-chord pad. Capo-1 guitar can keep `F – G – Am` if the sevenths feel crowded.

**Pre-Chorus**

`Gbmaj7 – Fm7`

“I know what she needs” does not change chords; the vocal climbs over the same vamp.

**Chorus**

`Gbmaj7 – Fm7`

The “popular” hook is the verse groove with a tighter rhythm. Some live reductions throw `Ab` in as a passing bass.

**Post-Chorus**

`Gbmaj7 – Fm7`

“Money on top of me” and Playboi Carti’s tags stay on the two chords.

**Bridge**

`Gbmaj7 – Fm7`

“I’m gettin’ money and I’m keepin’ it” is still the vamp; no new harmony.

**Outro**

`Gbmaj7 – Fm7`

Last “popular” repeats fade on the pad.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Post-Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Post-Chorus → Bridge → Chorus → Outro
