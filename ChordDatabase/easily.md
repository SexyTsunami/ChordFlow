Easily
Artist: Bruno Major

From A Song for Every Moon. Key: F major, 6/8. This is the common guitar/piano reduction of the studio cut; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Musicnotes and OKTAV publish F major; Singing Carrots lists the same original key. Tunebat tags G minor because the chorus starts on `Gm7` — keep F as the tonic. Guitar stays in F with no capo. TopChords, ChordsBase, and vchords agree on chorus `Gm7 – C7 – Fmaj7` and verse `Cm – F – Bbmaj7 – Ebmaj7`. Spell `A#maj7` / `D#maj7` charts as `Bbmaj7` / `Ebmaj7`. Easy Gm-only loops drop the verse’s Bb side.

**Chorus**

`Gm7 – C7 – Fmaj7 – F7`
`Gm7 – C7 – F`

Cold open on the hook. Some charts color the second “destiny” bar `Fmaj7` without the `F7`.

`Ebmaj7 – Cm7 – F – Bbmaj7`
