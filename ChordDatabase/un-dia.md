Un Día (One Day)
Artist: J Balvin, Dua Lipa, Bad Bunny, Tainy

Key: F-sharp minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original reggaeton arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Chordify prints `E – Bm7 – Dmaj7 – F#m` and labels F-sharp minor; Ultimate Guitar uses the triad reduction `E – Bm – D – F#m`. Some guitar sites call it E major because the loop starts on `E`. Guitar often uses capo 2 and plays `D – Am – C – Em`. Those same shapes with no capo sit a whole step below the record.

**Intro**

`E – Bm – D – F#m`

The opening vocal sits on this one loop. Color `Bm` as `Bm7` and `D` as `Dmaj7` to match Chordify.

**Pre-Chorus**

`E – Bm – D – F#m`

Same loop; the English pre-chorus and both Spanish verses use it.

**Chorus**

`E – Bm – D – F#m`

**Verse**

`E – Bm – D – F#m`

J Balvin’s verse and Bad Bunny’s later verse stay on the chorus loop.

**Bridge**

`E – Bm – D – F#m`

The short Spanish lift (“nunca me dejes”) does not change the harmony.

**Outro**

`E – Bm – D – F#m`

The chorus loop holds through the closing tags.

**Form**

Intro → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Verse → Bridge → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Outro
