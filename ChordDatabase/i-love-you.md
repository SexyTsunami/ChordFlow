i love you
Artist: Billie Eilish

Key: C major (relative A minor), 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original fingerstyle guitar adds extra passing tones and inner voices (Fmaj7b5, Cmaj7, G6). Guitar often uses no capo. Some analyses label the relative A minor.

**Intro**

`Fmaj7b5 – Cmaj7`

**Verse**

`Fmaj7b5 – Cmaj7`

**Chorus**

`Fmaj7 – G6 – Am – Dm`
`G7 – Am7`
`G – Am – Asus2 – C – Cadd9 – Cmaj7`
`Fmaj7 – Fmaj7b5 – Cmaj7`

**Bridge**

`Dm7 – G – Fmaj7 – Fmaj7b5`

**Outro**

`Fmaj7b5 – Cmaj7`

Simplified charts use `F – C` for the verse and `F – G – Am – Dm – G – C` for the chorus.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Bridge → Verse → Outro
