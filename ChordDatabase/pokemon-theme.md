Pokémon Theme
Artist: Pokémon

English opening of the original series (Jason Paige on Pokémon: 2.B.A. Master). Key: G minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Tunebat and Hooktheory agree on G minor around 145 BPM. Guitar commonly uses E-minor shapes, capo 3 (`Em – D – C – G`, sounding `Gm – F – Eb – Bb`). Open A-minor charts (`Am – G – F – C`) sit a whole step above the record. Musicnotes’ easy guitar TAB is a transposed E-minor edition.
