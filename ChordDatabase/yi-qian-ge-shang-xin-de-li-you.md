一千個傷心的理由 (Yi Qian Ge Shang Xin De Li You / A Thousand Reasons to Be Sad)
Artist: Jacky Cheung (Jackie Cheung / 張學友 / 张学友)

Key: G minor, 4/4. Chorus sits in the relative Bb; some Chinese sheets label Bb. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar commonly uses Em shapes, capo 3.

**Intro** (twice)

`Gm – Eb – F – Dm – Eb – Cm – D`

**Verse**

`Gm – Eb – F – Bb`
`Gm – Eb – F – Bb`
`Eb – F – Dm – Gm`
`Cm – F – D`

**Chorus** (two matching halves)

`Bb – F – Cm – Gm`
`Bb – Cm – Eb – F`

Second half ends on Eb, then the “被遺忘” pickup `Eb – D`.

**Interlude**

`Eb – D`
`Gm – Eb – F – Dm – Eb – Cm – D`

**Bridge**

`Gm – Cm – D7 – Gm – F – Bb – F – Bb – D`

**Outro**

`Eb – D – Gm – Eb – F – Dm – Eb – Cm – D – Gm`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Interlude → Verse → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus → Outro
