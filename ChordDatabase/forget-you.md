Forget You
Artist: CeeLo Green

Radio edit of Fuck You from The Lady Killer. Key: C major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original piano-and-bass arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. No capo. Hooktheory, Singing Carrots, and AmChords agree on C. Most guitar pages use `D7`; some write plain `D`. A passing `Fm` sometimes colors the last chorus hit.

The driving-around-town hook and the later “forget you” answers share this loop.

**Verse**
