Touch My Body
Artist: Mariah Carey

Key: B minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction of the E=MC² studio cut; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Musicnotes PVG publishes B minor as the original key (easy groove, about 80 BPM). Ultimate Guitar, E-Chords, and Chordify play `Em7 – A – Bm7 – F#m7` at concert pitch with no capo — that loop starts on `Em7`, which is why guitar blogs tag Em. Keep B minor as the tonic. Chordify’s A-major label is the `A` chord, not the home key.
