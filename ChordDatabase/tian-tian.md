天天 (Tian Tian / Everyday)
Artist: David Tao (陶喆)

Key: C major, last chorus in C#, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. No capo in C; after the lift, guitar often treats it as C# or slides the capo to 1. kanpu and Chord4 agree on C then C#; 91pu hosts a transposable chart. Some sheets write Am (or C/A) on the verse’s second bar instead of Am7.
