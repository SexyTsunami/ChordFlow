多遠都要在一起 (Duo Yuan Dou Yao Zai Yi Qi / Together No Matter How Far)
Artist: G.E.M. (鄧紫棋 / 邓紫棋)

Key: Eb major, 12/8; last chorus lifts a half step to E. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar commonly uses C shapes, capo 3, then capo 4 after the lift.

**Intro**

`Eb – Gm – Cm – Eb/Bb – Ab`

**Verse**

`Eb – Gm – Cm – Eb/Bb – Abmaj7`

**Prechorus**

`Fm – Eb/G – Ab – Bb`

**Chorus** (two matching halves)

First half:
`Eb – Gm – Ab – Bb`

Second half:
`Cm – Gm – Ab – Bb – Eb`

Chorus 2 second half ends on **Cm** instead of Eb.

**Bridge**

`Cm – Cm/B – Ab – Bb – B`

**Last chorus** (in E)

First half:
`E – G#m – A – B`

Second half:
`E – G#m – A – B7 – E`

**Outro**

`E – G#m – C#m – E/B – A`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Prechorus → Chorus → Intro vamp → Verse → Prechorus → Chorus (ends Cm) → Bridge → last chorus in E → Outro
