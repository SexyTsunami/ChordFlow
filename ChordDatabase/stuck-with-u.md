Stuck with U
Artist: Ariana Grande, Justin Bieber

Key: Ab major, 6/8. Musicnotes original published key is Ab; Hooktheory agrees. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar often uses capo 1 and plays `G – Bm – C – Em – D`. Some play-along charts count it in 4 at about 90 BPM.

`Ab – Cm – Db – Fm – Eb`

**Verse**

`Ab – Cm – Db – Fm – Eb`

**Pre-Chorus**

`Ab – Cm – Db – Fm – Eb`

Same five-chord loop.

**Chorus**

`Ab – Cm – Db – Fm – Eb`

**Bridge**

`Db – Ab`
`Db – Ebsus4`

Then back to the chorus loop.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus
