Sorry
Artist: Justin Bieber

Key: Eb major, 4/4 (some sheets mark 2/2 at 100). Musicnotes original published key is Eb; the loop also sits in relative C minor. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar often uses capo 3 and plays `F – Am – G` for the verse, `Dm – F – G` for the chorus.

**Intro**

`Ab – Cm – Bb – Bb`

**Verse**

`Ab – Cm – Bb – Bb`

**Pre-Chorus**

`Ab – Cm – Bb – Bb`

Same three-chord loop. Bb lasts two bars.

**Chorus**

`Fm – Fm – Ab – Bb`

Then back to `Ab – Cm – Bb – Bb` for the post-chorus / later verse feel.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Chorus
