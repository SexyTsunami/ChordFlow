Fortnight
Artist: Taylor Swift, Post Malone

Key: B major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar usually uses capo 4 and plays in G (`C – D – Bm – Em`, with `G` where the concert part has `B`). Ultimate Guitar’s original-key chart, ChordZone, and Tunebat’s acoustic listing agree on B major. ChordZone keeps `D#m` on some chorus lines where Ultimate Guitar writes `B`; those are I vs iii on the same bar — use `B` for the “weather / neighbors / wife” hits and `D#m` for the verse and bridge.

**Intro**

`E – F# – D#m – G#m`

Synth pad on the verse loop before the vocal.

**Verse**

`E – F# – D#m – G#m`
`E – F# – B – G#m`

Most lines are IV–V–iii–vi. The last phrase of each verse (“quiet treason,” “but I touched you”) lands `B` instead of `D#m`.

**Chorus**

`E – F# – B – G#m`

The “fortnight” hook is IV–V–I–vi. Some web charts print the verse’s `D#m` through the whole chorus; match the record and use `B` on “weather / neighbors.”

**Bridge**

`E – B – D#m – G#m`

“I love you, it’s ruining my life” / “I touched you for only a fortnight” — IV–I–iii–vi. Post Malone’s verse-three vocal sits on this loop.

**Outro**

`E – B – D#m – G#m`

Same as the bridge. “Another fortnight lost in America” repeats to the last “touch” hits.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Bridge → Outro
