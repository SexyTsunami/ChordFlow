Jackie Chan
Artist: Tiësto, Dzeko, Preme, Post Malone

Key: E-flat minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar usually uses capo 4 and plays `Bm – A – Em – G` (sounding `Ebm – Db – Abm – B`). Chordify and GuitarTuna’s original-key chart agree on E-flat minor; Tunebat spells the same key D-sharp minor. Ultimate Guitar’s F-sharp / `B – G#m – C#` chart is a different tonic and does not match the record (comments flag it). Fuller transcriptions walk `Abm7 – Bbm – Ebm – Db` under the vocal; that is a rotation of the same four pitch classes — keep the common EDM loop `Ebm – Db – Abm – B`.
