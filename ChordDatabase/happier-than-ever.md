Happier Than Ever
Artist: Billie Eilish

Key: C major, 4/4 (quiet half feels slower; the rock half is double-time). This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar often uses no capo in C. Piano for the ballad half; distorted guitar for the rock half.

**Intro**

`C – E7 – Am – F`

**Verse** (quiet)

`C – E7 – Am – Dm – G`

Later verse lines insert `C#m` as a passing chord: `Am – C#m`, then back to `C – E7`.

**Chorus** (quiet)

`C – E7 – Am – Dm – G`

**Rock verse**

`C – E7 – Am – F – Fm`
`C – E7 – Am – F`

**Rock chorus**

`C – E7 – Am – F`

**Outro**

`C – E7 – Am – F`

The quiet half leans `C – E7 – Am – Dm – G`; the rock half locks `C – E7 – Am – F`, with Fm as a color chord on the first rock pass. Some charts write E7 as E.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Rock verse → Rock chorus → Outro
