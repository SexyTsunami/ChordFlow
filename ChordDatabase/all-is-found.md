All Is Found
Artist: Evan Rachel Wood

Frozen 2 soundtrack, sung as Queen Iduna. Key: A minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. No capo. Musicnotes publishes A minor; Chordify and GuitarTuna match the studio cut. Final verse lifts a half step to Bb minor.

**Intro**

`Am`

Piano often colors this as `Amadd9 – Asus4 – Am`.

**Verse**

`Am – D – F – C`
`Dm – F – C – G – Am`

Two verses share this loop. Denser charts keep `Am7` on the last `Am`.

**Chorus**

`F – C – Dm – Bb`
`F – Em – Dm – Fm`

The `Fm` is the pivot into the key change.

**Verse 3** (half-step up)

`Bbm – Eb – Gb – Db`
`Ebm – Gb – Db – Ab – Bb`

Same shape as the opening verse, now in Bb minor.

**Outro**

`Bbm`

Studio voicings linger on `Bbmadd9 – Bbsus4 – Bbm`.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Verse → Chorus → Verse 3 → Outro
