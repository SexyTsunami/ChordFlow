小幸運 (Xiao Xing Yun / A Little Happiness)
Artist: Hebe Tien (田馥甄)

Also written 小幸运. Theme from 我的少女時代. Key: F major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Polygon結他友, Lawrence’s Collections, and 牛步音樂 agree on F. Guitar commonly uses C shapes, capo 5 (`C – D – G – C`, sounding `F – G – C – F`). Easy open-C charts sit a fourth below concert.

**Intro**

`F – G – C – F`

**Verse**

`F – G – C – F – C/E`
`Dm7 – G – C – F – C`
`F – G – C – F – C/E`
`Dm7 – G – C – F`

**Pre-Chorus**

`Bb – C – Am – Dm7`
`C – Gm7 – G – C`

**Chorus** (two matching halves)

First half:
`F – C – Dm7 – Am`
`Bb – F – G – C`

Second half:
`F – C – Dm7 – Am`
`Bb – F – G – C`

**Interlude**

`F – G – C`

**Verse 2** (short)

`F – G – C – F – C/E`
`Dm7 – G – C – F`

**Chorus**

`F – C – Dm7 – Am`
`Bb – F – G – C`
`F – C – Dm7 – Am`
`Bb – F – G – Bb – C`

Last pass often inserts `Bb` before the final `C`, and some sheets put a passing `A` into `Dm7`.

**Outro**

`F – G – C – F`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Interlude → Verse 2 → Chorus → Outro
