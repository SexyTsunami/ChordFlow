Superposition
Artist: Young the Giant

Key: G major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar stays in G with no capo. This is the Mirror Master album cut, not the later John Mayer remix. Tunebat and GuitarX agree on G major. E-Chords and ChordLines print the same `C – G – D` shapes but label the key C because the intro starts on `C`. Chordify hears the `D` cadences and lists D major. Verse `D` is often `Dsus4 – D`.

**Intro**

`C – G – Dsus4 – D`

**Verse**

`Am – G – Dsus4 – D`
`Am – G – D`

**Chorus**

`C – G – Dsus4 – D`

**Bridge**

`G – Em – Em7 – Am7`

**Outro**

`C – G – Dsus4 – D`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus → Outro
