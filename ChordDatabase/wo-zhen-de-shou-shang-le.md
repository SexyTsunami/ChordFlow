我真的受傷了 (Wo Zhen De Shou Shang Le / I'm Really Hurt)
Artist: Jacky Cheung (Jackie Cheung / 張學友 / 张学友)

Jacky Cheung’s widely played Mandarin recording of the Ivana Wong (王菀之) song. Key: G major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. No capo. Dominant is often D, D7, or D7sus4; some charts open the verse on Cmaj7.
