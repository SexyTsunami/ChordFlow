When You Believe
Artist: Whitney Houston, Mariah Carey

Key: B minor (Houston chorus in D major), 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction of the Houston/Carey pop single, not the film-only Schwartz arrangement. Verses stay in B minor; Houston's chorus is D, Carey's chorus lifts to E, and the last chorus lifts again to F-sharp. Guitar usually plays Bm shapes with no capo, or capo 2 and plays in A minor.

**Intro**

`Bm`

**Verse**

`Bm – A – F#m – Bm`
`Bm – D – Em – F#m`
`B – A – Bm`
`G – D – Em – A`

**Chorus** (Houston, D major)

`D – A/C# – Bm – A – G`
`D – A/C# – Bm – A`
`G – Em – A – Bm`

**Chorus** (Carey, E major)

`E – B/D# – C#m – B – A`
`E – B/D# – C#m – B`
`A – F#m – B – E`

**Bridge**

`E – C#m – A – B`
`C#m – B/D# – E – B – E/G#`
`A – E/G# – B`
`E – B/D# – C#m – B`

**Final chorus** (F-sharp major)

`F# – C#/F – Ebm – C# – B`
`F# – C#/F – Ebm – C#`
`B – Ebm`
`B – C# – Ebm – C#`
`B – C# – F#`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus (D) → Verse → Chorus (E) → Bridge → Chorus (F#)
