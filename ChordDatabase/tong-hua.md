童話 (Tong Hua / Fairy Tale)
Artist: Michael Wong (光良)

Also written 童话. Key: F# major, 4/4, with later choruses lifting to Ab then Bb. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. kanpu and Chords Haven agree on concert F#. Guitar usually uses G shapes sounding a half step high, or C shapes, capo 6 (`C – Am – F – G`, sounding `F# – D#m – B – C#`). Chord4 and Ultimate Guitar keep the same F# verse/chorus and the two upward chorus lifts. Easy G-only sheets skip the modulations.

**Intro**

`F# – D#m – B – C#`

Capo-6 charts color this `Cadd9 – Am9 – F9 – Gsus4 – G`.

**Verse**

`F# – D#m – B – C#`
`F# – D#m – B – C#`

**Pre-Chorus**

`A#m – D#m – B – C#`
`A#m – D#m – B – C#`

**Chorus**

`F# – C#/F – D#m – D#m7/C#`
`B – C# – F# – F#7`
`B – C# – F# – C#/F – D#m – D#m7/C#`
`B – C# – F#`

Walking-bass slashes are the piano/guitar inner voice; beginner charts flatten the first line to `F# – C# – D#m – C#`.

**Interlude**

`B – F# – G#m – F#`
`B – F# – G#m – C#sus4 – C#`

**Chorus (Ab)**

`Ab – Eb/G – Fm – Fm7/Eb`
`Db – Eb – Ab – Ab7`
`Db – Eb – Ab – Eb/G – Fm – Fm7/Eb`
`Db – Eb – Ab`

Whole step up from F#. Capo-6 players switch to D shapes.

**Chorus (Bb)**

`Bb – F/A – Gm – Gm7/F`
`Eb – F – Bb – Bb7`
`Eb – F – Bb – F/A – Gm – Gm7/F`
`Eb – F – Bb`

Another whole step. Last cadence often walks `Eb – F – Bb` on the final tag.

**Outro**

`Eb – F – Bb`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Interlude → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Chorus (Ab) → Chorus (Bb) → Outro
