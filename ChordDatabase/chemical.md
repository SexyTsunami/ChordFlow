Chemical
Artist: Post Malone

Key: D major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar stays in D with no capo. Ultimate Guitar, Hooktheory, ChordZone, and Songdata list D major and the `D – Em – G` loop. Chordify prints the same four chords (`D`, `Em`, `G`, `Bm`) but labels G major because the IV chord sits so long and the sung feel is half-time against a ~170 BPM grid — keep concert D. Pre-chorus `Bm – A – G` is vi–V–IV; do not make that the home loop.

**Intro**

`D – Em – G`

Two passes.

**Verse**

`D – Em – G`

**Pre-Chorus**

`Bm – A – G`

“You break me, then I break my rules.” This is the only new harmony before the hook.

**Chorus**

`D – Em – G`
`Bm – A – G`
`Bm – D – G`

Three “outside of the party” lines on I–ii–IV, then the “it’s chemical” tags: vi–V–IV and vi–I–IV. Later choruses add an extra `Bm – A – G` before the break.

**Outro**

`Bm – A – G`
`Bm – D – G`

The “chemical” tags and the “da-da-da” line, without returning to the `D – Em – G` verse vamp.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Intro → Outro
