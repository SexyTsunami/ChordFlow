你不知道的事 (Ni Bu Zhi Dao De Shi / Things You Don't Know)
Artist: Wang Leehom (Leehom Wang / 王力宏)

Key: Db major (C#), 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar commonly uses C shapes, capo 1. Some charts swap Gb–Ab for Ebm7–Ab on the middle chorus line.

**Intro**

`Db – Gb`

Often played twice.

**Verse**

`Db – Gb – Db – Ab`
`Db – Gb – Db – Ab`

**Pre-chorus**

`Gb – Absus4 – Ab`
`Gb – Absus4 – Ab`
`Gb – Absus4 – Ab – Db – Db7`

Some charts write plain Ab in place of Absus4–Ab, or walk Db – Ebm7 – Db into the chorus.

**Chorus**

`Gb – Ab – Fm – Bbm`
`Gb – Ab – Db – Db7`
`Gb – Ab – Fm – Bbm`
`Gb – Ab – Db`

Turnaround back to the verse is often Db – Gb.

**Interlude** (after chorus 2)

`Db – Gb – Db – Gb`
`Db – Ab – Gb – Ab`
`Gb – Ab – Db – Db7`

Then back into the chorus.

**Outro**

`Db – Gb – Db – Gb – Db`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Interlude → Chorus → Outro
