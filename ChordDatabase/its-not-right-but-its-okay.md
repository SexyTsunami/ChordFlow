It's Not Right But It's Okay
Artist: Whitney Houston

Key: C minor, 4/4 (about 130 BPM). This is the Rodney Jerkins album cut from My Love Is Your Love, not the Thunderpuss club remix. Musicnotes original published key is C minor; Wikipedia, Chordify, and Ultimate Guitar agree. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original Darkchild arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices (Cm7, Fm7, Abm7). Guitar usually uses Fm and Cm barre shapes with no capo, or capo 3 and plays in A minor (Am – Em).
