明明就 (Ming Ming Jiu / Obviously)
Artist: Jay Chou (周杰倫 / 周杰伦)

Key: A major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar often uses capo 2 and plays in G. Some charts label the key D because the intro starts on Dmaj7; the song cadences in A.

`F#m – B7 – E7`
`A – B7 – Dm – A`
`F#m – B7 – Esus4 – E7`

**Pre-chorus**

`C#sus4 – C# – F#m/E`
`Bm7 – Esus4 – E7`

**Chorus** (two matching halves)

First half:
`Dmaj7 – E7 – C#m7 – F#m – Bm7 – E7 – A`

Second half:
`Dmaj7 – E7 – C#m7 – F#m – Bm7 – E7 – A`

**Interlude 2**
