Without You
Artist: Mariah Carey

Key: F# major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction of Mariah’s Music Box cover of the Nilsson / Badfinger song; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Chordify, Sheeteria, and TempoPeek agree the recording is F# (about 61 BPM). Musicnotes’ Mariah PVG is a singer-friendly F; transpose that up a half step for concert. Guitar usually capos 2 and plays E-shapes (`E – C#m – F#m – B7`, sounding `F# – D#m – G#m – C#7`), or capos 4 and plays D-shapes. Nilsson’s original is E — do not mix the two keys.

**Intro**

`F# – C#/F`

Piano octaves on `F#` with the `C#` bass walk. Guitar charts often write `F#sus2 – F#/A#`.

**Verse**

`F# – D#m7`
`G#m – A# – A#/C – A#/C# – A#/D#`
`D#m – D#m/C# – G#add6 – G#`
`F# – C#7`

Easy sheets print `F# – D#m – G#m – C#`. Keep the climbing `A#` bass in the verse.

**Chorus**

`F# – D#m – G#m – C#7`
`F# – D#m – G#m – C#7`

**Outro**

`F# – D#m – G#m – C#7`

Chorus loop to fade.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Chorus → Outro
