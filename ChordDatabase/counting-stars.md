Counting Stars
Artist: OneRepublic

Key: C# minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction of the Native studio cut; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar usually uses capo 4 and plays in A minor (`Am – C – G – F`). GuitarTuna, Chordsup, and capo-4 charts agree on concert `C#m – E – B – A`. Auto-pages that label E major are hearing the relative major; keep the song starting on `C#m`.
