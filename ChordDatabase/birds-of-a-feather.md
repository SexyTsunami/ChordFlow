BIRDS OF A FEATHER
Artist: Billie Eilish

Key: D major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices (often Dmaj7, B9, Em7, Asus4). Guitar often uses no capo in D, or capo 2 and plays in C (`C – Am – Dm – G`).

**Intro**

`D`

**Verse**

`D – Bm – Em – A`

**Refrain**

`D – Bm – Em – A`

**Pre-Chorus**

`D – Bm – Em – A`

**Chorus**

`D – Bm – Em – A`

**Post-Chorus**

`D – Bm – Em – A`

**Outro**

`D – Bm – Em – A`

The same four-chord loop covers every section. Some charts stay on D for the spoken/sung intro pickup before the full loop starts.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Refrain → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Post-Chorus → Outro
