菊花台 (Ju Hua Tai / Chrysanthemum Terrace)
Artist: Jay Chou (周杰倫 / 周杰伦)

Key: F major, 4/4 (relative D minor; many guitar charts that show D minor or C# minor are capo/transposed). From *依然范特西*; theme of *Curse of the Golden Flower* (滿城盡帶黃金甲). This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar often uses capo 5 and plays in C. The last chorus often lifts a whole step to G.

**Intro**

`F – Dm/B – Bb – F/A – Gm7 – Am7 – G/B – C`
`F – Dm/B – Bb – Bbm – Am7 – Dm7 – Gm7 – C – F`

**Verse**

`F – C/E – Dm7 – Dm7/C – Bb – F/A – Gm7 – C`
`Dm – Am7 – Bb – F/A – Gm7 – C`

`F – C/E – Dm7 – Dm7/C – Bb – F/A – Gm7 – C`
`Dm – Am7 – Bb – F/A – Gm7 – C – F`

**Chorus** (two matching halves)

First half:
`F – Dm/B – Bb – F/A – Gm7 – Am7 – G/B – C`

Second half:
`F – Dm/B – Bb – Bbm – Am7 – Dm7 – Gm7 – C – F`

**Interlude**

`F – G – Bbm – F`
`Dm – G – Bbm – F`

**Last chorus**

Same pattern, up a whole step to G:

`G – Em/C# – C – G/B – Am7 – Bm7 – A/C# – D`
`G – Em/C# – C – Cm – Bm7 – Em7 – Am7 – D – G`

**Outro**

`G – Em – C – Bm7 – Am7 – D – G`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Interlude → last chorus in G → Outro
