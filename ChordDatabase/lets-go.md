Let's Go
Artist: Calvin Harris, Ne-Yo

Key: E minor, 4/4. Ultimate Guitar, Cifra Club, Chordify, and Hooktheory agree on E minor. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original synth arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar stays in E minor with no capo. Chordify simplifies the whole song to `Em – G – Am – C`. Ultimate Guitar and Cifra Club keep the verse walk `Em – Bm – C – Am – Bm – C – D`; a 2012 theory writeup spells the drop `Em – C – Am7 – Bm7 – Gmaj7` where guitar charts use `Em – Em/G – Am – Bm – C`.

**Intro**

`Em – Bm – C – Am – Bm – C – D`

**Verse**

`Em – Bm – C – Am – Bm – C – D`

**Pre-Chorus**

`Em – Bm – C – Am – Bm – C – D`

Same loop; the “it’s not about what you’ve done” lines do not change the reduction.

**Chorus**

`Em – Em/G – Am – Bm – C`

**Bridge**

`Em – Bm – C – Am – Bm – C – D`

The “let’s make it happen tonight” chant is the verse loop.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus
