Olalla
Artist: Blanco White

Key: Eb major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction of the On the Other Side cut; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. GuitarTabsExplorer and chords-and-tabs.net write capo 3 in C (`C – Am – F – G`, sounding `Eb – Cm – Ab – Bb`) and label concert Eb. Lamucal’s no-capo chart is the same Eb loop. Tunebat and Snaplyrics often tag C minor because the verse sits on `Cm`; keep Eb as the sounding tonic. Guitar usually uses capo 3. KhmerChords’ C-shape reprint with no capo sits a minor third below the record. Easy charts swap verse `Ab` for `Fm`.

`Eb – Cm – Ab – Bb`

`Eb – Cm – Ab – Bb`

Capo-3 G-shape is `G – C – Am – G`, last time `F/G`. Some ear charts print chorus `Bb – Eb – Ab – Fm` instead of `Cm`.
