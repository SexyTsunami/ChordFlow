真的愛妳 (Zhen De Ai Ni / Truly Love You)
Artist: Beyond (黃家駒 / 黄家驹 / Wong Ka Kui)

Also written 真的爱你. Cantonese original; title uses 妳. Key: C major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. No capo. Chordify, guitarworld, Chord4, and Ultimate Guitar agree on C. Jianpujia and many Chinese charts write the pre-chorus `F – G – Am – G/B`; UG and Chord4 simplify that bar to `F – G – F – G`.

**Intro** (guitar)

`C – G – Am – F`
`C – G – C`

The famous electric-guitar theme is this loop, played twice.

**Verse**

`C – G – Am – F`
`C – G – C`
`C – G – Am – F`
`C – G – C`

**Pre-Chorus**

`F – G – Am – G/B`

**Chorus**

`C – G – Am – F`
`C – G – C`
`C – G – Am – F`
`C – G – C`

**Solo**

`C – G – Am – F`
`C – G – C`

Middle guitar solo over the intro/chorus frame; the later double-length solo is the same loop twice.

**Outro**

`C – G – Am – F`
`C – G – C`

Last chorus plus a guitar tag of the intro.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Solo → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Solo → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Outro
