cardigan
Artist: Taylor Swift

From folklore (2020). Key: Eb major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Musicnotes PVG (MN0214753) publishes Eb; Chordify and Hooktheory agree on concert Eb (about 65 BPM, or 130 in double time). Verse and chorus sit on `Fm – Bb – Ab – Bb`; the pre-chorus opens on `Eb`. Ultimate Guitar capo-1 charts play `Em – A – G – D` (sounding `Fm – Bb – Ab – Eb` on the D shape). Guitar also uses capo 3 with Dm shapes (`Dm – G – F – G`, sounding `Fm – Bb – Ab – Bb`). Beginner capo-3 `C – G – Am – F` sheets are a four-chord approximation, not the record’s `Fm` start.
