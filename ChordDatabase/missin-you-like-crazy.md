Missin' You Like Crazy
Artist: Us The Duo

Key: B-flat major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction of the Us studio cut; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. ChordU and Snaplyrics/Tunebat-style listings agree on Bb (A#) major at about 79 BPM. Guitar usually uses capo 3 and plays `G – Am – C – G` (concert `Bb – Cm – Eb – Bb`). Ultimate Guitar’s capo-3 chart matches. Easy G-major charts with no capo sit a minor third below the record.
