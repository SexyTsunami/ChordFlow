Finesse
Artist: Bruno Mars

Key: Ab major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Album version is the faster new-jack-swing cut (~124 BPM), not the Cardi B remix. Guitar often uses capo 1 (`Em – D – G – C` sounding in Ab) or capo 3. Easy charts write `Fm – Eb – Ab – Db`; a closer R&B reduction is `Dbmaj7 – C7 – Fm7 – Abmaj7`.

`Db – Eb`

The “got it goin’ on” tag is `Db – Eb – Fm` (or `Dbmaj7 – C7 – Fm7` if using sevenths).
