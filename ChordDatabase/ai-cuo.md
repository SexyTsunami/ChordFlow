愛錯 (Ai Cuo / Loved Wrong)
Artist: Wang Leehom (Leehom Wang / 王力宏)

Also written 爱错. Key: Db major verse, chorus in Eb, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar commonly uses C shapes then D shapes, capo 1. Some charts use Gsus4–G in place of Dm7b5–G7; duet charts may raise the last chorus a whole step.

**Intro** (twice)

`Bbm7 – Gbmaj7 – Db – Ab`

**Verse**

`Bbm7 – Gbmaj7 – Db – Ab`
`Bbm7 – Gbmaj7 – Db – Ab`
`Bbm7 – Gbmaj7 – Db – Ab/C – B`
`Gb/Bb – Ebm7 – Ab – Bb`

**Chorus** (Eb)

`Eb – Dm7b5 – G7 – Cm7 – Bbm7 – Eb7`
`Abmaj7 – Gm – Cm7 – Fm7 – Bb – Eb`

**Bridge**

`Dm7b5 – G7 – Cm7 – Bbm7 – Eb/G`
`Ab – Gm – Cm7 – Fm7 – Bb – B`

**Final chorus**

Same as the chorus in Eb. Some live/duet charts then take it up to F.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus
