Hero
Artist: Enrique Iglesias

Key: G major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction of the Escape studio cut; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Musicnotes and GuitarTuna’s original-key edition agree on G major with no capo. The record’s guitar voicings are closer to `G – Em7 – Cadd9 – Dsus4`; beginner charts drop those to `G – Em – C – D`.

**Intro**

`G – Em7 – Cadd9 – Dsus4`

**Verse**

`G – Em7 – Cadd9 – Dsus4`

The last line of each verse cadences `Cadd9 – Dsus4 – G`.

**Chorus**

`G – Dsus4 – Cadd9`
`G – Dsus4 – Cadd9`
`G – Dsus4 – Cadd9`
`G – Dsus4 – Cadd9`

Beginner sheets print `G – D – C`. Some reductions add a passing `G` between `Cadd9` phrases.

**Bridge**

`Em7 – Cadd9 – Dsus4`
`G – Em7 – Cadd9 – Dsus4 – G`

“I just want to hold you” starts on `Em7`. Later hits vamp `G – Cadd9` as a breakdown before the last chorus.

**Outro**

`G – Dsus4 – Cadd9`

Chorus loop to the last “I can be your hero.”

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus → Outro
