Wow.
Artist: Post Malone

Key: F-sharp minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original trap arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Most of the song is an F-sharp minor pedal; the hook answers with `C#`. Guitar usually uses capo 2 and plays `Em`, or stays on `F#m` with no capo. Chordify and ChordZone agree on F-sharp minor. Chordify’s official-video pass also prints `Gbm – C#m – E` (enharmonic F-sharp minor with a relative `E`); another pass prints `F#m – D – C#`. Keep the reduction as `F#m` with `C#` on the “wow” hits.

**Intro**

`F#m`

**Verse**

`F#m`

Occasional `C#` stabs under the “LaCroix” / grin lines; the harmony does not leave F-sharp minor.

**Chorus**

`F#m – C#`

`C#` lands on the “bottles are lonely” / “wow” answer. Many guitar charts stay on `F#m` for the whole hook.

**Outro**

`F#m – C#`

The “wow” tags bounce between the pedal and the `C#` hit.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Outro
