Something Just Like This
Artist: The Chainsmokers, Coldplay

Key: B minor, 4/4. Official sheet music and Wikipedia list B minor; the loop can also be heard as D major without a tonic D chord. Wikipedia’s voicing is G(add9)–Asus–Bm–Asus. Guitar often uses capo 2 and plays F – G – Am – G. Many guitar charts wrongly use Bm – G – D – A.

**Intro**

`G – A – Bm – A`

**Verse**

`G – A – Bm – A`

**Pre-chorus**

`G – A – Bm – A`

**Chorus**

`G – A – Bm – A`

**Bridge**

`D – A – D – A`
`Bm – A – G – A`

Then back to the main loop.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus
