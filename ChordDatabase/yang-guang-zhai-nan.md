陽光宅男 (Yang Guang Zhai Nan / Sunny Homeboy)
Artist: Jay Chou (周杰倫 / 周杰伦)

Key: E major, 4/4. From *我很忙*. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar often uses capo 4 and plays in C.

**Intro**

`E – B – C#m – A`

**Verse**

`E – B – C#m – A – B`
`E – B – C#m – A – B`

**Pre-chorus**

`C#m – G#m – A – E – F#m7 – B`

**Chorus** (two matching halves)

First half:
`C#m – A – B – E`
`C#m – A – B – E`

Second half:
`C#m – A – B – E`
`C#m – A – B – E`

**Rap**

Same loop as the verse:

`E – B – C#m – A – B`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Rap → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Chorus
