Leave the Door Open
Artist: Silk Sonic (Bruno Mars, Anderson .Paak)

Key: C major, 2/2 (slow in 2; often counted in 4). This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Sevenths and slash chords are part of the groove. The last chorus modulates up a half step to Db.

**Intro**

`Fmaj7 – G/F – Em7 – Am7`

**Verse**

`Fmaj7 – G/F – Em7 – Am7`

**Pre-Chorus**

`Ebmaj7 – Abmaj7 – Gm7 – Fm7 – Gsus4`

**Chorus**

`Fmaj7 – G/F – Em7 – Am7`
`Fmaj7 – G11 – Cmaj9 – A7b9`

**Bridge**

`Abmaj9 – G7sus4`
`Gbmaj9 – F7sus4 – Ebm7`

Then the final chorus in Db:

`Gbmaj7 – Ab/Gb – Fm7 – Bbm7`
`Gbmaj7 – Ab – Dbmaj9 – Bb7b9`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus (in Db)
