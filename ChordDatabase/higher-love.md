Higher Love
Artist: Kygo, Whitney Houston

Key: A-flat major, 4/4. This is the Kygo/Houston 2019 concert key (Musicnotes original published key is A-flat). Steve Winwood's 1986 original is a different key and is not this chart. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar often uses capo 1 and plays in G (Am–G–F–G, C–G–D–Em), or plays A-flat barres with no capo.

**Intro**

`Bbm – Ab – Eb – Fm`
`Db – Ab – Eb – Fm`

**Verse**

`Bbm – Ab – Gb – Ab`
`Gb – Db – Bbm – Ab`
`Bbm – Ab – Gb – Ab`
`Gb – Db – Ab – Eb`

**Pre-Chorus**

`Eb – Fm`
`Eb – Cm – Db`
`Eb – Db – Ab`
`Eb – Cm – Fm`

**Chorus**

`Db – Ab – Eb – Fm`

The last chorus line often lands `Db – Ab – Fm – Eb` before the drop returns to the `Db – Ab – Eb – Fm` loop.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Chorus
