Go Flex
Artist: Post Malone

Key: F major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar usually uses capo 5 and plays `Fmaj7 – C – G – Am` in the verse (sounding `Bbmaj7 – F – C – Dm`) and `E7 – Fmaj7 – C – G` in the hook (sounding `A7 – Bbmaj7 – F – C`). Chordify and Tunebat call it F major because the loop is IV–I–V–vi resolving to F. No-capo charts that label B-flat major are reading the first chord as tonic; keep concert F. Chorus first-chord spellings disagree: the producer’s Genius note and Chordify use `Am` (capo 5 `Em`); the most-played capo-5 guitar tab uses `E7` (`A7` concert); some ears write plain `A`. Use `A7` for the guitar/piano reduction.

**Intro**

`Bbmaj7 – F – C – Dm`

**Verse**

`Bbmaj7 – F – C – Dm`

Beginner no-capo charts drop the major seventh and print `Bb – F – C – Dm`.

**Chorus**

`A7 – Bbmaj7 – F – C`

The “go flex” hook. If the `A7` bite feels strong, Chordify’s `Am` is the triadic stand-in.

**Bridge**

`A7 – Bbmaj7 – F – C`

Same four chords as the chorus (“knowing all of this just don’t make a difference”).

**Outro**

`A7 – Bbmaj7 – F – C`

Last hook; the final line often clips to `F – C`.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus → Outro
