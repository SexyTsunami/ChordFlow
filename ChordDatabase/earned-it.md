Earned It
Artist: The Weeknd

Key: D minor, 6/8. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original string arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Musicnotes and Hooktheory agree on D minor in compound time (Hooktheory marks 12/8; guitar reductions usually write 6/8). ChordLines labels G minor because the loop starts on `Gm7`; treat `Gm7` as the first chord of a D-minor vamp, not a new tonic. Guitar stays in D minor with no capo. Simplified charts drop the sevenths to `Gm – Dm`.

**Intro**

`Gm7 – Dm7`

Strings on the two-chord sway. The bass often walks `D – F – F#` into the next `Gm7`.

**Verse**

`Gm7 – Dm7`

**Pre-Chorus**

`Gm7 – Dm7`

Same oscillation; the vocal stacks on “I’ma care for you.”

**Chorus**

`Gm7 – Dm7`

**Bridge**

`Gm7 – Dm7`

Still the two-chord loop; later choruses sometimes walk a descending bass:

`Dm – F/C – Bdim – Bb`

**Outro**

`Gm7 – Dm7`

The two-chord vamp returns; some charts keep the descending `Dm – F/C – Bdim – Bb` walk to the fade.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus → Outro
