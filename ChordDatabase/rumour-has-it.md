Rumour Has It
Artist: Adele

Key: D minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar usually plays open Dm–Am–C–G with no capo. Some charts label the same loop G minor because the riff can feel Mixolydian on G.
