Howlin' for You
Artist: The Black Keys

Key: E minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar stays in E minor with no capo. Musicnotes and Hooktheory agree on E minor. Verse charts sit on `Em`; the chorus chant is a B–D–E power-chord riff that beginner pages flatten to `Em – D – A`.

**Intro**

`Em`

Bass and guitar walk `E – G – A` against `Em` (often written `E5`). Drums enter first; the riff repeats.

**Verse**

`Em`
`Bm – A – G – Em`

Most of the verse stays on `Em`. The last line of each stanza walks `Bm – A – G` back to `Em`.

**Chorus**

`B5 – D5 – E5`

The chant riff. Two bars of `E5` often follow a chorus pass. Easy charts reduce this to `Em – D – Em – A`.

**Solo**

`Em`

Same groove as the intro riff.

**Outro**

`Em`

Returns to the intro riff.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Solo → Chorus → Outro
