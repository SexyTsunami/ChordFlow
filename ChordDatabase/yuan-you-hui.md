園遊會 (Yuan You Hui / Carnival)
Artist: Jay Chou (周杰倫 / 周杰伦)

Key: B major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar often uses capo 4 and plays in G.

**Intro**

`B – G#m – E – F#`

**Verse**

`B – G#m – E – F#`

(same loop through both opening stanzas)

**Pre-chorus**

`C#m – G#m – E – F#`

**Chorus** (two matching halves)

First half:
`B – G#m – E – G#m – C#m – F#`

Second half:
`B – G#m – E – G#m – C#m – F# – B`

**Interlude**

`B – G#m – E – F#`

**Outro**

`B – G#m – E – F#`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Interlude → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Outro
