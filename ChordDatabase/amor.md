Amor
Artist: Derik Fein

Key: A minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Cifra Club, CIFRAS, and HopAmViet loop `Am7 – Dm7 – Fmaj7 – Em7` with no capo. 12NOTEZ lists A minor. Auto-key pages sometimes label E minor because the loop cadences on `Em7`; Cifra Club’s “E” tag is the same relative reading. Beginner charts drop the sevenths to `Am – Dm – F – Em`.
