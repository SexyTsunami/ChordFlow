Peter Pan Was Right
Artist: Anson Seabra

Key: F major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction of the 2022 single; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Musicnotes and Tunebat agree on F major around 84 BPM. Guitar stays in F with no capo. GuitarTwitt and GuitarTuna’s official-style chart loop `F – Am7 – Dm7 – Bbadd9`; easy sheets flatten those to `F – Am – Dm – Bb`. The only new harmony is the bridge `Bb – C`.

**Intro**

`F – Am7 – Dm7 – Bbadd9`

Chorus loop, often sung as the cold open.

**Verse**

`F – Am7 – Dm7 – Bbadd9`

**Pre-Chorus**

`F – Am7 – Dm7 – Bbadd9`

Same four bars as the verse; some charts label this the “ooh” lift rather than a new progression.

**Chorus**

`F – Am7 – Dm7 – Bbadd9`

**Bridge**

`Bb – C – F – Dm`
`Bb – C – F – Dm`

**Outro**

`F – Am7 – Dm7 – Bbadd9`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus → Outro
