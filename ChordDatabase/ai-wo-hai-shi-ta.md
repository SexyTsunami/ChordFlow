愛我還是他 (Ai Wo Hai Shi Ta / Love Me or Him)
Artist: David Tao (陶喆)

Also written 爱我还是他. Key: B major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar commonly uses G shapes, capo 4. Easy C-shape charts sit a half step above concert. Verse/chorus share the same four-bar loop; some C-shape sheets swap Em7 for D#m and add Esus4 into the pre-chorus.
