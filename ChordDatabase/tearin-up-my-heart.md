Tearin' up My Heart
Artist: *NSYNC

Key: A minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. ChordZone, GuitarTuna, and Musicnotes marketplace lead sheets agree on A minor. No capo.

**Intro**

`Am`

**Chorus**

`F – G – Am – G`
`F – Esus4 – E – Am`
`Gsus4 – G`

The record opens on the chorus. Denser charts color the third line `Fmaj7 – Esus4 – E7 – Am`.

**Verse**

`Am – G – E – Am`
`Am – G – E – Am – G – F`

**Pre-Chorus**

`Am – F – Am – F`
`C – Dm – Esus4 – E`

Second pre-chorus often starts on `F` instead of `Am` and may use `Csus4 – C`.

**Bridge**

`F – G – Am – G`
`F – Esus4 – E – Am`
`Gsus4 – G`

Same as the chorus; later hits stack the title line over it.

**Outro**

`F – G – Am – G`
`F – Esus4 – E – Am`
`Gsus4 – G – Am`

**Form**

Intro → Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus → Outro
