每天愛你多一些 (Mei Tian Ai Ni Duo Yi Xie / Love You More Each Day)
Artist: Jacky Cheung (Jackie Cheung / 張學友 / 张学友)

Cantonese original from 情不禁. Key: Db major (Chinese guitar charts often label C#), 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar commonly uses C shapes, capo 1. A Mandarin edition uses a similar loop but a slightly different chorus (often Gm7–C7 and E–Am in C-shape charts).

**Intro**

`Db – Ab – Bbm – Fm – Gb – Db – Ebm7 – Ab`

**Verse**

`Db – Ab – Bbm – Fm`
`Gb – Db – Ebm7 – Ab`

Second verse-pair ends `Ebm7 – Ab – Db`.

**Pre-chorus**

`Gb – Ab – Fm – Bb`
`Ebm7 – Gb – Ab`

**Chorus** (two matching halves)

`Db – Ab – Bbm – Fm`
`Gb – Fm – Ebm7 – Ab`

Second half ends `Ebm7 – Ab – Db`. Last chorus keeps the same loop; some charts only change the last vocal line.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Chorus → Outro (verse loop)
