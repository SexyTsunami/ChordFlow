Girls Like You
Artist: Maroon 5

Key: C major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction of the Red Pill Blues album cut (no Cardi B); the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Wikipedia, Hooktheory, and Musicnotes agree on C major at about 125 BPM. No capo. The whole song is `C – G – Am – F`. The Cardi B single uses the same loop and inserts a rap verse; this chart is the original/common album hit without that rap.

**Verse**

`C – G – Am – F`

**Chorus**

`C – G – Am – F`

**Bridge**

`C – G – Am – F`

The “maybe it’s 6:45” lift stays on the same four bars. Beginner charts never leave this loop; denser piano reductions add passing `C/E` or `G/B` that the guitar riff does not need.

**Outro**

`C – G – Am – F – C`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus → Outro
