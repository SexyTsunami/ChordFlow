十年 (Shi Nian / Ten Years)
Artist: Eason Chan (陳奕迅 / 陈奕迅)

Mandarin hit from 黑白灰; the Cantonese original is 明年今日. Key: Ab major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar commonly uses G shapes, capo 1. Kanpu labels the concert pitch G# (enharmonic with Ab). Easy C-shape charts sit a minor third below concert.
