Who Says
Artist: John Mayer

Key: D major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original fingerstyle arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. No capo on the studio cut. Musicnotes piano/vocal/guitar and guitar-tab editions, plus Ultimate Guitar–style charts, agree on D major. Live acoustic sets stay in D; a few TV performances sit elsewhere.

**Intro**

`D – Dsus2`

A short picked D figure, then the verse starts.

**Verse**

`D – G – D/F#`
`Em7 – A – Bbdim`
`Bm7 – E`
`G – A – Dsus2`

The `Bbdim` is the chromatic walk from `A` into `Bm7`. Easy charts drop it and go `A – Bm`. The `E` is a quick lift into the last `G – A – Dsus2` cadence.

**Chorus**

`G – D/F# – A`
`G – D/F# – A`
`G – D/F# – Bbdim – Bm7`
`Em7 – A – Asus4 – A7`

The first two phrases sit on `G – D/F# – A`. The last line walks `Bbdim – Bm7`, then the `Asus4 – A7` turnaround sets up the next verse.

**Bridge**

`G – Asus4 – A – D`
`G – D/F# – A`
`G – D/F# – Bbdim – Bm7`
`Em7 – A – Asus4 – A7`

Instrumental chorus. Mayer varies the `A` voicings and fills here; live versions add extra licks on that chord.

**Outro**

`G – Asus4 – A – D`
`G – D/F# – D`
`G – D/F# – D`

Settles on D after the last chorus.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Bridge → Verse → Chorus → Outro
