等你等到我心痛 (Deng Ni Deng Dao Wo Xin Tong / Waited Until My Heart Ached)
Artist: Jacky Cheung (Jackie Cheung / 張學友 / 张学友)

Key: G major, 4/4. The last reprise lifts a half step to Ab. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices.

**Intro**

`G – D/F# – Em – Bm – C – D – G`

**Verse**

`G – D/F# – Em – Bm – C – D – G`
`C – D – G – D/F# – Em – Bm – C – D – G`

**Chorus**

`G – D/F# – Em – Bm – C – D – G – C – D`
`G – D/F# – Em – Bm – C – D – G`

**Last reprise** (up a half step)

Same verse pattern in Ab:

`Ab – Eb/G – Fm – Cm – Db – Eb – Ab`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → last reprise in Ab
