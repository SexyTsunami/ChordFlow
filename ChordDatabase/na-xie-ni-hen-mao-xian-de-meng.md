那些你很冒險的夢 (Na Xie Ni Hen Mao Xian De Meng / Those Adventurous Dreams of Yours)
Artist: JJ Lin (林俊傑 / 林俊杰)

Also written 那些你很冒险的梦. Key: D major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar commonly uses C shapes, capo 2. Verse often uses D–A/C#–Bm–F#m in place of D–F#m7–Bm–F#m; some denser D-shape charts add a capo 1 and sit a half step above concert.

**Intro**

`Gmaj7 – A – F#m7 – Bm7 – Em7 – A – Dsus4 – D`

**Verse**

`D – F#m7 – Bm – F#m`
`G – A/G – F#m7 – Bm7 – Em7 – A`

Second verse adds a turnaround before the last line:

`D – F#m7 – Bm – F#m – Am – D7`
`G – A/G – F#m7 – Bm7 – Em7 – A`

**Chorus** (two matching halves)

First half:
`Gmaj7 – A/G – F#m7 – Bm7`
`Em7 – A – Dsus4 – D – D7`

Second half:
`Gmaj7 – A/G – F#m7 – Bm7`
`Em7 – A – Dsus4 – D`

**Interlude**

`Gmaj7 – A/G – F#m7 – Bm7 – Em7 – D/F# – Gm – A`

**Bridge**

`G – A – F#m7 – Bm7`
`Em7 – D/F# – G – A`

**Tag**

`Em7 – A – D`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Interlude → Bridge → Chorus → Tag
