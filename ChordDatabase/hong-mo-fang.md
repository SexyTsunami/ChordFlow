紅模仿 (Hong Mo Fang / Red Imitation)
Artist: Jay Chou (周杰倫 / 周杰伦)

From 依然范特西. Key: B minor, 4/4. Flamenco intro, then a hip-hop `Bm – Em – F#` vamp. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Chord4 and Guitarians write concert Bm (Am shapes, capo 2). Guitarworld labels the relative D with C shapes, capo 2; GetSongKEY’s Em is a whole-step miss of that same minor tonic.
