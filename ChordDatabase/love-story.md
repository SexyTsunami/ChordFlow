Love Story (Taylor's Version)
Artist: Taylor Swift

From Fearless (Taylor's Version) (2021). Key: D major, 4/4, with the last chorus lifting to E major. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Musicnotes chord/PVG editions publish D; Chordify, Hooktheory, and Tunebat/Snaplyrics tag the Taylor's Version cut D as well (about 119 BPM). Guitar usually uses capo 2 and plays C shapes (`C – G – Am – F`, sounding `D – A – Bm – G`). Ultimate Guitar’s TV charts match. No-capo charts stay in D, then jump to `E – B – C#m – A` for the final chorus. Same song as the 2008 original; this file is the Taylor's Version cut.
