All I Want for Christmas Is You
Artist: Mariah Carey

Key: G major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Musicnotes PVG, guitar tab, and leadsheet editions all publish G as the original key (about 69 BPM for the ballad open, then ~150). Ultimate Guitar, E-Chords, and Chordify agree on G with the `G – Em – C – D` loop. Guitar stays in G with no capo. Denser sheets keep `G/B`, `Cm6/Eb`, `E7`, and `Am7`; beginner charts drop those to `G – Em – C – D`. This is the 1994 Merry Christmas studio cut, not the SuperFestive remix.

**Intro** (ballad)

`G – Em7 – Cadd9 – Dsus4`

**Verse** (ballad)

`G – G/B – C – Cm6/Eb`
`G/D – Em – Cm6/Eb`
`G/D – E7 – Am7 – Cm/D – G`

Easy sheets print `G – Em – C – D` and skip the `Cm6/Eb` walk.

**Up-tempo vamp**

`G – Em – C – D`

**Verse** (up-tempo)

`G – G/B – C – Cm6/Eb`
`G – C – Cm6/Eb`

**Chorus**

`G – Em/G – Em – Cm6/Eb`
`G/D – E7 – Am7 – Cm/D`
`G – Em – Am7 – D`

Later choruses add a passing `B7` into `Em`. Beginner sheets stay on `G – Em – C – D`.

**Bridge**

`B7 – Em`
`B7 – Em`
`Cm6/Eb – G/D – E7`
`A7 – C/D`

**Outro**

`G – Em – Am7 – D`

Repeat the title vamp to fade.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Up-tempo vamp → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Bridge → Verse → Chorus → Outro
