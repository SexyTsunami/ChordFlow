Smooth Criminal
Artist: Michael Jackson

Key: A minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original bass-and-synth arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar is usually played with no capo in A minor. Capo 5 in E minor is a less common easy-shape option.

**Intro**

`Am – G/B – C – G/B – G`

Bass riff over the verse changes.

**Verse**

`Am – G/B – C – G/B – G`

Repeat. Some hits add a passing `B` (or `B7`) into the next `Am`.

**Pre-Chorus**

`F – G`
`F – G`
`F – Esus4 – E`

The pre-chorus chant sits on `F – G`. The last pass cadences `F – Esus4 – E`.

**Chorus**

`Am – G – F – G`

Repeat. A few charts land `E` instead of `G` at the end of a line.

**Tag**

`F – Esus4 – E – Am`

**Instrumental**

`Am – G/B – C – G/B – G`

Same as the verse riff.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Tag → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Tag → Instrumental → Chorus → Tag → Outro
