伊斯坦堡 (Yi Si Tan Bao / Istanbul)
Artist: Jay Chou (周杰倫 / 周杰伦)

From the debut album 杰倫 (2000), lyrics by 徐若瑄 (Vivian Hsu / 徐若瑄), not 八度空間. Key: A major, 4/4, with the chorus sitting in C. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. No capo in A; the chorus is often played as open C. Chord4, Guitarians, and 易谱库 agree on concert A for the verses. Easy D-major sheets are singer transposes.

**Intro** (twice)

`A – F#m – Dmaj7 – E`

**Verse**

`A – F#m – Dmaj7 – E`
`A – F#m – Dmaj7 – E`

**Pre-chorus**

`C#m7 – F#m – Bm7 – Dm7 – G`

The `Dm7 – G` bar is the pivot into C. Easy charts drop `Bm7` and write `D – F – E`.

**Chorus** (two matching halves)

First half:
`C – G/B – Am – Fmaj7 – G`

Second half:
`C – Am – Fmaj7 – G`

**Interlude**

`A – F#m – Dmaj7 – E`

**Outro**

`C`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Chorus → Interlude → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Chorus → Outro
