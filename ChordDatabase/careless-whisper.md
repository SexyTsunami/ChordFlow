Careless Whisper
Artist: George Michael

Wham! / Make It Big cut, issued as George Michael. Key: D minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Musicnotes and jazz/pop guitar lessons agree on D minor with no capo. The sax loop is `Dm7 – Gm7 – Bbmaj7 – Am7`; some charts reverse the last two as `Am7 – Bbmaj7`. Beginner sheets drop the sevenths to `Dm – Gm – Bb – Am`.

**Intro**

`Dm7 – Gm7 – Bbmaj7 – Am7`

Sax riff over the four-chord loop (twice).

**Verse**

`Dm7 – Gm7 – Bbmaj7 – Am7`

**Pre-Chorus**

`Dm7 – Gm7 – Bbmaj7 – Am7`

“I’m never gonna dance again” is the same vamp; some piano reductions color the second chord `Gm7 – F` as a passing tone.

**Chorus**

`Dm7 – Gm7 – Bbmaj7 – Am7`

**Solo**

`Dm7 – Gm7 – Bbmaj7 – Am7`

Sax chorus over the verse loop.

**Bridge**

`Dm7 – Gm7 – Bbmaj7 – Am7`

“Now that you’re gone” stays on the loop; the last `Am7` is often held into the final chorus.

**Outro**

`Dm7 – Gm7 – Bbmaj7 – Am7`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Solo → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus → Outro
