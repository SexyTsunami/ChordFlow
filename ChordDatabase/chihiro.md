CHIHIRO
Artist: Billie Eilish

Key: E minor (relative G major), 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices (Cmaj7, Am7/Amadd9, Em/Emadd9, Gmaj7). Guitar often uses no capo. Easy charts simplify to `C – Am – Em – G` and sometimes label the song C major because it starts on Cmaj7 (the VI chord).
