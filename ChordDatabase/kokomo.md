Kokomo
Artist: The Beach Boys

Key: C major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction of the Cocktail / Still Cruisin’ cut; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. GuitarTuna, E-Chords, and ChordLines agree on C with no capo. Denser charts use `Cmaj7`, `Gm7`, `Fm6`, and `D7`; easy sheets flatten those to `C – Gm – F – Fm – C – Dm – G`.

**Intro**

A cappella chorus hook, then the verse groove.

**Verse**

`C – Cmaj7 – Gm7 – F`
`Fm – C – D7 – G7`

Some reductions spell the second chord `Em` instead of `Cmaj7`. The `D7 – G7` is the turn “to get away from it all.”

**Chorus**

`C – F`
`C – F`
`Fm – C`
`Am – Dm – G7 – C`

“We’ll get there fast” is the `Fm – C` color. Island-name tags (`C` vamps on Martinique / Port-au-Prince) sit between choruses.

**Tag**

`C`

Place-name hits over tonic, then back to the verse or chorus.

**Solo**

`C – Cmaj7 – Gm7 – F`
`Fm – C – D7 – G7`

Same as the verse.

**Outro**

`C – F`
`C – F`
`Fm – C`
`Am – Dm – G7 – C`

Chorus to the fade.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Tag → Verse → Chorus → Tag → Solo → Verse → Chorus → Outro
