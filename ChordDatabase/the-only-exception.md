The Only Exception
Artist: Paramore

Key: B major, 6/8. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Published and no-capo charts agree on B major. Guitar usually uses capo 4 and plays in G (`G – Dm – Cmaj7`, concert `B – F#m – Emaj7`). Fender’s capo-2 A-shape lesson is a transposition.

**Intro**

`B – F#m – Emaj7`

**Verse**

`B – F#m – Emaj7`

**Chorus**

`B – F#m – E`

Beginner capo-4 charts print `C` instead of `Cmaj7` here; concert that is `E` rather than `Emaj7`.

**Bridge**

`C#m – B – F#`
`C#m – B – F# – E`

Capo-4 charts write `Am – G – D`. The “proof it’s not a dream” lift lands on `E`.

**Outro**

`A – E – B`

Capo-4 charts write `F – C – G`. This is the only new color.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus → Chorus → Outro
