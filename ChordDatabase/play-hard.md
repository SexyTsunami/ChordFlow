Play Hard
Artist: David Guetta, Ne-Yo, Akon

Key: G-sharp minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Chordify’s official-video pass is `G#m – B – F# – D#m` in B major / G-sharp minor; Tunebat’s Camelot 1A tag is G-sharp minor. Guitar often uses capo 4 and plays `Em – G – D – Bm`. Ultimate Guitar and E-Chords print capo 3 `C – G – Em – D` (concert E-flat), an easy-guitar transpose; a comment on that tab already suggests capo 4, which lands in this key as `E – B – G#m – F#`. Musicnotes’ singer PVG is published in A minor, not concert pitch.
