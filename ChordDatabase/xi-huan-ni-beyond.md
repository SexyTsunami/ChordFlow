喜歡妳 (Xi Huan Ni / Like You)
Artist: Beyond (黃家駒 / 黄家驹 / Wong Ka Kui)

Also written 喜欢你. Beyond original from 秘密警察, not the G.E.M. cover. Key: C major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. No capo. Ultimate Guitar, Chord4, GuitarBON, kanpu, and guitarworld agree on C. Do not use xi-huan-ni.md here — that file is G.E.M.’s F-then-G cover. Some guitar charts play G shapes capo 6 to reach this C concert pitch.

**Intro**

`Cadd9 – C – Cadd9 – C`

The open-string Cadd9 riff is the studio guitar figure; many reductions just sit on C.

**Verse**

`C – Am – C – Am`
`F – G`

**Chorus**

`C – Am – F – G`
`C – Am – F – G – C`

**Solo**

`C – Am – F – G`
`C – Am – F – G`

Muted electric-guitar interlude over the chorus frame; the original adds extra passing tones on the top string.

**Bridge**

`F – G – Am – F`
`F – G – Am – F – G`

**Outro**

`C – Am – F – G – C`

Last chorus often repeats the four-chord hook before landing on C.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Solo → Bridge → Chorus → Outro
