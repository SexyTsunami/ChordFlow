Mad
Artist: Ne-Yo

Key: C major, 4/4. Ultimate Guitar, Hooktheory, and ChordLines agree on C major with no capo. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. The main conflict is `Dm` vs `Am` on the verse loop: UG and WonderHowTo use `C – G – Dm – F`; E-Chords and ChordLines print `C – G – Am – F` for the intro and some verse lines. Keep `Dm` for the chorus; `Am` is the pre-chorus color.

**Intro**

`C – G – Dm – F`

**Verse**

`C – G – Dm – F`

Later verse phrases walk `Am – G – F – G` before returning to the loop.

**Pre-Chorus**

`Am – F – Dm – F – G`
`Am – F – Dm – G`

**Chorus**

`C – G – Dm – F`

**Bridge**

`Dm – Am`
`Dm – Am – G`
`Dm – C – F – C – Dm – G`

The last “we’re gonna be happy” line lands `C – G – Dm – F`.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus
