The Fate of Ophelia
Artist: Taylor Swift

From The Life of a Showgirl (2025). Key: F major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Hooktheory, Klangio, Chordify, and current Ultimate Guitar / acoustic charts agree the studio cut is F (about 124–125 BPM) with `Gm – Dm – F – C` and chorus `Bb – F – Dm – C`. Musicnotes PVG (MN0304940) publishes D minor — the relative minor of the same concert pitch, not a different recording. Guitar stays in F with no capo, or uses capo 5 C shapes (`Dm – Am – C – G`, sounding `Gm – Dm – F – C`). Keep `C7` / `Bbsus2` / `Fsus2` as passing color where denser tabs mark them.
