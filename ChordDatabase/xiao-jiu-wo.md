小酒窩 (Xiao Jiu Wo / Dimples)
Artist: JJ Lin (林俊傑 / 林俊杰) and Charlene Choi (蔡卓妍 / 阿Sa)

Also written 小酒窝. Duet with 蔡卓妍. Key: Eb major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar commonly uses C shapes, capo 3. D-major charts sit a half step below concert. Intro is often Eb–Ab–Eb–Ab; some charts open on Eb–Bb/D–Cm7–Ab–Bb.
