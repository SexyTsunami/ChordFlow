Hey Lover
Artist: LL Cool J, Boyz II Men

From LL Cool J’s Mr. Smith (1995). Key: C minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Chordify, Lamucal, ChordU, and Tency Music agree on concert C minor around 88 BPM. The bed samples Michael Jackson’s “The Lady in My Life” (that Thriller cut is C# minor); this record sits a half step lower. The loop is `Fm7 – Gm7 – Cm7`. Guitar stays in C minor with no capo, or uses Am-shapes, capo 3 (`Am7 – Dm7 – Em7`, sounding `Cm7 – Fm7 – Gm7`). Bettyloumusic’s capo-3 Am chart is the same concert pitch. ChordU’s extra `Ab` is an inner voice over the `Fm7`; keep C minor as the tonic.
