Gold on the Ceiling
Artist: The Black Keys

Key: G Mixolydian, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar stays in G with no capo. Hooktheory and guitar charts center the song on G (`G – F – C`); Tunebat and Klangio tag C major because of the C and F chords. Some chorus charts insert `Bb` after `C`.

**Intro**

`G – F – D – C – Bb – C`

Opening lick; then the verse vamp.

**Verse**

`G – F – C – G`

The lift into the pre-chorus is `F – C/E – G` (some charts write `F – E – C – G`).

**Pre-Chorus**

`F – C – G`

**Chorus**

`G – F – C – G`

A few reductions add `Bb` after `C`: `G – F – C – Bb – G`.

**Solo**

`F – C – Bb – G`

Same colors as the intro lick.

**Outro**

`G – F – C – G`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Solo → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Solo → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Outro
