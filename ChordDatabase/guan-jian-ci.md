關鍵詞 (Guan Jian Ci / Keywords)
Artist: JJ Lin (林俊傑 / 林俊杰)

Also written 关键词; also called The Key. Key: A minor (relative C major), 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar in Am/C, no capo. Some charts label the song C major.

**Intro**

`Fmaj7 – Em7 – Dm7 – Cmaj7`

**Verse**

`Fmaj7 – Em7 – Dm7 – Cmaj7`
`Fmaj7 – Em7 – Dm7 – C`

Second verse the same; some charts put Cmaj7 – C7 on the last bar.

**Chorus** (two matching halves)

First half:
`Gm7 – C7 – Fmaj7 – G – Em – Am – Dm7 – G – C`

Second half:
`Gm7 – C7 – Fmaj7 – G – Em – Am – Dm7 – G`

Turnaround into the intro vamp is Fmaj7. After the middle chorus some charts land G – C instead.

Simplified charts collapse the first chorus line to F – Em7 – Dm7, with optional G / Am7 / G passing chords on later repeats.

**Post-chorus**

`Fmaj7 – Em7 – Dm7 – C`
`Ab – G`

Then back into the chorus. Some charts stay on F – G through that bar and skip Ab.

**Interlude** (after chorus 1)

`Fmaj7 – Em7 – Dm7 – Am`

**Outro**

`Fmaj7 – Em7 – Dm7 – Dm7b5 – Bm – Gsus4 – Am`

Some charts end Fmaj7 – Em7 – Dm – C6 – G/B – Dm7 – G – Asus2.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Interlude → Verse → Chorus → Post-chorus → Chorus → Outro
