Un-Break My Heart
Artist: Toni Braxton

Key: B minor (chorus in D minor), 4/4. This is the common piano/guitar reduction of the Secrets studio cut; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Musicnotes and Wikipedia agree on B minor with the chorus in D minor. Guitar can stay in Bm with no capo. Charts that print the chorus as `Bm – Em – A` without the D-minor lift are missing the modulation.

**Intro**

`F#7 – Bm – Em – A`

The guitar fill is `F#7 – Bm – Em – A`. Some reductions open on `F#7` alone.

**Verse**

`Bm – Em – A – F#m`

Last line of the verse often stays on `A` instead of `F#m`, which becomes the pivot into D minor.

**Chorus**

`Dm – Gm – C – A`
`Dm – Gm – C – A`
`Dm – Gm – C – A`
`Dm – A – F#m`

Same cycle as the verse, now in D minor. The closing `A – F#m` (or `A – F#7`) is the pivot back to B minor. Easy sheets that jump from a Bm verse into this Dm chorus without labeling the key change are still describing the record.

**Bridge**

`G#m – C#m – F# – D#`

The middle-section lift (a minor third above the chorus). Some guitar charts instead repeat the B-minor verse loop here; keep the G#m color for the record.

**Outro**

`Dm – Gm – C – A`

Final “un-break my heart” tags stay in D minor to the end.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus → Outro
