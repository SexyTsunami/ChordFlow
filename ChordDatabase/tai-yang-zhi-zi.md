Artist: Jay Chou (周杰倫 / 周杰伦)

Title track of 太陽之子 (2026), not 最偉大的作品. Key: C minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar commonly uses Am shapes, capo 3 (`Am – F – C – G`, sounding `Cm – Ab – Eb – Bb`). Chord4 labels concert Cm; kanpu is the same pitch (Am, capo 3). 吉他世界 prints C shapes, capo 3, and calls the song Eb because that is the relative major, not a different concert tonic.
