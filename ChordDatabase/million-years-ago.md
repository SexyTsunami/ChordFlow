Million Years Ago
Artist: Adele

Key: C-sharp minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar often plays in the relative E major with no capo, or capo 1 and plays in C minor.

**Intro**

`E – D#m – C# – B – C#m`

**Verse**

`F#m – B – E – C#m`
`F#m – B – C#m`

**Chorus**

`F#m – B – E – A`

The tag back to the intro is `F#m` then `E – D#m – C# – B – C#m`.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Intro → Verse → Chorus → Intro
