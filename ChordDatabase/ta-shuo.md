她說 (Ta Shuo / She Said)
Artist: JJ Lin (林俊傑 / 林俊杰)

Also written 她说. Key: C major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. No capo. Denser charts replace G with G/F and add Fm–G into the last chorus cadence.

**Intro**

`F – G – Em – Am`
`G – Em7 – Dm7 – C – F – G`

**Verse**

`F – G – Em – Am – Dm7 – G – C – C7`
`F – G – Em – Am – Dm7 – G – C – C7`

**Chorus** (two matching halves)

First half:
`F – G – Em – Am`
`Dm7 – G – C – C7`

Second half:
`F – G – Em – Am`
`Dm7 – G – C`

Last time, the cadence is:

`Dm7 – Fm – G – C`

**Interlude**

`C – Em – Am – Em – F – Em – Dm7 – G`

**Tag**

`F – G – C`

**Outro**

`F – C – F – Cmaj7`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Interlude → Chorus → Tag → Outro
