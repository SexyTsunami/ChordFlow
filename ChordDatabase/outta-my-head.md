Outta My Head
Artist: Khalid, John Mayer

Key: C major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. No capo is needed. Verses start on F, so some guitar sites label the song F major; Musicnotes piano/vocal/guitar (original published key) and Chordify both list C major. Ultimate Guitar writes the same concert chords (`F – G – Am – C`) under a C-major key.

**Intro**

`F – G`

Short beat-and-guitar pickup on the verse pair.

**Verse**

`F – G`

Khalid’s verses stay on this two-chord vamp. Later verses add a third `F – G` line before the lift.

**Pre-Chorus**

`F – G`
`F – G – Em – F`

The pre-chorus lift is still `F – G`. Many guitar charts put `Em – F` on the last hit; Ultimate Guitar’s simpler cut stays on `F` there.

**Chorus**

`Am – G – F`

The title hook. Later choruses cadence `Am – G – C` on the last hit. Some easy charts insert `Em` (`Am – G – Em – F`); Ultimate Guitar and the common reduction omit it.

**Bridge**

`F – G`

John Mayer’s guitar break rides the verse pair. Charts sometimes write `G – F` for the solo voicings; sounding harmony is the same two chords.

**Outro**

`Am – G – F`

Last hook, same as the chorus.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus → Outro
