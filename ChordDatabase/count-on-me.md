Count on Me
Artist: Bruno Mars

Key: C major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. No capo is the usual guitar version. A few tools show capo 1; that is a transposition, not the record.

**Intro**

`C`

**Verse**

`C – Em – Am – G – F`

**Pre-Chorus**

`Dm – Em – F – G`

**Chorus**

`C – Em – Am – G – F`

The “ooh” tag continues `C – Em – Am – G – F` and often adds a `G` into the next verse.

**Bridge**

`Dm – Em – Am – G`
`Dm – Em – F – G`

**Outro**

`C – Em – Am – G – F – C`

Last line holds `F` into `C`.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus → Outro
