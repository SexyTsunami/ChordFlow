手寫的從前 (Shou Xie De Cong Qian / Handwritten Past)
Artist: Jay Chou (周杰倫 / 周杰伦)

Key: C major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original guitar part adds extra passing tones, walking bass, and inner voices.

**Verse**

`C – F – Fm – C – G/B`
`Am – Em – F – G`
`C – F – E7 – Am`
`F – C – Dm7 – G`

**Pre-chorus**

`Am – Dm7 – F – G`

**Chorus** (two matching halves)

First half:
`C – Bm7 – E7 – Am7 – Gm7 – C`
`F – Em – Am – Dm7 – G`

Second half:
same idea; the last line lands on `Dm7 – G` (or holds toward C into the next section)

**Verse 2**

Same as verse, then a longer middle:

`C – F – Fm – C – G/B`
`Am – Em – F – G`

**Bridge**

`F – E7 – Am`
`F – C – Dm7 – G`

**Form**

Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Verse 2 → Bridge → Chorus
