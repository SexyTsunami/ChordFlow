In The Stars
Artist: Benson Boone

From Walk Me Home... (2022); later on Fireworks & Rollerblades (2024). Key: Bb major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Musicnotes PVG, Chordify, and Hooktheory agree on Bb (relative G minor). Guitar usually uses capo 3 and plays G-shapes (`G – Bm – Em – C – D – Am`, sounding `Bb – Dm – Gm – Eb – F – Cm`). VChords, E-Chords, and GuitarTwitt agree on verse `Bb – Dm – Gm` and chorus `Eb – Bb – F – Gm`. Keep the chorus passing `Gbdim7` and the `Ebm` color; easy capo charts skip both.

**Verse**

`Bb – Dm – Gm`
`Bb – Dm – Gm`
`Bb – Dm – Gm`
`Cm – Bb – F`

Some sheets swap the first `Dm` for `F`. Spell `A#` charts as `Bb`.

**Chorus**

`Eb – Bb – F – Gm`
`Eb – Bb – F – Gm`
`Eb – Bb – F – Gbdim7 – Gm`
`Bb – Eb – Ebm – Bb`

First chorus often picks up on `Bb` before the `Eb` loop. Easy charts stay `Eb – Bb – F – Gm` all the way through.

**Bridge**

`Bb – Dm – Gm`
`Bb – Dm – Gm`
`Bb – Dm – Gm`
`Cm – Bb – F`

Same four-line shape as the verse.

**Form**

Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus
