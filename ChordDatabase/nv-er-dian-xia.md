女兒殿下 (Nv Er Dian Xia / Daughter Her Highness)
Artist: Jay Chou (周杰倫 / 周杰伦)

From 太陽之子. Key: F minor, 4/4, blues/funk feel. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitarworld labels concert Ab (the relative major) and uses G shapes, capo 1; piano writeups and 魔鏡 treat the groove as F-minor blues. Guitar commonly uses Em shapes, capo 1. The piano solo is F-minor blues, not a new progression.

`Fm7 – Bbm7 – C7 – Fm7`

Daughter spoken samples over the vamp.

**Chorus** (two matching halves)

First half:

Second half:
`Fm7 – Bbm7 – C7 – Fm7`

Easy reductions stay on the three-chord blues `Fm7 – Bbm7 – C7` through the chorus.
