說了再見 (Shuo Le Zai Jian / Said Goodbye)
Artist: Jay Chou (周杰倫 / 周杰伦)

Key: D major, 4/4. The last chorus lifts a half step to Eb. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original piano part adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar often uses capo 2 and plays in C.

**Intro**

`G – F#m – Bm – Em – F# – Bm – A`
`E/G# – F#m – Bm – Em – A – D`

**Verse**

`G – F#m – Bm – Em – A – D`
`G – F#m – Bm – Em – A`

**Chorus** (two matching halves)

First half:
`G – F#m – Bm – Em – F# – B`
`A – G# – G – F#m – Bm – Em – A`

Second half:
`G – F#m – Bm – Em – F# – B`
`A – G# – G – F#m – Bm – Em – A – D`

**Interlude**

`D – E/G# – G – A#dim – D – E/G# – A`

**Bridge**

`Bm – A# – A – G#`
`G – F# – B – Em – A – A#`

**Last chorus**

Same pattern, up a half step to Eb:

`G# – Gm – Cm – Fm – G – C`
`A# – A – G# – Gm – Cm – Fm – A#`
then the matching second half ending on **Eb**

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Interlude → Verse → Chorus → Bridge → last chorus in Eb
