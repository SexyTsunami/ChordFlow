蝸牛 (Wo Niu / Snail)
Artist: Jay Chou (周杰倫 / 周杰伦)

Classic campus/inspirational song from 範特西. Key: G major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar commonly uses C shapes, capo 3, which is why many beginner charts are written in C; 吉他世界, Chord Ark, and Guitarians agree the concert key is G.

`G – Bm7 – C – D – G`
`C – D – Bm7 – Em – F – D`

**Verse**

`G – Em – C – D – G`
`F – C – Cm – G – D – G`

**Chorus** (two matching halves)

First half:
`G – Bm7 – C – D – G`
`C – D – Bm7 – Em – F – D`

Second half:

`C – D – Bm7 – Em – Am7 – D – G`

**Last chorus**

`G – Bm7 – C – D – G`
`C – D – Bm7 – Em – Am7 – D – G`
`C – D – Bm7 – Em – Am7 – D`

**Outro**

`G – Cm – G`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Last chorus → Outro
