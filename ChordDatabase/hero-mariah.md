Hero
Artist: Mariah Carey

Key: E major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction of the 1993 Music Box studio cut, not Enrique Iglesias’s “Hero”; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Musicnotes PVG and Singer Pro editions publish E major (about 60–62 BPM). Ultimate Guitar, E-Chords, and Chordify agree on E with the descending-bass chorus. Guitar stays in E with no capo, or capo 4 and plays C-shapes. Easy piano Musicnotes in D is a beginner transposition. Keep the bass walk `E – E/D# – C#m – C#m/B – A`.

**Intro**

`E – E/D# – C#m – C#m/B – A – A/G# – F#m`

E-Chords adds a closing `B9`.

**Verse**

`E9 – D`
`C – B9`
`E9 – D/F#`
`C – Bsus4 – B9`

Some reductions write `Emaj7 – D – A/C# – B9` for the first phrase. Beginner sheets flatten this to `E – D – C – B`.

**Chorus**

`E9 – E/D# – C#m – C#m/B – A9`
`A/G# – F#m – B9 – E9`
`E/D# – C#m – C#m/B – A9`
`A/G# – F#m – B9 – E9`

**Bridge**

`C – G/B – G – D`
`C – G – D`
`C – G/B – G – D`
`C – G – A`

Borrowed C-major lift. Last `A` turns around to the chorus.

**Outro**

`E – E/D# – C#m – C#m/B – A9`
`A/G# – F#m – B9 – E9`

Intro piano figure under the last chorus tag.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus → Outro
