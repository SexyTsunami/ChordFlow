情人 (Qing Ren / Lover)
Artist: Beyond (黃家駒 / 黄家驹 / Wong Ka Kui)

Cantonese original; 黃家駒 composition with 黃家強 lead vocal. Key: C major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. No capo. Chordify, Ultimate Guitar, Guitarians, and daydayguitar agree on C. Easy charts write the chorus as `C – Am – F – G`; denser charts use `Dm7 – Gsus4` in those bars and `Fm` in the pre-chorus.

**Intro**

`C – Am – F – G`
`Fm – Bb – C`

**Verse**

`C – Am – F – G`
`C – Am – F – G`

**Pre-Chorus**

`F – G – C – G/B – Am`
`F – Fm – G`

**Chorus**

`C – Am – Dm7 – G`
`C – Am – Dm7 – F – G`

**Solo**

`C – Am – F – G`
`C – Am – F – G`

Guitar interlude over the verse frame after the first chorus, and again before the bridge.

**Bridge**

`F – G – C – Am`
`F – Fm – G`

**Outro**

`C – Am – F – G`

Fades on the verse loop; some charts write a last-chorus `Fm – G` tag into the fade.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Solo → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Solo → Bridge → Chorus → Outro
