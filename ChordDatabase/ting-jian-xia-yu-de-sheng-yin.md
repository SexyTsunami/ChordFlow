聽見下雨的聲音 (Ting Jian Xia Yu De Sheng Yin / The Sound of Rain)
Artist: Jay Chou (周杰倫 / 周杰伦)

Key: E major, 4/4. The last chorus lifts a half step to F. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar often uses capo 4 and plays in C (capo 5 for the lift).

**Intro**

`E – Esus4 – E – Esus4`

**Verse**

`E – G#m/D# – C#m – E/B`
`A – E/G# – C#m – F#m – B`

**Pre-chorus**

`C#m – G#/C – G#m/B – F#/A#`
`F#m – E/G# – A – B`

**Chorus** (two matching halves)

First half:
`E – Esus4 – C#m – A – G#m – C#m – F#m – B`

Second half:
`E – Esus4 – C#m – A – G#m – C#m – F#m – B`

**Interlude**

`E – Esus4 – C#m – A – B`

**Last chorus**

Same pattern, up a half step:

`F – Fsus4 – Dm – Bb – F/A – Dm – G – C`

**Outro**

`Dm – Bb – F – C`
`Dm – F/A – Bb`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Interlude → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus (lifts to F on the second half) → Outro
