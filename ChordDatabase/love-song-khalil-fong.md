Love Song
Artist: Khalil Fong (方大同)

From 未來. Key: C major, last chorus in C#, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. No capo in C. Chord4 and GuitarWorld agree on C, then a final-chorus lift. Passing `F/D` and `F/G` are extra guitar color; they collapse to `F` / `Dm7`. Do not confuse with Adele’s Lovesong.
