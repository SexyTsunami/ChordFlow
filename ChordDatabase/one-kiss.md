One Kiss
Artist: Calvin Harris, Dua Lipa

Key: A minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original house arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Ultimate Guitar and guitar lessons treat the track as A minor because the loop starts on `Am`. Wikipedia and Musicnotes publish F major (one-flat key signature) with `Am – Bbmaj7 – Fmaj7 – Gm9`. Keep A minor as the sounding tonic; F is the relative major, not a new concert pitch. Guitar stays in A minor with no capo. Some charts swap `Gm9` for `G`, `G7omit3`, or a passing `Dm`.

**Intro**

`Am – Bbmaj7 – Fmaj7 – Gm`

Long instrumental on the four-bar house loop.

**Verse**

`Am – Bbmaj7 – Fmaj7 – Gm`

**Pre-Chorus**

`Am – Bbmaj7 – Fmaj7 – Gm`

Same loop; a few guitar charts sneak `Dm` under the last line before `Gm`.

**Chorus**

`Am – Bbmaj7 – Fmaj7 – Gm`

The title hook is the verse loop with the bass heavier. The record often opens with this chorus after the intro.

**Post-Chorus**

`Am – Bbmaj7 – Fmaj7 – Gm`

Count-in hits over the same four chords; `Gm` is often just a bass pass.

**Bridge**

`G – Am – Bb – F`

The only real lift: starts on `G` instead of `Am`. Then the pre-chorus loop returns.

**Outro**

`Am – Bbmaj7 – Fmaj7 – Gm`

Final chorus and post-chorus vamp to the end.

**Form**

Intro → Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Post-Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Post-Chorus → Bridge → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Outro
