Artist: David Tao (陶喆), Jolin Tsai (蔡依林)

Also written 今天你要嫁给我. Duet with 蔡依林. Key: A major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar commonly uses G shapes, capo 2, or open A with no capo. 91pu, Chord4, Chords Haven, and pop-guitar blogs agree on concert A. Verse, chorus, and the “in the house” chant share the same I–Imaj7–I7–IV–iv loop.
