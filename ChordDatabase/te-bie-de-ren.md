特別的人 (Te Bie De Ren / Someone Special)
Artist: Khalil Fong (方大同)

Also written 特别的人. Key: B major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar usually uses capo 4 and plays in G (`Cmaj7 – Bm7 – Am7 – Gmaj7`). YoPu, GuitarWorld, and mymusic5 label concert B. Polygon’s open-C chart sits a half step above concert; keep the capo-4 G shapes transposed here.

**Intro**

`Emaj7 – D#m7 – C#m7 – Bmaj7`

**Verse**

`Emaj7 – D#m7 – G#m7 – C#m7 – F# – Bmaj7`
`Emaj7 – D#m7 – G#m7 – C#m7 – Em7`

Second half of verse 2 walks `D#m7 – G#7 – C#m7 – F#` into the chorus.

`C#m7 – F#`
