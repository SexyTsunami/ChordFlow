Fever
Artist: The Black Keys

Key: A minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar stays in A minor with no capo. ChordZone and key finders agree on A minor. The organ/bass riff is a descending walk; simplified pages skip the passing `E` and `C` and print only `Am – G – F – Dm – F`.

**Intro**

`Am – G – F – E – Dm – C – F – E`

**Verse**

`Am – G – F – E – Dm – C – F – E`

**Chorus**

`Am – G – F – E – Dm – C – F – E`

Same descending loop as the verse. Simplified charts collapse this to `Am – F – Dm – F`.

**Bridge**

`Am – G – Em – G – Dm – F`

Some charts use `C` in place of the second `G`: `Am – C – Em – G – Dm – F`.

**Outro**

`Am – C – Dm – C`

Often ends `Em – Dm – Am`.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Bridge → Outro
