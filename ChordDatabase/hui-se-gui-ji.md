灰色軌跡 (Hui Se Gui Ji / Gray Trajectory)
Artist: Beyond (黃家駒 / 黄家驹 / Wong Ka Kui)

Also written 灰色轨迹. Cantonese original; used in the film 天若有情. Key: F# minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar commonly uses Em shapes, capo 2. Guitarians, ComeGetChord, Chord4, and 91pu agree on F#m concert. Chord4 prints the capo-2 Em chart (`Em – G – D – Em`) as the playable version of the same song.

`F#m – Amaj7 – E – F#m`

**Verse**

`F#m – A – E – F#m`
`F#m – A – E – F#m`

**Pre-Chorus**

`B7 – F#m – B7 – C#7`

**Chorus**

`D – E – A – E – F#m`
`D – E – F#m`
`D – E – A – E – F#m`
`D – E – A`

Denser charts walk `A – E/G# – F#m` on the first and third lines.

**Solo**

`D – E – F#m`
`D – E – A – E – F#m`
`D – E – A`

Ending guitar over the chorus loop; Chord4 says to keep repeating the chorus frame for the close.

**Form**
