反方向的鐘 (Fan Fang Xiang De Zhong / Clock That Runs Backward)
Artist: Jay Chou (周杰倫 / 周杰伦)

Often cited as his first recorded song. Key: G minor (relative Bb major), 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar commonly uses Em shapes, capo 3.
