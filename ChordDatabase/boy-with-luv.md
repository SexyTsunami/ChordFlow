Boy With Luv (작은 것들을 위한 시)
Artist: BTS (방탄소년단), Halsey

MAP OF THE SOUL : PERSONA single. Key: B minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Musicnotes and studio multitrack listings agree on B minor. Guitar usually uses capo 2 and plays `F – Am – G – Em` (sounding `G – Bm – A – F#m`). No-capo G-shape charts match concert. Some theory pages tag D major because the loop also sits in the relative major; keep B minor.

**Intro**

`G – Bm – A – F#m`

**Verse**

`G – Bm – A – F#m`

**Pre-Chorus**

`G – Bm – A – F#m`

Same loop; the “listen my my baby” lift stays on these four chords. The last bar often lands `F#m – G` as a pickup into the chorus.

**Chorus**

`G – Bm – A – F#m`

**Post-Chorus**

`G – Bm – A – F#m`

“I want something stronger” is the same vamp; later hits add a unison `D` color on the last `A`.

**Bridge**

`G – Bm – A – F#m`

Same loop under the spoken-sung bridge; the last “let me fly” hits `F#m – G` into the final chorus.

**Outro**

`G – Bm – A – F#m`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Post-Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Post-Chorus → Bridge → Chorus → Outro
