小情歌 (Xiao Qing Ge / Little Love Song)
Artist: sodagreen (蘇打綠 / 苏打绿)

Also written 小情歌. Key: D major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction of the 小宇宙 cut; the original piano-and-strings arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. kanpu and 吉他世界 agree on concert D. Guitar usually uses C shapes, capo 2 (`C – Em – F – G`, sounding `D – F#m – G – A`). Chord4’s capo-2 chart is the same D concert. Easy open-C sheets with no capo sit a whole step below the record. Last-chorus `C` is borrowed from outside D major.

**Intro**

`Dadd9 – D – F#m – G`

Capo-2 `Cadd9 – C – Em – F`. Two times.

**Verse**

`D – F#m – G`
`D – F#m`
`G – A – F#m – Bm`
`G – Gm – A`

The `Gm` bar is borrowed minor color before the chorus.

**Chorus**

`D – Bm7 – A`
`G – F#m – Em7 – A`
`D – Bm7 – A`
`G – F#m – Em7 – A`

**Interlude**

`Dadd9 – D – F#m – G`

**Outro**

`D – C – D – A`
`Dadd9 – D – F#m – G`

Final “城堡” lift is concert `D – C – D – A` (capo-2 `C – Bb – C – G`), then the intro vamp.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Interlude → Verse → Chorus → Chorus → Outro
