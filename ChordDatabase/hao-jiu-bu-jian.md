好久不見 (Hao Jiu Bu Jian / Long Time No See)
Artist: Eason Chan (陳奕迅 / 陈奕迅)

Also written 好久不见. Mandarin hit from 認了吧. Key: C major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. No capo. Kanpu, Chord4, and Chinese guitar charts agree on C. Simplified charts stay on C–Am–F–G through the chorus; denser charts use G/B, Am7/G, and a secondary-dominant A (or A7) into Dm.
