Let Me Love You
Artist: DJ Snake, Justin Bieber

Key: C minor, 4/4. Wikipedia lists C minor at 100 BPM. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar often uses capo 3 and plays `Am – C – Em – F`. Wikipedia writes the bass as `Eb/G` where most guitar charts use `Gm`.

**Intro**

`Cm – Eb – Gm – Ab`
`Gm – Ab`

**Verse**

`Cm – Eb – Gm – Ab`
`Gm – Ab`

**Pre-Chorus**

`Ab – Cm`
`Bb – Fm`

**Chorus**

`Cm7 – Ebmaj7 – Gm – Ab`
`Gm – Ab`

Repeat. Many charts drop the sevenths to `Cm – Eb – Gm – Ab`.

**Bridge**

`Cm – Eb – Gm – Ab`
`Gm – Ab`

Same loop as the intro.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus
