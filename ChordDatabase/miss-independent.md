Miss Independent
Artist: Ne-Yo

Key: B-flat minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Chordify and Hooktheory agree on B-flat minor for the hook; the bridge leans into the relative D-flat major. Guitar usually uses capo 1 and plays `Dm – Am – F – G`. Concert pitch is `Ebm – Bbm – Gb – Ab` (ChordU writes `Ebm7` on the downbeat). Tunebat labels C-sharp major, the sharp spelling of that relative D-flat. E-Chords’ no-capo `D7sus4 – Em – Cadd9 – D` is an easy-key sketch, not the record.
