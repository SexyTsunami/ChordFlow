Drew Barrymore
Artist: Bryce Vine

Key: B minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction of the Carnival-era studio cut; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Chordify, ChordZone, and Singing Carrots agree on B minor around 76 BPM. Guitar stays in B minor with no capo (`G – Em – Bm – A`). Capo 2 in A minor is `F – Dm – Am – G`. Some chorus bars swap `A` for `F#m`. Easy three-chord sheets drop the `G` start.
