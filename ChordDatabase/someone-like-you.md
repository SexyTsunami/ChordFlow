Someone Like You
Artist: Adele

Key: A major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original piano part adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar often uses capo 2 and plays in G (G–Bm–Em–C, G–D–Em–C).

**Intro**

`A – C#m – F#m – D`

Some charts write the second chord as `A/G#` (same bass walk as C#m).

**Verse**

`A – C#m – F#m – D`

**Pre-Chorus**

`D – D – E – F#m`

**Chorus**

`A – E – F#m – D`

**Bridge**

`E – F#m`
`D – Bm – C#m – D – E`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus → Chorus
