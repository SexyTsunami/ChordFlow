What Do You Mean?
Artist: Justin Bieber

Key: Ab major, 4/4. Wikipedia and Musicnotes list Ab major at 125 BPM. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar often uses capo 1 and plays `Cadd9 – G – Em – D`. Some acoustic charts use capo 4 with `Dadd9 – A – F#m – E`.

**Intro**

`Dbadd9 – Ab – Fm – Eb`

**Chorus**

`Dbadd9 – Ab – Fm – Eb`

The song opens on the hook. Many charts drop the add9 to plain `Db`.

**Verse**

`Dbadd9 – Ab – Fm – Eb`

**Pre-Chorus**

`Ab – Fm – Eb – Dbadd9`

**Bridge**

`Dbadd9 – Ab – Fm – Eb`

Same loop as the chorus.

**Form**

Intro → Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus
