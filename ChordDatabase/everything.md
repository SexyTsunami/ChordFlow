Everything
Artist: Michael Bublé

Key: D major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Hooktheory and guitar charts agree on D major; the last chorus lifts a whole step to E major. No capo. Beginner charts drop `Bm` and play `D – E – G – A – D`.

**Intro**

`D – Bm – E – G`
`Bb – A – D – A`

**Verse**

`D – Bm – E – G – A – D – A`

The last verse line walks `Bb – A – D` (“you can see it when I look at you”).

**Chorus**

`G – A – D`
`D7 – G – F# – Bm – A`
`E – Bb – A – D`

Denser charts keep that `D7` / `F#` / `Bb` color; easy charts stay `G – A – D` and `E – G – A – D`.

**Post-Chorus**

`D – Bm – E – G`
`Bb – A – D – A`

**Bridge**

`D – E – G – A – D`

**Chorus (key change)**

`A – B – E`
`E7 – A – G# – C#m – B`
`F# – C – B – E`

Whole-step lift of the D-major chorus. The outro vamp is `E – F# – A – B – E`.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Post-Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus (key change)
