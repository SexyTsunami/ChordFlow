開不了口 (Kai Bu Liao Kou / Can't Speak Up)
Artist: Jay Chou (周杰倫 / 周杰伦)

Key: A major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar often uses capo 2 and plays in G.

**Intro** (twice)

`A – E – D – E`

**Verse**

`A – E – F#m – D – E`
`A – E – F#m – D – E`

**Pre-chorus**

`F#m – E – A`
`F#m – E – A`
`F#m – E – A – C#m`
`D – E`

**Chorus** (two matching halves)

First half:
`A – E/G# – F#m – C#m – D – C#m – Bm – E`

Second half:
`A – E/G# – F#m – C#m – D – C#m – Bm – E – A`

**Interlude**

`A – E – D – E`
`A – E – D – E`

**Outro**

Same as the intro, ending on **A**

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Interlude → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Outro
