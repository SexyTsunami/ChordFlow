Burn
Artist: Ellie Goulding

From Halcyon Days. Key: Bb minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar commonly uses Em shapes, capo 6. Musicnotes and Wikipedia publish Bb minor; Ultimate Guitar’s capo-6 chart transposes to the same pitch.

**Intro**

`Bbm – Gb – Db – Ab`

**Verse**

`Bbm – Ab – Ebm – Gb`

**Pre-Chorus**

`Bbm – Gb – Db – Ab`

Last bar of the pre-chorus is often empty before the chorus downbeat.

**Chorus**

`Bbm – Gb – Db – Ab`

**Bridge**

`Bbm – Gb – Db – Ab`

Same four-bar loop as the chorus.

**Outro**

`Bbm – Gb – Db – Ab – Bbm`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus → Outro
