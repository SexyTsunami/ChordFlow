湘女多情 (Xiang Nv Duo Qing / Sentimental Hunan Girls)
Artist: Jay Chou (周杰倫 / 周杰伦)

From 太陽之子. Original Jay / Vincent Fang Chinese-style song that borrows the 湘女多情 idiom; the coda interpolates 娘子. Key: G major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Chord4 and kanpu agree on concert G, capo 0.
