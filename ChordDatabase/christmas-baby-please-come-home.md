Christmas (Baby Please Come Home)
Artist: Mariah Carey

Key: Eb major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction of Mariah’s Merry Christmas recording of the Darlene Love / Spector classic; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Musicnotes PVG and piano solo editions publish Eb major (about 128 BPM). Ultimate Guitar, E-Chords, and Chordify commonly print the guitar-friendly E-shape loop `E – G#m – C#m – B`, which is a half-step above concert — capo 1 and those shapes sound Eb (`Eb – Gm – Cm – Bb`). Easy capo 3 uses C-shapes (`C – Em – Am – G`).
