床邊故事 (Chuang Bian Gu Shi / Bedtime Stories)
Artist: Jay Chou (周杰倫 / 周杰伦)

Title track of 周杰倫的床邊故事. Key: A minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. No capo. Chord4 and Daydayguitar write `Am – C/G – Fmaj7 – E`; Guitarians simplifies that to `Am – G – F – E` (same skeleton, no slash bass).

**Intro** (twice)

`Am – C/G – Fmaj7 – E`

**Verse**

`Am – C/G – Fmaj7 – E`
`Am – C/G – Fmaj7 – E7`

**Pre-chorus**

`Am – C/G – Fmaj7 – E`

**Chorus** (two matching halves)

First half:
`Am – C/G – Fmaj7 – E7`

Second half:
`Am – C/G – Fmaj7 – E7`

**Interlude**

`Am – C/G – Fmaj7 – E`

Spoken-story pass stays on the same loop, sometimes doubling bars on `Am` before `C/G`.

**Bridge**

`Am – C/G – Fmaj7 – Esus4 – E`

**Outro**

`Am – E7 – Am – Fmaj7 – Am – E7`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Interlude → Verse → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus → Outro
