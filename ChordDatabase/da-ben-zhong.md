大笨鐘 (Da Ben Zhong / Big Ben)
Artist: Jay Chou (周杰倫 / 周杰伦)

Key: E major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar commonly uses C shapes, capo 4.

**Intro** (twice)

`E – B/D# – C#m7 – B/D#`

**Verse**

`E – B/D# – C#m7 – C#m7/B – A – B`
`E – B/D# – C#m7 – C#m7/B – A – B`

**Pre-chorus**

`F#m – C#m – F#m7 – Bsus4 – B`

**Chorus** (two matching halves)

First half:
`E – D#m7b5 – G#7 – C#m7 – Bm7 – E`
`A – E/G# – F#m7 – B`

Second half:
`C#m – C#m/C – C#m/B – C#m/A#`
`F#m7 – Bsus4 – B`

**Turnaround**

`E – B – C#m7 – A`

**Outro**

`E – B/D# – C#m7 – C#m7/B – A – B`
`E – B/D# – C#m7 – C#m7/B – A – Am – E`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Turnaround → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Chorus → Outro
