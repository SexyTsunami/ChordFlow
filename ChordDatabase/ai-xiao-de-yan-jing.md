愛笑的眼睛 (Ai Xiao De Yan Jing / Smiling Eyes)
Artist: JJ Lin (林俊傑 / 林俊杰)

Also written 爱笑的眼睛. Key: Db major (C#), 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar commonly uses C shapes, capo 1.

**Intro**

`Db – Ab/C – Bbm – Gb – Absus4`

**Verse 1**

`Db – Ab/C – Bbm – Fm`
`Gb – Ab – Db – Ab/C – Bbm`
`Ebm7 – Gb – Absus4 – Ab`

Some charts use Bbm7 / Fm7, or Am/G-shape (Bbm/Ab) on the second bar.

**Verse 2**

`Db – Ab/C – Bbm – Fm – Ebm7 – Db7`
`Gb – Ab – Db – Ab/C – Bbm`
`Ebm7 – Gb – Absus4 – Ab`

The Fm – Ebm7 – Db7 walk is Gm7 – C7 in C shapes; some charts skip it.

**Chorus** (two matching halves)

First half:
`Db – Gb – Ab – Db – Ab/C`
`Bbm – Fm – Ebm7 – Ab`

Second half:
`Db – Gb – Ab – Db – Ab/C`
`Bbm – Fm – Ebm7 – Ab – Gb – Db`

Last chorus second half can land Db – Ab/C then E7-shape (F7) into Bbm, and a tag Bbm – Fm – Ebm7 – Ab – Db.

**Interlude**

`Db – Ebm7 – Fm – Gb – Ab – Gb/Db`
`Bbm – Ebm7 – Ab – Db`
`B – E/G# – Ab`

Some charts simplify the walk to Db – Ebm7 – Db/F – Gb – Db – B – E – Ab.

**Outro**

`Ab/C – Bbm – Gb – Absus4 – Db`

**Form**

Intro → Verse 1 → Verse 2 → Chorus → Interlude → Verse 2 → Chorus → Chorus → Tag → Outro
