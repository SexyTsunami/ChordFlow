Water Under the Bridge
Artist: Adele

Key: A-flat major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar often uses capo 1 and plays in G (G–Em–Bm–A, D–Em–Bm–G). Some piano charts label the same pitch set as E-flat major because the chorus opens on Eb.
