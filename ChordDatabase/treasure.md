Treasure
Artist: Bruno Mars

Key: Eb major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar often uses capo 3 and plays C shapes. Easy charts collapse the whole song to `Eb – Cm – Ab – Bb`; the recording is closer to the sevenths below.

**Intro**

`Abmaj7 – Gm7 – Fm7 – Abmaj7`
`Gm7 – Bb7sus4 – Cm7 – Bb7sus4`

Same loop as the verse. Some charts open on a held `Bb7sus4`.

**Verse**

`Abmaj7 – Gm7 – Fm7 – Abmaj7`
`Gm7 – Bb7sus4 – Cm7 – Bb7sus4`

**Pre-Chorus**

`Abmaj7 – Gm7 – Fm7 – Gm7 – Bb7sus4 – Cm7 – Bb7sus4`
`Abmaj7 – Gm7 – Fm7 – Bb7sus4`

**Chorus**

`Abmaj7 – Gm7 – Fm7 – Gm7 – Bb7sus4`
`Cm7 – Bb7sus4 – Abmaj7 – Gm7`
`Fm7 – Gm7 – Bb7sus4`
`Cm7 – Bb7sus4 – Abmaj7 – Gm7`
`Fm7 – Bb7sus4`

**Bridge**

`Gm7 – Cm7`
`Abmaj7 – Fm7`

Repeat. Then back into the chorus loop.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus
