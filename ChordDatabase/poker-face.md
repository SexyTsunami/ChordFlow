Poker Face
Artist: Lady Gaga

Key: G-sharp minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction of The Fame studio cut; the original synth arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Wikipedia and songparts agree on G# minor around 120 BPM. Guitar usually uses capo 4 and plays in E minor (`Em – C – G – D`). Musicnotes’ easy guitar TAB is that E-minor concert; keep G# minor for the record. Ultimate Guitar reprints sometimes mark G# / Ab minor or print the relative B major.

**Intro**

`G#m – E/G# – F#`

The “mum-mum-mum-mah” riff. Wikipedia lists this walk before the chorus colors enter.

**Verse**

`G#m – E – B – F#`

**Pre-Chorus**

`G#m – E – B – F#`

Same loop; the last “can’t read my poker face” often sits an extra bar on `F#`.

**Chorus**

`G#m – E – B – F#`

**Bridge**

`G#m – E – F#`

Drops the `B` and returns to the intro walk. Some live cuts stay on the chorus loop.

**Outro**

`G#m – E – B – F#`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus → Outro
