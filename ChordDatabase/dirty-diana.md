Dirty Diana
Artist: Michael Jackson

Key: G minor, 4/4. Musicnotes and concert-pitch guitar/piano charts agree on G minor. Guitar often uses capo 3 and plays Em–C–D shapes (sounding Gm–Eb–F). Easy Em charts with no capo are a minor third below the record. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original rock-guitar arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices.
