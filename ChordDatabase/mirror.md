Mirror
Artist: Lil Wayne, Bruno Mars

Key: F minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar often uses capo 1 and plays `Em – D – Bm – C` (sounding in F minor). Published analyses write `Fm – Eb – Cm – Eb – Db`; most guitar charts drop the extra `Eb` and loop four chords.

**Intro**

`Fm – Eb – Cm – Db`

**Verse**

`Fm – Eb – Cm – Db`

Bruno’s sung verses and Wayne’s rap verses share the loop. Some reductions insert `Ab` on the walk-up into the chorus.

**Chorus**

`Fm – Eb – Cm – Db`

**Bridge**

`Fm – Eb – Cm – Db`

Wayne’s later verses stay on the same progression.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus
