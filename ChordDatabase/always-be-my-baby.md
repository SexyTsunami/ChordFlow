Always Be My Baby
Artist: Mariah Carey

Key: E major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Musicnotes Original Keys for Singers and the leadsheet publish E major (about 80 BPM). Ultimate Guitar, GuitarTuna’s original-key chart, and Hooktheory agree on E, with the last chorus lifting to F. Guitar stays in E with no capo, or capo 4 and plays C-shapes (`C – G – Am – F`, sounding `E – B – C#m – A`). Easy sheets are `E – A – C#m – B`; keep the doo-doo walk `E – G#m7 – A – B`.

**Intro**

`E – G#m7 – A – B`
`C#m7 – G#m7 – A – B`

The doo-doo hook. Denser charts insert `Cdim7` (or `B#dim`) between `B` and `C#m7`.

**Verse**

`E – B/D# – C#m7 – G#m7 – Amaj7`
`F#m7 – Bsus4 – B`

**Chorus**

`E – G#m7 – A – B – Cdim7`
`C#m7 – G#m7 – A – B`
`E – G#m7 – A – B – Cdim7`
`C#m7 – G#m7 – A – B`

Beginner sheets print `E – A – C#m – B` and skip `Cdim7`.

**Bridge**

`C#m7 – G#m7 – F#`
`G# – A – G#m – C#m7`
`G# – A – B – C`

The last `C` is the pivot into F.

**Final chorus** (F major)

`F – Am7 – Bb – C – Dbdim`
`Dm7 – Am7 – Bb – C`

**Outro**

`F – Am7 – Bb – C`

Doo-doo vamp in F to fade.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Bridge → Final chorus → Outro
