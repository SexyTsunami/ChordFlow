我的秘密 (Wo De Mi Mi / My Secret)
Artist: G.E.M. (鄧紫棋 / 邓紫棋)

Key: D major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar commonly uses C shapes, capo 2.

**Intro**

`D – Gm – D – Gm`

**Verse**

`D – A – Bm – G`
`D – A – Bm – G`
`D – A – G – G`

**Chorus**

`D – A – Bm – F#m`
`G – F#m – Bm – Em – A`

Final chorus line can land on D instead of looping the A.

**Bridge**

`Bm – F# – D – E`
`G – G – A – A`

**Outro**

`Gm – D – Gm – D`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Intro → Verse → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus → Outro
