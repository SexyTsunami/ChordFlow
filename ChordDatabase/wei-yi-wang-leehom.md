唯一 (Wei Yi / The One)
Artist: Wang Leehom (Leehom Wang / 王力宏)

Key: Eb major (D#), 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar commonly uses C shapes, capo 3. Simplified D-major charts sit a whole step below concert.

**Intro**

`Eb – Bb/D – Ab – Abm`

Some charts keep the last bar on Ab instead of Abm.

**Verse**

`Eb – Gm – Ab – Gm`
`Fm7 – Bb – Eb – Cm7 – Db – Bb`

Verse 2 last line often walks Eb – Bb/D – Cm – F – Bb.

**Chorus**

`Eb – Bb – Ab – Bb`
`Eb – Bb – Ab – Bb`
`Ab – Gm – F`

Later choruses can substitute Cm – Bb/D for the second Eb – Bb, and repeat Ab – Gm – F as a tag.

**Interlude** (into the last chorus)

`Eb – Bb – Cm – Bb/D`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Chorus
