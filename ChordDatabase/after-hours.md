After Hours
Artist: The Weeknd

Key: C minor, 4/4. Ultimate Guitar and Cifra Club agree on C minor. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar often uses capo 3 and plays `Am – Em – Dm` (sounding `Cm – Gm – Fm`). Easy no-capo A-minor charts sit a minor third below the record. Late choruses add `G7`; the bridge is the only place `Bb` and `Ab` appear as full chords.

**Intro**

`Cm`

Held C-minor pad into the first line.

**Verse**

`Cm – Gm – Fm`

The dream-verse and “darkest hours” verse both rock this three-chord cell. Many bars sit on `Cm` alone before `Gm – Fm` answers.

**Chorus**

`Cm – Gm – Fm`

“Where are you now when I need you most” is the verse loop with a longer `Fm` into the next `Cm`. Final repeats color the last hit `G7`.

**Bridge**

`Cm – Gm – Fm`
`Gm – Bb – Ab`

The confession stays in C minor, then `Bb – Ab` walks “this house is not a home” back toward `Cm`.

**Outro**

`Cm – Gm – Fm – G7`

Last chorus tags add `G7` against `Cm` and do not return to the bridge’s `Bb – Ab`.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus → Outro
