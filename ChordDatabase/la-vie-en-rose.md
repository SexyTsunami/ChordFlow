La Vie En Rose
Artist: Emily Watts

English-lyric guitar cover of Édith Piaf’s song. Key: F# major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar commonly uses C shapes, capo 6. GuitarTuna and song-key listings agree on concert F#; Piaf’s original is a 3/4 waltz in a different key.

**Intro**

`F# – F#maj7`

**Verse**

`F# – F#maj7 – F#6 – G#m7 – C#7`
`G#m7 – C#7 – G#m7 – C#7 – F#`

**Bridge**

`F# – F#maj7 – F#6 – B`
`Bm – F#/A# – Adim – G#m7 – C#7`

**Final**

`F# – F#maj7 – G#m7 – C#7 – F#`

Short cover: one pass, then often an instrumental of the verse colors and a repeat of the final line.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Bridge → Final → Verse → Final
