On Bended Knee
Artist: Boyz II Men

From II (1994). Key: Eb major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Musicnotes PVG (MN0090520) publishes Eb as the original key at about 60 BPM. Chordify, GuitarTuna, and Lamucal agree on concert Eb with `Eb – Ebmaj7 – Eb7 – Abmaj7`. Cifra Club tags Ab because the intro opens on `Dbmaj7` — keep Eb as the tonic. The cut lifts a half step to E around the last “bended knee,” then again to F. Guitar usually uses capo 3 and plays C-shapes (`C – Cmaj7 – C7 – Fmaj7`, sounding `Eb – Ebmaj7 – Eb7 – Abmaj7`); move to capo 4 then capo 5 after the lifts. D-shape sheets without a capo sit a half step below the record.
