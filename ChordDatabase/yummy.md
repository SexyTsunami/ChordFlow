Artist: Justin Bieber

Key: D minor, 4/4. Musicnotes original published key is D minor. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Easy charts use `Dm – Gm – Am`. Fuller charts add `G7sus2`, `C`, and `Bbmaj7` on the chorus tags. Some guitar versions capo 3 and play Em shapes, which is a different starting point.
