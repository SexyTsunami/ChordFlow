Physical
Artist: Dua Lipa

Key: A minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original synth-and-bass arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Musicnotes and Ultimate Guitar agree on A minor with `Am – F – C – G` in every sung section. Chordify labels the relative C major because the loop never uses an E-major dominant. Guitar stays in A minor with no capo. Chordify sometimes colors `F` as `Fmaj7`.

**Intro**

`Am – F – C – G`

Synth bass riff arranged as this four-bar loop.

**Verse**

`Am – F – C – G`

**Pre-Chorus**

N.C. over the drums, or the last `G` of the verse held. Harmony drops out for the spoken pickup into the chorus.

**Chorus**

`Am – F – C – G`

**Bridge**

`Am – F – C – G`

Same loop; the vocal repeats the “hold on” figure before the last chorus. That chorus starts N.C., then the chords return on the second half.

**Outro**

`Am – F – C – G`

The loop holds through the stacked “physical” chants.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus → Outro
