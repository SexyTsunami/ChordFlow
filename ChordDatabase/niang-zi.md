娘子 (Niang Zi / Wife / Maiden)
Artist: Jay Chou (周杰倫 / 周杰伦)

From the debut album Jay. Key: Eb major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitarworld: concert Eb, D shapes, capo 1. ChordU lists Eb–Ab–Gm–Bb. The 湘女多情 coda quotes this riff in G; the concert original is a minor third lower.

**Intro**

`Eb – Eb/G – Ab – Abm`
`Gm – Cm – Fm – Bb`

**Verse**

`Eb – Bb/D – Cm – Gm`
`Ab – Eb/G – Fm – Bb`
`Eb – Bb/D – Cm – Gm`
`Ab – Bb – Eb`

**Pre-chorus**

`Ab – Eb – Fm – Bb`
`Ab – Eb – Bb – Eb`

**Chorus**

`Eb – Eb/G – Ab – Abm`
`Gm – Cm – Fm – Bb`

Same riff as the intro. Easy reductions flatten the slashes and the Abm to `Eb – Ab – Gm – Cm – Fm – Bb`.

**Interlude**

`Eb – Eb/G – Ab – Abm`
`Gm – Cm – Fm – Bb`

**Outro**

`Eb – Eb/G – Ab – Abm`
`Gm – Cm – Fm – Bb – Eb`

**Form**

Intro → Chorus → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Interlude → Chorus → Outro
