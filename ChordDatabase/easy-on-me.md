Easy On Me
Artist: Adele

Key: F major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original piano part adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar often uses capo 5 and plays in C, or plays F-shape barres with no capo.

**Intro**

`F – Dm7 – F/A – Bb`

**Verse**

`F – Dm7 – Am7 – Bbsus2`
`F – Dm7 – C – Bb`

**Chorus**

`F – Dm7 – C`
`F/A – Gm7 – F/A – Bb`
`F – Dm7 – C`
`F/A – Gm7 – F/A – Bb`

The turnaround back into the riff is `F – Dm7 – Am – Bbsus2`.

**Bridge**

`F – Bb/F – F`
`Dm7 – C – Bb – C`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus
