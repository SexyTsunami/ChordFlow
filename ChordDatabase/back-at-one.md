Back At One
Artist: Brian McKnight

Key: B major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction of the Back at One studio cut; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Musicnotes’ original published key is B major at about 72 BPM (some listings print 129–130 as double-time). Chordify’s loop is `B – G#m7 – E – C#m7`. Guitar often uses capo 4 and plays in G, or plays B barres with no capo. Last chorus lifts a half step to C.

**Intro**

`B – G#m7 – E – C#m7 – F#`

**Verse**

`B – G#m7 – E – C#m7 – F#`
`B – G#m7 – Amaj7 – E/G# – F#`

The second half’s `Amaj7` is the only new color; beginner charts stay on `E` there.

**Chorus**

`B – G#m7 – E – C#m7 – F#`
`B – G#m7 – E – C#m7 – F# – B`

**Bridge**

`B – E – B – G#m – C#m7`
`E – F# – G#m – C#m7 – F#`

**Chorus (in C)**

`C – Am7 – F – Dm7 – G`
`C – Am7 – F – Dm7 – G – C`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus (in C)
