#Beautiful
Artist: Mariah Carey, Miguel (Miguel Pimentel)

Key: E major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Musicnotes PVG publishes E major as the original key (about 104 BPM). Ultimate Guitar, E-Chords, Chordify, and Starakor agree on E with `E – A – B – C#m`. Guitar stays in E with no capo, or capo 4 and plays C-shapes (`C – F – G – Am`, sounding `E – A – B – C#m`). Keep the chorus landing on `C#m – A`.

**Intro**

`E – A`

Guitar lick over the tonic; some charts write a pickup `E – F#` before the verse.

**Verse**

`E – A – B – C#m – A`

Miguel’s opening verse. Easy sheets print `E – A – B – C#m`.

**Chorus**

`E – A – B – C#m – A`
`E – A – B – C#m – F#m – A`

The second ending adds `F#m` (some denser charts write `G#/A` into `A`). Beginner sheets stay on `E – A – B – C#m`.

**Verse 2**

`E – A – B – C#m – A`

Mariah’s verse uses the same loop.

**Bridge**

`E – A – C#m – B`
`C#m – E – A`

**Outro**

`E – A – B – C#m – A`

Chorus vamp to fade.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Verse 2 → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus → Outro
