My All
Artist: Mariah Carey

Key: G minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction of the Butterfly studio cut; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Musicnotes PVG (Original Keys for Singers) and PVG guitar editions publish G minor (sensually, about 54 in 2 / 124 in 4). GuitarTuna’s original-key chart, Tab4U, Ultimate Guitar, and E-Chords agree on `Cm7 – D7 – Gm`. Guitar usually barres Gm with no capo, or capo 3 and plays Em shapes (`Am7 – B7 – Em`, sounding `Cm7 – D7 – Gm`). Keep the Spanish-guitar `Cm7 – D7 – Gm` and the chorus half-diminished walk.
