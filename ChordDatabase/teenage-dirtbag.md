Teenage Dirtbag
Artist: Wheatus

Key: E major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction of the self-titled 2000 studio cut; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Spy Tunes, Jamwise, and GuitarTabsExplorer agree on E with no capo. The record is mostly power chords (`E5 – B5 – E5 – Asus2` in the verse; chorus `E5 – A5 – B5 – C#5 – G#5`). Easy acoustic charts (Ultimate Guitar capo 4) play C shapes (`C – G – C – F`) to the same concert pitch and often drop the chorus `G#m`. song2chord also lists E at about 95 BPM.

**Intro**

`E – B – E – A`

Two-bar vamp. Keep `Asus2` if matching the guitar part.

**Verse**

`E – B – E – A`
`C#m – A – B`

The last two lines of each verse sit on `C#m – A – B` twice.

**Chorus**

`E – A – B – C#m – G#m`

The “Iron Maiden” tag often repeats the loop, then walks `A – B` as a short turnaround. Beginner capo-4 sheets print `C – F – G` and skip `G#m`.

**Bridge**

`E – A`
`C#m – G#m – A – B`

The “oh yeah, dirtbag” middle eight vamps `E – Asus2` before that walk-up. Same harmony as the outro tag.

**Outro**

`E – A`
`C#m – G#m – A – B`

Ends on `E` after the last missing-tag walk.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Bridge → Verse → Chorus → Outro
