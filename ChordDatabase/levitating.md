Levitating
Artist: Dua Lipa, DaBaby

Key: B minor, 4/4. Levitating (feat. DaBaby) hit version: same production as the album cut, with DaBaby’s intro and rap verse on top. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original disco-funk arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Wikipedia, Hooktheory, Musicnotes-style writeups, and Ultimate Guitar agree on B minor and `Bm7 – F#m7 – Em7` (usually returning to `Bm7`). Guitar usually uses capo 2 and plays `Am – Em – Dm – Am`. E-Chords often prints `D` where the record has `F#m`; keep `F#m7`. Auto-charts that add `G` or `A` are filling in passing bass, not a new loop.

`Bm7 – F#m7 – Em7 – Bm7`

DaBaby’s spoken intro over the two-bar vamp.

**Verse**

`Bm7 – F#m7 – Em7 – Bm7`

Dua’s first verse, then DaBaby’s rap as the second verse, both on the same loop.

**Post-Chorus**

Fly-away tag; still the three-chord groove.

**Bridge**

`Bm7 – F#m7 – Em7 – Bm7`

Middle-eight rap over the vamp, then a brief a cappella hit into the last chorus. No new chords.

**Outro**
