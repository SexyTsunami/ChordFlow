Talking to the Moon
Artist: Bruno Mars

Key: C# minor (relative E major), 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar often uses capo 4 and plays in A minor (`C – E7 – Am – G – F`).

**Intro**

`E – G#7 – C#m – B – A`

**Verse**

`E – G#7 – C#m – B – A`

**Pre-Chorus**

`F#m – B – F#m – B`

**Chorus**

`A – B – C#m`
`A – B – C#m`
`A – B`
`E – B/D# – C#m`
`A – B – C#m – B – F#`

Some charts write the walkdown as `E – B – C#m – C#m7/B – A – B`.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Chorus → Outro
