不再猶豫 (Bu Zai You Yu / No Longer Hesitate)
Artist: Beyond (黃家駒 / 黄家驹 / Wong Ka Kui)

Also written 不再犹豫. Cantonese original; insert song for the film Beyond日記之莫欺少年窮. Key: G major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. No capo. Chordyes, ComeGetChord, guitarworld, and Ultimate Guitar agree on G. One Guitarians header says “Key: G / Capo 1,” which is an Ab singer transpose, not the studio concert pitch. Denser charts use Em7, Bm7, Am7, and Dsus4 in place of the triads.

**Intro**

`G – D – Em – Bm – Am – C – D`

**Verse**

`G – D – Em – Bm – Am – C – D`
`G – D – Em – Bm – Am – C – D`

**Pre-Chorus**

`Em – Em – D`
`Em – Em – D`

**Chorus**

`G – D – Em – Bm – Am – C – D`
`G – D – Em – Bm – Am – C – D – G`

**Solo**

`G – D – Em – Bm – Am – C – D`
`G – D – Em – Bm – Am – C – D`

Guitar solo over the chorus/intro frame; Chordyes writes this as the mid-song solo before the last choruses.

**Outro**

`G – D – Em – Bm – Am – C – D – G`

Last chorus often starts mid-hook, then repeats the second line into G.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Solo → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Outro
