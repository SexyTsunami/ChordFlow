Starboy
Artist: The Weeknd, Daft Punk

Key: A minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original vocoder-and-bass arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar stays in A minor with no capo (`Am – G – F`). Hooktheory and mix writeups agree on A minor. Chordify and some auto-charts label C major because they hear a passing `C` and read the loop as if it were in C; keep the verse as `Am – G – F – G` without forcing `C`.

**Intro**

`Am – G – F – G`

**Verse**

`Am – G – F – G`

**Pre-Chorus**

`Am – G – F – G`

Same loop as the verse; the vocal sits higher on the “house so empty” pickup.

**Chorus**

`Am – G/A – F/A – G/A`
`G – F – G – Am`

First cycle holds an A bass pedal under the Daft Punk vocoder `G – F – G` hits, then the bass follows the roots. Guitar charts often flatten this to `Am – G – F – G`.

**Bridge**

`G – F – G – Am`

Daft Punk verse over the same three chords; starts on `G` instead of `Am`.

**Outro**

`Am – G – F – G`

The chorus vamp repeats to the fade.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Bridge → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Outro
