愛在西元前 (Ai Zai Xi Yuan Qian / Love Before BC)
Artist: Jay Chou (周杰倫 / 周杰伦)

Key: Ab major, 4/4; verse 2 drops a half step to G. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar often uses capo 1 and plays in G (then F# shapes, still capo 1, for the G-major stretch).

**Intro** (loop)

`Ab – Fm – Db – Eb`

**Verse**

`Ab – Fm – Db – Eb`

**Pre-chorus**

`Bbm – C – Fm – Cm`
`Db – Eb`

**Chorus** (two matching halves)

`Ab – Fm – Db – Eb`

**Verse 2 / Chorus 2** (in G)

Same patterns, down a half step:

`G – Em – C – D`

Pre-chorus:

`Am – B – Em – Bm`
`C – D`

**Bridge** (still in G)

`Am – B – Em – Bm`
`C – G – D`

**Outro**

Returns to Ab:

`Ab – Bbm – Ab – Fm – Db – Ab`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Verse 2 in G → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus → Outro
