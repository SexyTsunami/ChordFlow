醉赤壁 (Zui Chi Bi / Drunk at Red Cliff)
Artist: JJ Lin (林俊傑 / 林俊杰)

Also written 醉赤壁. Key: Ab major, later sections in Eb then F, outro in Bb, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Numbered-score sources mark Ab → B → Ab → Bb; detailed guitar reductions put the verse in Gb and the chorus in Eb. Simplified C–F–G charts ignore the key changes. Some guitar sites list Db as the original (conflict with Ab).
