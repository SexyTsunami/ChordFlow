千里之外 (Qian Li Zhi Wai / Far Away)
Artist: Jay Chou (周杰倫 / 周杰伦), Fei Yu-ching (費玉清 / 费玉清)

Duet with Fei Yu-ching. Key: D major, 4/4. The last chorus usually lifts a half step to Eb. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar often uses capo 2 and plays in C.

**Intro**

`D – G – Bm – G`

**Verse**

`D – A – G – D`
`G – D – Em – A`
`D – A – G – D`
`G – D – Em – A`

**Pre-chorus**

`C – G – Bb – A`

**Chorus** (two matching halves)

First half:
`D – A – Bm – A – G – D – G – A`

Second half:
`D – A – Bm – A – G – D – G – A`

**Interlude** (Fei Yu-ching)

`D – G – Bm – G`

**Last chorus**

Same pattern, up a half step:

`Eb – Bb – Cm – Bb – Ab – Eb – Ab – Bb`
then the matching second half ending on **Eb**

**Outro**

`Ab – Cm – Ab`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Interlude → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Last chorus in Eb → Outro
