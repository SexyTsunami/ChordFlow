I Swear
Artist: All-4-One

1994 pop cut of the John Michael Montgomery song. Key: D major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. No capo. Chordyes and Hooktheory agree on concert D; the last chorus lifts a whole step to E.

`D – Bm – F#m – A`
