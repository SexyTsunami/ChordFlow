Psycho
Artist: Post Malone, Ty Dolla $ign

Key: E-flat major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. The whole song is a two-chord vamp: `Eb – Ab`. Guitar usually uses capo 1 and plays `D – G`. Musicnotes lists the original published key as E-flat major; ChordZone and guitar charts agree. No-capo `D# – G#` charts are the same concert key with sharp spelling. Easy D-major charts with no capo sit a half step below the record.

**Intro**

`Eb – Ab`

**Chorus**

`Eb – Ab`

The hook opens the track. `Eb` holds the first two lines; `Ab` answers on the “no-show / boatload” half.

**Verse**

`Eb – Ab`

Post’s verse and Ty Dolla $ign’s verse stay on the same two chords.

**Outro**

`Eb – Ab`

The vamp holds through the last chorus tags.

**Form**

Intro → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus
