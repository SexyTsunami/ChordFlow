Mind Over Matter
Artist: Young the Giant

Key: C major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar stays in C with no capo. This is the full 2014 album cut (~4:00, ~77 BPM) with the guitar riff and long instrumental, not the electronic reprise. ChordLines, GuitarTuna, and Beatport agree on C major. GuitarTuna spells the chorus `F` as `Fmaj7`; beginner charts use plain `F`. Some chorus tags land `C – Em` instead of `C – G`.

**Intro**

`C – Em – Am`

**Verse**

`C – Em – Am`

Last pickup into the chorus is `Am – F`.

`F – C – G`
`F – C – G`
`F – C – G – C`

**Bridge**

`F – C – G`

The mid-song “train / Tokyo” lift stays on this chorus loop, then a short `C – Em – Am` break before the last choruses. Album form keeps a long instrumental after the first chorus that the reprise does not.

**Outro**

`F – C – G`
`C`
