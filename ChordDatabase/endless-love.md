Endless Love
Artist: Luther Vandross, Mariah Carey

Key: Bb major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction of the 1994 Vandross/Carey duet (Luther is the lead; Mariah is the featured vocal); the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Sheet Music Direct editions of this recording publish Bb. Ultimate Guitar, ChordU, and Chordify agree on `Bb – Eb – F` at about 95 BPM. Musicnotes hymn/PVG copies of the Lionel Richie/Diana Ross original are the same concert key. Guitar usually capos 3 and plays G-shapes (`G – C – D`, sounding `Bb – Eb – F`), or capos 1 and plays A-shapes.
