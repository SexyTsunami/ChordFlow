The Hills
Artist: The Weeknd

Key: C minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original distorted-synth arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Hooktheory and most lyric-chord charts agree on C minor. Chordify labels A-flat major because `Ab` and `Fm` dominate the chorus loop; keep C minor as the concert center. Guitar often uses capo 3 and plays `Am – F – Dm`. Easy no-capo A-minor charts sit a minor third below the record. Do not substitute the generic `Cm – Ab – Eb – Bb` loop; the chorus is `Cm – Ab – Fm`.
