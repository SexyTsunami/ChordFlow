安靜了 (An Jing Le / It Became Quiet)
Artist: S.H.E (Selina 任家萱 / Hebe 田馥甄 / Ella 陳嘉樺 / 陈嘉桦)

Also written 安静了. Jay Chou composition from FM S.H.E. Key: C major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. No capo. Chordify, Chord4, Ultimate Guitar, and jianpujia/吉他帮 all list concert C. Some tutorial videos capo 1 for vocal range, not concert pitch. Chordify’s loop is a flattened G–C–Am; denser charts keep the walking bass (`G/B`, `Am7/G`, `F/E`) and the verse’s Bb–A7–Dm move.

**Intro**

`C – F – Am – F`

**Verse**

`C – G/B – Am7 – Am7/G`
`F – F/E – Fm – G`
`C – Bb – A7`
`Dm7 – Fm – E`

**Pre-chorus**

`Am7 – Am7/G – F – C/E`
`Dm7 – Gsus4 – G`

**Chorus** (two matching halves)

First half:
`C – G/B – Am7 – Am7/G`
`F – G – Em – Am7`
`Dm7 – Gsus4 – G`

Second half:
`C – G/B – Am7 – Am7/G`
`F – G – Em – Am7`
`Dm7 – Gsus4 – G – C`

**Interlude**

`C – F – Am – F`

**Verse 2**

Same as Verse; third line often stays on Fm a little longer before the Bb–A7 turn.

**Outro**

`C – F – Em – Am`
`F – G – C`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Interlude → Verse 2 → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Outro
