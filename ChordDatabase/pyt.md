P.Y.T. (Pretty Young Thing)
Artist: Michael Jackson

Key: B minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original synth-bass arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar often uses capo 2 and plays in A minor (`Am7 – D7 – Fmaj7 – Am7`, chorus `Dm7 – G`). Musicnotes piano/vocal editions publish the song in B minor.

**Intro**

`Bm7 – E7 – Bm7 – F#7sus4`

Vamp until the verse.

**Verse**

`Bm7 – E7 – Gmaj7 – Bm7`

Repeat for the second phrase.

**Pre-Chorus**

`Gmaj7 – E/F# – F#`
`A/B – B7`
`Gmaj7 – E/F# – F#`
`C/D – B7#5#9`

Some charts write `B7sus4` on the way to `F#`. The last hit is the altered `B7` into the hook.

**Chorus**

`Em7 – A`
`Em7 – A`
`G – D/F# – A/B – B7`

**Breakdown**

`F#5 – G5 – G#5 – A5`
`A5 – A#5 – B`
`A – E/G# – F#7sus4`

Chromatic walk-up, then back toward the B groove. The call-and-response sits on the `A5 – A#5 – B` climb, then `G – D/F# – A/B – B7` into the last choruses.

**Outro**

`Em7 – A`
`G – D/F# – A/B – B7`

Chorus vamp to the fade.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Breakdown → Chorus → Outro
