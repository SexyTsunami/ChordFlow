Time After Time
Artist: Cyndi Lauper

Key: C major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction of the She's So Unusual studio cut; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Wikipedia, Hooktheory, and Bell & Co Music agree on C major around 130 BPM. Guitar stays in C with no capo. Some chord sites label F because the intro starts on `F`; that chord is the first color, not the tonic. Denser verse voicings use `Dm/C` in place of `Dm`.
