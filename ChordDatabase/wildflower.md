WILDFLOWER
Artist: Billie Eilish

Key: F# minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar often uses capo 2 and plays in E minor (`Cmaj7 – Bm7 – Em – D` and `Cmaj7 – D – B7 – Em`). Concert shapes below.

**Intro**

`Dmaj7 – C#m7 – F#m – E`

**Verse**

`Dmaj7 – C#m7 – F#m – E`

**Pre-Chorus**

`Dmaj7 – C#m7 – F#m – A`

**Chorus**

`Dmaj7 – E – C#7 – F#m – E`
`Dmaj7 – E – C#7 – F#m`
`E – Dmaj7 – C#m7 – F#m`

**Bridge**

`E – C#7 – F#m – E`
`Dmaj7 – E – C#7 – F#m`

**Outro**

`Dmaj7 – E – C#7 – F#m – E`

Same as the bridge; it repeats to the end. Some charts write C#m7 as C#m and C#7 as C#.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus → Outro
