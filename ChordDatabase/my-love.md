My Love (마이 러브)
Artist: Lee Seung Chul (이승철)

Key: D-flat major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Korean sheet sources (akbobada, ezcho) publish Db; ChordU lists Db–Bbm–Ebm–Ab. Guitar usually uses capo 1 and plays in C (`Am – Dm – G – C – G/B`, concert `Bbm – Ebm – Ab – Db – Ab/C`).

**Intro**

`Bbm – Ebm – Ab – Db – Ab/C`

**Verse**

`Bbm – Ebm – Ab – Db – Ab/C`

The last pre-chorus bar often stays on `Ab` (some charts `Ab7`) instead of splitting `Db – Ab/C`.

**Chorus**

`Db – F – Bbm`
`Ab – Db – Gb – Db`
`Ebm – Ab`
`Db – F – Bbm`
`Ab – Db – Gb – Db`
`Ebm – Ab`

Denser capo-1 charts write `E7` / `C7` / `G7` for the concert `F` / `Db7` / `Ab7` walk. Beginner charts drop the slash bass and play plain `Db`.

**Interlude**

`Bbm – Ebm – Ab – Db – Ab/C`

**Outro**

`Bbm – Ebm – Ab – Db`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Interlude → Chorus → Outro
