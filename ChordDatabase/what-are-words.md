What Are Words
Artist: Chris Medina

Key: C major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Chordify and guitar charts (Guitaretab, Lamucal) agree on C major. No capo. Denser charts add `C/E`, `Cadd9`, and `Gsus4`.

**Intro**

`F – G – C – C/E – F`
`F – G – C`

**Verse**

`F – G – C – C/E – F`
`G – Am`
`F – G – C – C/E – F`
`G – C`

**Chorus**

`F – G – C`
`F – G – Am`
`F – G`
`C – G – Am`
`Em – F – G`

Guitaretab’s title lines use `Cadd9` for `C` and `Gsus4 – G` on the last walk.

**Outro**

`F – G – C – C/E – F`
`G – Am`
`F – G – C – C/E – F`
`G – C`

The last hold is often `C` / `Cmaj7`.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Outro
