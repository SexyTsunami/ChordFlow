Break My Heart
Artist: Dua Lipa

Key: E minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original disco-funk arrangement (which interpolates INXS’s Need You Tonight guitar feel) adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Musicnotes, Wikipedia, Chordify, and Hooktheory agree on E minor. Guitar stays in E minor with no capo. Chordify and simplified Ultimate Guitar charts loop `Em – Cmaj7 – Bm`; Wikipedia’s fuller verse/chorus walk is `Em – G – F#dim – Em – C – Bm – D`. Keep the three-chord guitar reduction on the page and treat `G`, `F#dim`, and `D` as inner-voice motion.

**Intro**

`Em – Cmaj7 – Bm`

Need You Tonight-style guitar over the three-chord pad.

**Verse**

`Em – Cmaj7 – Bm`

Piano reductions that follow the record more closely walk `Em – G – F#dim – Em` into `C – Bm – D`.

**Pre-Chorus**

`C – D – Bm – Em – D`

Wikipedia’s bridge colors as triads: `Cmaj7 – D6 – Bm7 – Em7 – D6` on the same roots.

**Chorus**

`Em – Cmaj7 – Bm`
`Em – Cmaj7 – Bm – Am`

The last hit of the title question often lands `Am`. Fuller charts still weave `G`, `F#dim`, and `D` under the `Em` bars.

**Bridge**

`C – D – Bm – Em`

Middle eight over the pre-chorus roots, then back into the chorus walk.

**Outro**

`Em – Cmaj7 – Bm – Am`

Final chorus holds the title cadence.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus → Outro
