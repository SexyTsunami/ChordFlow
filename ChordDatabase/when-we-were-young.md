When We Were Young
Artist: Adele

Key: E-flat major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original piano part adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar often uses capo 3 and plays in C (Am–C/G–F–C/G–Dm7–C, C–Em–F–G). Many C-major charts are a whole step down from the record.

**Intro**

`Cm – Eb/G – Ab – Eb/G – Fm7 – Eb/G`

**Verse**

`Cm – Eb/G – Ab – Eb/G – Fm7 – Eb/G`

The last verse line before the pre-chorus lands on `Bb` instead of `Eb/G`.

**Pre-Chorus**

`Ab – Bb – Gm – Ab`
`Ab – Bb – Gm – Bbsus4`

**Chorus**

`Eb – Gm – Ab – Bb`
`Eb – Gm – Ab – Bb`
`Cm – Eb/G – Ab – Fm – Fm7 – Bb`

**Bridge**

`Cm – Eb/G – Ab – Eb/G – Fm7 – Bb`

Later the same loop is sung over `Eb/G – Gm – Ab – Bb`.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Bridge → Pre-Chorus → Chorus
