Tighten Up
Artist: The Black Keys

Key: F-sharp minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar often uses capo 4 and plays in D minor (`Dm – Am – Gm – A`, or `Dm – F – Gm – A`). Wikipedia, Klangio, and E-Chords agree on F-sharp minor. Verse charts disagree on the second chord: rhythm tabs (E-Chords, Actiontab) use `C#m`; many lyric-chord pages print `A` instead.

**Intro**

`F#m – C#m – Bm – C#`

Harmonica over the verse loop.

**Verse**

`F#m – C#m – Bm – C#`

If using the `A` variant: `F#m – A – Bm – C#`. Acoustic reductions sometimes write `F# – E – Bm – C#`.

**Chorus**

`F#m – C#m – F#m – C#m`
`F#m – C#m – A – B`

Heavier guitar. The last two chords are `A – B` in most charts; a few write `A – Bm`.

**Bridge**

`F#m – C#m – F#m – C#m`
`F#m – C#m – A – B`

Same loop as the chorus.

**Outro**

`F#m – C#m – F#m – C#m`
`F#m – C#m – A – B`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus → Outro
