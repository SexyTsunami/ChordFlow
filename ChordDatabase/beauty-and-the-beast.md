Beauty and the Beast
Artist: Angela Lansbury, Disney

1991 film soundtrack (Mrs. Potts). Key: D-flat major, 4/4. This is the common piano reduction of the Lansbury studio cut; the original orchestra adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Wikipedia writes the film version in Db at about 84 BPM; Musicnotes’ piano-solo edition matches Db. The published PVG in Gb is a different edition (often sold with the Dion/Bryson pop duet) and is not this concert key. Guitar usually uses C shapes, capo 1. The restatement lifts a whole step to Eb.

**Intro**

`Db – Dbsus4 – Db`

**Verse**

`Db`
`Fm – Gb – Ab – Db`
`Abm7 – Gb`
`Fm – Ebm7 – Ab – Db`

**Pre-Chorus**

`Fm – Gb`
`Fm – Bbm – B – Eb`

The `B – Eb` bars are the only new color: they set up the lift.

**Chorus (in E-flat)**

`Eb – Bb – Eb`
`Gm – Ab – Bb`
`Eb – Bbm7`
`Ab – Fm7 – Bb – Eb`

**Outro**

`Ab – Eb – Bb – Eb`

Spoken tag over the last `Eb`.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus (in E-flat) → Outro
