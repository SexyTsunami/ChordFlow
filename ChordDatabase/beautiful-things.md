Beautiful Things
Artist: Benson Boone

From Fireworks & Rollerblades (2024). Key: Bb major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Musicnotes PVG, Chordify, and Tunebat agree on Bb. The verse opens on `Eb`, which is why some guitar blogs tag the song Eb — keep Bb as the tonic. Guitar usually uses capo 3 and plays C-shapes (`C – G – D – Em`, sounding `Eb – Bb – F – Gm`). AZChords, Third Coast, and CountryTabs agree on that loop plus chorus `Bb – Gm – F – Eb`. Keep the verse passing `Gbdim7`.
