Wild Child
Artist: The Black Keys

Key: G minor (G Dorian), 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar stays in G minor with no capo. E-Chords, vchords, and Produsso agree on G minor and the `Gm – C` vamp (C major against Gm is the Dorian color). SongBPM and KDUR tag F minor — keep `Gm` as the tonic; F minor would be an `Fm – Bb` song.

**Intro**

`Gm – C`

Opens on that two-chord vamp, then a pass of the chorus colors: `Gm – Bb – C` / `C – Bb – Am`.

**Verse**

`Gm – C`

**Chorus**

`Gm – Bb – C`
`C – Bb – Am`

Easy charts flatten this to `Gm – C`.

**Solo**

`Gm – Bb – C`
`C – Bb – Am`

Same as the chorus.

**Bridge**

`Gm – Bb – C – Bb`

Last line lands `Gm – Bb – C`.

**Outro**

`Gm – Bb – C`
`C – Bb – Am`

Final chorus.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Solo → Bridge → Chorus → Outro
