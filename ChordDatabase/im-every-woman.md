I'm Every Woman
Artist: Whitney Houston

Key: C minor, 4/4 (about 120 BPM). This is Houston's Bodyguard recording of the Ashford & Simpson song, not the 1978 Chaka Khan original. Musicnotes original published key is C minor; Hooktheory agrees. Chordify often labels the house groove E-flat (the relative major). This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original Narada Michael Walden arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar usually uses Cm7 / Fm7 shapes with no capo, or capo 3 and plays in A minor.
