傻笑 (Sha Xiao / Silly Smile)
Artist: Jay Chou (周杰倫 / 周杰伦), Cindy Yen (袁詠琳 / 袁咏琳)

Duet from 十二新作. Key: C major, 4/4; the chorus sits in Eb. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar can play the verses in C with no capo; chorus charts often stay in concert Eb. Chord4, daydayguitar, and Piano Cheats agree on the C-verse / Eb-chorus split. 吉他世界 labels the whole track Eb because that is where the hook lives.

**Intro**

`C – G – F – G`

**Verse**

`Fmaj7 – Am7 – Fmaj7 – C`
`Fmaj7 – Em – Am – Fmaj7 – G`

Second verse ends `Fmaj7 – G – Fm7 – Bb` into the chorus.

**Chorus** (two matching halves)

First half:
`Eb – G – Cm – Cm7/Bb`
`Ab – Cm7 – Fm7 – Bb`

Second half:
`Eb – G – Cm – Cm7/Bb`
`Ab – Cm7 – Fm7 – Bb – Eb`

**Interlude**

`Eb – Bb – Ab – Bb`
`Eb – Bb – Ab – F – G`

**Bridge**

`Eb – G – Cm – Cm7/Bb`
`Ab – Cm7 – Fm7 – Bb`

**Outro**

`Eb – Bb – Ab – Bb`
`Eb – Bb – Ab – Fm7 – Bb – Eb`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Interlude → Verse → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus → Outro
