告白氣球 (Gao Bai Qi Qiu / Love Confession)
Artist: Jay Chou (周杰倫 / 周杰伦)

Key: B major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar often uses capo 4 and plays in G.

**Intro**

`E – F# – B`
`E – F# – B – D#7`
`G#m7 – G#m/F# – E – B/D# – C#m7 – F#sus4 – F#`

**Verse**

`G#m7 – D#m7 – Eadd9 – Bsus2 – F#/A#`
`G#m7 – D#m7 – C#m7 – F#sus4 – Bsus2`

**Pre-chorus**

`Eadd9 – F#/E – D#m7 – G#m7`
`C#7 – C#/F – F#sus4 – F#`

**Chorus** (two matching halves)

First half:
`B – D#7 – G#m7 – G#m/F# – E – F# – B`

Second half:
`B – D#7 – G#m7 – F#m7 – B7 – E – F# – B`

**Interlude**

`B – F#/A# – G#m7 – F# – E – F# – B`

**Ending**

`B – D#7 – G#m7 – F#m7 – B7 – E – F#sus4 – Bsus2`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Interlude → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Chorus → Ending
