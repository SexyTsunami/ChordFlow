路小雨 (Lu Xiao Yu / Lu Xiaoyu)
Artist: Jay Chou (周杰倫 / 周杰伦), Sunny Li

Piano theme for the character 路小雨 from the film 不能說的秘密 (Secret) soundtrack. This is not the vocal song 不能說的秘密. Key: C major, 4/4. This is the common piano reduction; the original solo and three-hand film cues add extra passing tones and inner voices. 虫虫钢琴, PiaNoproblem, and LouisLiao treat concert C as the original key (easy C sheets are transpositions of that same tonic, not a different cue). Tunebat tags Michael Tai’s piano cover C major. The left-hand loop is the Bach-prelude-style walk `C – G/B – Am – Em – F – C/E – Dm – G`; hifiii’s writeup also notes the pop color `Am – G – Em – F` in the middle. No capo.

**Intro**

`C – G/B – Am – Em`
`F – C/E – Dm – G`

Left-hand arpeggios only on the film’s opening pass.

**Theme**

`C – G/B – Am – Em`
`F – C/E – Dm – G`

Same loop under the right-hand melody. Denser scores keep the slash bass; easy C-major tutorials flatten it to `C – G – Am – Em – F – C – Dm – G`.

**Middle**

`Am – G – Em – F`
`Am – E – Am – G`

The tighter, higher pass before the last theme. Some concert-C scores color a brief `Db` here; keep it as inner-voice tension, not a new key.

**Outro**

`C – G/B – Am – G – C`

**Form**

Intro → Theme → Theme → Middle → Theme → Outro
