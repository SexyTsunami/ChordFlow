偉大的渺小 (Wei Da De Miao Xiao / The Great Smallness)
Artist: JJ Lin (林俊傑 / 林俊杰)

Also written 伟大的渺小; also called Little Big Us. Key: D major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar commonly uses C shapes, capo 2. Verse often uses Dsus2 in place of D.

**Intro**

`Dsus2 – F#m – Bm – G – Asus4 – A`

**Verse**

`Dsus2 – F#m – Bm – G – A`
`Dsus2 – F#m – Bm – G – A`

**Pre-chorus**

`Em – A – Em – Asus4 – A`

**Chorus**

`Dsus2 – F#m – Bm – G – A`
`Dsus2 – F#m – Bm – G – A`
`D – F#m – Bm – G – A`

Later chorus second half can walk Bb – C before D, then Bb – C as a lift into the repeat. Tag:

`Bm – G – A – D`

**Interlude** (after chorus 1)

`Dsus2 – Bb – C`

Then back to verse 2.

**Outro**

`Dsus2 – F#m – Bm – G – A – D`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Interlude → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Tag → Outro
