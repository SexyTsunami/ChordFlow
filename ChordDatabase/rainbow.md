Rainbow
Artist: Kacey Musgraves

From Golden Hour. Key: Eb major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar commonly uses C shapes, capo 3 (`C – Am – F – C`, concert `Eb – Cm – Ab – Eb`). Musicnotes and Hooktheory publish Eb; easy G-shape internet charts are a whole step plus a half above or below concert and should be transposed.

**Intro**

`Eb – Cm7 – Ab – Eb`

**Verse**

`Eb – Cm7 – Ab – Eb`
`Eb – Cm7 – Ab – Bb`
`Cm7 – G – Cm7 – Bb`

**Chorus**

`Eb – Cm7 – Ab – Eb – Bb`
`Cm7 – F – Bb`
`Eb – Cm7 – Ab – Eb – Bb`
`Cm7 – Eb – Bb – Eb`

Denser charts color the `Eb` bars `Ebmaj7` and the `F` as `F7`.

**Bridge**

`Cm7 – G – Cm7 – Bb`
`Ab`

The `Ab` is the “everything is alright now” bar before the last chorus. Third chorus swaps the umbrella line to the same loop and tags `Cm7 – Eb – Bb – Eb`.

**Outro**

`Eb – Cm7 – Ab – Eb`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus → Outro
