End of the Road
Artist: Boyz II Men

From the Boomerang soundtrack (1992); later added to the Cooleyhighharmony reissue. Key: Eb major, 12/8. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Musicnotes PVG (MN0138887) publishes Eb in 12/8 at about 50 BPM; Hooktheory and Super Simple Piano agree on Eb (Hooktheory prints the same feel as 6/8). Easy Musicnotes piano in C and the Bb accompaniment edition are singer transposes, not the record. Guitar usually uses capo 3 and plays C-shapes (`C – G/A – Am – F – Em7 – Dm7`, sounding `Eb – Bb/C – Cm – Ab – Gm7 – Fm7`). Chords-and-tabs’ Dirk Cushenbery chart and the R&B-lesson capo-3 sheet match that concert map. Keep the pre-chorus descending bass `Cm – Gaug/B – Eb/Bb – Adim7`.

**Spoken intro**

`Eb – Gm7/C – Cm7`
`Ab – Bb/G – Ab/F – Ab/Bb`

Spoken cold open sits on the verse loop.

**Verse**

`Eb – Gm7/C – Cm7`
`Ab – Bb/G – Ab/F – Ab/Bb`
`Eb – Gm7/C – Cm7`
`Ab – Bb/G – Ab/F – Ab/Bb`

Easy charts collapse this to `Eb – Cm – Ab – Bb`.

**Pre-chorus**

`Cm – Gaug/B – Eb/Bb – Adim7`
`Abmaj7 – Bb/G – Abmaj7/F – Abmaj7/Bb`

Some sheets label this Bridge. Keep the C–B–Bb–A bass.

**Chorus**

`Eb – Fm7/Eb – Eb – Bb/C`
`Eb/C – Bb/G – Abadd2`
`Eb/G – Ab/F – Ab/Bb`

Repeat the three lines. Easy sheets stay on `Eb – Bb – Cm – Ab` and skip the inversions.

**Breakdown**

`Eb – Gm7/C – Cm7`
`Ab – Bb/G – Ab/F – Ab/Bb`

Spoken verse over the same loop, then the pre-chorus returns sung.

**Outro**

`Eb – Fm7/Eb – Eb – Bb/C`
`Eb/C – Bb/G – Abadd2`
`Eb/G – Ab/F – Ab/Bb`

Chorus repeats into the fade.

**Form**

Spoken intro → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Breakdown → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Outro
