Man in the Mirror
Artist: Michael Jackson

Key: G major, 4/4, then a half-step lift to A-flat. Wikipedia-style analyses, piano tutorials, and original-key guitar charts agree on G; the gospel choir enters when the last G chorus resolves up to A-flat. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original synth-gospel arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar often uses open G shapes with no capo.

**Chorus** (A-flat)

**Outro vamp** (A-flat, then hangs on D-flat)

`Ab – Eb/G – Fm – Eb – Db – Eb7sus4`

The last D-flat stays put for a long coda; after several bars it starts to feel like the new tonic.
