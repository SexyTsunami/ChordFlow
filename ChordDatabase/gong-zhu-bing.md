公主病 (Gong Zhu Bing / Princess Syndrome)
Artist: Jay Chou (周杰倫 / 周杰伦)

From 驚嘆號, not a 絕美女神 album. Key: F major, chorus in D, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar commonly uses C shapes, capo 5 for the F verse (`C – Am7 – Dm7`, sounding `F – Dm7 – Gm7`); move to open D or drop the capo for the D chorus. Chord4 and 吉他世界 agree on concert F with a D chorus. Easy C-shape-only sheets that never leave C sit a fourth below the record.
