I Was Never There
Artist: The Weeknd, Gesaffelstein

Key: A minor, then F minor, 4/4. Ultimate Guitar and GuitarTuna’s original-key charts open in A minor (`Am – F – Dm – Em`) and modulate for the second half; Beatport/DJ listings often tag the track F major from that later section. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar stays in open A-minor shapes with no capo, then jumps to barre `Fm` shapes. Easy charts that stay in A minor for the whole song omit the Gesaffelstein drop.

**Intro**

`Am – F – Dm – Em`

Four cycles of the piano loop before the first vocal.

**Verse**

`Am – F – Dm – Em`

The “what makes a grown man” verse is the same four-chord cycle.

**Chorus**

`Am – F – Dm – Em`

The “when it’s time” hook does not change chords; it only stretches the loop.

**Interlude**

`Fm – Eb – Db – Bbm – Cm`

The beat drop transposes the chorus idea into F minor. This is the split a lot of auto-charts miss.

**Bridge**

`Fm – Eb – Db – Bbm – Cm`

The “something toxic” section stays in the new key.

**Outro**

`Fm – Eb – Db – Bbm – Cm`

“It’s all because of you” holds the F-minor loop; a few charts sneak a `Bm` neighbor before the last `Cm`.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Interlude → Bridge → Outro
