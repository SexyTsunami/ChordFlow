月半小夜曲 (Yue Ban Xiao Ye Qu / Mid-Autumn Serenade)
Artist: Hacken Lee (李克勤)

Cantonese hit from 命運符號 (1987); adapted from 河合奈保子’s ハーフムーン・セレナーデ. Key: Bb major (verse in G minor), 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitarworld, 吉他网, and 谱全了 agree on concert Bb. Guitar usually uses G shapes, capo 3 (`Em – Am – D – G – B7`, sounding `Gm – Cm – F – Bb – D7`). Easy Am-shape charts with no capo sit a whole step above those G shapes and are not concert pitch.

**Intro**

`Gm – Cm – F – Bb – D7`

Violin-led on the record; guitar often previews the verse.

**Verse**

`Gm – Cm – F – Bb – D7`
`Gm – Cm – Eb – D7`

Second half of the verse repeats the same pair of lines; the last cadence of verse 1 stays on `D7`, while verse 2’s last cadence lands `D7 – Gm`.

**Chorus**

`Eb – F – Dm – Gm – Eb – Cm – D7`
`Gm – Cm – F – Bb – D7`
`Gm – Cm – Eb – F – Gm`

Capo-3 G-shape is `C – D – Bm – Em – C – Am – B7`, then `Em – Am – D – G – B7`, then `Em – Am – C – D – Em`.

**Interlude**

`Gm – Cm – F – Bb – D7`

Verse colors under the violin.

**Outro**

`Eb – F – Gm`

Last chorus cadence, held on `Gm`.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Interlude → Chorus → Outro
