Love Again
Artist: Dua Lipa

Key: F-sharp minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices (`F#m6`, `E/G#`, and the sampled horn hook). Musicnotes, Chordify, and Hooktheory agree on F-sharp minor; a few guitar sites label the relative A major because the loop is `F#m – D – Bm – E`. Guitar usually uses capo 2 and plays `Em – C – Am – D` (chorus `C – Am – D – Em`). Easy no-capo E-minor charts sit a whole step below the record.

**Intro**

`F#m – D – Bm – E`

Horn sample over the verse loop.

**Verse**

`F#m – D – Bm – E`

The last line of each verse (“got me in love again”) still uses this loop, landing back on `F#m`.

**Pre-Chorus**

`F#m – D – Bm – E`

Same four chords; the “show me that heaven” figure starts mid-loop on `D`.

**Chorus**

`D – Bm – E – F#m`

The chorus rotates the same four chords so it starts on `D` instead of `F#m`. The last “again” is `Bm – E`.

**Bridge**

`F#m – D – Bm – E`

Back to the verse starting point for the “I can’t believe” section.

**Outro**

`F#m – D – Bm – E`
`D – Bm – E – F#m`

Verse loop, then the chorus rotation through the final stacked tags.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Bridge → Verse → Outro
