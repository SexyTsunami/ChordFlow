突然好想你 (Tu Ran Hao Xiang Ni / Suddenly Missing You)
Artist: Mayday (五月天)

Key: D major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar commonly uses C shapes, capo 2. Polygon’s original-key chart and Chord4’s capo chart agree on concert D.

**Intro**

`D – C#m7b5 – F#7 – Bm – Bm7/A – Gm – A`

**Verse**

`D – A – Bm7 – Bm7/A`
`G – F#m7 – Em7 – A`
`G – A – F#m – Bm7`
`Em7 – Asus4 – D`

Capo charts write this as `C – G/B – Am7 – Am7/G` and so on.

**Chorus**

`G – G/A – F#m7 – Bm7`
`Em7 – A – D`
`G – G/A – F#m7 – Bm7`
`Em7 – A – D`

Second ending often colors the last `D` as `Dsus4 – D`, or slips `Gm7` before a `C7` into the next section.

**Verse 2**

`D – C#m7b5 – F#7 – Bm7 – Bm7/A`
`G – F#m7 – Em7 – A`
`G – A – F#m7 – Bm7`
`Em7 – A – D`

Opens with the intro’s half-diminished walk, then returns to the verse loop.

**Interlude**

`C – Bm7 – Am7 – D7`
`Gmaj7 – Gm7 – F – Em7 – A`

**Bridge**

`Bm7 – F#m – G – Gm – A`

**Outro**

`C#m7b5 – F#7 – Bm – Bm7/A – Gm7 – A – D`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Verse 2 → Interlude → Bridge → Chorus → Verse → Outro
