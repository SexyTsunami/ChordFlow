My Sacrifice
Artist: Creed

Key: D major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction of the Weathered studio cut; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Musicnotes’ recorded guitar version is D major around 71 BPM. Recorded guitars are drop D (`DADGBE`). Ultimate Guitar and GuitarTuna reduce the verse to `D – Dmaj7 – Bm – G` and the chorus to `D – A – Bm – G`; denser tabs spell `Dmaj7sus2`, `Bmadd9`, and `Gsus2`. Easy `D – A – Bm – G` sheets through the whole song drop the intro `Bm` riff.
