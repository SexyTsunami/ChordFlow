Blinding Lights
Artist: The Weeknd

Key: F minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original synth arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. The same `Fm – Cm – Eb – Bb` loop runs through every section. Guitar usually uses capo 3 and plays `Dm – Am – C – G`, or capo 1 with `Em – Bm – D – A`; both match concert F minor. Easy no-capo E-minor charts sit a half step below the record. Production writeups and guitar charts agree on F minor; some theory essays call it C minor or F Dorian because the loop never cadences and the track ends on `Bb`.

**Intro**

`Fm – Cm – Eb – Bb`

Synth hook over the four-bar loop. The record opens on an F pedal before the chords start walking.

**Verse**

`Fm – Cm – Eb – Bb`

**Pre-Chorus**

`Fm – Cm – Eb – Bb`

Same harmony as the verse; the vocal lifts into the higher register.

**Chorus**

`Fm – Cm – Eb – Bb`

**Bridge**

`Fm – Cm – Eb – Bb`

Breakdown over the same loop; drums drop out, then the synth hook returns.

**Outro**

`Fm – Cm – Eb – Bb`

The loop holds to the end; the last sustained harmony is `Bb`.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus → Outro
