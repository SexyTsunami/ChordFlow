I Like You (A Happier Song)
Artist: Post Malone, Doja Cat

Key: F major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar stays in F with no capo. Hooktheory, Cifra Club, Songmode, and GuitarTwitt agree on F major and the `Bbmaj7 – C7 – Fmaj7` loop. Jazzier charts swap in `Gm9 – C13` for `Bbmaj7 – C7` (ii–V instead of IV–V); those are voicings of the same cadence. Auto-keys that list F minor are misreading the minor sevenths. `F7` is the turnaround back into the next phrase; `Dm7` is a one-bar color in verse 1.

**Intro**

`Bbmaj7 – C7 – Fmaj7`
`Bbmaj7 – C7 – Fmaj7 – F7`

Wordless “ooh” pads on the chorus changes.

**Chorus**

`Bbmaj7 – C7 – Fmaj7`
`Bbmaj7 – C7 – Fmaj7 – F7`

Four “I like you, I do” phrases; the last one adds `F7`.

**Verse**

`Bbmaj7 – C7 – Fmaj7 – F7`

Doja Cat’s verse uses the same loop. One interior line walks `Fmaj7 – Dm7` (“share this heart with me”) before `Bbmaj7 – C7` returns.

**Bridge**

`Bbmaj7 – C7 – Fmaj7`
`C7 – Fmaj7 – F7`

“I just want you” stays on the chorus colors; the tag hits `C7` a bar early.

**Outro**

`Bbmaj7 – C7 – Fmaj7`

Last “I like you” / “don’t want it now, I’m with you” over the three-chord pad.

**Form**

Intro → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus → Outro
