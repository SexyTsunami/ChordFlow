Please Me
Artist: Cardi B, Bruno Mars

Key: F# minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. The hook/verse loop barely changes. Guitar often uses capo 2 and plays E-minor shapes. Some charts write `F#m/B` in place of `Bm7`; both are reductions of the same bass.

**Intro**

`F#m`

**Chorus**

`Bm7 – C#7 – E/F# – F#7`

Repeat. The “please” call-and-response stays on this loop.

**Verse**

`Bm7 – C#7 – E/F# – F#7`

Same loop. The recording’s `C#7` is often a `C#7b9b13` color; most guitar charts use plain `C#7`.

**Pre-Chorus**

`Bm7 – C#7 – E/F# – F#7`

**Bridge**

`Bm7`
`C#m7`
`D#m7b5`
`C#sus4 – C#`

**Form**

Intro → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus
