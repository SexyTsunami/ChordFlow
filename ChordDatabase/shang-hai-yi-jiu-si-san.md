上海一九四三 (Shang Hai Yi Jiu Si San / Shanghai 1943)
Artist: Jay Chou (周杰倫 / 周杰伦)

Key: D major, 4/4. The last chorus lifts a half step to Eb. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar commonly uses C shapes, capo 2.

**Intro**

`D – Bm – G – A`

**Verse**

`D – Bm – G – F#m – G – A`
`D – Bm – G – F#m – G – A`

**Pre-chorus**

`Bm – F#m – G – A – D`
`Bm – F#m – G – A`

**Chorus** (two matching halves)

First half:
`D – Bm – G – F#m – G – A`

Second half:
`D – Bm – G – F#m – G – A – Bm`

**Interlude**

`G – D – A – Bm – G – A`

**Last chorus**

Same pattern, up a half step to Eb:

`Eb – Cm – Ab – Gm – Ab – Bb`
then the matching second half ending on **Cm**

**Outro**

`Cm – Ab – Eb – Bb – Eb`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Interlude → Verse → Pre-chorus → last chorus in Eb → Outro
