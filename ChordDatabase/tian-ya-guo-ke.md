天涯過客 (Tian Ya Guo Ke / Wanderer at the End of the World)
Artist: Jay Chou (周杰倫 / 周杰伦)

Chinese-style ballad from 哎呦，不錯哦. Key: D major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar commonly uses C shapes, capo 2. 易譜庫 and Polygon agree on concert D.

**Intro**

`D – Bm7`
`Gmaj7 – F#7`
`Bm – D/A`
`Gmaj7 – A – D`

**Verse**

`Gmaj7 – F#7`
`Gmaj7 – A`
`D – D/A`
`Gmaj7 – F#7`
`Em7 – A – D – D/A`

**Chorus** (two matching halves)

First half:
`D – A/C# – Bm7`
`Gmaj7 – Gm`
`D/A – D`

Second half:
`D – A/C# – Bm7`
`Gmaj7 – A – D`

**Interlude**

`Gmaj7 – F#7`
`Em7 – A – D`

**Last chorus**

Same pattern, with a darker turnaround:

`Gm – Gm6 – F – Dm7 – Em7`
`D – A/C# – Bm7`
`Gmaj7 – A – D`

**Outro**

`Gmaj7 – F#m7 – Em7 – A – D`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Interlude → Verse → Chorus → Last chorus → Outro
