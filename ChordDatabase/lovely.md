lovely
Artist: Billie Eilish, Khalid

Key: E minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original piano/strings arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar often uses no capo (`Cmaj7 – Em – Bm`), or capo 7 and plays in A minor (`Fmaj7 – Am – Em`).

**Intro**

`Em – Cmaj7`

**Verse**

`Cmaj7 – Em – Bm`

Some verse charts sit on `Cmaj7` for the first half before moving to `Em – Bm`.

**Pre-Chorus**

`Cmaj7 – Em – Bm`

**Chorus**

`Cmaj7 – Em – Bm`
`Cmaj7 – Em – Bm`

**Outro**

`Cmaj7 – Em – Bm`

Simplified charts drop the major-7 color and loop `Em – C – Bm`.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Outro
