黑色幽默 (Hei Se You Mo / Black Humor)
Artist: Jay Chou (周杰倫 / 周杰伦)

Key: Bb major, 4/4. The chorus slips toward Db. From the debut album *Jay*. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar often uses capo 1 and plays in A.

**Intro**

`Bb – Eb – Bb – Eb`

**Verse**

`Bb – Gm7 – Eb – F`
`Bb – Gm7 – Eb – F`

**Pre-chorus**

`Dm7 – Gm7 – Cm7 – F`
`Dm7 – Gm7 – Cm7 – F`

**Chorus** (two matching halves)

First half:
`Bb – Ebm – Ab – Db`
`Bbm – Ebm – B – Ab`

Second half:
`Bb – Ebm – Ab – Db`
`Bbm – Fm – Ebm – Ab – Db`

**Interlude**

`Bb – Bb/A – Gm7 – Fm7 – Eb – F`

**Last chorus**

Same pattern; the tag often settles in Db:

`Gb – Db/F – Bbm – Ebm – Ab – Db`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Interlude → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → last chorus tag in Db
