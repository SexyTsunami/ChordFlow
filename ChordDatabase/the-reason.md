The Reason
Artist: Hoobastank

Key: E major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction of The Reason album / single cut; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Musicnotes publishes E major around 84 BPM. songparts, GuitarAdda, and Chordify agree on E and `E – C#m – A – B`. Guitar stays in E with no capo. Denser tabs color `C#m7`, `A9`, and `B7` / `Badd11`; easy sheets stay on triads. The only borrowed hit is chorus-into-bridge `D`.
