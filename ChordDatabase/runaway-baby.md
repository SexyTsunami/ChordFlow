Runaway Baby
Artist: Bruno Mars

Key: Eb minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Concert pitch is Eb minor (D# minor). Easy guitar tabs are often written in E minor (a half step up); capo 1 with D-minor shapes matches the record.

**Intro**

`Ebm – Ab`

**Verse**

`Ebm – Ab`

**Pre-Chorus**

`Ebm – Ab`

**Chorus**

`B – Bb – Ebm – Ab`

Repeat. The last line of the hook often stays on `Ebm – Ab` as a turnaround.

**Bridge**

`Ebm – Ab`

Same two-chord vamp as the verse; the horn/guitar breaks sit on this groove.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus
