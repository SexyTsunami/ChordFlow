愛, 很簡單 (Ai Hen Jian Dan / Love Is Simple)
Artist: David Tao (陶喆)

Also written 爱, 很简单. Key: C# major, 4/4. Chords are written here in Db for readability. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar commonly uses C shapes, capo 1. Chordify labels the studio cut Db; Chinese charts (kanpu, GTU, 91pu) and Ultimate Guitar say C# with capo 1. Simplified charts flatten the verse to `Db – Ab – Bbm – Gb`; denser ones keep Fm, Bbm7/Ab, and Db/F.
