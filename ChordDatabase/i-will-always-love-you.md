I Will Always Love You
Artist: Whitney Houston

Key: A major, 4/4 (a cappella intro marked freely, then about 66–68 BPM). This is Houston's Bodyguard arrangement, not the Dolly Parton original's groove. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original piano/strings add extra passing tones and inner voices (some piano charts write D7, C#m, and Bm7 inside the A–F#m–D–E loop). Guitar usually plays open A-shape chords with no capo, or capo 2 and plays in G. The last chorus lifts a whole step to B.

**Intro**

A cappella (no chords), then the band enters on the chorus.

**Verse**

`A – F#m7 – D – E`

Some guitar charts insert a passing E: `A – E – A – F#m – E – D – E`.

**Chorus**

`A – F#m – D – E`

**Tag**

`F#m – E`

**Sax solo**

Same as the verse/chorus loop in A.

**Final chorus** (B major)

`B – G#m – E – F#`

**Form**

Intro (a cappella) → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Sax solo → Verse → Chorus (B) → Tag
