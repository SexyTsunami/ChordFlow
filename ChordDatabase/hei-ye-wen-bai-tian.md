黑夜問白天 (Hei Ye Wen Bai Tian / Night Asks Day)
Artist: JJ Lin (林俊傑 / 林俊杰)

Also written 黑夜问白天; also called 53 Dawns. Key: C major (relative A minor), last chorus in D, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar in C, no capo.

**Intro**

`Am – Fmaj7 – Am – Fmaj7`
`Am – Fmaj7 – Dm7 – G`

**Verse**

`Fmaj7 – Am – Fmaj7 – C`
`Dm7 – Gsus4 – G`

After verse 1, a bar of rest before the chorus. Verse 2 goes straight in.

**Chorus** (two matching halves)

First half:
`Cadd9 – E7 – Am – Em7`
`F – G – Em – Am – Dm7 – G`

Second half:
`Cadd9 – E7 – Am – Em7`
`F – G – Em – Am – Dm7 – Gsus4 – G`

Chorus 2 second half starts F – G – Am – G – E7 instead of Cadd9 – E7, then Am – Am7/G on the next bar. Some charts keep Cadd9 there, or spell that bar F – G – Am – G/B.

**Interlude** (after chorus 1)

`Fmaj7 – Am – Fmaj7 – Am`
`Fmaj7 – Am – Dm – Gsus4 – G`

**Bridge**

`F – Em – Am – Ab – G – A`

**Chorus** (D)

Second half, up a whole step:

`F#7 – Bm – F#m`
`G – A – F#m – Bm – Em – A`

Then a G6 vamp. Some charts stay in C through this last chorus and skip the D lift.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Interlude → Verse → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus (D) → Outro (G6)
