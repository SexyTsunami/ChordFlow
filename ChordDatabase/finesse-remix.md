Finesse (Remix)
Artist: Bruno Mars, Cardi B

Key: F minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. The remix is a slower, minor-mode rework of the album track (original album version is Ab major, ~124 BPM). Chordify, Hooktheory, and Beatport agree on F minor; Wikipedia/Musicnotes label the remix Ab minor. Guitar often uses capo 1 and plays E-minor shapes.

**Verse**

`Dbmaj7 – C7 – Fm7 – C7`

Cardi’s opening verse and Bruno’s sung verses share this loop.

**Pre-Chorus**

`Dbmaj7 – C7 – Fm7 – C7`

**Chorus**

`Dbmaj7 – C7 – Fm7 – C7`

**Bridge**

`Fm7 – C7`
`Fm7 – C7 – Dbmaj7`

The “got it goin’ on” tag stays on `Fm7 – C7`, with `Dbmaj7` as a lift back into the chorus.

**Form**

Verse → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus
