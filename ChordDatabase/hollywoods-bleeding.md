Hollywood's Bleeding
Artist: Post Malone

Key: E minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar stays in E minor with no capo. Ultimate Guitar, Chordify, and ChordLines agree on E minor. Chordify colors the verse IV as `Cmaj7`; triad charts print `C` — same function. Tunebat’s 9A Camelot listing matches E minor.

**Intro**

`Em – D – C – Am`

Two passes of i–VII–VI–iv.

**Verse**

`Em – D – C – Am`

The title lines (“Hollywood’s bleeding, vampires feedin’”) are this four-chord verse, not the chorus. Verse 2 keeps the same loop, then a pre-hook lift of `C – D – Em – G` on “more than I can handle / blood in my Lambo.”

**Chorus**

`C – D – Em – G`
`C – D – Em – D`
`C – D – Em – G`
`C – D – Em`

“I just keep on hopin’ that you call me.” The second line walks back through `D` instead of `G`; the last line cadences on `Em` (“we call it home”).

**Outro**

`C – D – Em – G`
`C – D – Em`

Final chorus without a new section; the last hold is `Em`.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Outro
