Locked Out of Heaven
Artist: Bruno Mars

Key: F major (relative D minor), 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. The reggae-funk riff is built on C and Dm, so many guitar charts look as if the song is in C; the chorus cadence is Bb–Gm–F–C.

**Intro**

`C – Dm – C – Bb – F/A – Gm`

**Verse**

`C – Dm – C – Bb – F/A – Gm`

Same riff as the intro. Some reductions drop the slash bass and write `C – Dm – C – Bb – F – Gm`.

**Pre-Chorus**

`C – Bb – F/A – Gm`
`F – A7`

**Chorus**

`Bb – Gm – Fmaj7 – C`

Repeat. The last pass often lands `Csus4 – C` (or `C – Dm` into the riff).

**Bridge**

`Bb – Gm – Fmaj7 – C`

Same loop as the chorus.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus → Outro
