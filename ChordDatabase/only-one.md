Only One
Artist: BoA (보아)

Key: C major (hooks sit in A minor), 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Tunebat labels C major; guitar charts (Kpop Chords, KhmerChords, Songsterr) print the concert loop `F – G – Em – Am` with no capo. Some reprints call the song F major because the loop starts on `F`. Denser charts use `G7` for `G`.

**Intro** (three times)

`F – G – Em – Am`

**Chorus**

`F – G – Em – Am`

The record opens on the chorus. Kpop Chords colors `G` as `G7`.

**Verse**

`F – G – Em – Am`

**Pre-Chorus**

`F – G – Em – Am`

Same loop; this is the lift into the title hook.

**Interlude**

`F – G – Em – Am`

**Outro**

`F – G – Em – Am`

**Form**

Intro → Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Interlude → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Chorus → Outro
