drunk
Artist: keshi

Playlist cut is the 2020 live acoustic (not the denser studio/electric arrangement). Key: E major, 4/4. This is the common guitar reduction of that acoustic performance; the original fingerstyle adds extra passing tones and inner voices. ChordU’s live-acoustic track and VChords agree on E with no capo. The walking line is `E – G#7 – C#m7` then a bass descent into `A – B`; easy charts that loop only `E – C#m – A – B` miss the `G#7`.

**Intro**

`E – G#7 – C#m7 – C#m7/B – C#m/A# – A – B`

Some reductions spell `G#7` as `E(b9)/G#` on the second half of the bar.

**Chorus**

`E – G#7 – C#m7 – C#m7/B – C#m/A# – A – B`

The live acoustic opens on the chorus. Last pass of the final chorus lands `A – B – E`.

**Verse**

`E – G#7 – C#m7 – C#m7/B – C#m/A# – A – B`

Same walk as the chorus; “smell like smoke” / “I never thought” sit on this loop.

**Outro**

`E – G#7 – C#m7 – C#m7/B – C#m/A# – A – B – E`

Wordless tags of the walk, ending on `E`.

**Form**

Intro → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Outro
