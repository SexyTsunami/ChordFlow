Cry
Artist: Benson Boone

From Fireworks & Rollerblades (2024). This is Benson Boone’s song, not Adele’s. Key: Bb major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Musicnotes PVG, Singing Carrots, ChordsEasy, and UkuTabs agree on Bb. Guitar usually uses capo 3 and plays G-shapes (`G – Em – C – Am`, sounding `Bb – Gm – Eb – Cm`) to match concert pitch. Capo-1 A-shape sheets (VChords) sit a half-step below the record. Keep the chorus `Ebm` color; easy charts skip it.

**Intro**

`Bb – Fm – Gm – Eb`
`Ebm – Bb`

Spoken false-start on `Bb`, then the verse.

**Verse**

`Bb – Gm – Eb – Cm – Bb`
`Bb – Dm – Gm – Eb – Cm – Bb`

Turnaround is often `Cm – Dm`. Verse 2 swaps the first `Gm` approach for `F – Gm`.

**Pre-chorus**

`Eb – Ebm – Bb`

Verse 2 extends this with `Eb – F – Bb` / `Eb – F – Gm` walks before the `Ebm` drop.

**Chorus**

`Gm – Eb – Bb`
`Gm – Eb – Ebm – Bb`

**Bridge**

`Gm – Eb – Ebm – Bb`
`Gm – Eb – Ebm – Bb`

Same chorus colors; last phrase reuses the pre-chorus `Eb – Ebm – Bb` into the outro.

**Outro**

`Gm – Eb – Bb`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Bridge → Outro
