Silvertongue
Artist: Young the Giant

Key: D minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar stays in D minor with no capo. Beatport, ChordsBase, and GuitarTuna agree on D minor. E-Chords labels G minor because the intro/hook can start on `Gm – F`. ChordsBase/GuitarTuna chorus is `F – Gm – Dm – C`; E-Chords flips the first two chords to `Gm – F`. Spell `A#` charts as `Bb`.

**Intro**

`Dm – Gm`

Some charts open on the chorus loop `Gm – F – Dm – C` instead of the verse vamp.

**Verse**

`Dm – Gm`

**Chorus**

`F – Gm – Dm – C`
`F – Gm – Dm – Bb`
`Gm – F – Dm – Bb`
`Dm`

**Bridge**

`Gm – Am – Bb – C`

ChordsBase’s other spelling of this lift is `Gm – Bb – Am – F – Bb – Gm – Dm`.

**Outro**

`Dm – Gm`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus
