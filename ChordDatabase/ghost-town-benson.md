Ghost Town
Artist: Benson Boone

This is Benson Boone’s song, not JVKE’s. Debut single (October 2021), then Walk Me Home... (2022) and Fireworks & Rollerblades (2024). Filename is ghost-town-benson.md because ghost-town.md is already JVKE. Key: A minor, 12/8. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Musicnotes PVG publishes A minor in 12/8; Chordify tags the relative C major. Guitar stays in open C/Am with no capo (`F – G – Em – Am`). GuitarTwitt, Gitagram, Lamucal, and Starakor agree on verse `F – G – Em – Am` and chorus `F – G – C – Am – G`. Guitar charts often mark 4/4; keep the compound piano feel as 12/8.
