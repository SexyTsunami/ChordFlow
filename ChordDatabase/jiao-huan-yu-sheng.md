交換餘生 (Jiao Huan Yu Sheng / Exchange the Rest of Life)
Artist: JJ Lin (林俊傑 / 林俊杰)

Also written 交换余生; also called No Turning Back. Key: D major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar commonly uses C shapes, capo 2.

**Intro**

`D – Am – C – G`
`D – Am – C – Asus4 – A`

Some charts keep the second line on G instead of Asus4 – A.

**Verse**

`D – G – A – F#`
`Bm – E – Em – A`
`F#m – Bm – F#m – Bm`
`Em – D – Gm – A`

Some charts use Gmaj7, A/C# – F#7, and Em – Asus4 – A in place of the plainer G – A – F# / Em – A.

**Chorus**

`G – A – F#m – Bm`
`Em – A – Am7 – D`
`G – A – F#m – Bm`
`Em – A – D`

**Turnaround** (after chorus 1)

`Ddim7 – D`

Repeated. Some charts use Dm – D instead of Ddim7 – D.

**Bridge**

`G – F#m – Bm`
`Em – A – D`
`G – A – Bm`
`E – Gm – A`

**Final chorus**

Same as chorus, then:

`Em – A – Bm – E/G# – G – A – D`

**Outro**

`Am – C – G/B – D`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Turnaround → Verse → Chorus → Bridge → Final chorus → Outro
