Dilemma
Artist: Nelly, Kelly Rowland

Key: D minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction of the Nellyville studio cut; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Hooktheory, Klangio, and songdata.io agree on D minor (half-time around 84–85 BPM). Cifra Club and ChordLines loop `Gm7 – C – Am7 – Dm` all the way through; beginner charts drop the sevenths to `Gm – C – Am – Dm`. Some guitar pages label Gm because the loop starts there; keep D minor as the concert tonic. The track interpolates Patti LaBelle’s “Love, Need and Want You.” No capo.
