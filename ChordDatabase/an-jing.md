安靜 (An Jing / Silence)
Artist: Jay Chou (周杰倫 / 周杰伦)

Key: Bb major, 4/4. The last chorus usually lifts a whole step to C. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original piano part adds extra passing tones and inner voices.

**Intro** (twice)

`Bb – Eb – Gm – F`

**Verse**

`Bb – Gm – Eb – Cm – F`
`Bb – Gm – Eb – Ebm`

**Pre-chorus**

`Cm – F – Dm – Gm`
`Cm – Dm – Eb – F`

**Chorus** (two matching halves)

First half:
`Bb – Dm – Gm – F – Eb – Dm – Gm – Cm – F – F7`

Second half:
`Bb – Dm – Gm – F – Eb – Dm – Gm – Cm – F – Bb`

**Last chorus**

Same pattern, up a whole step:

`C – Em – Am – G – F – Em – Am – Dm – G – G7`
then the matching second half ending on **C**

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → last chorus in C
