最長的電影 (Zui Chang De Dian Ying / The Longest Movie)
Artist: Jay Chou (周杰倫 / 周杰伦)

Also called Very Long Movie. Key: E major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original piano part adds extra passing tones and inner voices.

**Intro** (twice)

`E – E/D – A/C# – Am/C`

**Verse 1**

`E – E/D – A/C# – Am/C`
`E – C#m7 – F#7 – B`
`E – E/D – A/C# – Am/C`
`E/B – B – E – Esus`

**Verse 2**

Same as verse 1, except the last line ends on E7:

`E/B – B – E – E7`

**Chorus** (two matching halves)

First half:
`E7/G# – A – B/A – G#m7 – C#m – F#m – B – E – E7`

Second half:
`E7/G# – A – B – G#m7 – C#m – F#m – B – E`

**Interlude**

`E – Am/C – E – C#m – F#m – A/B`

**Outro**

`E – E/D – A/C# – Am/C`

**Form**

Intro → Verse 1 → Verse 2 → Chorus → Interlude → Verse 2 → Chorus → Chorus → Outro
