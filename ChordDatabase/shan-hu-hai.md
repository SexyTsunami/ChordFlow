珊瑚海 (Shan Hu Hai / Coral Sea)
Artist: Jay Chou (周杰倫 / 周杰伦), Lara Veronin (梁心頤)

Key: Ab major, 4/4. Duet with Lara Veronin (梁心頤). This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar often uses capo 1 and plays in G.

`Fm7 – Eb/G – Ab – Eb/G`
`Fm7 – Eb/G – Ab`

`Fm7 – Eb/G – Ab`
`Db – Cm7 – Bbm7 – Ab – Gb – Eb`

Second verse ending:
`Db – Cm7 – Bbm7 – Cm7 – Db – Eb`

First half:
`Ab – Cm7 – C7/E – Fm7 – Cm7 – Db – Eb/Db – Cm7 – Fm7 – Bbm7 – Eb`

Second half:
`Ab – Cm7 – C7/E – Fm7 – Cm7 – Db – Eb/Db – Cm7 – Fm7 – Bbm7 – Eb – Ab`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Verse → Chorus
