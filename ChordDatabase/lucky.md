Lucky
Artist: Jason Mraz, Colbie Caillat

Key: C major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Musicnotes’ published key is C major. Guitar stays in C with no capo. The verse’s `E7` leads into `Am`; some easy charts swap it for `Em`.

**Intro**

`C`

**Verse**

`C – Am – Dm7 – G – E7 – Am – Dm7 – G`

**Chorus**

`Am – Dm7 – G – C – C/B – Am`
`Dm7 – G`

The “coming home again” walk is often `Dm7 – C/G – G`. Beginner charts drop `C/B` and play `C – Am`.

**Post-Chorus**

`C – Am – Em – G`

**Bridge**

`Dm7 – Am – G – Dm7`
`Dm7 – Am – G – Dm7`
`Dm7 – Am – G`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Post-Chorus → Bridge → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Post-Chorus
