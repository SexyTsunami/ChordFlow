Sofia
Artist: Clairo

Key: C major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction of the Immunity studio cut; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Musicnotes publishes C major. GuitarTuna’s original-key chart and Chordify loop `Dm7 – G7 – Cmaj7 – Fmaj7`. Cifra Club is the same idea with `Am` on some last bars. Easy guitar reprints loop `F – G – Em – Am` (a different C-major color, not the record). Capo-5 charts color `Am7 – Am6 – G – G6` as guitar voicings over the C-major loop. About 113 BPM.
