Chunky
Artist: Bruno Mars

Key: F minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. The groove is a walking `Fm9` bass over `Gm7`. Guitar often stays in open-ish F minor or uses capo 1.

**Intro**

`Fm – Ebmaj7 – Cm/Db – Dbmaj7`

Horn/synth hits; some charts skip this and start on the verse vamp.

**Verse**

`Fm9 – Fm9/G – Fm9/Ab – Fm9/Bb – Gm7 – Gm7/C`

**Pre-Chorus**

`Fm9 – Fm9/G – Fm9/Ab – Fm9/Bb – Gm7 – Gm7/C`
`Fm7 – Gm7 – Abmaj7 – Bb – Dm7b5 – G7`

**Chorus**

`Fm9 – Fm9/G – Fm9/Ab – Fm9/Bb – Gm7 – Gm7/C`

Easy charts drop the slash bass and write `Fm – Gm – Ab`.

**Bridge**

`Abmaj7 – Bb – C – Gm7`
`Abmaj7 – Bb – C`
`Fm7 – Gm7 – Abmaj7 – Bb – Dm7b5 – G7`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus
