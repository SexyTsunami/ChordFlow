大地 (Da Di / The Earth)
Artist: Beyond (黃家駒 / 黄家驹 / Wong Ka Kui)

Cantonese original from 秘密警察. Key: A minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. No capo. ComeGetChord and Guitarians label Am. Chinese guitarworld charts say C because numbered notation uses C=1 (relative major of A minor), not a different concert pitch. Verse charts disagree on the second chord: ComeGetChord writes G, Guitarians/mimenor write Em.

**Intro**

`Am – C – G – Em`

**Verse**

`Am – Em – C – Am`
`Am – Em – C – Am`

**Pre-Chorus**

`C – Em – G – Am`
`C – Em – G`

**Chorus**

`Am – G – Dm – Am`
`F – G – Am`
`Am – G – Dm – Am`
`F – G – Am`

**Solo**

`Am – C – G – Em`
`Am – C – G – Em`

Guitar solo over the intro figure (studio 秘密警察 cut has both a mid-song solo and a closing electric solo on this frame).

**Outro**

`Am – G – Dm – Am`
`F – G – Am`

Last chorus plus the intro guitar figure as a fade.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Solo → Chorus → Outro
