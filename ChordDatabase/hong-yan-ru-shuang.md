紅顏如霜 (Hong Yan Ru Shuang / Beauty Like Frost)
Artist: Jay Chou (周杰倫 / 周杰伦)

Key: Bb major, 4/4. From *最偉大的作品*. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar often uses capo 3 and plays in G.

**Intro**

`Eb – Dm – Cm – F – Bb`

**Verse**

`Eb – Dm – Cm – Bb`
`Eb – Dm – Cm – Bb`

**Pre-chorus** (the 鳳求凰 / 凤求凰 call)

`D7 – Gm – D7 – Gm`
`Eb – Dm – Cm – F`

**Chorus** (two matching halves)

First half:
`Eb – Dm – Cm – F – Bb`

Second half:
`Eb – Dm – Gm – Cm – F – Bb`

**Interlude**

`Eb – Dm – Gm – Db7`
`Cm – Gbmaj7 – F`

**Outro**

`Eb – Db – B – Gb – Bb`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Interlude → Chorus → Outro
