Don't Blame Me
Artist: Taylor Swift

From reputation (2017). Key: A minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Musicnotes PVG (MN0179490), Chordify, and Hooktheory agree on concert A minor (about 136 BPM). Guitar stays in A minor with no capo. The chorus loop is `Am – G – F` then `C – Dm – Am`; verse lines add `Fmaj7` as an inner voice on `F`. Ultimate Guitar and Lamucal match that reduction. Easy four-chord `Am – F – C – G` sheets drop the `Dm` cadence.
