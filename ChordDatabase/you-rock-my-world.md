You Rock My World
Artist: Michael Jackson

Key: E minor, 4/4. Wikipedia, isolated-track listings, and original-key guitar charts agree on E minor. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original Jerkins synth-horn arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar usually plays Em shapes with no capo. Some pages simplify the loop to `Em – A5`.

**Intro / verse / chorus loop**

`Em7 – Am7 – Am7 – Em7`

Wikipedia’s summary of the same groove:

`Em7 – Am7 – D – Em7`

**Pre-chorus** (same loop)

`Em7 – Am7 – Am7 – Em7`

**Bridge**

`Cmaj7 – Am7 – D – Em7 – Fmaj7`
`Cmaj7 – Am7 – D – Em7`
