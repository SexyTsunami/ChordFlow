學不會 (Xue Bu Hui / Can't Learn)
Artist: JJ Lin (林俊傑 / 林俊杰)

Also written 学不会. Key: F# major (Gb), last chorus in Ab, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar commonly uses C shapes, capo 6; last chorus is a whole-step lift. Some charts spell the home key Gb.
