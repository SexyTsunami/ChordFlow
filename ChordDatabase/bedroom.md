Bedroom
Artist: JJ Lin (林俊傑 / 林俊杰), Anne-Marie

Key: B major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar commonly uses G shapes, capo 4. Many play-along charts leave the shapes in G and call that the key.

**Intro**

`B`

**Verse**

`B – F#/A# – A – E/G#`
`Em/G – B/F# – C#/F – F#sus4 – F#`

(twice)

**Chorus**

`B – D#m/A# – A – Em – G – B`
`B – D#m/A# – A – Em – B`

Chorus 2 / last chorus swap the A for B7/A, then:

`Em – Em/G – B – F#/A# – G#m – Em/G`

Some charts use F#/A# in place of D#m/A# through the chorus.

**Post-chorus**

`B – D#m/A# – B7/A – Em – Em/G – F# – B`

**Bridge**

Same as verse.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Post-chorus → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus → Post-chorus → Chorus
