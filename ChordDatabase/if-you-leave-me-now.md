If You Leave Me Now
Artist: Charlie Puth, Boyz II Men

From Charlie Puth’s Voicenotes (2018). This is the Charlie Puth featuring Boyz II Men cut ranked on Boyz II Men’s chart page, not Chicago’s 1976 original (that recording is a different arrangement in B major). Key: D major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Chordify, Hooktheory, and ChordZone agree on concert D at about 73 BPM. TabAndChord and myChords share verse `D – G – D – Gm` and the `Bbdim` approach into the pre-chorus. Guitar stays in D with no capo, or uses C-shapes, capo 2 (`C – F – C – Fm`, sounding `D – G – D – Gm`). Keep borrowed `Gm` and the chorus `E7`. Denser last-chorus pages lift a whole step to E; easy sheets stay in D — keep D as the concert tonic of the cut.
