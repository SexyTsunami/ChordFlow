One Sweet Day
Artist: Mariah Carey, Boyz II Men (Nathan Morris, Wanya Morris, Shawn Stockman, Michael McCary)

Key: Ab major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction of the Daydream duet; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Musicnotes PVG publishes Ab major (about 76 BPM). Wikipedia, Hooktheory, Tab4U, Ultimate Guitar, and E-Chords agree on Ab, with the last chorus lifting to B. Guitar usually capos 1 and plays G-shapes (`G – Cmaj7 – F – Dsus`, sounding `Ab – Dbmaj7 – Gb – Ebsus`), or stays in Ab with no capo. Do not duplicate this file under Boyz II Men.
