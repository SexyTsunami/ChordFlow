Look What You Made Me Do
Artist: Taylor Swift

From reputation (2017). Key: A minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Musicnotes PVG (MN0177446), Chordify, and Hooktheory agree on concert A minor (about 128 BPM). Guitar stays in A minor with no capo. Verse and pre-chorus walk `Am – G – F – E`; the chorus drone often holds `Am` with `F – E` hits. Ultimate Guitar and Andy Guitar match that Andalusian loop. Some web charts print `Am – F – C – G`; keep `E` (not `C`) for the studio cut.
