Sugar Sweet
Artist: Benson Boone

From Fireworks & Rollerblades (2024); first released on Pulse (2023). Key: D major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Chordify, E-Chords, and Cifras agree on concert D with `D – A – G` plus borrowed `Gm`. Ultimate Guitar–style charts swap the V for Mixolydian `C` (`D – C – G – Gm`); keep `A` for the studio cut, and keep `C` on the bridge where both families meet. No capo.

**Verse**

`D – A – G`

Verse 2 adds `Gm` after `G` on the second phrase.

**Pre-chorus**

`Bm – A – G – Gm`

**Chorus**

`D – A – G – Gm`
`D – A – G – Gm`

Spoken N.C. tag after the first chorus. UG-style sheets print `C` in place of `A`.

**Bridge**

`D – C – G – Gm`

**Form**

Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus
