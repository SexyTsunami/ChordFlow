Cough Syrup
Artist: Young the Giant

Key: D major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar usually uses capo 2 and plays in C (`C – G – F – G` verse, `Am – G – F` chorus). Hooktheory, GuitarAdda, and UkuTabs agree on concert D; E-Chords and ChordLines print the capo-2 C shapes without transposing and label the song C. Intro guitar often colors `D` as `Dmaj7`/`D6` (capo-2 `Cmaj7`/`C6`).

**Intro**

`D – A – G – A`

**Verse**

`D – A – G – A`

**Chorus**

`Bm – A – G`
`Bm – A – G`

Second choruses add a longer `Bm – A – G` tail on the “restore me” lines.

**Solo**

`D – A – G – A`

**Bridge**

`D – A – G – A`

Lead guitar over the verse loop; some charts tag this as an interlude with `Bm` mixed into the last pass (`D – A – G – Bm – A`).

**Outro**

`D – A – G – A`

Same colors as the intro, including the `Dmaj7`/`D6` walk.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Solo → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Bridge → Chorus → Outro
