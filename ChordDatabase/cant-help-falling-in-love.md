Can't Help Falling in Love
Artist: Elvis Presley

Key: D major, 12/8. This is the common guitar/piano reduction of the Blue Hawaii studio cut; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Musicnotes publishes D major in 12/8; song-key databases agree. Guitar often uses capo 2 and plays in C (`C – Em – Am – F – C – G – C`). Easy C charts that skip capo sit a whole step below concert. Some reductions swap the turnaround `Em` for another `G`.

**Intro**

`D – A – Bm – G – D – A`

**Verse**

`D – F#m – Bm – G – D – A`
`G – A – Bm – Em – D – A – D`

**Bridge**

`F#m – C#7 – F#m – C#7`
`F#m – C#7 – F#m – B7 – Em – A`

**Outro**

`G – A – Bm – Em – D – A – D`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Verse → Bridge → Verse → Bridge → Verse → Outro
