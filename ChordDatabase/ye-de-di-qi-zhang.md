夜的第七章 (Ye De Di Qi Zhang / Seventh Chapter of the Night)
Artist: Jay Chou (周杰倫 / 周杰伦) feat. Paner (潘兒 / 潘儿)

Key: D minor, 4/4, then F minor, then G# minor (often labeled F → Ab → B). From *依然范特西*. Dark rap-rock / detective narrative. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar often uses capo 5 and plays in A minor, then moves the capo for the later lifts.
