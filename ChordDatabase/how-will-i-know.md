How Will I Know
Artist: Whitney Houston

Key: G-flat major (F-sharp major), 4/4. Wikipedia and guitar charts in the original key agree on G-flat; Musicnotes piano/vocal editions published in G are a half step above the record. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original synth arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar often uses capo 2 and plays in E, or capo 6 and plays in C. After the bridge, the sax solo and last choruses drop to E-flat.
