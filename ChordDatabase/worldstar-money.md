worldstar money (interlude)
Artist: Joji

Key: G major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction of the In Tongues studio cut; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. UkuTabs, tucuatro, and GuitarTuna agree on G major with no capo. The sung verses sit on `Am7 – Cmaj7`; the “cause I do” hook lands `Gmaj7 – G`. GuitarTuna spells the first chord `C6` (same pitch-class set as `Am7`: A–C–E–G); keep `Am7` for the common guitar charts.
