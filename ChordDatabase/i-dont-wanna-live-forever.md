I Don't Wanna Live Forever
Artist: ZAYN, Taylor Swift

From the Fifty Shades Darker soundtrack (2016). Key: A minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Musicnotes PVG (MN0170410) publishes A minor; Chordify and Hooktheory agree on the same tonic. The loop is `F – C – G – Am` at concert pitch (no capo). Ultimate Guitar and common guitar tutorials stay on that four-chord cycle for verse, pre-chorus, and chorus. Sheets that tag the song C are reading the relative major, not a different concert key.
