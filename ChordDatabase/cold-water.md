Cold Water
Artist: Major Lazer, Justin Bieber, MØ

Key: A major (relative F# minor), 4/4. Musicnotes original published key is A major; many guitar charts label it F# minor because the loop starts on F#m. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar often uses capo 2 and plays `Em – G – Bm – C`. Spotify’s top listing is sometimes the Don Omar Remix; that remix keeps this same harmonic loop.
