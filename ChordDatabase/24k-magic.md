24K Magic
Artist: Bruno Mars

Key: F minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Recorded color is closer to `Fm11 – Bbm7`, with `Dbmaj7` and `Cm` as the turnaround.

**Intro**

`Fm – Bbm – Dbmaj7`

**Verse**

`Fm – Bbm`

Same two-chord vamp as the chorus body. Some charts keep the intro’s `Dbmaj7` on the last bar of each phrase.

**Chorus**

`Fm – Bbm`
`Fm – Bbm – Dbmaj7 – Cm`

The pickup into the loop often walks `Bbm – Cm – Dbmaj7`.

**Bridge**

`Fm – Bbm`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus
