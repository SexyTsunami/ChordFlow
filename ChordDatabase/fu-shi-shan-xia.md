富士山下 (Fu Shi Shan Xia / Under the Fuji Mountain)
Artist: Eason Chan (陳奕迅 / 陈奕迅)

Cantonese hit from What's Going On…?; the Mandarin counterpart is 愛情轉移. Key: F major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar commonly uses C shapes, capo 5. Chord4, ChordU, and Chinese guitar charts agree on F. Simplified C-shape charts with capo 1 sit a tritone away and are for vocal range, not concert pitch. Denser charts add C#dim into the third chorus line and Bbmaj7–Bb/C into the tag.

**Intro**

`F – Fmaj7 – Dm – Am`
`Bb – C – F`

**Verse**

`F – C/E – Dm – F/C`
`Bb – F/A – Gm7 – C`
`F – C/E – Dm – F/C`
`Bb – F/A – G7 – C – F`

**Chorus**

`Dm7 – Am – Bb – C – F`
`Dm7 – Gm – G7/B – C`
`Dm7 – Am – Bb – C – Fmaj7`
`Dm7 – G – G7/B – Bbmaj7 – Bb/C – F`

**Outro**

`Bbmaj7 – Bb/C – F`
`Gm/F – Fmaj7`

Last chorus adds one extra tag (`Bbmaj7 – Bb/C – C – Fmaj7`) before the outro.

**Form**
