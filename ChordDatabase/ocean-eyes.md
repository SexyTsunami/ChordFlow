ocean eyes
Artist: Billie Eilish

Key: E minor (relative G major), 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original synth arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices (often Cmaj7, Dsus2/B, Em, G6). Guitar often uses no capo. Easy charts simplify to `C – D – Em – G`. Some analyses label the relative G major.

**Intro**

`Cmaj7 – D/B – Em`

**Verse**

`Cmaj7 – D/B – Em`
`Cmaj7 – D/B – Em – G – C`

Cadences into “ocean eyes” often walk `G – D/B – Em` or `G – D/B – C`.

**Chorus**

`Cmaj7 – D/B – Em`
`Cmaj7 – D/B – Em – G`
`G – D/B – C`

**Instrumental**

`Cmaj7 – D/B – Em`

**Outro**

`Cmaj7 – D/B – Em`
`G – D/B – C`

The same loop covers verse and chorus; later passes add the G–C tag. Some charts write D/B as Dsus2/B.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Instrumental → Chorus → Outro
