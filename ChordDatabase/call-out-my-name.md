Call Out My Name
Artist: The Weeknd

Key: G-sharp minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original piano-and-pad arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Chord sites agree on G-sharp minor (`G#m7 – D#m7`). Charts often spell the second chord `Ebm7(9)` (same pitches as `D#m7(9)`). Musicnotes PVG editions are published in E minor and a guitar-tab edition in G minor; those are convenient transpositions, not the record. Guitar usually uses capo 4 and plays `Em – Bm`. Easy no-capo E-minor charts sit a major third below concert pitch.

**Intro**

`G#m7 – D#m7(9)`

Piano arpeggios on the two-chord vamp.

**Verse**

`G#m7 – D#m7(9)`

**Pre-Chorus**

`G#m7 – D#m7(9)`

Same vamp; the vocal stacks before the title line.

**Chorus**

`G#m7 – D#m7(9)`

The whole song is this two-chord oscillation.

**Outro**

`G#m7 – D#m7(9)`

Holds the vamp to the fade. A few ukulele charts add a brief `B – F# – C#` walk; the record stays on the two-chord loop.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Outro
