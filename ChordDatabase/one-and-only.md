One And Only
Artist: Adele

Key: F major, 12/8 (gospel shuffle; some charts count it in 6/8). This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original piano part adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar usually plays F shapes with no capo.

`F – Fsus4 – F – C – Csus2 – C`

**Verse**

`F – Fsus4 – F – C – Dm7 – C`
`Gm – Dm – C`

**Pre-Chorus**

`Bb – C – Dm`
`Eb – Bb – F – C`

**Chorus**

`F – Fsus4 – F – Dm – Dm7 – Dm`
`Am – Bb – Am – Gm – C`

**Bridge**

`Eb – Gm – F – Bb`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus
