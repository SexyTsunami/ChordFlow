給我一首歌的時間 (Gei Wo Yi Shou Ge De Shi Jian / Give Me the Time of a Song)
Artist: Jay Chou (周杰倫 / 周杰伦)

Key: C# major, 4/4. Verse sits on the relative minor (Bbm). This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original piano part adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar often uses capo 1 and plays in C.

**Intro**

`C# – G# – Bbm – Fm`
`F# – C# – D#m – G#`

**Verse**

`Bbm – Fm – F# – G#`
`Bbm – Fm – F# – G#`
`Bbm – Fm – F# – G#`
`Bbm – Fm – D#m – G#`

**Chorus** (two matching halves)

First half:
`C# – G# – Bbm – G# – F# – Fm – F# – G#`

Second half:
`C# – G# – Bbm – G# – F# – Fm – F# – G# – C#`

**Bridge**

`B – Bb – D#m – C# – F# – G# – Bbm – C#`
`Fm – F# – Fm – Bbm – F# – G# – A`

**Last chorus**

Same pattern, up a semitone to D:

`D – A – Bm – A – G – F#m – G – A`
then the matching second half ending on **D**

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Bridge → last chorus in D
