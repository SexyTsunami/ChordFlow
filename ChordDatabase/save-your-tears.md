Save Your Tears
Artist: The Weeknd

Key: C major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction of the After Hours studio cut; the original Max Martin synth-and-guitar arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Wikipedia, Musicnotes, and Hooktheory agree on C major. Guitar stays in C with no capo (`C – Am – Em – G`). Easy capo-3 A-shape charts (`A – F#m – C#m – E`) match concert pitch; no-capo A-major charts sit a minor third below. Chordify sometimes tags a live cut as E minor because the verse loop can be heard that way; keep C major for the original.
