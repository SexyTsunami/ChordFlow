東風破 (Dong Feng Po / East Wind Breaks)
Artist: Jay Chou (周杰倫 / 周杰伦)

Key: G major, 4/4. The last chorus lifts a half step to Ab. From *葉惠美*. Classic Chinese-style; Em and Am appear as diatonic chords, but published charts put the concert key in G, not E minor or A minor. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices.

**Intro**

`G`

**Verse**

`G – Em – C – Am – D – G`
`Dm – E – Am – C – D`

**Pre-chorus**

`G – Em – C – Am – D – G`
`Dm – E – Am – C – D – G`

**Chorus** (two matching halves)

First half:
`G – Em – C – G`
`B – Em – C – D`

Second half:
`G – Em – C – G`
`B – Em – C – D – G`

**Interlude**

`Em – C – G`
`B – Em – C – D – G`

**Last chorus**

Same pattern, up a half step to Ab:

`Ab – Fm – Db – Ab`
`C – Fm – Db – Eb`
`Ab – Fm – Db – Ab`
`C – Fm – Db – Eb – Ab`

**Outro**

`Fm – Db – Ab`
`C – Fm – Db – Eb – Ab`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Interlude → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → last chorus in Ab → Outro
