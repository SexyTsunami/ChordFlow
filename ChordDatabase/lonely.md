Lonely
Artist: Justin Bieber, benny blanco

Key: B minor, 4/4. Wikipedia and Musicnotes list B minor at about 80 BPM. This is the common piano reduction; the original electric-piano part adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar can play the barres below, or capo 2 with `F – Em – Am` for the verse. Some guitar FAQs mislabel the capo-2 version as C# minor.

**Intro**

`G – F#m – Bm`

**Verse**

`G – F#m – Bm`

**Pre-Chorus**

`G – A – F#m – G`

**Chorus**

`G – A – F#m – G`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Chorus
