永不失聯的愛 (Yong Bu Shi Lian De Ai / The Love That Never Loses Contact)
Artist: Eric Chou (周興哲 / 周兴哲)

Also written 永不失联的爱. From 如果雨之後. Key: Bb major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar commonly uses G shapes, capo 3. Kanpu and 易譜庫 label concert Bb; Lamucal’s piano chart stays in Bb with extra `F7` and `Ebmaj7` color.

**Intro**

`Eb – F/Eb – Dm7 – Gm7`
`Cm7 – Fsus4 – Bb – F`

**Verse**

`Eb – F/Eb – Dm7`
`Gm7 – Cm7 – Fsus4 – Bb – Bb7`

Second half of the verse repeats the same walk. Denser Bb piano charts spell the first move `Ebmaj7` and walk `Cm – D7 – Dm7`.

**Chorus**

`Eb – F/Eb – Dm7 – Gm`
`Cm7 – Fsus4 – Bb – Bb7`
`Eb – F/Eb – Dm7 – Gm`
`Cm7 – Fsus4 – Bb`

**Interlude**

`Gm/E – Ebm – Dm7 – Gm7`
`Cm7 – F – Ebm – F`

Kanpu’s G-shape middle eight. Last chorus often holds `Bb` and tags `Eb – F – Dm – Gm – Cm – F – Bb`.

**Outro**

`Dm – Gm – Cm – F – Bb`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Interlude → Chorus → Outro
