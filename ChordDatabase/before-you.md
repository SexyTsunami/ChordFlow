Before You
Artist: Benson Boone

2022 single (Walk Me Home... era). Key: B major, 6/8. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Musicnotes publishes B major (PVG, dotted-quarter about 72). Chordify and Lamucal agree on concert `B – F# – G#m – E`. Guitar commonly uses G shapes, capo 4 (`G – D – Em – C`, sounding `B – F# – G#m – E`). E-Chords prints those G shapes without a capo note; that is the same concert pitch, not a G-major record. Tunebat’s G#m tag is the relative minor, not a different tonic.

**Verse**

`B – F# – G#m – E – F#`

**Pre-chorus**

`Em – C#m – E`

Borrowed `Em` on the turn into `C#m – E`. Capo 4 plays `Cm – Am – C`.

**Chorus**

`B – F#/A# – G#m – E – F#`
`B – F#/A# – G#m – E – F#`

Piano charts sometimes print `A#m` where guitar reductions keep `F#/A#`. Capo 4 is `G – D/F# – Em – C – D`.

**Bridge**

`B – F# – G#m – E – F#`

Same rotation as the verse, three times.

**Outro**

`B – F# – G#m – F# – E`

**Form**

Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Bridge → Outro
