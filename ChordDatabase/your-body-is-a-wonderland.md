Your Body Is a Wonderland
Artist: John Mayer

Key: F major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar usually uses capo 3 and plays in D (`D – Asus4 – G – Asus4`). Easy D-major charts with no capo sit a minor third below the record. Musicnotes and Chordify agree on F major; many guitar tabs label the key as D because they print capo shapes, not concert pitch.

**Intro**

`F – Csus4 – Bb – Csus4`

**Verse**

`F – Csus4 – Bb – Csus4`

Later verses often color `Bb` as `Bbadd9`. The guitar figure is the same loop either way.

**Pre-Chorus**

`Bb – Csus4`
`Bb – Csus4`
`Bb – Csus4`
`Gm – Am – Bb`

The last bar walks `Gm – Am – Bb` (“this is bound to be a while”) into the chorus.

**Chorus**

`Csus4 – F – Csus4 – Bb`
`Csus4 – F – Csus4 – Bb`
`Csus4 – F – Csus4 – Bb – Csus4`

**Bridge**

`Dm9`

The “damn baby, you frustrate me” section sits on `Dm9` (capo-3 `Bm9`) before the last chorus.

**Outro**

`Csus4 – F – Csus4 – Bb – Csus4`

Same as the chorus loop, repeating the title line.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus → Outro
