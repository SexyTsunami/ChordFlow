我的歌聲裡 (Wo De Ge Sheng Li / You Exist In My Song)
Artist: Wanting (曲婉婷)

Also written 我的歌声里. Key: F-sharp major, last choruses in A-flat, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar commonly uses C shapes, capo 6; move the capo to 8 for the lift. Chinese sheets (guitarworld, 扣谱网) and bemusic-guitar agree on F# then Ab. Easy C charts without a capo sit a tritone below concert.

**Intro**

`B – C# – F# – C#/F – D#m`
`B – C# – F#`

**Verse**

`F# – C#/F – D#m – D#m7/C#`
`B – F#/A# – G#m – C#`
`F# – C#/F – D#m – D#m7/C#`
`B – F#/A# – G#m – C#`

**Chorus**

`B – C# – F# – C#/F – D#m`
`B – C# – F#`

Second pass often ends `F#7` into the next verse.

**Verse 2** (short)

`F# – C#/F – D#m – D#m7/C#`
`B – F#/A# – G#m – C#`
`F# – C#/F – D#m – D#m7/C#`
`B – C#`

Last line holds `C#` instead of returning through `G#m`.

**Chorus**

`B – C# – F# – C#/F – D#m`
`B – C# – F#`

**Bridge**

`Emaj7 – B – Emaj7 – D#`

Still in F# (capo 6). Then the capo jumps two frets and the chorus returns in Ab.

**Chorus** (Ab)

`Db – Eb – Ab – Eb/G – Fm`
`Db – Eb – Ab`

Final pass often tags `Gbmaj7 – Fm – E – Eb – Ab`.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Verse 2 → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus (Ab)
