你，好不好？ (Ni Hao Bu Hao / Are You Okay)
Artist: Eric Chou (周興哲 / 周兴哲)

Also written 你好不好. TVBS drama 遺憾拼圖 ending theme. Key: Bb major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar commonly uses G shapes, capo 3. Polygon, Chord4, and 易譜庫 agree on concert Bb; easy C-shape charts sit a whole step above concert.

**Intro**

`Bb – F/A – Gm – Dm/F`
`Eb – Bb/D – Cm7 – F`

Canon-style eight-bar loop. Some guitar charts flatten `Dm/F` to `F`.

**Verse**

`Bb – F/A – Gm – Dm/F`
`Eb – Dm7 – Gm – Cm7 – F`

**Chorus**

`Bb – F/A – Gm – Dm/F`
`Eb – Dm7 – Gm – Cm7 – F`

Same eight-bar color as the verse. Last pass often lands on `Bb`.

**Interlude**

`Bb – Dm7 – Dbmaj7 – Ab – F`

**Interlude 2**

`Dbmaj7 – Eb – Dbmaj7 – F`

**Outro**

`Cm7 – F – Cm7 – Ebm – F7 – Bb`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Interlude → Verse → Chorus → Interlude 2 → Chorus → Outro
