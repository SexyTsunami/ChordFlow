What Was I Made For?
Artist: Billie Eilish

Key: C major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original piano part adds extra passing tones and inner voices (F often as Fmaj7). Guitar often uses no capo in C. Piano original.

**Intro**

`C – Em – F`

**Verse**

`C – Em – F`
`C – Em – F`
`C – Em – F`
`Am – C – F`

The “what was I made for?” tag is often `Am – G – F` on later lines.

**Chorus**

`Em – Dm – G`
`C – Em – F`
`C – Em – F`
`Am – G – F`

**Post-Chorus**

`C – Em – F`

**Outro**

`C – Em – F`
`Am – G – F – Em – Dm – G`

Same verse loop under the last section; the final tag walks `Am – G – F` into `Em – Dm – G`.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Post-Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Outro
