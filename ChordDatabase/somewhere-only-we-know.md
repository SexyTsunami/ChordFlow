Somewhere Only We Know
Artist: Keane

Key: A major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction of the Hopes and Fears studio cut; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Hooktheory and Musicnotes-matching charts agree on A major (felt around 86; some guitar pages print 172 as double-time). Guitar usually uses capo 2 and plays in G (`G – G/F# – Am – D`, concert `A – A/G# – Bm – E`). No-capo charts write `A – Amaj7 – Bm – Esus4 – E`; `Amaj7` and `A/G#` are the same piano color.

**Intro**

`A – Amaj7 – Bm – Esus4 – E`

**Verse**

`A – Amaj7 – Bm – Esus4 – E`

**Pre-Chorus**

`F#m – C#m – D – E`

Capo-2 charts write `Em – Bm – C – D`. Some guitar lessons spell the second chord `Gmaj7/B` in capo-2 shapes (concert `Amaj7/C#`).

**Chorus**

`Bm – A/C# – E`
`Bm – A/C# – E`
`Bm – A/C# – E`
`D – E – A`

The last title tag often repeats `D – E` before returning to `A`. Beginner capo-2 charts flatten this to `C – G – D`.

**Outro**

`Bm – A/C# – E`
`D – E – A`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Outro
