LOVE SCENARIO
Artist: iKON (아이콘)

Also written 사랑을 했다 (Sarang-eul Haetda). Key: C major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction of the Return studio cut / official MV; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. No capo. ChordLines, Gitagram, and matching Korean guitar charts loop `C – D – Bm – Em`. Chordify labels B minor and starts the same four chords on `D`; keep C as the guitar reduction tonic. Some verse reprints add a passing `G`.
