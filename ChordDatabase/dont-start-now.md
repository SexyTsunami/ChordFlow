Don't Start Now
Artist: Dua Lipa

Key: B minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original disco arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Musicnotes, Wikipedia, and Hooktheory agree on B minor. Chordify and some Ultimate Guitar headers label D major because they read the relative major; keep B minor. Guitar usually uses capo 2 and plays `Dm – Am – Fmaj7 – C – G` (sounding `Em – Bm – G – D – A`). Easy no-capo A-minor charts sit a whole step below the record. Three-chord `Bm – F#m – G – D` sheets drop the `Em` start and the `A` turnaround.

**Intro**

`Bm – D – Em`

Piano fragment of the pre-chorus, cut short before the verse bass enters.

**Verse**

`Em – Bm – G – D – A`

Four bars of `Em – Bm – G – D`, then `A` as a half-bar pivot back to `Em`. Sevenths (`Em7`, `Bm7`) match the bass more closely than plain triads.

**Pre-Chorus**

`Bm – D – Em7 – F#m7 – Gmaj7`

The piano climb. Capo-2 guitar is `Am – C – Dm – Em7 – Fmaj7`.

**Chorus**

`Em – Bm – G – D – A`

Same loop as the verse; strings and cowbell sit on top. The last chorus switches to the pre-chorus walk `Bm – D – Em7 – F#m7 – Gmaj7`.

**Bridge**

`Em – Bm – G – D – A`

Breakdown over the verse colors, then the pre-chorus piano returns.

**Outro**

`Bm – D – Em7 – F#m7 – Gmaj7`

Final-chorus walk repeats to the end.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Bridge → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Outro
