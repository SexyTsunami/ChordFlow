夜曲 (Ye Qu / Nocturne)
Artist: Jay Chou (周杰倫 / 周杰伦)

Key: F minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original piano part adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar charts often swap the inner chords to Fm – Cm – Db – Ab – Bbm – C; a simplified C#m – A – E – B loop circulates online but is not the original key.

**Intro** (twice)

`Fm – Eb – Db – Cm – Bbm – C – Fm`

**Verse**

`Fm – Eb – Db – Cm – Bbm – C – Fm`

**Pre-chorus**

`Fm – Eb – Db – Cm – Bbm – C – Fm`

**Chorus** (two matching halves)

First half:
`Fm – Eb – Db – Cm – Bbm – C – Fm`

Second half:
`Fm – Eb – Db – Cm – Bbm – C – Fm`

**Interlude**

`Fm – Eb – Db – Cm – Bbm – C – Fm`

**Outro**

Same loop as the verse.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Interlude → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Outro
