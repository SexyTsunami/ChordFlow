rockstar
Artist: Post Malone, 21 Savage

Key: G minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar usually uses capo 3 and plays `Em – C` (two bars each), which matches concert `Gm – Eb`. Chordify and original-key charts color `Eb` as `Ebmaj7` and sometimes fill with `Bb` or `Cm`. Streaming-key databases that list F minor sit a half step below the record. Easy no-capo E-minor charts are a minor third low.

**Intro**

`Gm`

Spoken count-in over a G-minor pedal, then the loop starts.

**Chorus**

`Gm – Ebmaj7`

Two bars of each. The guitar reduction is the same two chords through the hook.

**Verse**

`Gm – Ebmaj7`

Same two-bar vamp as the chorus. 21 Savage’s verse sometimes walks `Bb` or a passing `A` against the `Gm – Ebmaj7` pad; keep those as inner-voice extras, not a new progression.

**Outro**

`Gm – Ebmaj7`

The vamp holds to the last “rockstar” hits.

**Form**

Intro → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Outro
