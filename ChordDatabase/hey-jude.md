Hey Jude
Artist: The Beatles

Key: F major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction of the 1968 single; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Hooktheory and Alan W. Pollack agree on F major (coda in F Mixolydian) around 75 BPM. Guitar stays in F with no capo. Ultimate Guitar’s official-style charts use `F – C – C7 – F – Bb`; the na-na coda is `F – Eb – Bb – F`. Easy C-shape charts sit a fourth below concert.

**Verse**

`F – C – C7 – F`
`Bb – F – C7 – F`

**Verse 2**

`F – C – C7 – F`
`Bb – F – C7 – F`

**Bridge**

`F7 – Bb – F – C – F`
`F7 – Bb – F – C – F`

The `F7` is the only new color: it pivots into `Bb`. Pollack notes the rest of the first half stays diatonic F.

**Verse 3**

`F – C – C7 – F`
`Bb – F – C7 – F`

**Bridge**

`F7 – Bb – F – C – F`
`F7 – Bb – F – C – F`

**Verse 4**

`F – C – C7 – F`
`Bb – F – C7 – F`

Last verse often hits `F7` straight into the coda.

**Outro**

`F – Eb – Bb – F`

The na-na refrain. Repeats through the fade; `Eb` is the Mixolydian color.

**Form**

Verse → Verse 2 → Bridge → Verse 3 → Bridge → Verse 4 → Outro
