You're Welcome
Artist: Dwayne Johnson, Disney

Moana (Lin-Manuel Miranda). Key: C major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction of the soundtrack cut; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Musicnotes and GuitarTuna’s original-key edition agree on C. The verse’s island groove is `C – F – Bb`; the chorus is `Am – F – C – E7`. Easy sheets that stay on `C – F – G` miss the borrowed `Bb` and the `E7` turn. No capo.

**Intro**

`C`

Spoken “okay, okay,” then the verse groove.

**Verse**

`C – F – Bb – C`

Denser charts use pedal voicings `C – F/C – Bb/C`. The “staring at a demigod” pickup is often `Bb – Am` into the chorus.

**Chorus**

`Am – F – C – E7`
`Am – F – C – E7`

“You’re welcome” lands on `C`; `E7` is the turn back to `Am` (or on to the next verse). Beginner charts print `E` for `E7`.

**Verse 2**

`C – F – C – G`
`Am – F – C – G`

List-of-feats verse. Some reductions keep the first-verse `Bb` instead of `G`; the soundtrack guitar is closer to `F – C – G` on these lines.

**Bridge**

`C`
`Am – F – C – E7`

Rap-sung hold on `C`, then `Am – F – C – E7` for the tapestry lines into the last chorus.

**Outro**

`Am – F – C – E7`
`F – C`

Stacked “you’re welcome” tags; last hits often stay on `C`.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Verse 2 → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus → Outro
