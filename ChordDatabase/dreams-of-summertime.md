Dreams Of Summertime
Artist: GoldFord

Key: D minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction of the 2021 studio cut; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Ultimate Guitar, Akkordov, and Chordify agree on D minor (Tunebat and 12NOTEZ match, about 134–136 BPM). Verse and chorus loop `Dm – C – Gm`; the bridge adds `Bb`. No capo.

**Intro**

`Dm`

**Verse**

`Dm – C – Gm`

**Chorus**

`Dm – C – Gm – Dm`
`C – Gm – Dm`

The title line often hits `C – Gm – Dm`. Later repeats break to N.C. on the same changes.

**Bridge**

`Bb – Gm – Dm`
`C – Bb – Gm – Dm`

**Outro**

`Dm – C – Gm – Dm`
`C – Gm – Dm`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus
