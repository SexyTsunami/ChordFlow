All I Want
Artist: Kodaline

Key: C major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction of the In a Perfect World studio cut; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Musicnotes and Singing Carrots agree on C major. Some guitar tabs tune a half step down and still print C shapes (sounding B); keep concert C. No capo in standard tuning. Beginner `C – G – Am – F` sheets flatten the verse’s `F` and `G/B` motion into one loop.

**Intro**

`C`

Held piano/guitar C; later hits sometimes preview `C – F – C`.

**Verse**

`C – F – C`
`C – G/B – Am`
`F – C`
`F – C`

The `G/B` (some charts `Em/B` or plain `G`) is the walk into `Am`. The last verse line often lands `F – C – G/B` as a pickup into the chorus.

**Chorus**

`Am – F – C – G`
`Am – F – C – G`

The “like you” tag is `C – F – C`, then a short `C – G – Am` vocalise before the next verse.

**Bridge**

`Dm – G`
`Dm – G`

Held `G` into the stacked “oh” chorus. The following lift is the same `Am – F – C – G` loop as the chorus, often treated as a solo.

**Outro**

`Am – F – C – G`
`Am – F – C – G – C`

Last “like you” settles on `C`.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus → Outro
