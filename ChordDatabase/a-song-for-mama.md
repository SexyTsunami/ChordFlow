A Song for Mama
Artist: Boyz II Men

From Evolution (1997) and the Soul Food soundtrack. Key: G major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Musicnotes PVG (MN0026867) publishes G as the original key at about 76 BPM; Chordify and Lamucal agree on concert G with `G – D – C`. Guitar stays in G with no capo, or uses E-shapes, capo 3 (`E – B – A`, sounding `G – D – C`). Keep the borrowed `F` into the chorus and the chorus `B7 – Em – Dm7 – Cmaj7` color; easy `G – D – C` loops skip both.
