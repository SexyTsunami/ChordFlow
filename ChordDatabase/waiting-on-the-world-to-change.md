Waiting On the World to Change
Artist: John Mayer

Key: D major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar stays in D with no capo; Mayer plays these as Hendrix-style thumb chords (`D`, `Bm`, `G`, `A`). Chordify and lesson charts agree on D major. Some tabs write `Bm7` for `Bm`; both are the same loop.

**Intro**

`D – Bm – G – D`
`A – Bm – G – D`

**Verse**

`D – Bm – G – D`
`A – Bm – G – D`

The whole verse rides this two-line vamp.

**Chorus**

`D – Bm – G – D`
`A – Bm – G – D`
`D – Em7 – Bm – Em7`
`A – Bm – G – D`

The title line stays on the verse loop. The lift (“it’s hard to beat the system”) is `D – Em7 – Bm – Em7`, then the `A – Bm – G – D` turnaround. Mayer’s guitar sometimes colors the `G` as `G6` (no 3rd) on later choruses.

**Solo**

`D – Bm – G – D`
`A – Bm – G – D`

Same as the verse. The recorded solo is mostly a one-string blues line over this groove.

**Outro**

`A – Bm – G – D`
`G – D`

Later choruses stack extra `A – Bm – G – D` hits, then tag `G – D` on “waiting on the world to change.”

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Solo → Chorus → Outro
