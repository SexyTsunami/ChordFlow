Obsessed
Artist: Mariah Carey

Key: C minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Musicnotes PVG publishes C minor as the original key (about 84 BPM). Ultimate Guitar, E-Chords, and Chordify track the same concert loop from `Ab` and `Cm`. Guitar usually plays the Ab/Cm shapes with no capo, or capo 1 and plays G/Bm shapes (`G – Bm`, sounding `Ab – Cm`). Charts that tag “G#” are naming the first chord, not the tonic — keep C minor.

**Intro**

`Ab – Cm – Ab – Cm`
`Bb – Fm – Eb – G`

Some sheets print `G#` for `Ab`. The opening hook sits on `Ab – Cm`.

**Verse**

`Ab – Cm`
`Ab – Cm – Bb`
`Ab – Cm`
`Fm – Eb – G`

**Chorus**

`Ab – Cm – Ab – Cm – Bb – Ab`
`Ab – Cm`
`Fm – Eb – G`

Easy charts stay on `Ab – Cm` and treat `Fm – Eb – G` as the turnaround only.

**Bridge**

`Ab – Cm`
`Ab – Cm – Bb`
`Ab – Cm`
`Fm – Eb – G`

Same loop as the verse; the last hit runs into the chorus on `G`.

**Outro**

`Ab – Cm – Ab – Cm`
`Bb – Fm – Eb – G – Ab`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus → Outro
