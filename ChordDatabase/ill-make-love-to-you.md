I'll Make Love to You
Artist: Boyz II Men

From II (1994). Key: D major, 12/8. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Musicnotes PVG (MN0053490) publishes D as the original key; the piano/vocal/chords edition (MN0140462) is the same concert D in 12/8 at about 48–52 BPM (the PVG also prints 6/8 for the same slow two-feel). GuitarSongs, chords-and-tabs, and the widely copied AJ guitar chart agree on concert `D – Bm7 – Em7`. Musicnotes easy guitar TAB in C is a whole-step singer transpose, not the record. Guitar stays in D with no capo, or uses C-shapes, capo 2 (`C – Am – Dm – G/A`, sounding `D – Bm – Em – A`). Denser guitar keeps a `Dsus2` shape and walks the bass (`D/G – D/F#`); R&B sheets write that same walk as `A/G – F#m7`. Keep chorus `C` before `G/A`.
