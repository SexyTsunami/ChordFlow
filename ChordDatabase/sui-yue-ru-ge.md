歲月如歌 (Sui Yue Ru Ge / The Years Like a Song)
Artist: Eason Chan (陳奕迅 / 陈奕迅)

Also written 岁月如歌. Cantonese hit from 七; TVB 金枝欲孽 theme. Key: Eb major, last chorus in E, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar commonly uses C shapes, capo 3; last chorus is a half-step lift (move the capo to 4, or play D shapes). Guitarworld and yipuku mark Eb → E. Chord4’s denser piano reduction labels the home key Ab because the intro sits on Ab (IV); the verse and chorus cadence in Eb.
