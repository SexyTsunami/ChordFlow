Lonely Boy
Artist: The Black Keys

Key: E Mixolydian, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar stays in E with no capo. Musicnotes lists E minor; guitar charts (E-Chords, chords.vip) write `E` or `E5` with `G – A`, not Em. Tunebat tags D major and TempoPeek/Klangio tag A because of the G and A chords — keep the riff’s E tonic.

**Intro**

`E – G – A`

Single-note guitar riff over E; some charts mark the pickup as `E – A` before the full three-chord loop.

**Verse**

`E – G – A`

**Chorus**

`E5 – G – A`

Same loop as the verse. Chorus charts often swap `E` for power-chord `E5`.

**Instrumental**

`E – G – A`

Same three chords under the post-chorus guitar riff.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Instrumental → Chorus
