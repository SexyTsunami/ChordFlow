Daughters
Artist: John Mayer

Key: D major, 6/8. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Felt in 2 (Musicnotes marks “slowly, in 2”); some guitar charts write 4/4 with a swing, and Hooktheory counts 3/4. Guitar usually uses capo 2 and plays `Am7 – D7sus2/A – G7sus4 – C` (sounding `Bm7 – E7sus2 – A7sus4 – D`). Easy no-capo C-shape charts sit a whole step below the record. Musicnotes piano/vocal/guitar and guitar-tab editions agree on D major; the easy-piano edition is labeled B minor because the loop starts there.

**Intro**

`Bm7 – E7sus2 – A7sus4 – D`

Twice. Same four-chord guitar figure as the verse.

**Verse**

`Bm7 – E7sus2 – A7sus4 – D`

Auto-charts often simplify to `Bm – Em – G – D`. The record’s guitar is the sus voicings above.

**Chorus**

`Bm – E – A – D`

Same centers as the verse, with an E-major lift on the second chord (lead guitar moves that voice up). Capo-2 charts that keep `Am7 – D7sus2 – G7sus4 – C` still sound this loop; the D-shape at capo 2 is the E.

**Bridge**

`Am – Gm – D`
`Dmaj7 – Bm`
`Em – F#m`
`Gm – A`

Capo-2 guitar writes `Gm/D – Fm/D – C`, then `Cmaj7 – Am7`, `Dm`, `Em`, and a walk back through `Fm/D` into `G` (sounding the line above). Simplified no-capo charts stay on `Am – Gm – D`.

**Outro**

`Bm – E – A – D`

Final choruses, last line repeated. A tag verse after the bridge is another pass of the chorus loop before the last repeats.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Outro
