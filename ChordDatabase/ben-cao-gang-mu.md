本草綱目 (Ben Cao Gang Mu / Compendium of Materia Medica)
Artist: Jay Chou (周杰倫 / 周杰伦)

Chinese-style hip-hop from 依然范特西. Key: Eb minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar world and 易譜庫 label the track Gb (relative major) and often use G shapes with half-step-down tuning; Chord4 and Guitarians treat the groove as an Eb-minor pentatonic vamp. Simplified charts stay on Ebm; denser G-shape charts add passing Eb, Db, Ab, and Gb. The last dance hook lifts a half step.
