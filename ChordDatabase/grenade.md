Grenade
Artist: Bruno Mars

Key: D minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices.

**Intro**

`Dm – Bb – F – C`

**Verse**

`Dm – Am`

Last line of some verses adds `A7` (or a held A) before the pre-chorus.

**Pre-Chorus**

`Dm – Am – Dm – Bb – A`

**Chorus**

`Dm – Bb – F – C`
`Dm – Bb – F – C`
`Dm – Bb – F – C`
`Bb – C – F – A – Dm – C – Bb – A`

The first four lines are the `Dm – Bb – F – C` loop; the last two lines break out to `Bb – C – F – A` then `Dm – C – Bb – A`.

**Bridge**

`Gm – Dm – Gm – A`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus → Outro
