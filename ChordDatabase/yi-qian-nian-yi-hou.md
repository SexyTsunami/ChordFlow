一千年以後 (Yi Qian Nian Yi Hou / A Thousand Years Later)
Artist: JJ Lin (林俊傑 / 林俊杰)

Also written 一千年以后. Key: D major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar commonly uses C shapes, capo 2. Simplified charts stay on D – A – Bm – A through the verse.

**Intro / Verse**

`D – A/C# – G – A`
`D – A/C# – G – A`

Some charts use D – A – Bm – A, or D – A – Am7 – A on the first line. A few put F#m on the second-line second chord.

**Pre-chorus**

`Bm – F#m – G – D – A/C#`
`Bm – F#m – G – Em – Em/D – Asus4`

Some charts hold G – A on the last bar instead of the Em walk.

**Chorus** (two matching halves)

First half:
`D – A/C# – Bm – Bm/A`
`G – D/F# – Bm – Em7 – A`

Second half:
`D – A/C# – Bm – Bm/A`
`G – D/F# – Bm – Em7 – G – Gm – D`

Simplified charts use D – A – Bm – A / G – F#m – G – A, and end the second half G – F#m – Em – Cm – D.

**Form**

Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Pre-chorus → Chorus
