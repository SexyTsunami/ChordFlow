Don't Stop 'Til You Get Enough
Artist: Michael Jackson

Key: B major (B Mixolydian), 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original funk-guitar and string arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar often uses capo 2 and plays in A (`A – G/A`, or `A11 – A`), or capo 4 and plays in G. Musicnotes piano/vocal editions publish the song in B major. The groove is `A` against a B bass (`A/B` / `B11`) flipping to `B`.

**Intro**

`A/B – B`

Spoken intro over `B` with A-major color, then the verse vamp: `B – B11` (same as `B – A/B`).

**Verse**

`B – B11`
`B – Bsus2`

Write it as `A/B – B` if you prefer slash chords. The two bars repeat through the verse.

**Chorus**

`B11 – A/B`
`B`

Four-line hook: odd lines sit on `B11` (with `A6/B` / `A/B` color); even lines land on `B`.

**Bridge** (instrumental)

`B – D/E – F#11 – G#m7 – A – B`

Three times, then `B – D/E – F#11 – G#m7 – N.C.` back into the vamp. Some charts write `Bm/E` and `E/F#` for `D/E` and `F#11`.

**Outro**

`B11 – A/B`
`B`

Chorus vamp to the fade.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Bridge → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Outro
