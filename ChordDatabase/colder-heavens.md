Colder Heavens
Artist: Blanco White

Colder Heavens EP studio cut, not the later acoustic single. Key: F minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Chordify and Snaplyrics (Camelot 4A) agree on F minor. Guitar usually uses capo 1 and plays in E minor (`Em – Bm – A`, sounding `Fm – Cm – Bb`). chords-and-tabs.net’s capo-8 Am-shape chart is the same concert (`Am – Em – D` = `Fm – Cm – Bb`). KhmerChords and LosAcordes print those Em shapes and label the song Em — treat that as the capo-1 edition, not concert pitch. Acoustic-version detectors sometimes tag C minor.
