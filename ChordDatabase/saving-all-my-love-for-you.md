Saving All My Love for You
Artist: Whitney Houston

Key: A major, 12/8 (slow R&B; some charts count it in 4/4 at about 84 BPM). Wikipedia and Musicnotes agree on A major. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar usually plays A-shape chords with no capo, or capo 2 and plays in G. Charts that label C-sharp minor are reading the relative minor, not the published tonic.

**Intro**

`Amaj7 – F#m7 – Bm7 – E11`

**Verse**

`Amaj7 – F#m7 – Bm7 – E11`
`F#m7 – B7 – F#m7 – B7`
`A – E/G# – F#m7 – A/E – G#m7 – C#7`
`Dmaj7 – C#m7 – Bm7 – E11`

Later verses repeat the last line as a tag: `Dmaj7 – C#m7 – Bm7`.

**Bridge**

`G#m7 – C#7 – F#m7`
`Bm7 – E11 – A – Amaj7`
`D#m7 – G#7 – C# – C#maj7`
`F#m7 – B9 – Bm7 – E11 – E`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Verse → Bridge → Verse (extended tag) → Verse / outro vamp
