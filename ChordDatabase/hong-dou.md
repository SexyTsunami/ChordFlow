紅豆 (Hong Dou / Red Bean)
Artist: Khalil Fong (方大同)

Also written 红豆. Khalil Fong’s Timeless arrangement of the 柳重言 / Faye Wong song, not the C-major Faye Wong cut. Key: F# major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar usually uses capo 6 and plays in C (`Cmaj7 – Am7 – Fmaj7 – Em7 – Dm7 – G`). kanpu and Chords Haven agree on F#. Faye Wong charts in open C sit a tritone below this cover.

**Intro**

`F#maj7 – D#m7 – Bmaj7 – A#m7 – G#m7 – C#`

**Verse**

`F#maj7 – D#m7`
`Bmaj7 – A#m7 – G#m7 – C#`

**Chorus**

`F#maj7 – C#/F – D#m7 – D#m7/C#`
`Bmaj7 – A#m7 – G#m7 – C#`
`F#maj7 – C#/F – D#m7 – C#m7 – F#7`
`Bmaj7 – A#m7 – G#m7 – C# – F#maj7`

Capo-6 `G/B` is concert `C#/F` (some spell `C#/E#`). Denser charts keep `maj7` colors; some reductions flatten the first two bars to `F# – C#/F – D#m7`.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus
