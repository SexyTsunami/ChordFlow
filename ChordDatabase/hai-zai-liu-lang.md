還在流浪 (Hai Zai Liu Lang / Still Wandering)
Artist: Jay Chou (周杰倫 / 周杰伦)

Key: Db major (C#), 6/8, with a 2/4 bar at the end of the intro/outro loop. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar often uses capo 1 and plays in C.

**Intro**

`Db – Fm7 – Gb – F#m`

**Verse**

`Db – Fm7 – Gb – F#m`

(same loop through both opening stanzas)

**Pre-chorus**

`Ebm – Bbm – Gb – Gb/Ab – Ab`

**Chorus** (two matching halves)

`Db – Bbm – Gb – F#m – Gb/Ab – Ab`

**Interlude**

Short turnaround after the first chorus:

`Db – Fm7/C – Gb – F#m`

Later bridge-like interlude:

`Bbm – Db/Ab – Gb – Ebm – Fm7 – F#m – Gb/Ab`

**Outro**

`Db – Fm7 – Gb – F#m`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Interlude → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Interlude → Chorus → Outro
