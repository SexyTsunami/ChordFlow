退後 (Tui Hou / Step Back)
Artist: Jay Chou (周杰倫 / 周杰伦)

Key: C major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices.

**Intro**

`C – G – Am – G – F – C – F – G – C – Fm – C – Fm`

**Verse 1**

`C – Em – Am – Em`
`F – G – Em – Am – F – G`

**Verse 2**

Same as verse 1, except the last line uses D:

`C – Em – Am – Em`
`F – G – Em – Am – D – G`

**Chorus** (two matching halves)

First half:
`C – Am – Dm – G`

Second half:
`C – Am – D – F – G – C`

**Interlude**

`Am – F – G`

**Outro**

`Am – F – G – C`

**Form**

Intro → Verse 1 → Verse 2 → Chorus → Interlude → Verse 1 → Verse 2 → Chorus → Chorus → Outro
