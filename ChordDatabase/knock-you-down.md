Knock You Down
Artist: Keri Hilson, Kanye West, Ne-Yo

Key: B-flat major, 4/4. Ultimate Guitar and Chordify agree on B-flat major; Hooktheory and Songmode list the same tonic around 153–155 BPM. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar often uses capo 3 and plays `C – D – G – Em` (sounding `Eb – F – Bb – Gm`). No-capo charts stay in B-flat. Chordify’s 79 BPM listing is the half-time count of the same groove.

**Intro**

Spoken pickup, then the verse loop:

`Eb – F – Bb`
`Eb – F – Gm`

**Verse**

`Eb – F – Bb`
`Eb – F – Gm`

Keri Hilson’s verses, Kanye West’s rap, and Ne-Yo’s verse share this eight-bar loop.

**Chorus**

`Eb – F – Bb`
`Eb – F – Gm`

Same harmony as the verse. The “sometimes love comes around” hook does not change the reduction.

**Outro**

`Eb – F – Bb`
`Eb – F – Gm`

The “won’t see it coming” tag holds the loop to the end.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Outro
