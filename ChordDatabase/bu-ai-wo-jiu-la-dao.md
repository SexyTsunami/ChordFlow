不愛我就拉倒 (Bu Ai Wo Jiu La Dao / If You Don't Love Me, It's Fine)
Artist: Jay Chou (周杰倫 / 周杰伦)

Key: D major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar often uses capo 2 and plays in C.

**Intro** (twice)

`Bm – G – D – A`

**Verse**

`Bm – G – D – A`
`Bm – G – D – A`

**Pre-chorus**

`Bm – Bm/A – G#m7b5 – G – F#`
`Bm – Bm/A – G – A`

**Chorus** (two matching halves)

First half:
`D – A – G – Gm`

Second half:
`D – A – G – Gm`

**Bridge** (sung over the chorus loop)

`D – A – G – Gm`
`D – A – G – Gm`

**Interlude**

`Bm`

**Outro**

`D – A – G – Gm – D`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Bridge → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Interlude → Chorus → Outro
