Beat It
Artist: Michael Jackson

Key: E-flat minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original riff-and-horn arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar often uses capo 6 and plays in A minor (`Am – G – F – G`), or capo 1 and plays in D minor (`Dm – C – Bb – C`). Easy E-minor / D / C charts with no capo are a half step above the record. The Eddie Van Halen solo sits over the same verse loop.

**Intro**

`Ebm – Db`

Repeat. Some charts write power chords `Eb5 – Db5`.

**Verse**

`Ebm – Db`
`Ebm – Db`
`B – Db – Ebm – Db`

**Chorus**

`Ebm – Db`

The chorus riff loops `Ebm – Db`. A few reductions keep the verse’s `B – Db` insert in the middle of the hook; most of the chant stays on the two-chord riff.

**Guitar solo**

`Ebm – Db`
`Ebm – Db`
`B – Db – Ebm – Db`

Same loop as the verse. Repeat.

**Outro**

`Ebm – Db`

Often a stretch of `Eb5` before the last riff loops.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Guitar solo → Chorus → Outro
