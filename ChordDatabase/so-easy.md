So Easy (To Fall In Love)
Artist: Olivia Dean

Key: Eb major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction of The Art of Loving studio cut; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Musicnotes and Hooktheory agree on Eb major (Hooktheory hears the chorus in the relative C minor) at about 140 BPM. Cifra Club’s concert chart is the verse `Bb7sus4 – Ebmaj7 – Em7b5` and a chorus built on `Fm7 – Bb7 – Cm7`. Guitar usually uses capo 3. song2chord lists Eb and the diatonic set `Eb – Fm – Gm – Ab – Bb – Cm`.

**Intro**

`Fm7 – Gm7`

**Verse**

`Bb7sus4 – Ebmaj7 – Em7b5`

Acoustic charts often spell `Bb7sus4` as `Ab/Bb`.

**Chorus**

`Em7b5 – Fm7 – Bb7 – Cm7`
`C7 – Fm7 – Bb7 – Cm7`
`Cm7 – Adim – Fm7 – Gm7`

The `C7` is the turnaround into `Fm7`; `Adim` colors the “anyone with a heart” bar before the title tag. Beginner capo-3 sheets flatten this toward `Dm7 – G7 – Am7`.

**Bridge**

`Bb7sus4 – Ebmaj7 – Em7b5`

Same as the verse.

**Outro**

`Fm7 – Bb7 – Cm7`
`C7 – Fm7 – Bb7 – Cm7`
`Cm7 – Adim – Fm7 – Gm7`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus → Outro
