Wherever You Will Go
Artist: The Calling

Key: D major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction of the Camino Palmero studio cut; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Musicnotes’ original published key is D major. Guitar usually uses capo 2 and plays `C – G – Am – F` (concert `D – A – Bm – G`). No-capo D-shape charts and Chordie agree. Easy C-major charts with no capo sit a whole step below the record.

**Intro**

`D – A – Bm – G`

Capo-2 charts often color these as `C – Gadd4/B – Am7 – F`.

**Verse**

`D – A – Bm – G`

**Chorus**

`D – A – Bm – G`

**Bridge**

`Bm – G – A`
`F#m – Bm`
`F – G – Em`

The `F` is borrowed; capo-2 charts write `Am – F – G`, `Em – Am`, `F – G – Em`.

**Outro**

`D – A – Bm – G – D`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Bridge → Verse → Chorus → Outro
