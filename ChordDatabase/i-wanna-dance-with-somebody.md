I Wanna Dance with Somebody (Who Loves Me)
Artist: Whitney Houston

Key: G-flat major (F-sharp major), 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original synth-horn arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar often uses capo 2 and plays in E, or capo 6 and plays in C. Easy G-major charts are a half step above the record (or G shapes with the guitar tuned down a semitone). The last choruses lift a whole step to A-flat.
