Shake It Off
Artist: Mariah Carey

Key: D major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction of Mariah’s Emancipation of Mimi (2005) studio cut — kept in the 2008 E=MC²-era live set — not Taylor Swift’s 2014 song; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Musicnotes PVG publishes D major (slowly, about 66 BPM; some sheets mark 2/2). Wikipedia cites the same key and the `Bm7 – Am7 – Gmaj7` loop. Ultimate Guitar and Chordify often display G-shape charts; those are a transposition — concert is D. Guitar plays `Bm7 – Am7 – Gmaj7` with no capo.

**Intro**

`Bm7 – Am7 – Gmaj7`

**Verse**

`Bm7 – Am7 – Gmaj7`
`Bm7 – Am7 – Gmaj7`

Some sheets write `B7` on the first title pickup; treat that as a passing dominant into `Am7`.

**Chorus**

`Bm7 – Am7 – Gmaj7`
`Bm7 – Am7 – Gmaj7`

The stacked title hook stays on this three-chord vamp. Easy sheets drop the 7ths to `Bm – Am – G`.

**Bridge**

`Bm7 – Am7 – Gmaj7`

Phone-call breakdown uses the same loop; last hit runs back into the chorus on `Gmaj7`.

**Outro**

`Bm7 – Am7 – Gmaj7`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus → Outro
