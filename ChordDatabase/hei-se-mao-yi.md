黑色毛衣 (Hei Se Mao Yi / Black Sweater)
Artist: Jay Chou (周杰倫 / 周杰伦)

Key: A minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices.

**Intro** (twice, then Am)

`Am – C – F – E`

**Verse**

`Am – Dm – E – Am`
`F – Bb – E`
`Am – Dm – E – Am`
`F – E – Am – A`

**Pre-chorus**

`Dm – G – C`
`Bb – Eb – E`

**Chorus** (two matching halves)

First half:
`Am – Dm – G – C – Am – D/F# – Fm – G`

Second half:
`Am – Dm – G – C – Am – D/F# – F – G`

**Interlude**

`Am – Dm – G – C`
`Am – D/F# – F – G`

**Outro**

`C – Fm – C`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Interlude → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Chorus → Outro
