愛愛愛 (Ai Ai Ai / Love Love Love)
Artist: Khalil Fong (方大同)

Also written 爱爱爱. Key: A major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar commonly uses G shapes, capo 2. Chord4, kanpu, and Polygon agree on A. Chordify’s loop of `C#m7 – Bm7 – E – A` is the chorus skeleton only. Easy G-shape charts drop the `Fmaj7 – Ebm7` side of the verse.

**Intro**

`Amaj7 – Em7 – A7 – Dmaj7 – C#m7 – Bm7`

**Verse**

`Amaj7 – Em7 – A7 – Dmaj7 – C#m7 – Bm7`
`Amaj7 – Em7 – A7 – Dmaj7 – C#m7 – Bm7`
`Fmaj7 – Em7 – Ebm7 – Dm7 – Bm7 – E`

**Chorus**

`Amaj7 – E/G# – F#m – Em7 – A7`
`Dmaj7 – C#m7 – Bm7 – E`
`Amaj7 – E/G# – Em7 – F# – F – E – Amaj7`

**Interlude**

`Bm7 – D – E`

**Bridge**

`A7 – Dmaj7 – Em – Dmaj7`
`Dm – C#m – F# – Bm7 – E`

**Outro**

`Amaj7 – Dm – Amaj7`
`Bm7 – C#m7 – Dmaj7 – E – Amaj7`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Interlude → Verse → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus → Outro
