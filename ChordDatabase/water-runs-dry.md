Water Runs Dry
Artist: Boyz II Men

From II (1994). Key: C major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Musicnotes PVG (MN0082598) publishes C as the original key at about 90 BPM; Tunebat agrees on C. Chordify’s G tag is the dominant — keep C as the tonic. ChordZone, GuitarTabsExplorer, and the widely copied Ultimate Guitar chart share verse `C – Am7 – G` with a B bass on the second chord: piano sheets write `Cmaj7`, guitar pages often print `Bm(no5)` (`x2xx30`) for that same /B color. No capo. Pre-chorus keeps the descending `Am – Am/G – Am/F#` bass.
