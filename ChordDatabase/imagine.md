Imagine
Artist: John Lennon

Key: C major, 4/4. This is the common piano/guitar reduction of the 1971 studio cut; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Musicnotes and standard guitar/piano charts agree on C major with no capo. The verse’s `Cmaj7` is the signature inner-voice drop; beginner sheets that stay on `C – F` lose that color.

**Intro**

`C – Cmaj7 – F`

**Verse**

`C – Cmaj7 – F`

Four times through the couplet.

**Pre-Chorus**

`F – Am/E – Dm7 – Dm7/C`
`G – C/G – G7`

“Imagine all the people.” Some reductions spell `Dm7/C` as `F/C`. The last `G7` is the turn back to `C`.

**Chorus**

`F – G – C – Cmaj7 – E – E7`
`F – G – C – Cmaj7 – E – E7`
`F – G – C – Cmaj7 – E – E7`
`F – G – C`

The `E – E7` is borrowed E major into the next `F`. The last line of the first chorus often cadences on `C` without the `E`.

**Outro**

`F – G – C`

Final chorus lands “the world will live as one” on `C`.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus
