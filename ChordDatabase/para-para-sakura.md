Para Para Sakura
Artist: Aaron Kwok (郭富城), Simon Li (李燦森)

Theme from the 2001 movie 浪漫樱花 (Para Para Sakura). Key: B minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar commonly uses Am shapes, capo 2, or plays Bm open. Chord4 prints concert `Bm – A – G`; 虫虫吉他 labels B. Some C-shape capo-2 charts call the pitch D because that is the relative major.

`G – A – Bm`

`G – A – Bm`

The four-bar `G – A – Bm` chant repeats; the “come and dance with me” tag is the same three chords.

**Outro**

`G – A – Bm`
