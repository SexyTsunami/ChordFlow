The Way You Make Me Feel
Artist: Michael Jackson

Key: E major, 4/4 (felt in 12/8; Musicnotes publishes 12/8). This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original synth-bass arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar is usually played with no capo in E. Capo 2 in D (`D – C/D`, pre-chorus `G – F/G – A`) is a common easy-shape option. A few auto-transcribed charts sit in F, a half step above the record.

**Intro**

`E – D/E`

Repeat. Some reductions add `F#m/E` as a passing color between `D/E` and `E`.

**Verse**

`E – D/E`

The whole verse is the `E – D/E` vamp.

**Pre-Chorus**

`A – G/A`
`A – G/A – A – B`
`E – D/E`

The first lines sit on `A – G/A`. The pickup is `A – B`, then back to `E – D/E`.

**Chorus**

`E – D/E`

Same vamp as the verse. Repeat for each hook line.

**Bridge**

`E – D/E`

The breaks stay on the verse/chorus vamp. Late ad-libs keep the same `E – D/E` loop.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Bridge → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Outro
