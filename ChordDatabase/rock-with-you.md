Rock With You
Artist: Michael Jackson

Key: E-flat minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original Rhodes-and-string arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar often uses capo 6 and plays in A minor (`Am9 – E/F – E7`, pre-chorus `Cmaj7 – G/B – Fmaj7`), or capo 1 and plays in D minor. Musicnotes editions publish the song in E-flat minor. A few guitar charts lift the last hook a half step to E minor; the studio cut stays in E-flat through the fade.

**Intro**

`Ebm9 – Ab/Bb – Bb7`
`Ebm9 – Ab/Bb – Db`

Some charts write `Bb11` for `Ab/Bb – Bb7` and `Db11` for the Db hit.

**Verse**

`Ebm9 – Ab/Bb – Bb7`
`Ebm9 – Ab/Bb – Bb7`

**Pre-Chorus**

`Gbmaj7 – Db/F`
`Bmaj7 – Ab`
`Ebm7 – Fm7 – Gbmaj7`
`Ebm7 – Fm7 – Gb – Ab`

The last climb is often `Gb – G – Ab` into the hook.

**Chorus**

`Ebm9 – Ab/Bb – Bb7`
`Ebm9 – Ab/Bb – Db`
`Ebm9 – Ab/Bb – Bb7`
`Ab – Ab7`

**Bridge**

`Bbm7 – Eb7`
`Gbmaj7 – Db/F`
`Bmaj7 – Ab`

**Outro**

`Ebm9 – Ab/Bb – Bb7`
`Ebm9 – Ab/Bb – Db`

Same as the intro vamp, held through the fade.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Bridge → Outro
