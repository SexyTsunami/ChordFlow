二十二 (Er Shi Er / Twenty-Two)
Artist: David Tao (陶喆)

Key: F major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar commonly plays F shapes with no capo, or C shapes, capo 5. Chord4, 91pu, and yipuku agree on F. Intro is `F – Dm – Bb – C` on Chord4 and `F – Am – Bb – C` on some TXT sheets; both are I–vi–IV–V families. A few “original” picture-tabs use E shapes, capo 1, for the same concert F.

**Intro** (twice)

`F – Dm – Bb – C`

**Verse**

`F – Am – Bb – Am`
`Bbm – Gm – Bbm – C`
`F – Am – Bb – Am`
`Bbm – Gm – Bbm – C`

**Chorus**

`F – Am – Dm – Am`
`Gm – F – C – Bb – C`
`F – Am – Dm – Am`
`Bb – C – F`

**Bridge**

`Bb7 – C7 – Am – D7`
`Bb – Am – Eb – C – F`

**Outro**

`Bb – C – Gm – C – F`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus → Outro
