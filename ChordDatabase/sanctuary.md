Sanctuary
Artist: Joji

Key: Ab major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction of the Nectar studio cut; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Chordify and Musicnotes piano editions agree on Ab major. Guitar usually uses capo 1 and plays in G (`G – Cmaj7 – Am – F`, concert `Ab – Dbmaj7 – Bbm – Gb`). No-capo charts sometimes open on `Ab – Bbm – Ab` instead of the verse’s `Db`; Chordify colors the loop `Ab – Dbmaj7 – Bbm7 – Gbmaj7`.

**Intro**

`Ab – Dbmaj7 – Ab`

**Verse**

`Ab – Dbmaj7`

**Pre-Chorus**

`Cm – Bbm7 – Gb`

Capo-1 charts write `Bm – Am – F`. `Bbm` and `Bbm7` are interchangeable.

**Chorus**

`Ab – Dbmaj7 – Bbm – Gb`

Chordify’s production pass is `Ab – Dbmaj7 – Bbm7 – Gbmaj7`; beginner charts drop the sevenths to `Ab – Db – Bbm – Gb`.

**Bridge**

`Gb – Fm – Eb`

Capo-1 charts write `F – Em – D`. Some no-capo reprints swap `Fm` for `Bbm` (`Gb – Bbm – Eb`).

**Outro**

`Db – Bbm – Ab`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus → Outro
