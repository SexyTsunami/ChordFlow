最偉大的作品 (Zui Wei Da De Zuo Pin / Greatest Works of Art)
Artist: Jay Chou (周杰倫 / 周杰伦)

Key: G minor, 4/4 (short 3/4 bars in the piano intro). This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original piano part adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar often uses capo 3 and plays in E minor. Many guitar charts label the song Bb because capo-3 G shapes sound as Bb; the harmony is G minor.

**Intro** (piano)

`Gm – F – Eb – D7`
`Gm – F – Eb – D7`

then a short 3/4 stretch on Gm, landing `Gm – D7`

**Verse** (rap)

`Cm7 – D7 – Gm`
`Eb – A7 – D7`

(some lines drop the A7 and go `Eb – D7 – Gm` instead)

**Chorus** (two matching halves)

First half:
`Cm7 – D7 – Gm – Eb – A7 – D7`

Second half:
`Cm7 – D7 – Gm – Eb – D7 – Gm`

**Guitar solo**

Same as the chorus.

**Double-time interlude** (piano)

`G – Cm7 – F – Bb – Eb – Gm – D7 – Gm`

**Bridge**

`Gm – F – Eb – D7`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Verse → Chorus → Guitar solo → Double-time interlude → Bridge → Chorus → Guitar solo
