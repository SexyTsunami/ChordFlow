Company
Artist: Justin Bieber

Key: E minor, 4/4. Musicnotes original published key is E minor; Wikipedia agrees. Chordify labels the relative G major because the verse leans on G and Bm. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. No capo needed. Some charts drop the sevenths to `C – Am – G – Em`.

**Intro**

`Cmaj7 – Am7 – Gmaj7 – Em7`

**Chorus**

`Cmaj7 – Am7 – Gmaj7 – Em7`

**Verse**

`Cmaj7 – Em7 – G – Bm`

**Pre-Chorus**

`Cmaj7 – Em7 – G – Bm`

**Bridge**

`Cmaj7 – Em7 – G – Bm`

Then back to the chorus loop.

**Form**

Intro → Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus
