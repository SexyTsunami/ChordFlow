Give Me Everything
Artist: Pitbull, Ne-Yo, Afrojack, Nayer

Key: E-flat major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Chordify and Hooktheory agree on E-flat major with the chorus loop `Cm – Ab – Eb – Bb`. Guitar often uses capo 3 and plays `Am – F – C – G`; Ultimate Guitar prints capo 1 in D (`Bm – G – D – A`). E-Chords leaves those Am shapes with no capo, a minor third below the record. Spotify key tags sometimes read A-flat major because the loop includes `Ab`; keep E-flat as home.
