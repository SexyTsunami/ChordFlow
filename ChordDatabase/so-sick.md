So Sick
Artist: Ne-Yo

Key: E-flat minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Hooktheory and Musicnotes piano editions spell the concert key D-sharp minor; guitar charts usually write E-flat minor. E-Chords and matching piano reductions use `Ebm – Bbm – B – Db` (or `D#m – A#m – B – C#`). Ultimate Guitar’s popular capo-2 chart `C#m – A – E – B` sounds `Ebm – B – Gb – Db` and swaps the middle two colors for the generic four-chord loop. Musicnotes’ singer PVG is published in E minor, a half step above the record. Tunebat tags F-sharp major, the relative major of E-flat minor. Guitar often uses capo 1 and plays `Dm – Am – Bb – C`.

**Intro**

`Ebm – Bbm – B – Db`

The piano doo-wop riff is this four-chord cycle.

**Verse**

`Ebm – Bbm – B – Db`

**Pre-Chorus**

`Ebm – Bbm – B – Db`

**Bridge**

`Ebm – Bbm – B – Db`

**Outro**

`Ebm – Bbm – B – Db`

The chorus vamp repeats through the last “turn off the radio” tags.
