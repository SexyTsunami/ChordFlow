Weight of Love
Artist: The Black Keys

Key: E minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar stays in E minor with no capo. Chordify and Klangio agree on E minor. ChordLines labels the page G (relative major); Cifra Club headers say B minor while printing the same Em-centered changes. Intro/solo vamps are `Em – D` on some tabs and `Em – A` on others.

**Intro**

`Em – D`

Long opening guitar. Some charts write `Em – A – Asus4 – A` for this vamp.

**Solo**

`Em – D`

Same as the intro. Live and album cuts stretch this.

**Break**

`Em – G – D – C`

Held chords before the vocal.

**Verse**

`Asus2 – G – D – C`

**Chorus**

`Em – D – A – G – D`
`Em – D – C – B`

**Outro**

`E – B – D – A`

Some endings add `C` or `G#` color before a last `E`.

**Form**

Intro → Solo → Break → Verse → Chorus → Solo → Break → Verse → Chorus → Solo → Outro
