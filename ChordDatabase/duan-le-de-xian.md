斷了的弦 (Duan Le De Xian / Broken String)
Artist: Jay Chou (周杰倫 / 周杰伦)

Key: A major, 4/4. From the *尋找周杰倫* EP (often grouped with the *八度空間* era). This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar often uses capo 2 and plays in G.

**Intro**

`A – E – F#m – C#m`
`D – C#m – F#m – D – E`

**Verse**

`D – A – D – E – A`
`C# – D – A – D – C#`

**Pre-chorus**

`F#m – C#m – D – A – C#`
`F#m – C#m – D – C#`

**Chorus** (two matching halves)

First half:
`A – E – F#m – A – D – A – D – E`

Second half:
`A – E – F#m – A – D – A – D – E`

**Bridge**

`D – A – D – C#`
`D – A – D – E`

**Outro**

`A – E – F#m – C#m`
`D – C#m – F#m – D – E`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-chorus → Bridge → Chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-chorus → Bridge → Chorus → Chorus → Outro
