依然愛你 (Yi Ran Ai Ni / Still Love You)
Artist: Wang Leehom (Leehom Wang / 王力宏)

Also written 依然爱你. Key: A major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar commonly uses G shapes, capo 2. Simplified charts drop many of the sevenths and bass walks.

**Verse**

`A – E/G# – F#m – F#m/E – D – A/C# – B7 – E`
`A – E/G# – F#m – F#m/E – D – A/C# – Bsus4 – E`
`A – E/G# – F#m – F#m/E – A7 – D – A/C# – B7 – E`
`Dmaj7 – E – C#m7 – F#m – Bm7 – A/C# – D – E7`

**Chorus**

`A – E/G# – F#m – Em7 – A7 – D – E – A – A7`
`D – E – C#m7 – F#m – Bm7 – E – Asus4 – A`

The middle chorus (“命中注定”) keeps the first line, then:

`D – E – C#m7 – F#m – Bm7 – E`

**Interlude** (after chorus 2)

`A – E – F#m – F#m/E – D – A/C# – B7 – E`

(twice)

**Final chorus**

Same as the chorus, with the last line ending E7 – A.

**Form**

Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Interlude → Chorus → Chorus
