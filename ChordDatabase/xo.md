XO
Artist: John Mayer

Key: D major, 4/4. Solo cover of Beyoncé’s “XO”; credit stays John Mayer. This is the common guitar/piano reduction of the Paradise Valley studio cut; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar usually uses capo 2 and plays in C (`C – Cmaj7 – C6`, `Fmaj7`, then `C – Am – F – G`). Easy C-major charts with no capo sit a whole step below Mayer’s record (that C concert key matches Beyoncé’s published sheet). Cifra Club (capo 2) and no-capo D/`Bm`/`G`/`A` charts agree on D for Mayer.

**Intro**

`D – Dmaj7 – D6`

**Verse**

`D – Dmaj7 – D6`
`Gmaj7`
`D – Dmaj7 – D6`

The “baby, kiss me” lines move to `Gmaj7`. Later phrases walk the tonic as `D/F#`. Simplified charts flatten this to `D – G`.

**Chorus**

`D – Bm – Gmaj7 – A`
`D`

“Baby, love me lights out” lands back on `D`. Campfire charts use `G` and `A` in place of `Gmaj7` and `A6`; the function is the same.

**Bridge**

`D – Bm – Gmaj7 – A`
`D`

The “I love you like XO” section is the chorus loop with the title on `D` / `Bm`.

**Outro**

`D – Dmaj7 – D6`

Same tonic figure as the intro, repeating “you can turn my lights out.”

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus → Outro
