一點點 (Yi Dian Dian / A Little Bit)
Artist: Jay Chou (周杰倫 / 周杰伦)

Key: G major, 4/4. From *周杰倫的床邊故事*. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar sometimes uses D shapes with capo 5.

**Intro**

`G – C#m7b5 – F#7 – Bm7 – Am7 – D7 – Gmaj7`
`Cmaj7 – Bm7 – Am7 – D`

**Verse**

`G – F#m7b5 – B7 – Em – Em/D – C – G/B – Am7 – D`
`G – F#m7b5 – B7 – Em – Em/D – C – D – G`

**Pre-chorus**

`C#m7b5 – F#7 – Bm7 – E7 – Am7 – D`

**Chorus** (two matching halves)

First half:
`C – D/C – Bm7 – Em7 – Am7 – D – B7 – Em – Dm7 – G7`

Second half:
`C – D/C – Bm7 – Em7 – Am7 – D – G`

**Interlude**

`Em – D – C – G – Em – Bm7 – C`

**Last chorus**

Same pattern; the last pass often adds:

`C – D – Bm7 – Em7 – Am7 – D`

before landing on G.

**Outro**

`G – C – G – C – G`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Interlude → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → last chorus → Outro
