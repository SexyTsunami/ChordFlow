謝謝你的愛 (Xie Xie Ni De Ai / Thank You for Your Love)
Artist: Andy Lau (劉德華 / 刘德华)

Also written 谢谢你的爱. Mandarin hit; a separate Cantonese song shares the Chinese title. Key: E minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. PTT guitar threads and Pop Guitar Chords agree on Em with no capo. ChordU auto-detect sometimes tags G major because the verse walks through `G`; keep E minor. Easy C-shape charts sit a minor third below concert.

**Intro**

`Em – C – Am – B7 – Em`

Denser charts color the first bar `Em – Em7`.

**Verse**

`Em – Bm – C – D – G – B7`
`Am – D – G – Cmaj7`
`Am – B7 – Em`

Some sheets replace `Cmaj7` with `Em` on that bar.

**Pre-Chorus**

`Em – Bm – C – D – G – B7`
`C – D – G – Em`
`C – D – G – Em`
`Am – D – Em`

**Chorus**

`Em – Bm`
`C – D – G – Em`
`Am – D – G – B7`
`Em – Bm`
`C – D – G – Em`
`Am – D – Em`

The last chorus often stays on `Em` instead of returning through `G – B7`.

**Interlude**

`C – G – Em – C`
`Am – B7 – Em – C`
`Am – B7 – Esus4 – E`

The `Esus4 – E` lift is the turn back into the second pre-chorus.

**Outro**

`Am – D – Em`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Interlude → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Outro
