光輝歲月 (Guang Hui Sui Yue / Glorious Years)
Artist: Beyond (黃家駒 / 黄家驹 / Wong Ka Kui)

Also written 光辉岁月. Cantonese original from 命運派對; skip Mandarin karaoke transposes. Key: E major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar commonly uses C shapes, capo 4. ComeGetChord, guitarworld, and Guitarians agree on E. Ultimate Guitar’s widely saved chart is the C-shape capo-4 version of the same concert pitch. Easy no-capo C charts are for vocal range, not the studio cut. Guitarians writes G#m on the first pre-chorus bar where UG/hopamchuan write B.

**Intro** (twice)

`E – B – C#m – G#m – A – B – E – B`

**Verse**

`E – B – C#m – G#m – A – B – E – B`
`E – B – C#m – G#m – A – B – E – B`

**Pre-Chorus**

`C#m – B – A – B`
`C#m – B – A – B7`

**Chorus**

`E – B – C#m – G#m – A – B – E – B`
`E – B – C#m – G#m – A – B – E`

**Solo**

`E – B – C#m – G#m – A – B – E – B`

Guitar interlude over the intro loop; guitarworld labels this the E-major studio solo.

**Outro**

`E – B – C#m – G#m – A – B – E`

Final choruses keep repeating the same eight-bar cycle; some live cuts stay on E at the close.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Solo → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Solo → Chorus
