Dance The Night
Artist: Dua Lipa

Key: B minor, 4/4. From Barbie the Album. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original disco arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices (`E9` on the intro, `Gmaj13` colors on later choruses). Musicnotes publishes B minor; Chordify sometimes labels the relative D major because the chorus sits on `D – Gmaj7`. Guitar often uses capo 2 and plays `Am7 – D7 – C – Fmaj7`. Easy no-capo A-minor charts sit a whole step below the record.

**Intro**

`Bm7 – E7 – D – Gmaj7`

The disco guitar figure is this chorus loop; some charts spell the first two hits `Bm – E9`.

**Verse**

`Bm7 – E7`

Two-chord vamp under the verses.

**Pre-Chorus**

`Bm7 – E7`
`G – A – Bm – E`

The “every romance” lines stay on the verse vamp; the “I could dance” lift is `G – A – Bm – E`.

**Chorus**

`Bm7 – E7 – D – Gmaj7`

**Post-Chorus**

`Bm7 – E7 – D – Gmaj7`

Same chorus loop; later hits color the last bar `Gmaj13`.

**Bridge**

`Bm7 – E7 – D – Gmaj7`

Same four chords as the chorus; the groove thins, then the last chorus returns.

**Outro**

`Bm`

A short B-minor tag after the final post-chorus.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Post-Chorus → Bridge → Chorus → Post-Chorus → Outro
