回到過去 (Hui Dao Guo Qu / Back to the Past)
Artist: Jay Chou (周杰倫 / 周杰伦)

Key: B major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original piano part adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar often uses capo 4 and plays in G, or capo 2 and plays in A.

**Intro**

`B`

**Verse**

`B – F#/A# – G#m – B/F# – E – B/D# – C#m – F#`

**Pre-chorus**

`A#m – D#7 – G#m`
`E – B/D# – C#m – F#`

**Chorus** (two matching halves)

`B – C#m – A#m – D#7`
`G#m – E – B/D# – C#m – F#`

Second half ends on **B**.

**Interlude**

`B`

**Bridge**

`D#7 – G#m`
`E – B/D# – C#m – F#`

**Outro**

Same as verse, ending on B.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Interlude → Bridge → Chorus → Outro
