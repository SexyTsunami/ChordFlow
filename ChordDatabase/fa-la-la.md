Fa La La
Artist: Justin Bieber, Boyz II Men

From Justin Bieber’s Under the Mistletoe (2011). Key: Eb major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Musicnotes piano solo (MN0222406) publishes Eb; ChordU and Guvna Guitars write the same concert `Eb – Bb – Ab`. Gitagram’s capo-1 D-shape chart (`D – A – G`, sounding `Eb – Bb – Ab`) is that pitch, not a D-major record. No capo if you play the Eb shapes. Ukulele/ear charts that sit on `Bm – D – A – E` are a different guitar-friendly transpose. Keep the bridge on `Cm – Ab – Bb`.
