聽海 (Ting Hai / Listen to the Sea)
Artist: A-Mei (張惠妹 / 张惠妹)

Also written 听海. From 1997’s Bad Boy. Key: Bb major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar commonly uses G shapes, capo 3. ComeGetChord and Chordify agree on concert Bb; 虫虫吉他 labels the same pitch Bb with capo 3.

**Intro**

`Bb – Ebm6/Bb – Bb`

Piano charts often walk `Bb – Bbaug` into the verse.

**Verse**

`Bb – Eb – Bb`
`Bb – Eb – Dm – G7`
`Eb – Ebm – Bb – F/A – Gm`
`Eb – Cm7 – F`

Denser ComeGetChord voicings keep `Ebm6/Bb` under the `Bb` bars and `Ebadd9` on the lift. Second verse leans on `G7` before the climb into chorus.

**Chorus**

`Bb – F – Gm – F – Eb`
`F – Dm – Gm`
`Eb – C7/E – F`
`Bb – F – Gm – F – Eb`
`F – Dm – Gm`
`Eb – F – Bb`

Last chorus tags `Bbdim – Bbmaj7` on the piano cut.

**Outro**

`Eb – F – Bb`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Outro
