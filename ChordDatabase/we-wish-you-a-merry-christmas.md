We Wish You a Merry Christmas
Artist: The Disney Holiday Chorus

Disney’s Family Christmas Collection / A Very Merry Disney Christmas choral cut (~1:44), not a cartoon-dialogue medley. Key: G major, 3/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction of that chorus arrangement; the original voices add extra passing tones and inner voices. GetSongKEY and Chordify’s Disney Christmas Collection page agree on G. GuitarTuna’s Disney Holiday Chorus chart stays on `G – C – D – A – E` with no capo. Intro often sits on D (dominant) before the G verses. Easy C-major carol sheets sit a fifth below this recording.

**Intro**

`D – G – D – A – D`
`G – C – D – G`

**Verse**

`G – C – D – G`
`E – A – D`
`G – C – D – G`

The second phrase’s `E – A – D` is the secondary-dominant move into “merry Christmas.” Some easy charts flatten that bar to `G – C – D`.

**Refrain**

`G – D`
`G – A – D`
`G – C – D – G`

“Good tidings we bring.” Last pass often tags `D` as a turnaround into the next verse.

**Outro**

`G – C – D – G`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Verse → Refrain → Verse → Refrain → Verse → Outro
