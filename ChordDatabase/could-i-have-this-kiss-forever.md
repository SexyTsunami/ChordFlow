Could I Have This Kiss Forever
Artist: Whitney Houston, Enrique Iglesias

Key: A minor (chorus in B-flat minor), 4/4 (Metro Mix about 94 BPM). This is the Metro Mix / radio hit — the streamed Greatest Hits cut — not the slower David Foster ballad on Enrique. Musicnotes publishes the song in A minor; Ultimate Guitar and E-Chords use the same verse-in-Am / chorus-in-Bbm shape. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original Metro arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar usually plays open Am – Dm – G – F with no capo for the verses, then barre Bbm – Ebm – Ab – Gb for the chorus.
