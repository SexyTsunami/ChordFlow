好久不見 (Hao Jiu Bu Jian / Long Time No See)
Artist: Jay Chou (周杰倫 / 周杰伦)

Jay Chou's song from 我很忙 — not Eason Chan's 好久不見. Key: C major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. No capo. Guitarians and 吉他世界 agree on concert C. Verse hangs on C–Gm7–F–Fm; the chorus adds the Taiwanese-hook walk through Em7, A, and D7/F#.
