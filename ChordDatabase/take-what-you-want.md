Take What You Want
Artist: Post Malone, Ozzy Osbourne, Travis Scott

Key: C-sharp minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar usually uses capo 4 and plays in A minor (`Am – Em/B – C – F`, then `Am – Fdim – F – E7`). Ultimate Guitar, Tunebat, and Toca Aquela agree on C-sharp minor. Simpler web charts replace `G#m/D#` and `A#dim` with plain `B`; those are VII stand-ins for v and the diminished passing chord — keep the UG voicings. ChordLines’ “key of C” header is a site default, not the concert pitch. Tunebat’s 140 BPM is double-time of the sung ~70 feel.

**Intro**

`C#m – G#m/D# – E – A`
`C#m – A#dim – A – G#7`

Two four-bar phrases: i–v–III–VI, then i–dim–VI–V7.

**Chorus**

`C#m – G#m/D# – E – A`
`C#m – A#dim – A – G#7`
`C#m – G#m/D# – E – A`
`C#m – A#dim – A – G#7`

“Take what you want from me” uses the intro pair twice. The second “take what you need” line of each couplet is the dim / `G#7` phrase.

**Verse**

`C#m – G#m/D# – E – A`
`C#m – A#dim – A – G#7`

Same eight-bar cycle. Travis Scott’s verse does not change the chords.

**Pre-Chorus**

`C#m – G#m/D# – E – A`
`C#m – A#dim – A – G#7`

“And it finally came the day” / the later “love / shorty gon’ back” stack sit on the same cycle.

**Solo**

`C#m – G#m/D# – E – A`
`C#m – A#dim – A – G#7`

Ozzy’s guitar solo over the intro loop; later hits tag the chorus lyric on top.

**Outro**

`C#m – G#m/D# – E – A`
`C#m – A#dim – A – G#7`

Solo texture into the last “take what you want” calls.

**Form**

Intro → Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Solo → Outro
