她來聽我的演唱會 (Ta Lai Ting Wo De Yan Chang Hui / She Came to Hear My Concert)
Artist: Jacky Cheung (Jackie Cheung / 張學友 / 张学友)

Key: F# major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar commonly uses G shapes tuned down a half step; some F-shape charts sit a half step below concert. Verse Cadd9/Em7/B and chorus Em9 colors on G-shape charts become Badd9, D#m7/A#, and D#m9 here.
