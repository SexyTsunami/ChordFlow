說好不哭 (Shuo Hao Bu Ku / Won't Cry)
Artist: Jay Chou (周杰倫 / 周杰伦), Ashin (阿信 / Mayday)

Key: Bb major, 4/4. Duet with Ashin of Mayday. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original piano part adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar often uses capo 3 and plays in G.

**Intro** (twice)

`Bb – D7 – Gm – Gm/F – Eb – F – Bb`

**Verse**

`Bb – Adim – D7 – Gm`
`Fm – Bb – Eb – Dm7`
`Gm – Cm – F`

**Chorus** (two matching halves)

First half:
`Bb – D7 – Gm – Gm/F – Eb – F – Bb – Eb – F`

Second half:
`Bb – D7 – Gm – Gm/F – Eb – F – Bb`

**Bridge**

`Eb – Edim – Dm7 – Bdim – Cm7 – F`

**Interlude**

`Bb – D7 – Gm – Gm/F – Eb – F`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Verse → Chorus → Bridge → Verse → Chorus → Interlude → Chorus
