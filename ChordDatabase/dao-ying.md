倒影 (Dao Ying / Reflection)
Artist: Jay Chou (周杰倫 / 周杰伦)

Key: A major, 4/4. The last chorus lifts a half step to Bb. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar commonly uses G shapes, capo 2.

**Intro**

`A – C#m7 – F#m7 – Em7 – A`
`D – A/C# – Bm7 – E`

**Verse**

`A – C#7 – F#m7 – Em – A7`
`D – A/C# – Bm7 – E`

**Pre-chorus**

`D – A/C# – Bm7 – A`
`F#m – C#m7 – D – Bm7 – E`

**Chorus** (two matching halves)

First half:
`Dmaj7 – E/D – C#m7 – F#m7`
`Bm7 – E7 – A – A7`

Second half:
`Dmaj7 – E/D – C#m7 – F#m7`
`Bm7 – E – A`

**Interlude**

`A – C#m7 – F#m7 – Em7 – A`
`D – A/C# – Bm7 – E`

**Last chorus**

Same pattern, up a half step to Bb:

`Ebmaj7 – F/Eb – Dm7 – Gm7`
`Cm7 – F7 – Bb – Bb7`
then the matching second half ending on **Bb**

**Outro**

`Bb – D7 – Gm – F – Eb – F – Bb`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Interlude → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → last chorus in Bb → Outro
