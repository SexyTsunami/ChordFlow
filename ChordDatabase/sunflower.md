Sunflower
Artist: Post Malone, Swae Lee

Key: D major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction of the Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse soundtrack cut; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar stays in D with no capo. Musicnotes and Ultimate Guitar-style charts agree on D major and the `D – G – Em – G` loop. Chordify prints `D – Gmaj7 – Em7 – Gmaj7` and labels the track G major because the G color sits so long; keep concert D major and treat the maj7/min7 spellings as voicings of the same four bars.

**Intro**

`D – G – Em – G`

Vocal ad-libs over the four-bar loop.

**Verse**

`D – G – Em – G`

**Pre-Chorus**

`D – G – Em – G`

Same harmony as the verse; the vocal lifts into the “some things you just can’t refuse” setup.

**Chorus**

`D – G – Em – G`

**Outro**

`D – G – Em – G`

The loop holds; later hits sometimes leave `G` ringing as `Gmaj7`.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Outro
