I Fall Apart
Artist: Post Malone

Key: C-sharp minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Concert loop is `C#m – G#m – E – F#` (often `C#m7 – G#m7`). Guitar usually uses capo 4 and plays `Am – Em – C – D`. Ultimate Guitar-style charts and Cifra Club agree on those concert chords. Analysis sites that list G-sharp minor or B major are reading the same four chords as a loop that never cadences; keep C-sharp minor because every section starts on `C#m`. Easy capo-4 charts that print `Am – C – Em – G` are the wrong shapes (they sound `C#m – E – G#m – B`).

**Intro**

`C#m – G#m – E – F#`

**Verse**

`C#m – G#m – E – F#`

**Chorus**

`C#m – G#m – E – F#`

**Bridge**

`C#m – G#m – E – F#`

Spoken-sung break over the same loop; some live cuts sit longer on `E` before `F#`.

**Outro**

`C#m – G#m – E – F#`

The last chorus loop holds to the end.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus
