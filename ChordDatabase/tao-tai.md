淘汰 (Tao Tai / Elimination)
Artist: Eason Chan (陳奕迅 / 陈奕迅)

Mandarin hit from 認了吧; Jay Chou wrote the song. Key: A major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar commonly uses G shapes, capo 2. Easy G-shape charts with no capo sit a whole step below concert. Verse borrows G and F (bVII and bVI) before the V–I cadence.

**Intro** (chorus loop, twice)

`D – E – C#m – F#m`
`Bm7 – E – A`

Second pass often ends `A – Asus4 – A`.

**Verse**

`A – E/G# – F#m – C#m`
`D – A/C# – B7 – E`
`A – G – F#m – F`
`A/E – E – A`

**Chorus** (two matching halves)

`D – E – C#m – F#m`
`Bm7 – E – A`

**Turnaround**

`G – F#m – Fdim`

After the last chorus the turnaround adds `E – A`.

**Interlude** (chorus loop)

`D – E – C#m – F#m`
`Bm7 – E – A`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Verse → Chorus → Turnaround → Verse → Chorus → Interlude → Chorus → Outro (turnaround into A)
