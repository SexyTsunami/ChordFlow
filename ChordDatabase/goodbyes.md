Goodbyes
Artist: Post Malone, Young Thug

Key: F major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar stays in F with no capo. Chordify, ChordZone, and Hooktheory agree on F major. Verse loop is `F – A – Dm – Bb` (Chordify colors the `A` as `A7`). The chorus lifts to `Gm – C – Am – Dm – Bb – Bbm` before returning to `F`. Some outro charts swap `A` for `Am` on the “goodbyes” chant; keep `F – A – Dm – Bb` to match the verse harmony.

**Intro**

`F – A – Dm – Bb`

**Verse**

`F – A – Dm – Bb`

Young Thug’s verse uses the same four chords.

**Chorus**

`Gm – C – Am – Dm`
`Bb – Bbm – F`

The borrowed `Bbm` is the “saved too” color before `F` resolves the hook.

**Outro**

`F – A – Dm – Bb`

The “goodbyes” chant returns to the verse loop; later repeats sometimes walk `F – Am – Bb – Bbm`.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Outro
