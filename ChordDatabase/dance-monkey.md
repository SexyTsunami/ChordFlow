Dance Monkey
Artist: Tones And I

Key: F# minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction of the studio cut; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. National Guitar Academy, Lamucal, and ChordsBase agree on F# minor. Guitar often uses capo 2 and plays `Em – C – D – Bm` (sounding `F#m – D – E – C#m`). Musicnotes beginner editions in A minor are transpositions, not concert pitch. No-capo barre charts match F#m.

**Intro**

`F#m – D – E – C#m`

**Verse**

`F#m – D – E – C#m`

**Pre-Chorus**

`F#m – D – E – C#m`

“Ooh I see you” is the same loop; the last `C#m` is the pickup into the title.

**Chorus**

`F#m – D – E – C#m`

**Bridge**

`F#m – D – E – C#m`

Instrumental/vocalise over the four chords, then back to the chorus.

**Outro**

`F#m – D – E – C#m`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus → Outro
