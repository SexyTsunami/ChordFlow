Starry Night (별이 빛나는 밤)
Artist: MAMAMOO (마마무)

Yellow Flower title track. Key: A minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Kpop Chords, Chordyes, and Korean sheet listings (Akbobada) agree on Am with no capo. ChordU follows the same `Am – C – G – Dm` loop. Beginner charts that swap the last chord to `D` or `F` are hearing passing bass, not a new progression.

**Intro**

`Am – C – G – Dm`

**Verse**

`Am – C – G – Dm`

**Pre-Chorus**

`Am – C – G`
`Am – C – G`

The pre-chorus drops `Dm` and sits on `G` as the lift into the title.

**Chorus**

`Am – C – G – Dm`
`Am – C – G`

Second line often holds `G` instead of returning to `Dm`.

**Post-Chorus**

`Am – C – G – Dm`

The “야야야” hook is the intro loop.

**Bridge**

`Am – C – G – Dm`

Same vamp; the “leave me alone” line stays on `Dm` a bar longer before the last chorus.

**Outro**

`Am – C – G – Dm`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Post-Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus → Outro
