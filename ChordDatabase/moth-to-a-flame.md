Moth To A Flame
Artist: Swedish House Mafia, The Weeknd

Key: C minor, 4/4. Musicnotes’ original published key is C minor; guitar sites often label A-flat because the verse opens on `Ab`. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar usually uses capo 1 and plays `G – Em – Bm – A` (sounding `Ab – Fm – Cm – Bb`). Easy no-capo G charts sit a half step below the record. GuitarTuna’s “official” verse writes `Gm` where capo-1 charts and ChordU have `Cm`; keep `Cm` for concert pitch.

**Intro**

`Ab – Fm – Cm – Bb`

Wordless “woah” vocal on the verse loop.

**Verse**

`Ab – Fm – Cm – Bb`

The verses sit on this four-chord loop. Some piano reductions color `Bb` as a short passing chord rather than a full bar.

**Pre-Chorus**

`Ab – Fm – Cm – Bb`

Same four chords; the pre-chorus is a lift in the vocal, not a new harmony.

**Chorus**

`Eb – Bb – Ab – Fm`
`Bb – Cm – Bb`

The “does he know” hook opens on `Eb` (capo 1: `D5`). The last “where your heart lies” lands back toward `Ab` / `Cm`.

**Outro**

`Ab – Fm – Cm – Bb`

“Where it truly lies” returns to the intro loop over the house breakdown.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Outro
