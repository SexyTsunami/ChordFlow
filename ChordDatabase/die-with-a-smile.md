Die With A Smile
Artist: Lady Gaga, Bruno Mars

Key: A major, 6/8 (guitar charts often count it in 4). This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar often uses capo 2 and plays in G (G–C, Am–D7–Bm–Em).

**Intro**

`Amaj7 – Dmaj7`

**Verse**

`Amaj7 – Dmaj7`

**Pre-Chorus**

`Amaj7 – C#m7 – F#sus4 – F#`

**Chorus**

`Bm – E7 – C#m7 – F#m`
`Bm – E7 – C#m7 – F#m`
`Bm – E7 – A – E/G# – F#m`
`Bm – E7 – Amaj7 – Dmaj7`

Later choruses swap the last line for `Bm – C#7 – F#m – B` before returning to `Bm – E7 – Amaj7 – Dmaj7`. Some charts write E7 as Bm/E.

**Bridge**

`C#m7 – F#m – Bm – E7`
`C#m7 – F#m`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus → Outro
