All at Once
Artist: Whitney Houston

Key: B-flat major, 4/4 (about 66 BPM). Musicnotes original published key is B-flat major. Wikipedia and Hooktheory: verses in B-flat, chorus in D-flat (C-sharp), last chorus in D. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original Michael Masser arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar usually uses capo 3 and plays in G for the verses (Ultimate Guitar / GOTABS beginner charts); those G-shape charts are capo-transposed, not concert pitch. Concert shapes are Bb for the verses and Db for the first choruses.
