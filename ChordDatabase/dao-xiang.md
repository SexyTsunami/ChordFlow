稻香 (Dao Xiang / Rice Fragrance)
Artist: Jay Chou (周杰倫 / 周杰伦)

Key: A major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar often uses capo 2 and plays in G.

**Intro** (twice)

`A – E – F#m – C#m – Bm – E – A`

**Verse**

`A – E – F#m – C#m – Bm – E – A`

**Chorus** (two matching halves)

First half:
`A – E – F#m – C#m – Bm – E – A`

Second half:
`A – E – F#m – C#m – Bm – E – A`

**Interlude** (twice)

`A – E – F#m – C#m – Bm – E – A`

**Bridge**

`A – E – F#m – C#m – Bm – E – A`

(same loop through the middle eight and the pickup back into the chorus)

**Outro**

`A – E – F#m – C#m – Bm – E – A`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Interlude → Bridge → Chorus → Chorus → Outro
