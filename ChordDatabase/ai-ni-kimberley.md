愛你 (Ai Ni / Love You)
Artist: Kimberley Chen (陳芳語 / 陈芳语)

Ending theme of 翻糖花園. Not G.E.M.’s 愛你 (see `ai-ni.md`). Key: A major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Chord4 and Kac Music Road agree on A. Guitar commonly uses G shapes, capo 2 (`G – Em – C – D`, sounding `A – F#m – D – E`). Piano sheets keep `Asus2 – Asus2/F#`. Easy open-G charts sit a whole step below concert.
