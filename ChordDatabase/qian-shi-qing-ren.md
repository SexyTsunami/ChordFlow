前世情人 (Qian Shi Qing Ren / Past-Life Lover)
Artist: Jay Chou (周杰倫 / 周杰伦)

From 哎呦，不錯哦. Composed by Jay Chou with Hathaway; vocal is Jay solo. Key: A minor, 3/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. No capo. Chord4 / Daydayguitar and Polygon agree on concert Am in waltz time. Hooktheory lists the same song as A minor.
