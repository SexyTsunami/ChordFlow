Wanna Be Startin' Somethin'
Artist: Michael Jackson

Key: E Mixolydian (notated as E major with D naturals), 4/4. Wikipedia, MusicRadar, and original-key analyses agree on an E-centered D–E loop; guitar pages often capo 2 and play C–D shapes (sounding D–E). SATB choir editions published in F are a whole step above the record. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original bass-and-percussion arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices.

**Groove** (whole song)

`D/E – E`

or, as two bars of triads:

`D – E`

Bass stays on E (riff tones `E – D – B`). Every verse, chorus, bridge, and coda uses this same loop.

**Form**

Intro (bass) → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Bridge → Verse → Chorus → Bridge → Final verse → Coda (same loop, repeat / fade)
