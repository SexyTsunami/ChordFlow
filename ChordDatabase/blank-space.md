Blank Space
Artist: Taylor Swift

From 1989 (2014). Key: F major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Musicnotes PVG/leadsheet (MN0143767 / MN0143708), Chordify, and Hooktheory agree on concert F. Guitar usually uses capo 5 and plays C shapes (`C – Am – F – G` sounding `F – Dm – Bb – C` in the verse; chorus `C – Am – Dm – F` sounding `F – Dm – Gm – Bb`). Ultimate Guitar’s capo-5 charts match. No-capo charts stay in F and need the `Bb` / `Gm` barres.

**Intro**

`F`

**Verse**

`F – Dm – Bb – C`

**Chorus**

`F – Dm – Gm – Bb`

The chorus swaps the verse’s `C` for `Gm`. Easy sheets that keep `F – Dm – Bb – C` through the hook miss that color.

**Bridge**

`F – Dm – Bb – C`

Same as the verse.

**Outro**

`F – Dm – Gm – Bb`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus → Outro
