Heartbreak Warfare
Artist: John Mayer

Key: A major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original delay-guitar arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. No capo. Musicnotes piano/vocal/guitar and guitar-tab editions, Chordify, and Ultimate Guitar agree on A major. Audio-key detectors sometimes tag D major because the riff leans on `D/F#`; the chord key and published editions are A.

**Intro**

`Asus2 – Dsus2/F#`

The delay riff. Easy charts write `A – D/F#`.

**Verse**

`Asus2 – Dsus2/F#`

Same two-chord figure. Later verses stay on it.

**Chorus**

`Asus2 – Dsus2/F#`

The title line lands on the `Dsus2/F#`. Harmony does not change from the verse; the vocal and stacked guitars do.

**Pre-Chorus**

`F#m – A – Dsus2/F#`

Some charts write `F#m – Asus2 – Dsus2`.

**Bridge**

`Fmaj7/G – C/G – G`

Easy charts write `F – C – G`. The second half often sits on `Fmaj7/G – G` before a bar of `A` dumps back into the riff.

**Outro**

`A – Dmaj7/F#`

Last title hits; ends on the `Dmaj7/F#` color.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Pre-Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Pre-Chorus → Bridge → Verse → Chorus → Outro
