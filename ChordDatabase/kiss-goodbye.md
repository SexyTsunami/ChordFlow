Kiss Goodbye
Artist: Wang Leehom (Leehom Wang / 王力宏)

Official title is the English Kiss Goodbye; Chinese listings keep that name. Key: D major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar can also use C shapes, capo 2. Some charts use a D – A/C# – C – Bm bass walk in place of D – F#m – Am – B.

**Intro**

`D – Bm – G – Gm`

**Verse**

`D – F#m – Am – B – G`
`Gm – F#m – Bm – G – A`

**Chorus**

`D – Bm – G – Gmaj7 – A`
`D – Gm – G – A`

**Interlude**

`D – F#m – Am – B – G`
`Gm – F#m – Bm – G – A7`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Interlude → Chorus
