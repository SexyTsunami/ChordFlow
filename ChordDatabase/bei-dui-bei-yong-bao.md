背對背擁抱 (Bei Dui Bei Yong Bao / Back-to-Back Embrace)
Artist: JJ Lin (林俊傑 / 林俊杰)

Also written 背对背拥抱. Key: Eb major (verse in C minor), 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar commonly uses C shapes, capo 3; some charts use Bm shapes, capo 1, and label the song C minor from the verse start.
