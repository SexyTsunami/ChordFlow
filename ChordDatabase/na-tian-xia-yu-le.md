那天下雨了 (Na Tian Xia Yu Le / It Rained That Day)
Artist: Jay Chou (周杰倫 / 周杰伦)

Ballad from 太陽之子. Jay solo; 楊瑞代 (Yang Ru-dai / 杨瑞代) is billed on 聖誕星 and 等你下課, not this track. Key: F major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar commonly uses C shapes, capo 5. Kanpu and 吉他世界 agree on concert F.

**Intro**

`F – Dm7 – Bb – Bbm`
`F – Dm7 – G/B – Bb – C`
`F – Eb – Dm7 – Bbm`
`F/C – Csus4 – Fsus4 – F`

**Verse**

`F – C/E – Dm7 – Dm7/C`
`Bb – F/A – G/B – Gm7/C`
`Dm7 – Am7 – Bb – C – F`
`Dm7 – Am7 – Gm7 – Gm7/C – F`

**Chorus** (two matching halves)

First half:
`F – Dm7 – Gm7 – Bbm`
`Am7 – Dm7 – G/B – Gm7/C`

Second half:
`F – Eb – Dm7 – Bbm`
`Csus4 – Fsus4 – F`

**Bridge**

`Bb – Am – Gm7 – Gm7/C – F`
`Bb – Am – Gm7 – C`

**Last verse**

Same as the verse, ending `Gm7 – C – F`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Bridge → Last verse → Chorus
