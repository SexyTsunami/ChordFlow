I Could Not Ask for More
Artist: Edwin McCain

Diane Warren song from Messenger / the Message in a Bottle soundtrack. Key: F major, 2/2. This is the common guitar/piano reduction of the studio cut; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Musicnotes’ easy piano and Alfred’s original sheet are F major around 70 BPM (half-note pulse). Guitar often uses capo 1 and plays in E (`E – A9 – G#m7 – C#m7 – F#m7`, concert `F – Bbadd9 – Am7 – Dm7 – Gm7`). Ultimate Guitar and azchords agree on concert F; some reprints tune down a half step and write E shapes without transposing. Easy `F – Bb – C` sheets drop the `Am7` pre-chorus walk and the chorus `Eb` / `Ab`.

The “every prayer” bar is borrowed `Eb`; beginner charts sometimes print `Ab` there instead. The last “here with you / here with me” walk is `Ab – C`.
