Stargirl Interlude
Artist: The Weeknd, Lana Del Rey

Key: A-flat major, 4/4. Hooktheory and original-key guitar charts agree on A-flat; DJ/Camelot listings call the relative F minor. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar usually uses capo 1 and plays `G – Em` (sounding `Ab – Fm`). Easy no-capo G charts sit a half step below the record. Capo-1 tabs add `E – A` on “hitting it,” which is concert `F – Bb`; some charts also color a bar as `Abaug` or `Abmaj7`.

**Verse**

`Ab – Fm`

Lana Del Rey’s opening vision sits on this two-chord vamp. The turnaround into the last line of the verse is often `F – Bb` (or `Fsus4`) before `Ab` returns.

**Refrain**

`Ab – Fm`

The “I shouldn’t cry… Starboy” chant stays on the same two chords. Guitar sometimes lifts one repeat with `Abmaj7` or `Abaug`.

**Chorus**

`Ab – Fm`

The Weeknd’s “stargirl” hook is the same `Ab – Fm` loop, usually voiced cleaner and without the `F – Bb` turnaround.

**Outro**

`Ab – Fm`

The last “stargirl” repeats fade on the vamp.

**Form**

Verse → Refrain → Chorus → Outro
