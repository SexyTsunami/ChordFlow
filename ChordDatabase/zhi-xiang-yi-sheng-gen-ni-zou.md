祇想一生跟你走 (Zhi Xiang Yi Sheng Gen Ni Zou / Only Want to Walk With You for Life)
Artist: Jacky Cheung (Jackie Cheung / 張學友 / 张学友)

Also written 只想一生跟你走. Key: G major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. No capo. Some charts omit the C/E–D/F# walkup and the C–F–Bm–C–D7 bridge.

**Intro** (twice, then a 2/4 bar)

`G – Em – C – Am – Dsus – D7`

**Verse**

`G – Em – C – Am – Dsus – D`
`G – Em – C – D – G – D`
`G – Em – C – Am – Dsus – D`
`G – Em – C – D – G`

**Pre-chorus**

`Em – C – Am – D7`

**Chorus** (two matching halves)

First half:
`G – Em – C – D – C/E – D/F#`

Second half:
`G – Em – C – D – G`

Last time into the bridge, end on G7.

**Bridge**

`C – F – Bm – C – D7`

**Outro**

`G – Em – C – Am – Dsus – D7 – G`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Intro → Verse (second half) → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus → Outro
