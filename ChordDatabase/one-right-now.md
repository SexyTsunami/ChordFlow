One Right Now
Artist: Post Malone, The Weeknd

Key: D-flat major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar usually uses capo 1 and plays `F – Am – C – F` (sounding `Gb – Bbm – Db – Gb`). Musicnotes publishes D-flat major; Tunebat spells the same pitch C-sharp major; Chordify and Hooktheory agree on D-flat. GuitarTuna labels B-flat minor from the `Bbm` color (relative minor). Easy no-capo F charts sit a half step below the record. Charts that call it G-flat major are treating the opening `Gb` (IV) as tonic — Hooktheory’s most-used chord is IV, which matches starting on `Gb`.

**Intro**

`Gb – Bbm – Db – Gb`

“Na-na-na” vocal over IV–vi–I–IV.

**Chorus**

`Gb – Bbm – Db – Gb`

The “said you love me / one right now” hook is this loop. The last tag sometimes walks `Gb – Ab` (capo-1 `F – G`) on “and one right now.”

**Verse**

`Gb – Bbm – Db – Gb`

Post Malone’s verse and The Weeknd’s second verse stay on the chorus changes. Some play-along charts color a passing `Ebm`; keep it as an inner voice.

**Outro**

`Gb – Bbm – Db – Gb`

Final “one right now” repeats on the intro vamp.

**Form**

Intro → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Outro
