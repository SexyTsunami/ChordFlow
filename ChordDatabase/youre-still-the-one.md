You're Still The One
Artist: Shania Twain

Key: Eb major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction of the Come On Over studio cut; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Musicnotes (via published-sheet citations) and Klangio agree on Eb. Guitar often plays D shapes, capo 1, or open D a half step below concert. Easy `D – G – A – Em` charts are that D-shape transposition.

**Intro**

`Eb – Ab – Bb`

Spoken cold open, then a short `Ab – Bb` pad into the verse. Some charts sit on `Eb` alone.

**Verse**

`Eb – Gm – Ab – Bb`

Denser piano reductions use `Eb – Eb/G – Ab – Bb` or `Eb – Ebsus2 – Ab – Bb`.

**Pre-Chorus**

`Eb – Gm – Ab – Bb`
`Eb – Ab – Bb`
`Eb – Ab – Bb`

“They said I bet they’ll never make it.” The last hit is often `Bb – Bbsus4 – Bb`.

**Chorus**

`Eb – Ab – Fm7 – Bb`
`Eb – Ab – Bb`
`Eb – Ab – Fm – Bb`
`Eb – Ab – Bb`

Beginner charts drop `Fm7` to `Fm` or replace it with `Gm`. A passing `Ab` (some sheets `G#`) sometimes colors the “want for life” bar.

**Solo**

`Eb – Ab – Bb`
`Eb – Ab – Bb`

**Outro**

`Eb – Ebsus2 – Ab – Bb – Eb`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Solo → Chorus → Outro
