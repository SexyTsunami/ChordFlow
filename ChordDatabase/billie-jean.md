Billie Jean
Artist: Michael Jackson

Key: F-sharp minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original synth-and-bass arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar often uses capo 2 and plays in E minor (`Em – F#m/E – Am/E – F#m/E`, with `Am7` on the turnaround hits and `C – Em` / `C – B7` in the pre-chorus). Easy E-minor charts with no capo sit a whole step below the record. Musicnotes bass editions and original-key guitar charts agree on F-sharp minor.

**Intro**

Drums, then the F-sharp minor bass riff. Pads:

`F#m – G#m/F# – A/E – G#m/F#`

**Verse**

`F#m – G#m/F# – A/E – G#m/F#`
`Bm7`
`F#m – G#m/F# – A/E – G#m/F#`

The groove stays on the F-sharp minor vamp. `Bm7` is the verse/chorus turnaround, then the vamp returns.

**Pre-Chorus**

`D – F#m`
`D – F#m`
`D – F#m`
`D – C#7`

**Chorus**

`F#m – G#m/F# – A/E – G#m/F#`
`Bm7`
`F#m – G#m/F# – A/E – G#m/F#`

Same as the verse loop. Later choruses sometimes walk `F#m – F#m/E – A/E – G#m/F#` on the last phrase.

**Outro**

`F#m – G#m/F# – A/E – G#m/F#`

The F-sharp minor vamp holds to the fade.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Outro
