bad guy
Artist: Billie Eilish

Key: G minor (relative Bb major), 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original bass/synth arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar often uses no capo in G minor, or capo 3 and plays in E minor (`Em – Am – B7`). Published sheet music sometimes labels the relative Bb major.

**Intro**

`Gm`

**Verse**

`Gm – Cm – D`

**Chorus**

`Gm`
`Cm – D`
`Gm`

The “duh” hits land on `Gm`, with `Cm – D` under the later “I’m the bad guy” tag. Some charts write the chorus as a `Gm – Cm` two-chord loop and treat D as D7.

**Bridge**

`N.C.` (spoken/filtered vocal over the bass drone)
`Gm`

**Outro**

`Cm – D – Gm`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus → Outro
