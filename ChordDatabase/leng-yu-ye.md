冷雨夜 (Leng Yu Ye / Cold Rainy Night)
Artist: Beyond (黃家駒 / 黄家驹 / Wong Ka Kui)

Cantonese original from 現代舞台. Key: C major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. No capo. ComeGetChord, guitarworld, and daydayguitar agree on C for the vocal cut. ChordU labels A minor because the verse cadence keeps landing on Am; same relative pitch. The kworb Spotify #8 ranking cut is the instrumental 雨夜之後, not this vocal: 雨夜之後 is a related instrumental (often listed A minor, a shade slower) and should not replace these concert chords.

**Intro**

`Cmaj7 – F/C – Dm – Fmaj9`

**Verse**

`Fmaj9`
`Bm7b5 – E7 – Bm7b5 – E7 – Am`

The Fmaj9 bar is a four-bar vamp; denser charts keep Fmaj9 moving against a walking inner voice rather than a static F.

**Chorus**

`Dm7 – G7 – C – Fmaj7 – E7 – Am`
`Dm7 – G7 – C – Fmaj7 – E7 – Am`

**Solo**

`Dm – G – C – F – E7 – Am`
`Dm – G – C – F – E7 – Am`

Instrumental chorus over the hook; the original is arpeggiated rather than a single-note rock solo.

**Outro**

`Am7 – Fmaj7 – Dm7 – Fmaj7`

Studio close fades on this figure after the last chorus; ComeGetChord also loops `Dmadd9 – G6` as a guitar tag into the final hook.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Solo → Chorus → Outro
