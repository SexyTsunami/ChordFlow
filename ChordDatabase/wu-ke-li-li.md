烏克麗麗 (Wu Ke Li Li / Ukulele)
Artist: Jay Chou (周杰倫 / 周杰伦)

From 十二新作. Key: C major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. No capo; the same C shapes also work on ukulele. Chord4, kanpu, and ukulele C sheets agree on concert C.

**Intro** (four times)

`C – F – G – C`

**Verse**

`C – F – G – C`
`Am – F – G – C`

Later verse lines keep the same two-bar swap: some start on `C`, some on `Am`.

**Pre-chorus**

`C – F – G – C`
`Am – F – G – C`

**Chorus** (two matching halves)

First half:
`C – F – G – C`
`Am – F – G – C`

Second half:
`C – F – G – C`
`Am – F – G – C`

Chord4 writes the first three chorus lines all on `C – F – G – C` and saves `Am` for “沙灘上有你的腳印”; kanpu uses `Am` every other line. Same concert pitch.

**Interlude**

`C – F – G – C`

**Outro**

`C – F – G – C`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Interlude → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Chorus → Outro
