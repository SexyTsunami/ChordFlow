Human Nature
Artist: Michael Jackson

Key: D major, 4/4. Piano tutorials, original-key guitar charts, and Hooktheory-style analyses agree on D; some guitar pages transpose the same changes up a fourth to G. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original Toto synth arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar usually plays in D with no capo.
