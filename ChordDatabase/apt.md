APT.
Artist: ROSÉ, Bruno Mars

Key: Eb major (relative C minor), 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar often uses capo 1 and plays in B minor (`Bm – A – G – D`), or capo 3 and plays in A minor (`Am – G – F – C`). Opening choruses are mostly chant/N.C. before the chords lock in.

**Intro**

`N.C.`

**Verse**

`Cm – Bb`

**Pre-Chorus**

`Ab – Bb – Cm – Eb`
`Ab – Bb – Cm`

**Chorus**

`Ab – Bb – Cm – Eb`

Early choruses stay N.C. or sit on `Cm – Bb`; later choruses use the full `Ab – Bb – Cm – Eb` loop.

**Bridge**

`G7`
`Cm – Bb – Eb – Cm`
`Cm – Bb – F`

**Form**

Intro → Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Bridge → Pre-Chorus → Chorus
