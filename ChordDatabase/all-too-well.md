All Too Well (10 Minute Version) (Taylor's Version) (From The Vault)
Artist: Taylor Swift

From Red (Taylor's Version) (2021). This is the ranked 10-minute vault cut, not the 2012 five-minute original. Key: C major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Musicnotes PVG (MN0246193), Chordify, and Hooktheory agree on concert C. Guitar stays in C with no capo. Ultimate Guitar’s 10-minute chart and GuitarZero2Hero loop `C – G – Am – F` for verse, chorus, and bridge; the late verses flip to `C – Am – F – G`, and the outro stays on that order. Live guitar often plays `Gsus4` and `Fsus2` for `G` and `F`.
