You're Gonna Live Forever in Me
Artist: John Mayer

Key: G major, 4/4. This is the common piano/guitar reduction; the original piano part adds extra passing tones and inner voices. No capo; it is a piano ballad in G. Musicnotes lists G major as the original published key. Simplified guitar/piano charts collapse the walk to `G – C – Em – Am – D`; keep the descending `G – Gmaj7 – G7 – G6` and the `Cm6` color to match the record.

**Intro**

`G – Gmaj7 – G7 – G6 – C – G – Cm6`
`Em – A7 – C – G – Am`

**Verse**

`G – Gmaj7 – G7 – G6`
`C – G – Cm6`
`Em – A7`
`C – G`
`Am – D7`

Each verse ends on the title line (`C – G`) and the “I guarantee” tag (`Am – D7`). There is no separate pop chorus; that tag is the refrain.

**Instrumental**

`G – Gmaj7 – G7 – G6 – C – G – Cm6`
`Em – A7 – C – G – Am`

Same as the intro, between verse 3 and verse 4.

**Outro**

`Am – D7`

The last “I guarantee, just wait and see” hangs on the `D7` turnaround.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Verse → Verse → Instrumental → Verse → Outro
