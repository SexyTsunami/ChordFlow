bellyache
Artist: Billie Eilish

Key: E minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original acoustic guitar adds extra passing tones and inner voices (C/G, Em/B). Guitar often uses no capo. Easy charts sometimes label it C major because it starts on C (the VI chord).

**Intro**

`C – Am – Em`

**Verse**

`C – Am – Em`

**Pre-Chorus**

`C – Am – Em`

**Chorus**

`C – Am – Em`

**Post-Chorus**

`C – Am – Em`

The same three-chord loop covers every section. Fuller charts alternate `C – C/G` and `Em – Em/B` inside each bar.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Chorus
