White Iverson
Artist: Post Malone

Key: G major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar stays in G with no capo. Hooktheory, Chordify, and original-key piano charts agree on G major and the `Cmaj7 – G` color. Ultimate Guitar’s beginner `C – G` chart is the same two-chord idea a fourth too low; a common comment-loop of `G – D – Em – C` is the pop I–V–vi–IV stand-in, not the record. Keep `Cmaj7` (IV) against `G` / `G/D` (I).

**Intro**

`Cmaj7 – G/D – Cmaj7 – G`

The guitar/piano reduction is this two-chord sway from the first bar. `G/D` is the same I chord many charts spell as plain `G` or `Gmaj7`.

**Verse**

`Cmaj7 – G/D – Cmaj7 – G`

**Pre-Chorus**

`Cmaj7 – G/D – Cmaj7 – G`

Same harmony as the verse; the vocal sits on “White Iverson” over the IV–I flip.

**Chorus**

`Cmaj7 – G/D – Cmaj7 – G`

The “saucin’” hook is the intro loop with a tighter rhythm. Later hits sometimes leave `G` ringing as `Gmaj7`.

**Outro**

`Cmaj7 – G/D – Cmaj7 – G`

The IV–I vamp holds through the last hook.

**Form**

Intro → Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Outro
