when the party's over
Artist: Billie Eilish

Key: C# minor (relative E major), 3/4 (guitar charts often count it in 4). This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original piano part adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar often uses capo 4 and plays in A minor (`F – G – Am – G – C – G – F`). Piano original; no capo if playing concert shapes.

**Intro**

`A – B – C#m – B – E – B – A`

**Verse**

`A – B – C#m – B – E – B – A`

**Pre-Chorus**

`A – C#m – B – E – B – A`
`F#m – C#m`

**Chorus**

`A – B – C#m – B – E – B – A`
`A – C#m – B – E – B – A`
`F#m – C#m – E – A`

**Bridge**

`C#m/G#`
`B7 – A – A#dim`
`C#m/G#`

Some charts write the diminished color as Bbdim7. The four-chord verse loop is most of the song; F#m is the main chorus extra.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus → Outro
