Titanium
Artist: David Guetta, Sia

Key: E-flat major (chorus in C minor), 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction of the Nothing but the Beat studio cut; the original electro arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Musicnotes and Hooktheory agree on Eb major / C minor around 126 BPM. Guitar usually uses capo 3 and plays in C (`C – G – Am – F`, chorus `F – G – Am – Em`). ChordLines keeps concert `Eb – Bb – Cm – Ab`. Some auto-chord pages print the chorus as `Ab – Bb – Eb – Cm`; keep `Ab – Bb – Cm – Gm` for the guitar reduction.
