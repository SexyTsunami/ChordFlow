Apartment
Artist: Young the Giant

Key: B minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar usually uses capo 2 and plays in A minor (`Am – Em – Dm – C – G7 – Em – Am` verse). Cifra Club and ChordGitar agree on concert B minor. Acoustic/keyboard charts (GoTabs) color the chorus `D – Am6 – Cmaj7 – G`; Cifra Club simplifies that to `D – Am7 – Em – G`. A few reductions collapse the verse to `D6 – A` and sit a third away from the record.

**Intro**

`Bm – F#m – Em – D – A7 – F#m – Bm`

**Verse**

`Bm – F#m – Em – D – A7 – F#m – Bm`

**Pre-Chorus**

`A – D – A – D – A7`

**Chorus**

`D – Am7 – Em – G`

**Bridge**

`Bm – F#m – Em – D – A7 – F#m – Bm`

Wordless verse loop; some acoustic charts keep the intro walk `D – Dmaj7 – Cmaj7 – D – A7 – F#m – Bm` here.

**Outro**

`Bm – F#m – Em – D – A7 – F#m – Bm`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Verse → Bridge → Chorus → Outro
