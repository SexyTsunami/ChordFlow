Dreams
Artist: Fleetwood Mac

Key: A minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction of the Rumours studio cut (2004 remaster; same song as the 1977 original); the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Musicnotes publishes A minor. ChordLines and standard guitar lessons vamp `Fmaj7 – G` (or `F – G6`) for the verses; the chorus sits on `Am – G6`. Theory writeups sometimes call the vamp F Lydian or C major because `C` never arrives; keep the published A minor. No capo.

**Chorus**

`Am – G6`

Beginner charts print `Am – G`. ChordLines stays on `G6` for the chorus guitar part.

`Fmaj7 – Am – G`
