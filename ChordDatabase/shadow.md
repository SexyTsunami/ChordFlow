Shadow
Artist: Derik Fein

Key: Bb minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Cifra Club and Uke Cifras agree on Bb minor (they also spell the same concert pitch `A#m – C# – D#m – G#`). 12NOTEZ lists A# minor at about 180 BPM. Prefer the flat spelling: `Bbm – Db – Ebm – Ab`. No capo. Beginner charts drop nothing; it is already a four-triad loop.

`Bbm – Db – Ebm – Ab`

Same loop; the “tried to be all the things you need” lines do not change harmony.
