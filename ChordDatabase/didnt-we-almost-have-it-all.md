Didn't We Almost Have It All
Artist: Whitney Houston

Key: B-flat major, 4/4 (about 60 BPM). Musicnotes original published key is B-flat major. Wikipedia, Hooktheory, and Ultimate Guitar agree the chorus opens in G major, then returns to B-flat. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original Masser / Holdridge arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar usually uses capo 3 and plays in G, or capo 1 and plays in A. Charts that stay in G for the whole song are capo-transposed beginner versions, not the concert key.
