Die For You
Artist: The Weeknd

Key: C-sharp minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. The loop starts on `Amaj7`, so guitar sites and Chordify often list A major; Musicnotes piano editions and the title-hook landing agree on C-sharp minor. Guitar usually uses capo 2 and plays `Gmaj7 – F#m7 – Bm7 – Em7`. Easy no-capo G-shape charts sit a whole step below the record.

**Intro**

`Amaj7 – G#m7 – C#m7 – F#m7`

**Verse**

`Amaj7 – G#m7 – C#m7 – F#m7`

**Pre-Chorus**

`Amaj7 – G#m7 – C#m7 – F#m7`

Same loop; some charts drop the sevenths to triads (`A – G#m – C#m – F#m`).

**Chorus**

`Amaj7 – G#m7 – C#m7 – F#m7`

The title line sits on `C#m7`.

**Bridge**

`Amaj7 – F#m7 – G#m7 – C#m7 – F#m7`

The order changes here: `F#m7` comes second, then `G#m7` before `C#m7`.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus
