不為誰而作的歌 (Bu Wei Shei Er Zuo De Ge / A Song Not Written for Anyone)
Artist: JJ Lin (林俊傑 / 林俊杰)

Also written 不为谁而作的歌. Key: D major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar commonly uses C shapes, capo 2. Verse lines often swap G for Gm (the borrowed iv); the bridge is G–F#m–Gm–Bb–A on most guitar charts, while some write Em–D–G–Bb–A.

**Verse**

`D – F#m – Bm – Gm`
`G – A – G – A`

**Chorus** (two matching halves)

First half:
`D – F#m – Bm – Gm`
`D – F#m – Bm – G`

Second half:
`D – F#m – Bm – G`

**Bridge**

`G – F#m – Gm – Bb – A`

**Outro** (twice)

`D – F#m – Bm – G`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus → Outro
