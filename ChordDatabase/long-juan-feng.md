龍捲風 (Long Juan Feng / Tornado)
Artist: Jay Chou (周杰倫 / 周杰伦)

Key: A major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar often uses capo 2 and plays in G.

**Intro** (twice)

`A – F#m – D – E`

**Verse**

`A – F#m – D – E`
`A – F#m – D – Dm`

**Pre-chorus**

`D – C#m – Bm – E`

**Chorus** (two matching halves)

First half:
`A – F#m – D – E`

Second half:
`A – F#m – D – E`

**Post-chorus**

`A – F#m – D – E`

**Interlude** (twice)

`A – Bb – A – Bb`

**Outro**

`A – F#m – D – E`
`A – F#m – D – A`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Post-chorus → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Post-chorus → Interlude → Chorus → Outro
