They Don't Care About Us
Artist: Michael Jackson

Key: D minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original marching-band and synth arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar is usually played with no capo in D minor. Capo 5 in A minor (`Am – G`) is a common easy-shape option. Musicnotes piano/vocal editions publish the song in D minor.

**Intro**

`Dm – C`

**Verse**

`Dm – C`

Two-chord chant. Repeat through each verse block.

**Hook**

`Dm – C`

Same loop as the verse. Later hooks sometimes shift to `Bb – C`.

**Bridge**

`Dm – C`

The sung-spoken bridge stays on `Dm – C`. Near the end of the second bridge some charts add `A7`, `Bb`, and `Gm` on the turnaround.

**Late hook**

`Bb – C`

Used on some later hook entries, then back to `Dm – C`.

**Outro**

`Dm – C`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Hook → Verse → Hook → Bridge → Hook → Verse → Bridge → Late hook → Verse → Hook → Outro
