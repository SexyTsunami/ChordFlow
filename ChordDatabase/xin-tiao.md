心跳 (Xin Tiao / Heartbeat)
Artist: Wang Leehom (Leehom Wang / 王力宏)

Key: G major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. No capo. Some charts start the intro on Em–Bm–C instead of G–D/F#.

**Intro**

`G – D/F# – C – D – Em/B`
`G – D/F# – C`

**Verse**

`G – D/F# – Em7 – Bm7 – C – D`
`G – D/F# – Em7 – Bm7 – C – D`

**Pre-chorus**

`C – Bm7 – C – G – D`

**Chorus**

`Em – Bm7 – C – D – G – D/F#`
`Em – Bm7 – C – D`
`Em – Bm7 – C – Cm`
`Bm7 – Em – C – D – G`

**Interlude** (after chorus 1)

`G – C – D`

After chorus 2, a longer vamp:

`G – D/F# – Em7 – Bm7 – C – D`

(twice)

**Outro**

`G – C – D – C – G`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Interlude → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Interlude → Chorus → Outro
