I Will Follow You into the Dark
Artist: Death Cab for Cutie

Key: F major, 4/4 (felt in 2). This is the common guitar/piano reduction of the Plans studio cut; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Musicnotes’ original published key is F major (printed 2/2 at 77); Hooktheory lists F major in 4/4. Guitar usually uses capo 5 and plays in C (`C – Am – F – G`, concert `F – Dm – Bb – C`). Easy C-major charts with no capo sit a fourth below the record.

**Intro**

`Dm – F – Bb – F – C`
`Dm – F – C`
`Dm – F – A – Dm – C`
`Bb – Bbm – F`

**Verse**

`F – Dm – Bb – F – C`

**Chorus**

`Dm – F – Bb – F – C`
`Dm – F – C`
`Dm – F – A – Dm – C`
`Bb – Bbm – F`

The `A` is a secondary dominant into `Dm`; the `Bbm` is the borrowed color on the title line. Capo-5 charts write `E` and `Fm` for those two spots.

**Outro**

`Bb – Bbm – F`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Outro
