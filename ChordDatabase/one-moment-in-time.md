One Moment in Time
Artist: Whitney Houston

Key: C major, 4/4 (about 80 BPM). 1988 Summer Olympics anthem. Musicnotes original published key is C major; Wikipedia notes the last chorus lifts to C-sharp (D-flat). This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original London Symphony arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar usually plays open C-shape chords with no capo, or capo 5 and plays in G. Ultimate Guitar and Chordify charts in C match the record until the half-step lift.
