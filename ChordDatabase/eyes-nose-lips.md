EYES, NOSE, LIPS
Artist: TAEYANG (태양)

Also written 눈, 코, 입. Key: C major, last chorus in C#, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction of the RISE studio cut; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. No capo. Kpopalypse and Busk (ori C) agree on C then a final-chorus lift; piano sheets mark no sharps/flats plus one key change. Guitar charts often write `G/B` where piano reductions use `Em/B`.

**Intro**

`C`

Some charts vamp `C – Fm/C` before the vocal.

**Verse**

`C – Em/B – Am – E/G#`
`F – C – E – F – Fm`

**Chorus**

`C – E – Am – Am/G – F – Fm`

**Bridge**

`C – C/Bb – Am7 – Am7/G#`
`C – C/Bb – F – Fm`

Three passes of the first line, then the `F – Fm` turn into the lift.

**Chorus** (C#)

`C# – F – Bbm – Bbm/Ab – F# – F#m`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus (C#)
