擱淺 (Ge Qian / Stranded)
Artist: Jay Chou (周杰倫 / 周杰伦)

Key: F major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original piano part adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar often transposes to C.

**Intro**

`F/A – Bb – A7 – Dm – Dm/C – Gm – Csus4 – C`

**Verse**

`F – A – Dm – F/C – Bb – Am – Gm – C`
`F – A – Dm – F/C – Bb – Am – Gm – C`

**Pre-chorus**

`Em – A – Dm – G – C`

**Chorus** (two matching halves)

First half:
`Bb – C – Am – Dm – Gm – C – F`

Second half:
`Bb – C – A – Dm – Gm – C – F`

**Interlude**

`F – Ab – Eb – Fm – Db – Eb – C`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Interlude → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Chorus
