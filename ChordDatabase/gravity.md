Gravity
Artist: John Mayer

Key: G major, 6/8. This is the common guitar/piano reduction of the Continuum studio cut; the original organ-and-guitar arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar stays in G with no capo. E-Chords and theory writeups agree on G major in 6/8. Beginner tabs often print `Fmaj7` where the record has `Ebmaj7`; keep `Gm/Bb – Ebmaj7 – D7` for the lift. `Gm/Bb` is the same sound some charts spell `Bb6`.

**Intro**

`G – C`

Two bars of each in 6/8.

**Verse**

`G – C`

The “gravity is working against me” hook and the later “stay the hell away from me” verse both sit on this I–IV vamp.

**Pre-Chorus**

`Am7 – D7 – Gm/Bb – Ebmaj7 – D7`

This is the only new harmony: “I’ll never know” / “twice as much ain’t twice as good.” The `Gm/Bb – Ebmaj7` bars are borrowed minor-key color before `D7` returns to G.

**Solo**

`G – C`

Same as the intro. Live versions stretch this for the guitar solo.

**Outro**

`G – C`

“Just keep me where the light is” repeats the verse vamp to the fade. Later hits sometimes color `C` as `Cmaj7` or walk `Em7 – C`.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Solo → Pre-Chorus → Verse → Outro
