She Knows
Artist: Ne-Yo, Juicy J

Key: G-sharp minor, 4/4. Hooktheory, Chordify, and Songmode agree on G-sharp minor. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original sample-and-synth arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar usually uses capo 4 and plays `Em` on the verse and `C – Am – Em – G` on the hook (sounding `G#m` and `E – C#m – G#m – B`). Lamucal labels the relative E major. Chordify often prints `E – G#m – F# – B` where capo-4 guitar charts use `C#m` instead of `F#`.

**Intro**

`G#m`

Juicy J’s opening rap sits on a G-sharp minor vamp; pads often color `Emaj7` against the tonic.

**Verse**

`G#m`

Ne-Yo’s verse and the later rap verses stay on this one-chord groove. Some auto-charts alternate `E – G#m`.

**Pre-Chorus**

`E – C#m – G#m – B`

**Chorus**

`E – C#m – G#m – B`

Chordify’s remix detection (`E – G#m – F# – B`) is the same loop with `F#` in place of `C#m`.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus
