Slow It Down
Artist: Benson Boone

From Fireworks & Rollerblades (2024). Key: Ab major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Musicnotes PVG and piano solo both publish Ab; Wikipedia lists the same original key. Guitar usually uses capo 1 and plays C-shapes (`C – C6 – G – Gmaj7`, sounding `Db – Db6 – Ab – Abmaj7`). AZChords, GOTABS, and GuitarTwitt agree on that verse color and pre-chorus `Fm – Eb/G – Ab – Eb/G – Db`. Easy no-capo sheets drop the `6` / `maj7` and print plain `Db – Ab`.

**Intro**

`Db – Db6 – Ab – Abmaj7`

Piano vamp on the verse colors; some guitar charts skip it and cold-open the verse.

**Verse**

`Db – Db6 – Ab – Abmaj7`

Verse 2 often simplifies to `Db – Ab` without the `6` / `maj7` lifts.

**Pre-chorus**

`Fm – Eb/G – Ab – Eb/G – Db`
`Fm – Eb/G – Ab – Eb/G – Db`

Capo-1 shapes are `Em – D/F# – G – D/F# – C`.

**Chorus**

`Db – Db – Ab – Ab`
`Db – Db – Ab`

Last hold is often N.C. before the tag.

**Outro**

`Db – Bbm – Ab`
`Db – Bbm – Ab – Eb`
`Fm – Eb/G – Ab – Eb/G – Db`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Outro
