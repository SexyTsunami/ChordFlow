Brighter Than Sunshine
Artist: Aqualung

Key: A major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction of the Still Life studio cut; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Musicnotes’ original published key is A major at about 80 BPM; Faber’s PVG matches A. Common guitar charts print the verse from `D` as `D – A – E – B`. Easy C-shape reprints (`C – G – D – A`) sit a whole step below the record.

**Intro**

`D – A – E – B`

**Verse**

`D – A – E – B`
`D – A – E`

**Chorus**

`B – E – G#m – G – F#`
`A – E – B – E`
`G#m – G – F#`
`A – E – Em – B`

The `G#m – G – F#` walk is the title lift; beginner charts drop it to `E – D – C#` or skip the chromatic.

**Bridge**

`Em – A`
`Em – A – B`

**Outro**

`B – E – G#m – G – F#`
`A – E – Em – B – A`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Bridge → Verse → Chorus → Outro
