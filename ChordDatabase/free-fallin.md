Free Fallin'
Artist: John Mayer

Key: F major, 4/4. Live Nokia Theatre 2007 arrangement from Where the Light Is (the charting Spotify Tom Petty cover). This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original fingerpicked part adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar usually uses capo 3 and plays D shapes (`D/F# – G – G – D/F# – A`), the same as Petty. Easy D-major charts with no capo sit a minor third below this live key. Chordify concert charts and capo-3 guitar tabs agree on F.

**Intro**

`F/A – Bb – Bb – F/A – C`

Fingerpicking, not a strummy campfire loop. The first `Bb` is often voiced as `Fsus4/Bb`.

**Verse**

`F/A – Bb – Bb – F/A – C`

Same loop as the intro. Live, Mayer keeps this pattern under every lyric line.

**Chorus**

`F/A – Bb – Bb – F/A – C`

The chorus is the same three-chord figure; the lift is vocal, not a new harmony. `C` sometimes blooms to `Csus4`.

**Outro**

`F/A – Bb – Bb – F/A – C`

The live ending repeats the chorus vamp with the crowd on the hook.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus
