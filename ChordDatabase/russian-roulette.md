Russian Roulette
Artist: Rihanna

Key: F-sharp minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction of the Rated R studio cut; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Hooktheory and Klangio agree on F# minor around 80 BPM. Guitar usually uses capo 2 and plays in E minor (`Em – C – Am – B7`). Ultimate Guitar and ChordLines keep concert `F#m – D – Bm – C#7`; some reprints swap `C#7` for plain `C#`. Easy A-minor charts sit a minor third below the record.

**Intro**

`F#m`

**Verse**

`F#m – D – Bm – C#7`

**Pre-Chorus**

`F#m – D – E – Bm`
`F#m – D – E – Bm – C#7`

**Chorus**

`F#m`

The title hit is a bar of tonic after the pre-chorus `C#7`. Some charts write the whole pre-chorus loop again here.

**Bridge**

`A – Bm – F#m – E`
`D – B/D# – E`

ChordLines spells the walk `B/Eb`; keep `B/D#`. A few sheets add a passing `F°` into the last chorus.

**Outro**

`F#m – D – E – Bm`
`F#m`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Bridge → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Outro
