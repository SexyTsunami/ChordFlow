Emotions
Artist: Mariah Carey

Key: C major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction of the 1991 studio cut; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Musicnotes PVG, beginner notes, and leadsheet editions publish C major (about 116 BPM). Hooktheory tags verse/chorus C and the pre-chorus A minor; Chordify’s house-piano loop is `Am – C – Fmaj7 – F`. Ultimate Guitar and E-Chords often display F-shape charts (`F – Em – Dm`) a fourth above concert — transpose those down to C (`C – Bm – Am`) for the record. Guitar in concert stays in C with no capo.
