I Don't Care
Artist: Ed Sheeran, Justin Bieber

Key: F# major, 4/4. Wikipedia and Musicnotes list F# major at about 100 BPM. The song is one four-chord loop all the way through. Guitar often uses capo 2 and plays `E – C#m – A – B`.

**Intro**

`F#`

Then into the main loop.

**Verse**

`F# – D#m – B – C#`

Typical phrasing is two bars of F#, two of D#m, then one each of B and C#.

**Pre-Chorus**

`F# – D#m – B – C#`

**Chorus**

`F# – D#m – B – C#`

Same loop as the verse.

**Bridge**

`F# – D#m – B – C#`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus
