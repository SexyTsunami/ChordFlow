霍元甲 (Huo Yuan Jia / Fearless)
Artist: Jay Chou (周杰倫 / 周杰伦)

Jet Li film theme. Chinese-style / rock. Key: D minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar commonly uses Am shapes, capo 5.

`Dm – Bb – Gm – Am`

`Dm – Bb – Gm – Am`

**Pre-chorus**

`Dm – Bb – Gm – Am – Dm`

**Chorus**

`Dm – Bb – Gm – Am`
`Dm – Bb – Gm – Am`

**Interlude**

`Dm – Bb – Gm – Am`

**Outro**

`Dm – Bb – Gm – Am – Dm`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Interlude → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Interlude → Chorus → Outro
