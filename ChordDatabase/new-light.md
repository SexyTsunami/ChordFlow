Artist: John Mayer

Key: G major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Musicnotes and Chordify agree on G major; simplified charts drop the sevenths to `Am – D – G – C` but the loop is the same.
