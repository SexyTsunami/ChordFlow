Sun
Artist: Derik Fein

Key: F# minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. 12NOTEZ and SongBPM agree on F# minor at about 100 BPM. Guitar usually uses capo 2 and plays `Am – D – G – Em` (concert `Bm – E – A – F#m`). VChords prints those concert shapes with no capo. The loop starts on `Bm`; analysis pages still treat `F#m` as tonic.
