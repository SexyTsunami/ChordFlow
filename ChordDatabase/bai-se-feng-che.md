白色風車 (Bai Se Feng Che / White Windmill)
Artist: Jay Chou (周杰倫 / 周杰伦)

Key: F major in the verse, lifting to D major for the chorus, 4/4. From *依然范特西*. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar often uses capo 5 and plays in C (chorus then in A), or capo 2 with G-to-E shapes.

**Intro**

`F – Dm7 – Bb – Bbm`

**Verse**

`F – Dm7 – Bb – Bbm`
`F – Dm7 – Bb – Bbm`

**Pre-chorus**

`Gm7 – F/A – Bb`
`Bbm – Gm7 – F/A`
`G – A`

**Chorus** (two matching halves)

First half:
`D – Bm7 – G – A`
`F#m7 – Bm7 – E7 – A`

Second half:
`D – Bm7 – G – A`
`F#m7 – Bm7 – Em7 – A`

**Interlude**

`D – Bm7 – G – Gm`

**Outro**

`F#m7 – E7/G# – Em7 – A`
`D – Bm7 – G – Gm – D`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Interlude → Chorus → Outro
