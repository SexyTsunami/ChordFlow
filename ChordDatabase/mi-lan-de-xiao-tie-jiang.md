米蘭的小鐵匠 (Mi Lan De Xiao Tie Jiang / Little Blacksmith of Milan)
Artist: Jay Chou (周杰倫 / 周杰伦)

From 八度空間. Key: D minor, 4/4 (relative of F major). The last chorus lifts a half step to Eb minor. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitarworld and 易譜庫 list concert F with C shapes, capo 5; Day Day Guitar and Chord4 write the same harmony from D minor. Guitar commonly uses Am shapes, capo 5.

**Intro**

`Bb – Am – Dm`
`Bb – Am – Dm`
`Gm – Am – Dm`

**Verse**

`Dm – Am – Dm – Am`
`Dm – Am – Dm – A`

**Pre-chorus**

`Gm – Dm – Gm – Dm`
`Gm – Dm – Gm – A`

Some charts color the last bar `Adim7 – Bbdim7` into the chorus.

**Chorus**

`Dm – Am – Dm – Am`
`Dm – Am – Dm – Am`

**Interlude**

`Dm – Gm – A – Dm – A`
`Dm – Gm – A – Dm – A`
`Dm – Gm – A`

**Last chorus**

Same pattern, up a half step to Eb minor:

`Ebm – Bbm – Ebm – Bbm`

**Outro**

`Ebm – Bbm – Ebm`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Interlude → last chorus in Ebm → Outro
