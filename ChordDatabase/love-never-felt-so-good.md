Love Never Felt So Good
Artist: Michael Jackson, Justin Timberlake

Key: F major, 4/4. Musicnotes, Hooktheory, and original-key guitar/piano charts agree on F for both the 1983 piano demo and the 2014 Justin Timberlake duet. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original electric-piano arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar usually plays in F with no capo.
