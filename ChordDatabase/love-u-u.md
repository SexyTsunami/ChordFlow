Love U U
Artist: JJ Lin (林俊傑 / 林俊杰)

Key: D major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar commonly uses C shapes, capo 2.

**Intro**

`D – A/C# – Bm7 – Bm7/A`

**Verse**

`D – A/C# – Bm7 – Bm7/A`
`G – A – F#m – Bm7`
`Em7 – A7 – A`

Some charts use D – A – Bm7 – F#m on the first two bars, without the slash bass.

**Chorus**

`D – A/C# – Bm7 – Bm7/A`
`G – D/F# – Em7 – G/A – A`
`D – A/C# – Bm7 – Bm7/A`
`G – D/F# – Em7 – A – D`

Some charts use Bm – D/A and G – F#m in place of the slash-bass walk. Later choruses can swap G – D/F# – Em7 – A for G – Fm – E7 – A7.

**Bridge**

`G – D/F# – Em7 – A7 – A`

**Interlude**

`C – G – Dm – Am – F – Em – A`

Then back to chorus.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Interlude → Bridge → Chorus → Chorus
