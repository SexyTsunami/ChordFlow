I Guess I Just Feel Like
Artist: John Mayer

Key: B major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar usually uses capo 2 and plays `A – D – F#m` (sounding `B – E – G#m`). Easy no-capo A-major charts sit a whole step below the record. Chordify and ChordU on the official audio, plus Musicnotes’ live piano edition, agree on B major; TuneBat’s E-major tag is hearing the prominent E, not the tonic.

**Intro**

`B – E`

Same two-chord vamp as the title line.

**Verse**

`B – E`
`G#m – E`
`B – E`

The title phrase is `B – E`. Mid-verse moves to `G#m – E`, then the hook returns. Studio guitar colors the E as a `Dadd9` shape under capo 2.

**Bridge**

`A – G#m`
`A – G#m – F#`

The wordless lift. Capo-2 charts write `G – F#m – E`. After this break, the `B – E` vamp returns for the solo and the last verse.

**Outro**

`B – E`

Holds the intro vamp under the closing solo.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Verse → Bridge → Verse → Outro
