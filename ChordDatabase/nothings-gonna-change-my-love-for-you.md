Nothing's Gonna Change My Love for You
Artist: Khalil Fong (方大同)

Khalil Fong’s Timeless arrangement, not the Glenn Medeiros / George Benson singles. Key: C major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. No capo. Chord4, Chords Haven, and AcousticBrainz agree on C. Some live cuts lift a half step on the last chorus; the album reduction stays in C.
