The Way I Am
Artist: Ingrid Michaelson

Key: F major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction of the Girls and Boys studio cut; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Musicnotes publishes F major around 120 BPM. Guitar and ukulele stay in F with no capo. Ultimate Guitar and Parka Blogs loop `F – Bb – Dm – C`; the title line uses borrowed `Db`. Denser sheets color `Bbmaj7`, `Dm7`, and `Gm7 – Db` on that last bar. Easy C-shape charts sit a fourth below concert.
