蘭亭序 (Lan Ting Xu / Orchid Pavilion Preface)
Artist: Jay Chou (周杰倫 / 周杰伦)

Key: D major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar often uses capo 2 and plays in C.

**Intro**

`G – F# – Bm – Em – A – D`
`C – B – Em – A – D`

**Verse**

`D – A – Bm`
`G – F#m – Em – A`
`G – A – F#m – Bm`
`Em – A – D`

Second stanza of each verse uses the same loop; the last line often stops on A into the chorus.

**Chorus** (two matching halves)

First half:
`G – F# – Bm – Em – A – D – C – B – Em – A – D`

Second half:
`G – F# – Bm – Em – A – D – C – B – Em – A – D`

**Interlude**

Same as intro.

**Outro**

`D – G – F#m – Em – A – D`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Interlude → Verse → Chorus → Chorus → Outro
