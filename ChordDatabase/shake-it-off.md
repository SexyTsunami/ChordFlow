Shake It Off
Artist: Taylor Swift

From 1989 (2014). Key: G mixolydian, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Musicnotes PVG (MN0138575) publishes G major; Chordify, Wikipedia’s published analysis, and Hooktheory agree the record is G mixolydian with `Am – C – G`. Ultimate Guitar’s original-key charts stay on that three-chord loop. Guitar stays in G with no capo. This is Taylor’s 1989 cut, not Mariah Carey’s different song of the same title.
