Paris in the Rain
Artist: Lauv

Key: E-flat major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Tunebat is Eb major; guitar reductions (ChordLines, chords-and-tabs) print the concert loop `Fm – Bb – Eb – Cm`. Auto-key pages sometimes label Fm, the relative minor. Guitar usually uses capo 3 and plays in C (`Dm – G – C – Am`). Denser voicings are `Fm9 – Bb13 – Ebmaj7 – Cm7`.

**Intro**

`Abmaj7 – Bb7 – Ebmaj7 – Cm7`

Beginner charts flatten this to `Ab – Bb – Eb – Cm`.

**Verse**

`Fm – Bb – Eb – Cm`

**Chorus**

`Fm – Bb – Eb – Cm`

**Bridge**

`Fm – Bb – Eb – Cm`

Same loop as the chorus; the “days in Paris / nights in Paris” stack sits on it.

**Outro**

`Fm – Bb – Eb – Cm`
`Fm – Gm – Bb – Eb – Cm`

The empty-street tag often walks `Gm` before `Bb`. Keep the sevenths if matching the record’s Rhodes voicings.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Bridge → Outro
