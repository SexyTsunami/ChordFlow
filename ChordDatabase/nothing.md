Nothing
Artist: Bruno Major

From To Let a Good Thing Die. Key: D major, 3/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction of the studio cut; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Musicnotes publishes D major, 3/4 at 140 (gentle sway); Tunebat and Hooktheory agree on D. Guitar stays in D with no capo. ChordLines and TopChords walk `D – A/C# – Bm – D7/A – G – D/F# – E7 – Asus4`; the title hook is `Em9 – Gm9 – C7`. Easy charts flatten the bass walk to `D – A – Bm – G` and skip the borrowed `Gm9`. Guitar reprints that mark 4/4 at 70 are grouping two bars of the published 3/4.
