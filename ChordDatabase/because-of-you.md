Because Of You
Artist: Ne-Yo

Key: C minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Hooktheory, Tunebat, and Chordify’s remix pass agree on C minor. Guitar usually uses capo 1 and plays `G – A – Bm – Em`, sounding `Ab – Bb – Cm – Fm`. Ultimate Guitar labels that chart A-flat because the verse starts on `Ab`; keep C minor as home. E-Chords prints the G-shape loop without capo, a half step below the record.

**Intro**

`Ab – Bb – Cm`

**Verse**

`Ab – Bb – Cm`
`Ab – Bb – Cm – Fm`

Line endings often drop to `Fm` before the next pickup.

**Pre-Chorus**

`Ab – Bb – Cm`

The “you have become my addiction” climb uses the verse colors and holds `Cm`.

**Chorus**

`Fm – Ab – Bb – Cm`
`Ab – Bb – Fm`

The tag “never get enough / sweetest drug” is `Ab – Bb – Fm`.

**Bridge**

`Dbmaj7 – Cm7 – Bmaj7 – Bbm`
`Dbmaj7 – Cm7 – Bmaj7 – Bbm`
`Amaj7 – Abm7 – Dbmaj7 – Fm`

Chromatic walk-down. Capo-1 shapes are `Cmaj7 – Bm7 – Bbmaj7 – Am`, then `Abmaj7 – Gm7 – Cmaj7 – Em`.

**Outro**

`Fm – Ab – Bb – Cm`
`Ab – Bb – Fm`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus → Outro
