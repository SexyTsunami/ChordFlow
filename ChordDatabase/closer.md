Closer
Artist: Ne-Yo

Key: E minor, 4/4. Chordify, Hooktheory, and GuitarTuna agree on E minor. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original synth-and-guitar arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar usually stays in E minor with no capo on `Csus2`/`Cmaj7` and `Em`/`Em9`. Capo-2 charts on E-Chords and ChordLines play `Bb – Dm7` and sound `C – Em7`. Chordify and Lamucal add `D`/`Dadd9` on the chorus; two-chord guitar lessons omit it and loop only C and Em.

**Intro**

`Em`

Synth “Closer” hits over an E-minor pad. The guitar riff enters on the verse colors.

**Verse**

`Cmaj7 – Em`

Four bars of each. Some reductions spell `Csus2` or `C5` for the first chord.

**Pre-Chorus**

`Cmaj7 – Em`

The “come closer” calls sit on the same two chords.

**Chorus**

`C – D – Em`

Hooktheory’s chorus color is `Dadd9`; beginner charts stay on `C – Em`. A few tabs add `Am` on the escape line.

**Bridge**

`Cmaj7 – Em`

Same two-chord vamp as the verse, stretched on the stacked “come closer” lines.

**Outro**

`Cmaj7 – Em`

Final “come closer” tags hold the verse loop.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus → Outro
