Artist: David Tao (陶喆)

Also written 就是爱你. Key: A major, last chorus in Bb, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar commonly uses G shapes, capo 2; move the capo to 3 for the final lift. Chord4 writes the song in open A; kanpu’s G-shape sheet is the same concert pitch.
