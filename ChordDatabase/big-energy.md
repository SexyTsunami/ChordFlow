Big Energy (Remix)
Artist: Latto (Alyssa Stephens / Mulatto), Mariah Carey, DJ Khaled (Khaled Khaled)

Key: G major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction of the 2022 remix with Mariah and DJ Khaled, not Latto’s solo single; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Chordify, Chordyes, and the Logic remake listings publish G (about 106 BPM) because the track interpolates Mariah’s “Fantasy.” Ultimate Guitar–style charts loop `G – Em`. Tunebat’s B-minor tag is a relative-side read of the rap vocal — keep G as the tonic, same as “Fantasy.” Guitar stays in G with no capo.
