句號 (Ju Hao / Full Stop)
Artist: G.E.M. (鄧紫棋 / 邓紫棋)

Key: G minor (relative Bb major), 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar commonly uses Em shapes, capo 3. Charts often label the original as Bb because of the relative major.

**Intro**

`Gm – Eb – Bb – F`

**Chorus** (song opens here)

`Gm – Eb – Bb – F`

Last line of some chorus passes can substitute F for the usual Bb–F pair. Guitar charts often write the F as F/A.

**Verse**

`Gm – Eb – Bb – F`

**Pre-chorus**

Same loop as the chorus:

`Gm – Eb – Bb – F`

**Bridge**

`Cm – Dm – Eb – F`

**Outro**

`Gm – Eb – Bb – F`

**Form**

Intro → Chorus → Verse → Pre-chorus → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Bridge → Chorus → Outro
