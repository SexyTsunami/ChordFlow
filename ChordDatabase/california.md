California
Artist: 88rising, Rich Brian, NIKI, Warren Hue

Key: Eb major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction of the studio cut; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Chordify’s official-video chart and capo-3 guitar editions (C shapes sounding Eb) agree on Eb. Guitar usually uses capo 3 and plays `C – Cmaj7 – Am7` (concert `Eb – Ebmaj7 – Cm7`). Acoustic-live charts in open Eb/Bb are the same harmony without the capo. Easy triads drop the sevenths to `Eb – Cm`.

**Intro**

`Ebmaj7 – Cm7`

**Verse**

`Ebmaj7 – Cm7`

Warren Hue and Rich Brian verses stay on this two-chord vamp. Some bars color a plain `Eb` between the sevenths.

**Chorus**

`Ebmaj7 – Cm7 – Eb`
`Ebmaj7 – Cm7 – Eb`

“You made it, California” often lands the last hit on triad `Eb` instead of `Ebmaj7`.

**Bridge**

`Eb – Ebmaj7 – Cm7`

NIKI’s “antihero” / “sky looks like a painting” section is the same loop, sometimes with a longer hold on `Eb`.

**Outro**

`Ebmaj7 – Cm7`

Title tags to the fade.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus → Outro
