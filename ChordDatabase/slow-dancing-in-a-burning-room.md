Slow Dancing in a Burning Room
Artist: John Mayer

Key: C-sharp minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar often uses capo 4 and plays in A minor (`Am – F – C`, with `G` on the “going down” lift and `Dm` in the chorus/bridge). Easy A-minor charts with no capo sit a major third below the record. Lesson charts and no-capo reductions agree on C-sharp minor; some sites list E major (the relative major) because the vamp also uses `A` and `E`.

**Intro**

`C#m – A – E`

**Verse**

`C#m – A – E`

The groove stays on this three-chord vamp. The last line of each verse hits `B` as a pickup into the chorus.

**Chorus**

`B – C#m – A`
`B – C#m – F#m`
`C#m – A – E`

“We’re going down” is the `B` lift. Some beginner capo-4 charts keep `F` (concert `A`) on the second line instead of `F#m`; the Continuum guitar part uses the minor iv there.

**Solo**

`C#m – A – E`

Same as the verse vamp. Live versions stretch this for the guitar solo.

**Bridge**

`B – F#m7 – C#m`
`B – A – C#m – A – E`

“Go cry about it” sits on `F#m7`. The last phrase returns to the chorus cadence.

**Outro**

`C#m – A – E`

The vamp holds through the “don’t you think we oughta know” fade.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Solo → Bridge → Outro
