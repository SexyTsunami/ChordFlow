Glimpse of Us
Artist: Joji

Key: Ab major, 6/8. This is the common guitar/piano reduction of the SMITHEREENS studio cut; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Musicnotes and Hooktheory agree on Ab major in 6/8 (dotted quarter around 57). Some guitar reprints mark 3/4; keep 6/8 for the published meter. Guitar usually uses capo 1 and plays in G (`Am – D7 – G – Em`, concert `Bbm – Eb7 – Ab – Fm`). Easy charts swap `D7` for plain `D`. Auto-chord pages sometimes print `Db` where the guitar charts have `Eb7`.

**Intro**

`Bbm – Eb7 – Ab – Fm`

**Verse**

`Fm – Bbm – Eb7 – Ab`

**Pre-Chorus**

`Bbm – Eb7`

**Chorus**

`Bbm – Eb7 – Ab – Eb/G – Fm`
`Bbm – Eb7 – Ab – Eb/G – Fm`
`Bbm – Eb7 – Ab – Eb/G – Fm`
`Bbm – Eb7 – Fm`

The `Eb/G` bass walk is the piano left hand; beginner charts omit the slash and stay on `Ab`. The last title hit often lands `Bbm – Eb7 – Fm` instead of returning to `Ab`.

**Bridge**

`Bbm – Eb7 – Ab – Fm`
`Bbm – Eb7 – Ab – B – F7`

The last bar is the only new color: capo-1 charts write `G – Bb – E7` (some spell `Bb` as `A#`).

**Outro**

`Bbm – Eb7 – Ab – Eb/G – Fm`
`Bbm – Eb7`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus → Outro
