Eenie Meenie
Artist: Sean Kingston, Justin Bieber

Key: Db major, 4/4. Wikipedia lists Db major at 120 BPM. Easy-piano Musicnotes is transposed to B minor and is not the concert key. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar often uses capo 1 and plays `C – Am – F – G`. Charts in sharps write `C# – A#m – F# – G#`.

**Bridge**

**Form**
