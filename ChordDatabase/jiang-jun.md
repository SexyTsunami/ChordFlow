Artist: Jay Chou (周杰倫 / 周杰伦)

From 七里香, not 八度空間. Key: C# minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar commonly uses Am shapes, capo 4 (`Am – Dm – E`, sounding `C#m – F#m – G#`). Chord4 and Mojim agree on concert C#m. Easy C-shape sheets that call the song E are reading the relative major, or they are capo-4 Am charts mislabeled as E.
