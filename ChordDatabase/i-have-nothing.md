I Have Nothing
Artist: Whitney Houston

Key: G major (choruses in G minor), 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original David Foster piano part adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar usually plays G shapes with no capo. Verses stay in G; the first two choruses move to the parallel minor. The last chorus lifts a half step to G-sharp minor and cadences in B major.

**Intro**

`Gmaj7 – Em7 – Cadd9 – Am7 – D`

**Verse**

`Gmaj7 – Em7 – C – G/B – Am7 – D`

**Pre-Chorus**

`Am – Bm – Em`
`Am – Bm`
`C – Am7 – D`

**Chorus** (G minor)

Pickup `D`, then:

`Ebmaj7 – Cm7 – Dm7 – Gm7`
`Ebmaj7 – Cm7 – Dm7 – Gm7`
`Cm7 – Dm7 – Gm7`
`Ebmaj7 – Bb/D – F7sus`
`F7 – Bb – Gm7 – F – Ebmaj7 – D7`

**Bridge**

`Em7 – Cadd9 – Gmaj7`

**Final chorus** (G-sharp minor, then B)

Same chorus colors a half step up:

`Emaj7 – C#m7 – D#m7 – G#m7`

The “I have nothing” close resolves in B:

`Emaj7 – B/D# – F#7sus`
`F#7 – B – G#m7 – F# – Emaj7 – F#7 – B`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus (G#m/B)
