Lo/Hi
Artist: The Black Keys

Key: A minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar stays in A minor with no capo. Musicnotes and guitar charts (TopChords, KhmerChords) agree on A minor (`Am – Dm – C` / `Am – G`). Auto-key sites (Klangio, SnapLyrics, KDUR) tag D major because D and G appear in the mix — keep A as the tonic.

**Intro**

`Am`

**Verse**

`Am – Dm – C`

Each line of the verse is that three-chord cell, often repeated as a pickup into the next line.

**Pre-Chorus**

`Am – Dm – C`

Same loop as the verse; the band leans on it harder.

**Chorus**

`Am – G – Am`

**Instrumental**

`Am – Dm – C`

**Outro**

`Am – G – Am`

The last chorus repeats the `Am – G` hook.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Instrumental → Outro
