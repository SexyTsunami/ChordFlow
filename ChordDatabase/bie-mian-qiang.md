別勉強 (Bie Mian Qiang / Don't Force It)
Artist: G.E.M. (鄧紫棋 / 邓紫棋), Eric Chou (周興哲 / 周兴哲)

Key: A major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar commonly uses G shapes, capo 2. Capo charts often write C#m/E where concert-A charts write A/E.

**Intro**

`A`

**Verse**

`A – C#7 – F#m – A/E`
`D – C#m – F#m7`
`Bm7 – Dm/E – E`

**Chorus** (two matching halves)

First half:
`Aadd9 – C#7 – F#m – A/E`
`D – C#m – F#m7`
`Bm7 – Dm/E – E`

Second half:
`Aadd9 – C#7 – F#m – A/E`
`D – C#m – F#m7`
`Bm7 – E – A`

**Bridge**

`Dadd9 – C#m – F#m7`
`Bm7 – C#7 – F#m – A/E`
`Dadd9 – C#m – F#m7`
`G – C#7`

**Last chorus**

Same as chorus, except the second-half opening substitutes G#m7b5 – C#7 for C#7:

`Aadd9 – G#m7b5 – C#7 – F#m`

**Outro**

`A – E – F#m – D`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Bridge → last chorus → Outro
