Can't Feel My Face
Artist: The Weeknd

Key: A minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original disco-funk arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Wikipedia and Musicnotes agree on A minor. Guitar stays in A minor with no capo (`G – F – Am`); the loop starts on `G`, so some players hear C major instead. Keep A minor as the listed key.

**Intro**

`G – F – Am`

**Verse**

`G – F – Am`

**Pre-Chorus**

`G – F – Am`

Same three chords. Some charts add a passing `C` on the last “oh” of the phrase; the record is still an A-minor landing.

**Chorus**

`G – F – Am`

**Bridge**

`G – F – Am`

Pre-chorus refrain returns over the same loop.

**Outro**

`G – F – Am`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus → Outro
