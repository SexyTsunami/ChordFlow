分手總要在雨天 (Fen Shou Zong Yao Zai Yu Tian / Breakups Always Happen on Rainy Days)
Artist: Jacky Cheung (Jackie Cheung / 張學友 / 张学友)

Key: E major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar commonly uses C shapes, capo 4. Simplified C-shape charts often write the chorus as E–G#m–A–B; original-key charts use E–B–A–B.

**Intro**

`E – Am – E – B`

**Verse**

`E – G#m – C#m – B`
`A – G#m – F#m – B – E`

First verse sometimes uses E7 in place of B at the end of line 1.

**Pre-chorus**

`F#m – C#m – A – G#m7`
`G#7 – C#m – A – B`

**Chorus**

`E – B – A – B`
`E – B – A – B – E`

Last chorus ending: `A – B – E`.

**Turnaround**

`Am – E – B`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Turnaround → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Chorus
