I Look to You
Artist: Whitney Houston

Key: E major, 2/2 (power ballad; half note = 52). 2009 title track, written by Robert Kelly. Musicnotes original published key is E major; Wikipedia quotes the same E – E/D# – C#m7 loop. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original piano/choir arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar usually plays E-shape chords with no capo, or capo 2 and plays in D. Ultimate Guitar and Chordify charts in E match the record.
