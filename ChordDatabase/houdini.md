Houdini
Artist: Dua Lipa

Key: D minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction of the studio single; the original bass-and-synth arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Musicnotes publishes D minor. Ultimate Guitar and Chordify hear the disco groove as `Dm – Gm – C` (UG spells the back half `Csus2 – C`; Chordify often prints `Dsus4` over a D pedal). Many guitar charts substitute `Dm – Bb – Am – C`, which fits the melody but changes the bass. Guitar usually stays in D minor with no capo.

**Intro**

`Dm`

Drums and bass on D minor, then the chorus loop.

**Chorus**

`Dm – Gm – C`

The single opens on the hook. Keep `Csus2` as a passing color into `C`. If a beginner chart uses `Dm – Bb – Am – C`, that is the simplified substitute, not the bass the record plays.

**Verse**

`Dm – Gm – C`

Same groove as the chorus; some reductions sit on `Dm – C` and treat `Gm` as optional.

**Post-Chorus**

`Dm – Gm – C`

The “if you’re good enough” figure stays on the chorus loop. The last pass before the breakdown sometimes walks `Bb`.

**Bridge**

`Bb – Am – C`

Breakdown over `Bb – Am` with `C` as the lift; this is where the `Bb`/`Am` guitar charts actually match the record.

**Outro**

`Dm – Gm – C`
`Dm`

The chorus loop returns, then a D-minor tag.

**Form**

Intro → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Post-Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus → Outro
