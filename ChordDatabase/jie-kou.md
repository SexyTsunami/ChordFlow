藉口 (Jie Kou / Excuse)
Artist: Jay Chou (周杰倫 / 周杰伦)

Key: D major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar often uses capo 2 and plays in C.

**Intro**

`Gm – D – Gm – D – Em – A`

**Verse**

`D – A – Bm – F#m`
`G – F#m – Bm – Em – A`
`D – A/C# – Bm – F#m`
`G – F#m – Bm – Em – A`

**Pre-chorus**

`C#7 – F#m – Bm – Bm/A`
`G – F#m – Bm – Em – A`

**Chorus** (two matching halves)

First half:
`D – A/C# – Bm – A – G – F#m – Bm – Em – A`

Second half:
`D – A/C# – Bm – A – G – F#m – Bm – Em – A – D`

**Outro**

`G – Gm – D – Bm – Em – A – D`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Outro
