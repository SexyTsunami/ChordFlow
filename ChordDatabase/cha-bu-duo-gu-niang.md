`F#m – A/E`

**Bridge**

`F#m – E`
`Dmaj7 – A – C#7`

Later bridge lines hold C#7 an extra bar.

**Outro**

`F#m – A/E – Dmaj7 – A`

**Form**

Chorus → Rap verse → Chorus → Rap verse → Chorus → Bridge → Outro
