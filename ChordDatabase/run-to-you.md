Run to You
Artist: Whitney Houston

Key: A-flat major, 4/4 (about 80 BPM). This is Houston's Bodyguard ballad (Allan Rich / Jud Friedman), not Bryan Adams's song of the same name. Musicnotes original published key is A-flat major. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar usually uses capo 1 and plays in G (the E-Chords shape), or capo 4 and plays in E. Ultimate Guitar listings in A-flat match the record; the last chorus lifts a whole step to B-flat.
