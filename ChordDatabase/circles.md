Circles
Artist: Post Malone

Key: C major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar stays in C with no capo. Ultimate Guitar, E-Chords, and Chordify agree on C major. The studio piano loop is `Cmaj7 – G/B – Fmaj7 – Fm`; E-Chords simplifies `G/B` to `G` and the sevenths to triads. Some phrases swap the last chord to `Gmaj7` instead of the borrowed `Fm`.

**Intro**

`Cmaj7 – G/B – Fmaj7 – Fm`

**Verse**

`Cmaj7 – G/B – Fmaj7 – Fm`

**Chorus**

`Cmaj7 – G/B – Fmaj7 – Fm`

Later “can’t let go” turns sometimes replace `Fm` with `Gmaj7`. Beginner charts print `C – G – F – Fm`.

**Bridge**

`Cmaj7 – G/B – Fmaj7 – Fm`

Same loop; the vocal sits more spoken over the borrowed `Fm`.

**Outro**

`Cmaj7 – G/B – Fmaj7 – Fm`

The circle holds to the last “run away” hits; the final chord is often a plain `C`.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus → Outro
