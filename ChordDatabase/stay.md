STAY
Artist: The Kid LAROI, Justin Bieber

Key: Db major, 4/4 (often felt in 2 at about 170). Official sheet music is in Db. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original production adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar often uses capo 1 and plays `F – G – Am – Em`. Some easy charts start on `Em – D – G – C` with capo 1, which is a different rotation and not the usual chorus start.

**Intro**

`Gb – Ab – Bbm – Fm`

**Verse**

`Ab – Db`
`Gb – Ab – Bbm – Fm`

Early verse lines sit on Ab and Db before locking into the main loop.

**Chorus**

`Gb – Ab – Bbm – Fm`

Same four-chord loop for the rest of the hook.

**Bridge**

`Ab – Bbm`
`Gb – Ab – Bbm – Fm`

Then back to the chorus loop.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus
