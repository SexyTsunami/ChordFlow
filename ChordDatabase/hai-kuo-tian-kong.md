海闊天空 (Hai Kuo Tian Kong / Boundless Oceans, Vast Skies)
Artist: Beyond (黃家駒 / 黄家驹 / Wong Ka Kui)

Also written 海阔天空. Cantonese original from 樂與怒; the Mandarin remake sits in the same concert key. Key: F major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar commonly uses C shapes, capo 5. Guitarworld album charts, ComeGetChord, Chord4, and Chordyes agree on F. Ultimate Guitar easy charts sit in G (open G, or C shapes capo 7) and are a whole step above the studio cut, not concert pitch. Denser charts use Bbadd9, Fmaj7, and Csus4 in place of Bb, F, and C.

**Intro**

`F – Bbadd9 – Eb – Bbadd9 – Csus4 – C`

**Verse**

`F – C – Dm – Bb`
`F – C – Bb – F`

Second verse uses the same loop. The short verse after the first chorus is the opening four bars again.

**Chorus**

`Bb – C – F`
`Bb – C – F`
`Bb – C – F – C/E – Dm7`
`Bb – C – F`

The first chorus sometimes tags `Eb` in place of the last F before the verse reprise. Later choruses stay in F; the last line is often sung twice.

**Solo**

`Bb – C – Dm – Bb`
`Bb – C – F – Bb`
`Dm – Gm – D – Dm`

Studio guitar solo after the second chorus. Chord4 and ComeGetChord agree on the Bb–C–Dm frame; the D major bar is the common rock-turnaround into the bridge.

**Bridge**

`Dm – C – Bb – C`
`Dm – C – F`

**Outro**

`Bb – C – Dm7 – Bb – C – F`

Closing guitar over a chorus vamp; live cuts stretch this into a longer solo.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Solo → Bridge → Chorus → Outro
