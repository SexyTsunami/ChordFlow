IDGAF
Artist: Dua Lipa

Key: E minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original guitar-and-piano arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Wikipedia, Hooktheory, Ultimate Guitar, and GuitarTuna agree on E minor. Wikipedia writes the core as `Em – D – G/Bm – C`: bars alternate `Em – D – G – C` and `Em – D – Bm – C`. Guitar stays in E minor with no capo. Charts that stay on `Em – D – G – C` the whole way omit the `Bm` answer bars.

**Intro**

`Em – D – G – C`

Palm-muted guitar riff.

**Verse**

`Em – D – G – C`
`Em – D – Bm – C`

First phrase uses `G`; the answering phrase uses `Bm` before `C`.

**Pre-Chorus**

`Em – D – G – C`
`Em – D – Bm – C`

Same two cells as the verse.

**Chorus**

`Em – D – G – C`
`Em – D – Bm – C`

**Post-Chorus**

`Em – D – G – C`
`Em – D – Bm – C`

**Bridge**

`G – C – Em – D – Bm – C`

Opens on `G` instead of `Em`, then the verse cells return and the pre-chorus tag (“cut you off”) leads back.

**Outro**

`Em – D – G – C`
`Em – D – Bm – C`

Final chorus and post-chorus on the same loop.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Post-Chorus → Bridge → Chorus → Post-Chorus
