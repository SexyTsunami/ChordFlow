Mates of Soul
Artist: Manuel the Band

Acoustic cover of Taylor John Williams’s The Mates of Soul (not that G-major studio/The Voice cut). Key: F# major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. songdata.io tags the 2020 acoustic single F# major (Camelot 2B); detectors also print ~201 BPM, which is double-time for a ~100 feel. ChordU, E-Chords, and KhmerChords agree the Williams original is G on `G – Em – C – D` and `Am`; this cover sits a half step below that, so the same shapes concert as `F# – D#m – B – C#` and `G#m`. Guitar usually uses capo 2 and plays in E (`E – C#m – A – B`, sounding F#). Easy open-G charts are the Williams key, not this recording.

**Verse**

`F# – D#m – B – F#`

Second phrase often walks `B – C# – F#` instead of landing `B – F#`.

**Chorus**

`F# – G#m – B – C# – F#`

Later cycles add the matching second half on the same loop.

**Outro**

`F# – D#m – B – F#`

**Form**
