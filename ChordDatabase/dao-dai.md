倒帶 (Dao Dai / Rewind)
Artist: Jay Chou (周杰倫 / 周杰伦)

Jay wrote and produced the song (lyrics 方文山) around the 葉惠美 period; the studio hit is 蔡依林 (Jolin Tsai / 蔡依林) on 城堡. This file is the popular Jay-sung concert cut (2004 無與倫比 and later lives). Key: B major, last chorus in C, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Guitar commonly uses A shapes, capo 2 (`F#m – A – Dmaj7`, sounding `G#m – B – Emaj7`). Daydayguitar labels the Jay live Abm then Am (relative minors of B then C). Jolin’s Chord4 / 吉他世界 studio cut is the same progression a half step higher: C, last chorus Db. Tune down a half step, or play these B-then-C chords, to match Jay.

**Intro**

`G#m – Emaj7 – B – F#sus4 – F#`

**Verse**

`B – A – G#m – Emaj7`
`B – A – G#m – Em – F#`

**Pre-chorus**

`G#m – Emaj7 – G#m – A`
`C#m – G#m – A – F#`

**Chorus** (two matching halves)

First half:
`B – G#m – C#m – Em – F#`

Second half:
`B – G#m – C#m – E – F# – B`

Denser Jay-live charts walk the bass `B – F#/A# – G#m – B/F# – C#m – D#m7 – Em`.

**Interlude**

`B – A – G#m – Em`

**Final chorus** (up a half step)

`C – Am – Dm – Fm – G`
`C – Am – Dm – F – G – C`

Same progression as Jolin’s last chorus, just starting from C instead of Db.

**Outro**

`C – Bb – Am – Fm`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Interlude → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Chorus (in C) → Outro
