Timeless
Artist: The Weeknd, Playboi Carti

Key: B minor, 4/4. Klangio and original-key piano reductions list B minor; Chordify often labels the relative D major. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Ultimate Guitar’s no-capo chart is `G – Em – Bm – F#m` in concert pitch. Guitar often uses capo 2 and plays `F – Dm – Am – Em` (same concert key). Easy no-capo F-shape charts sit a whole step below the record. Beginner tabs flatten `Gmaj7` to `G`; keep the major-seventh color when the pad is holding G in the bass under `Bm` / `F#m`.

**Intro**

`G – Em – Bm – F#m`

Two cycles, then a short `G – F#m` tag into the first verse. Pads often color `G` as `Gmaj7`.

**Verse**

`F#m – G`

Playboi Carti’s verse (and The Weeknd’s later verse) is mostly this two-chord vamp. Some reductions write `Bm` on the downbeat and treat `F#m` as the walk-off.

**Chorus**

`Em – Bm – F#m – G`
`F#m – G`

The “ever since I was a kid” hook opens on `Em – Bm`, then snaps back to the verse’s `F#m – G` for “timeless.”

**Outro**

`F#m – G`

The title chant stays on the verse vamp to the end.

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Outro
