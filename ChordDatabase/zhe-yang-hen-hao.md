這樣很好 (Zhe Yang Hen Hao / Isha's Song)
Artist: Eason Chan (陳奕迅 / 陈奕迅), Arcane, League of Legends Music

Also written 这样很好. Mandarin theme for Arcane season 2 (Isha’s Song); billed on the soundtrack as Arcane, League of Legends Music, and Eason Chan. Key: C major, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. No capo. Chord4 and guitarworld agree on C. Pre-chorus/chorus bass often walks C–F/C–C–Gm/Bb; some charts write Bbsus2 in place of Gm/Bb. The Ab color in the pre-chorus and chorus is a borrowed bVI.
