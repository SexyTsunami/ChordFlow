Killing Me Softly With His Song
Artist: Fugees, Ms. Lauryn Hill

Key: E minor, 4/4. This is the common guitar/piano reduction of The Score studio cut; the original arrangement adds extra passing tones and inner voices. Musicnotes’ original published key is E minor; Hooktheory agrees. No capo. The Fugees cut opens on the chorus. Popular Am guitar charts (`Am – Dm – G – C`) are a fourth above concert; play those shapes with capo 7 to match the record, or keep the Em concert loop below. Roberta Flack’s original is a different arrangement/key and is not this chart.

**Intro**

`Em – Am – D – G`
`Em – Am – D – C`
`G – C – F – E`

**Chorus**

`Em – Am – D – G`
`Em – Am – D – C`
`G – C – F – E`

Denser charts color this `Em7 – Am7 – D7 – G`, then `Em – A – D – C`, landing `Fmaj7 – E` on the title. Beginner sheets flatten `A` to `Am` and `Fmaj7` to `F`.

**Verse**

`Am7 – D – Em`
`Am7 – D7 – G – B7`

**Outro**

`Em – Am – D – G`
`Em – Am – D – C`
`G – C – F – E`

**Form**

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Outro
